# Google Business Profile (GBP) setup

Source: BowTiedBills, "How To Set Up An SEO Optimized Google Business Profile" (X article, 2026-11-25, https://x.com/BowTiedBills/status/2125764026529624137). He sells this service, so the numbers are his own claims, not verified. The method matches Google's published guidelines; the case-study results (e.g. 169 of 169 grid points, calls 32 to 104 a month) are marketing, not evidence. Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors and BrightLocal's 2026 survey are the underlying sources, and we have not re-read them.

HomePowerRebate has no storefront, so we likely cannot hold a profile ourselves (virtual office, mailbox or home address = suspension risk). The main use is advising installers and energy advisors on the pages we rank, and auditing profiles we link to. Never create a profile for HPR with an address nobody works at.

## How Google picks the top 3
Relevance (categories, services, description, reviews), distance (searcher to business) and prominence (reviews, rating, links, web mentions).

Whitespark's top 10 map factors: 1 primary category, 2 distance, 3 keywords in business name, 4 physical address in the searched city, 5 open at time of search, 6 high rating (4 to 5), 7 address showing on profile, 8 additional categories, 9 number of reviews with text, 10 pin placed correctly. Eight of ten are set inside the profile or earned through it.

## Setup, in order

**1. Claim, verify, lock down**
- Search the name on Google Maps; claim an existing profile or create at business.google.com. Request access if an old agency or employee owns it.
- Have ready for verification (methods change; Google began testing government ID and EIN requests in Oct 2026): signage inside and outside, business licence, utility bill or lease in the business name, EIN (US).
- Primary owner is an email the business controls. Agencies are managers, never owner. Turn on all email notifications (Google emails owners about suggested edits and allows about 4 days to block them).
- Several offices: one profile per office with its own address, staff and hours, each linking to that office's own web page, with its own reviews, photos and posts.
- Suspended: do not create a new profile. Fix the cause (usually name, address or category), then file reinstatement with proof.

**2. Name, address, pin**
- Name exactly as on the sign, website and paperwork. No taglines, services, locations, phone numbers, URLs, hours or ALL CAPS. A keyword is fine only if it is the real legal name ("Smith Divorce Law"), never bolted on ("Smith Law | Best Divorce Lawyer Springfield").
- Show the address if clients visit. Write it as the post office does and use the identical form on the website and every directory.
- Service-area businesses can hide the address and add up to 20 service areas (cities or postal codes, within about 2 hours' drive; whole states or countries are not allowed since June 2025). Hybrids can show the address plus service areas.
- Trouble addresses: virtual office or mailbox service, old P.O. box, a home address shown when clients do not come there.
- Zoom in on Maps and drag the pin to the real entrance. Re-check the pin after any address change.

**3. Categories and services (moves rankings most)**
- Primary category = the most valuable service, as specific as possible ("Personal injury attorney", not "Law firm"). Test: "This business IS a...", never "has a...".
- Up to 9 additional categories, only real parts of the business. Fewest, most specific categories.
- To choose: search the main keyword on Maps, look at the top 3 profiles' categories (GMB Everywhere extension), match the primary of those ranking for the service wanted.
- List every service separately with a short description. Reviews can now attach to specific services.
- Products, if the category allows: photo, description, link.

**4. Hours, description, attributes, links**
- Real hours plus special hours for every holiday, set before it arrives. Open-at-search-time is a ranking factor.
- Description: 750 characters, only the first ~250 show. No links, prices or promotions. First sentence says what you do and where; then who you help, main services, experience, areas served. Keyword stuffing does nothing. Make every line true and specific.
- Fill every true attribute; Google keeps adding them, so re-check every few months.
- Website link: homepage for one location, that office's page for several. Add booking and social links.
- Messaging (WhatsApp, Messenger, Maps messaging): only turn on if someone answers fast.
- A local phone number the business controls, not a call-centre number.

**5. Photos, posts, reviews (weekly habit)**
- Real photos you took: outside (so people find it), inside, team, short videos. Never stock. Add new ones regularly.
- Posts a couple of times a week (case result or project with no client details, a common question answered, team photo, holiday schedule, offers). Google does not say posts help rankings; they show higher on the panel and keep the profile looking active. Phone numbers in posts must be verified.
- Reviews: rating and count of text reviews are two of the top 10 factors. Recency beats total: 80 reviews with 15 in the last 3 months beats 300 with 2. BrightLocal: 74% want reviews from the last 3 months, 31% need 4.5+ stars, 47% want 20+ reviews, 89% expect an owner reply.
- Ask every client at the moment the job ends, with a direct review link by email and text, follow up a few times. Never offer anything in return, never tell people what to write, no staff review quotas, no asking customers to name staff (April 2026 policy lines). A spike of fake reviews can get reviews paused and a warning banner shown, so steady beats sudden. Target about 5 a month in a quiet market, closer to 20 in the busiest cities.
- Reply to every review within 24 to 48 hours, written fresh, calm, no case details, never confirm someone is a client (privacy). Bad review: apologise, offer to talk offline with a name and number.

**6. AI answers and tracking**
- Q&A is gone (API shut Nov 3, 2025). AI answers (Gemini, Ask Maps launched March 2026, ChatGPT) draw on the profile, website, reviews, photos, social media and Reddit.
- Put the 10 most common client questions on the website with short direct answers. Keep profile and website identical on services, hours and areas.
- Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Ask Maps who they would recommend in the city and fix errors at the source.
- Check the "Collected Info" tab monthly (Google phones businesses to check hours and prices; wrong answers from whoever picks up can land on the profile).
- Citations (same name, address, phone on directories, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing, chamber of commerce, industry sites). Whitespark: 3 of the top 5 AI-visibility factors are citation factors. Quick check: search the exact business name in quotes for you and for a competitor and compare result counts.
- Track monthly: profile Performance (calls, website clicks, directions, views) and a grid scan from points around the office. Record the starting point. Expect gains in about 30 days, #1 in a 20 to 25 square mile radius around 3 months, up to 6 months in very competitive cities.

## Most common mistakes
Generic primary category; keywords added to the name; wrong or missing holiday hours; multi-location profile pointing at the homepage; old or stock photos; a review burst then nothing; reviews with no replies; name, address or phone differing between website and directories; notifications going to an unread inbox.

## Checklist
1. Claimed, verified, owner email controlled, notifications on
2. Real name, address shown or service areas set, pin on entrance
3. Specific primary category, real additional categories, every service listed with description
4. Regular and holiday hours, description (service and town first), attributes, correct website and booking links
5. Real photos on a schedule, posts, automatic review requests, replies within 48 hours
6. FAQ answers on the website, profile matches website, directory citations match, monthly performance and grid check

## Evaluating an installer's profile (to support them)
Use the Whitespark top 10 as the audit table. For each installer, mark every factor Set (a field they control), Earned (reviews) or Fixed (distance, address in the searched city), then say which gaps they can close this week. Eight of ten are in their hands; the two they cannot move are distance and being based in the city.

| # | Factor | Type | How to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary category | Set | Is it the trade ("Heat pump contractor", "Solar energy contractor") or generic ("Contractor", "HVAC contractor" for a heat pump-only shop)? |
| 3 | Keywords in name | Set | Real legal name only. Flag names stuffed with city or service as a suspension risk, never recommend adding one. |
| 5 | Open at search time | Set | Hours listed, holiday hours set, emergency or after-hours line stated truthfully. |
| 6 | Rating 4 to 5 | Earned | Rating on the Google listing. |
| 7 | Address showing | Set | Shown, or a service area set (up to 20, not whole provinces or states). |
| 8 | Additional categories | Set | Only real services (heat pump, solar, battery, insulation). |
| 9 | Reviews with text | Earned | Count, and how many in the last 90 days. |
| 10 | Pin correct | Set | Check on Maps. |
| 2, 4 | Distance, address in city | Fixed | Note only; cannot be fixed. |

Then add the non-score checks: every service listed with a description, real photos, owner replies on reviews, website link to the right page, phone local, name, address and phone identical on their website and directories.

Output a short note per installer: three fixes ranked by effort, in Grade 6 words, each tied to a field above. No score out of 100 and no guessing when a field could not be read; say it is unknown.

Rules for HPR:
- Support only. The audit never changes a ranking: rankings stay ordered by Google reviews, and money or help given never affects them.
- Read ratings, review counts and categories through the Places data we already hold under its attribution rules; do not store photos or reviews, and do not copy review text into notes.
- Installer outreach is paused (CLAUDE.md, 2026-11-08). Build and test the audit now; send nothing until it resumes. Never say "free" or mention price to installers.
- Heat pump rebate registration (BC registered contractor, NYS Clean Heat) is a separate check already on ranking cards; it is not a GBP factor.
