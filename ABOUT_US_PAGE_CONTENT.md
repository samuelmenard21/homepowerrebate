## Section 1: My Story

I got frustrated. Really frustrated.

Two years ago, I was researching heat pumps for my own home in BC. I called three installers, got three wildly different quotes, and none of them could explain what rebates actually applied to my situation. One said $6K, another said $12K, another said I didn't qualify. So I spent three weeks digging into BC Hydro's website, FortisBC's programs, federal incentives, and utility demand response credits. By the time I had an answer, I was exhausted.

Then I realized: if I—someone who works in tech and isn't afraid of confusing websites—had this much trouble, what about everyone else?

I started talking to homeowners. Dozens of conversations. The story was always the same: "I want to install solar" or "I need a new heating system," but they'd hit a wall trying to figure out rebates. Some gave up entirely. Others got fleeced because they didn't know what they qualified for.

That's when it clicked. This wasn't a homeowner problem. It was a rebate problem. Utilities, governments, and incentive programs aren't designed around how homeowners actually shop. They're fragmented, contradictory, and impossible to navigate alone.

So I started building HomePowerRebate to be the shortcut I wish I'd had.

## Section 6: How We Stay Unbiased

This is critical. Homeowners worry: "If I'm getting matched with installers, are you taking kickbacks?"

We don't. Here's how we stay honest:

1. **We publish our vetting criteria** — [Link to Installer Vetting Criteria page]. You can see exactly how we choose who makes the cut. Not all certified installers qualify.

2. **We require case studies** — Every installer on our platform shares 2+ completed jobs with before/after data. You can see their actual work.

3. **We match you with 2-3 options** — You're not locked into one installer. You get to choose.

4. **We enforce "no sales pressure"** — That means no surprise upgrades, no aggressive follow-ups, no gatekeeping. Our installers know we boot them if they pressure homeowners.

5. **We make our money by helping you** — Not by taking cuts from installers. Your success is our success.
