## VANCOUVER
**Utility:** BC Hydro
**Peak Sun Hours:** 3.5/day (cloudy, winter production lower)
**Home Types:** Pre-1980 wood frame, heritage homes, newer townhouses
**Common Heating:** Furnace, some electric resistance
**Grid Reliability:** 2-3 outages/year (winter storms, construction)

**Local Incentives:**
- Solar property tax exemption (permanent, $100-200/year savings)
- Strata energy retrofit programs (for condos)
- Expedited solar permitting (2-3 weeks vs. standard 6-8)

**Permitting Timeline:** 8-12 weeks total (city permit 2-3 weeks, Hydro 4-8 weeks)

**Special Notes:** 
- Condo solar is complex but possible with strata approval
- Weather favors heat pumps (milder winters)
- Peak Saver highly utilized for battery owners

## KELOWNA
**Utility:** BC Hydro
**Peak Sun Hours:** 5.0+/day (sunniest interior BC city)
**Home Types:** Mix of older homes and newer suburbs. Many pre-1980s.
**Common Heating:** Oil furnace common, natural gas furnace, some electric
**Grid Reliability:** Generally reliable (rare outages)

**Local Incentives:**
- City of Kelowna heat pump rebate ($500-1,000, income-qualified)
- Solar installations exempt from property tax increases
- Reduced electrical inspection fees for solar

**Permitting Timeline:** 6-10 weeks total (city permit 4-6 weeks, Hydro 3-5 weeks)

**Special Notes:**
- Oil-to-heat-pump conversions are major market here
- High solar potential makes payback 6-8 years (fastest in BC)
- Oil heating means larger heat pump rebates apply
- Summer cooling is concern—some customers add A/C capability

## VICTORIA
**Utility:** BC Hydro (some areas: CRD Utilities)
**Peak Sun Hours:** 4.0/day (mild, but more cloud cover than interior)
**Home Types:** Older heritage homes (pre-1960), bungalows, some newer townhouses
**Common Heating:** Furnace, some electric, older oil systems
**Grid Reliability:** Very reliable (rare outages, strong grid)

**Local Incentives:**
- City of Victoria climate action grants (varies annually)
- Temporary exemption from property tax on solar improvements
- Accelerated permitting for solar (similar to Vancouver)

**Permitting Timeline:** 8-12 weeks (city permit 3-4 weeks, Hydro 4-8 weeks)

**Special Notes:**
- Mildest BC winter climate—heat pump is less critical but still saves money
- Heritage home restrictions on roof access (affects solar placement)
- Strong community solar interest (many renters, apartment dwellers)
- Water heating is popular upgrade (high electric usage)
