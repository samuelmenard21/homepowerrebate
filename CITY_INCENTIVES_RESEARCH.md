# City-Level Incentives & Programs

## Types of Municipal Programs That Exist

### 1. **Property Tax Exemptions**
- Solar installations exempt from property tax assessment increases
- Some cities: 10-year exemption, some: permanent
- Example: Vancouver exempts solar from property tax hikes

### 2. **City Grants & Rebates**
- Direct cash grants for solar, heat pumps, batteries
- Usually smaller than provincial ($500-$2,000) but add up
- Example: Some BC cities offer $500-$1,000 for heat pump retrofits

### 3. **Expedited Permitting**
- Faster approval = saves money + time
- Some cities prioritize solar/heat pump permits
- Example: City processes solar permits in 2 weeks vs. standard 6-8

### 4. **Reduced Permitting Fees**
- Waived or reduced electrical inspection fees
- Waived building permit fees for certain upgrades
- Example: Might save $200-$500 per project

### 5. **Community Solar Programs**
- Allows renters/people with poor roof access to participate
- City aggregates demand, negotiates bulk pricing
- Example: Vancouver Community Solar program

### 6. **Cool Roof/Reflective Material Programs**
- Rebate for cool roofs (reduces cooling needs)
- Often paired with solar permits
- Example: Summer cooling incentives

### 7. **EV Charging Infrastructure**
- City rebates for Level 2 charger installation
- Public charging station grants
- Example: Subsidized charger installation in city homes

### 8. **Energy Audit Programs**
- City-funded energy audits (free or reduced cost)
- Leads to retrofit recommendations
- Example: City partners with local utilities

### 9. **Community Energy Programs**
- Municipal utilities offering better terms than Hydro
- Community Choice Aggregation (buying power collectively)
- Example: Some interior BC cities have municipal hydro

### 10. **Strata/Condo-Specific Programs**
- Easier approval process for multi-unit buildings
- Shared solar incentives
- Example: Vancouver's Strata Energy Retrofit Program

### 11. **First-Time Retrofit Incentives**
- Extra rebate if it's your first green upgrade
- Example: $500 bonus for first solar + heat pump combo

### 12. **Low-Income/Equity Programs**
- Extra rebates for low-income households
- Example: Some cities add $1-2K for income-qualified families

## How to Research City Incentives

### Quick Research Path (2-3 hours per city)

**Step 1: City Government Website**
- Google: "[CITY] solar incentive" + "[CITY] heat pump rebate"
- Look for: Sustainability dept, Energy dept, Climate action pages
- Check: Building permits page (often lists incentive details there)

**Step 2: City Energy/Sustainability Coordinator**
- Call: City hall main number, ask for "energy coordinator" or "sustainability officer"
- They often maintain a list of active programs
- Some cities email you a full incentive sheet

**Step 3: Local Utility**
- If BC Hydro: Check if that specific area has local programs
- If municipal utility: They usually have city-specific programs listed
- Example: Check [City] Hydro website directly

**Step 4: Ask the Installer**
- They'll know about local programs from running projects there
- **Add to installer brief:** "What city-specific incentives do your customers use most?"
- They're your best source of truth

**Step 5: Community Groups**
- Search: "[CITY] environmental commission" or "[CITY] climate action group"
- They often maintain updated incentive lists
- Example: Vancouver Renewable Energy Cooperative

## Pro Tips

- **Tax exemptions are big:** Many BC cities have them but don't advertise. Installers know these.
- **Permitting saves money:** A 2-week vs. 8-week process can save $1K+ in soft costs. Installers know how to trigger fast-track.
- **City programs change:** Review annually (usually Jan/Feb when budgets reset)
- **Ask installers first:** They're your best source before official research. Build it into the brief.
- **Strata programs are underutilized:** If you find one, feature it heavily (condo owners don't know they qualify)
