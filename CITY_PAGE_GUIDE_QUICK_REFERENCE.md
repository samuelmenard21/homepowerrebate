## City Pages: What Changes for Each City

### 1. Hero Section
**Copy:** "[City] Heat Pump Rebates"

### 2. Rebate Table (Section 1)
**Header:** "What [City] Homeowners Qualify For"

**Rows to update:**
- BC Hydro Heat Pump Rebate → $[X]–$[Y] *(verify with BC Hydro for your city)*
- BC Hydro Peak Saver → $[X]–$[Y] *(island cities only; coastal + interior cities don't have Peak Saver)*
- Federal Clean Energy Tax Credit → Up to $5,000 *(same for all BC cities)*
- Local / Municipal Incentives → $[X]–$[Y] *(varies by city; check with city)*
- Total Available → $[X]–$[Y]

**Rule of thumb:**
- Island cities (Victoria, Nanaimo, etc.): Include Peak Saver row
- Lower Mainland (Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, etc.): Same BC Hydro, no Peak Saver
- Interior (Kelowna, Kamloops, Penticton): Same BC Hydro (unless FortisBC), colder so shorter payback
- Northern (Fort St. John, Prince George): Check if FortisBC (different rebate table)

### 3. Why Homeowners Choose Heat Pumps (Section 2)
**Template opening:**
> "Most [CITY] homeowners I've talked to are replacing 15+ year old furnaces. A new furnace is $4–7K with zero rebates. A heat pump costs about the same installed, but [UTILITY] gives you $[X]K back. That cuts your upfront cost to basically zero."

**Customize:**
- [CITY] → Your city name
- [UTILITY] → BC Hydro (most cities) or FortisBC (northern)
- [X]K → Rebate amount (from table above)
- Savings amount → Varies by heating system; use typical range ($250–400/year for furnace users, $600+/year for baseboard)
- Add region-specific context:
  - **Island cities:** Add Peak Saver + grid resilience mention
  - **Interior cities:** Add solar opportunity + colder winters = faster payback
  - **Mainland:** Keep general; emphasize competition from other installers

### 4. How It Works Timeline (Section 3)

### 5. Trusted Installers (Section 4)
**This is the only section that requires real research.**

**For each installer card, you need:**
- Name + company
- Phone + website
- Service area (neighborhoods or radius)
- Response time (e.g., "24 hours")
- Expertise tags (✓ Heat Pump | ✓ Mini-Split | ✗ Solar, etc.)
- About Them (1–2 sentences on their specialty)
- **2+ Case Studies** (this is critical for trust):
  - Location (neighborhood in city)
  - What was replaced (furnace → HP, oil heat → HP, etc.)
  - System type (air-source, mini-split, ground-source)
  - Cost after rebate ($X,XXX)
  - Annual savings ($XXX–$YYY/year)
- Customer testimonial (1–2 sentences, real quote)
- "Get a Quote" link

**Where to find installers:** 
- Google Search: "[City] HPCN certified heat pump installer"
- BC Hydro rebate program (some list approved installers)
- Ask other installers who they recommend
- Look for Google reviews (4.5+ stars is good)
- Call them directly (ask for case studies + references)

- Gather installer data for 3-4 cities (highest priority: Victoria, Vancouver, Nanaimo, Kelowna)
- Write/edit 3-4 city pages using Victoria template
- Write/edit 3-4 city guides using Victoria template

### Week 2:
- Gather installer data for remaining 10-11 cities
- Write/edit remaining 10-11 city pages
- Write/edit remaining 10-11 city guides

### Time Savings:
- Using templates cuts writing time ~50% per page
- Batch writing (doing similar pages back-to-back) reduces context switching
- If you can gather installer data in parallel, total time = 2 weeks vs. 3-4 weeks
