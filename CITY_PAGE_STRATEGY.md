# City Page Value Strategy

## What Each City Page Should Deliver

Beyond national rebates, each city page creates local advantage by answering these questions:

### 1. **Local Utility Differences**
**BC Hydro vs FortisBC vs other local utilities**
- Different rebate amounts
- Different application processes
- Different timelines for approval
- Example: "If you're on FortisBC in Kamloops, here's what changes..."

### 2. **Local Weather & Solar Data**
**Real numbers for that specific city**
- Peak sun hours per day (December vs June)
- Average cloud cover
- Winter solar potential (many homeowners don't realize winter sun helps)
- Example: "Kelowna gets 300+ days of sun vs Vancouver's 150 days—your payback is 2 years faster"

### 3. **Local Permitting & Timeline**
**What's the real timeline in that city?**
- Does the city require permits? How long?
- Utility interconnection timeline
- Inspection requirements
- Example: "Vancouver's interconnection takes 6 weeks vs Abbotsford's 3 weeks"

### 4. **Local Installer Context**
**Why THIS installer in THIS city**
- Years of experience in the community
- Specialization (solar-heavy? heat pump experts? battery specialists?)
- Local reputation (Google reviews, community standing)
- Typical projects they've done
- Example: "They've installed 200+ systems in Kelowna and know every roofline"

### 5. **Local Cost Reality**
**What does it actually cost in that city?**
- Typical solar system cost (8 kW)
- Labor costs (rural vs urban)
- Soft costs (permits, interconnection fees)
- Example: "Expect $25-30K for an 8kW system in Vancouver, $22-26K in Kamloops"

### 6. **Local Case Studies**
**Real projects from real people in that city**
- Before/after photos
- Customer quote ("I saved $X per year")
- Specific system specs (kW, kWh, brands)
- Timeline (how long did it take?)
- Example: A real Burnaby homeowner's project with actual numbers

### 7. **Local Issues & Solutions**
**What's unique about that area's homes?**
- Roof age/condition (older homes in Vancouver vs newer suburbs)
- Heating type (oil furnace common in some regions)
- Grid reliability (outage-prone areas = battery makes sense)
- Example: "Many Nanaimo homes still have oil heat—heat pump replacements are a major opportunity"

### 8. **Local Community Programs**
**Other incentives beyond provincial/federal**
- City grants or rebates
- Strata/condo programs (if applicable)
- Community solar projects
- EV charging incentives
- Example: "City of Vancouver offers additional incentives for heat pump upgrades"

### 9. **Local Permitting Hacks**
**What installers know that saves homeowners time**
- Pre-approval letters to speed things up
- Common rejection reasons (and how to avoid them)
- Best time of year to apply
- Example: "Apply in March, not December—the queue is 8 weeks shorter"

**For Installers:**

- SEO benefit (city + solar = search traffic)

**For You:**

## Content Creation Strategy

### Option A: Get Installer to Provide Local Data
**Timeline: 1 week per city**
- Email installer the brief (see below)
- They provide: projects, photos, local insights, testimonials
- You write the page

### Option B: You Research + Installer Edits
**Timeline: 2 weeks per city**
- You pull: weather data, permitting info, cost estimates
- Installer validates and adds anecdotes
- Faster, but less personal

### Option C: Hybrid (Recommended)
**Timeline: 1-2 weeks per city**
- You build template with standard data
- Installer adds: projects, photos, personal stories
- You publish with their byline

## What Makes a City Page Rank

Google loves:
1. **Unique local data** (not copied from national site)
2. **Real photos + names** (authentic, not stock)
3. **Entity depth** (mentions specific streets, neighborhoods, local weather)
4. **Regular updates** (new case studies, seasonal tips)

Example search results you want:
- "[City name] solar rebate" → Your page
- "[City name] heat pump installers" → Your page
- "[City name] solar costs" → Your page
