
## California pages, source registry and link hygiene (added 2026-10-02)

- **Shared builder, hand-written prose.** California city and category pages come from `scripts/build_ca_pages.py` in the same layout as the Ontario and BC pages (hero badge, rebate cards, local sections, installers, claim steps, visible FAQ with schema). The prose for each page is hand-written in `data/ca/pages/<city>/<category>.json` (and `index.json` for the city hub); never a fill-in template (see the `hpr-category-page-localization` and `hpr-no-doorway-pages` skills). The builder refuses a page under 750 words, with fewer than 4 FAQ items or 3 outside references, with more than one em dash, or containing "waitlist", "unclear" or "fully subscribed" (PowerScore reads those as a status). A page without a JSON file is not built; its URL redirects to the city hub. Cities that share one utility and offer identical amounts (Rancho Cordova and Folsom share SMUD with Sacramento) get a hub only. Run `python3 scripts/check_duplicate_content.py --near-duplicate-threshold 0.90` before committing. Add `git add -f data/ca` (data/ is gitignored).
- **Research first.** Read each program page in the browser pane (many utility sites block simple fetchers), put every amount in `data/verified-facts/*.json` with `status`, `verified_on` and `source_url`, then write the page. Re-read the page when you cite it; figures change (SMUD, LADWP and BWP all had stale numbers here).
- **Source registry for the monthly check.** `python3 scripts/build_source_registry.py` writes `data/rebate-sources.json` (every fact source, every page reference, plus extra program pages). Re-run it after adding facts or pages and `git add -f data/rebate-sources.json`. The `monthly-rebate-check` scheduled task (Step 1B) re-opens each entry.
- **Link hygiene after any generator run:** `python3 scripts/fix_redirected_links.py`, `python3 scripts/follow_official_sources.py` (official program sources are normal links, not nofollow), then `python3 scripts/check_internal_links.py` (fails on missing pages, links to redirected URLs, and pages shadowed by a redirect), then `python3 scripts/generate_sitemap.py`.
- **Hub pipeline order for California changes:** build_ca_pages, then `python3 scripts/build_hubs.py --powerscore`, then build_home_powerscore, build_home_feed, then the link steps above. Old generators `build_ca_la.py`, `build_ca_sf.py`, `build_ca_sj.py` and `build_ca_sacramento.py` skip any city that has a folder in `data/ca/pages`.

## Page templates and shared CSS (added 2026-10-11)

- **Template families.** Generated pages of one kind share a design file: `_partials/templates/article.css` (category, city hub, program, blog, guide and question pages: body class `tpl-article`) and `_partials/templates/ranking.css` (installer rankings: `tpl-ranking`). ## Energy advisors and home energy auditors (added 2026-10-17)
A new category beside the installer trades: registered energy advisors in Canada (service `energy-advisor`) and home energy auditors in the US (service `energy-auditor`). Advisors explain rebate stacking and are trusted referrers, so the pages offer them the calculator embed, a printable city rebate sheet and the badge.
- **Who is listed comes from official registers, never a Google search.** Canada: NRCan's table of licensed EnerGuide service organizations for existing homes (`data/nrcan-service-organizations.json`, 184 rows with the postal FSAs each serves; read in the browser pane, the page is a DataTable). US: RESNET's 2026 HERS rating companies PDF by state (`data/resnet-rating-companies-2026.json`). A Google "energy advisor" search returned HVAC dealers, financial planners and home inspectors; `verify_energy_advisors.py` (website check) is kept only as a tool, not the source. Advisor teams that tell us they work under an organization's licence go in `data/energy-advisors-extra.json` (Thrive Energy, Kelowna).
- **`scripts/build_energy_advisor_list.py`** maps each Canadian city to its FSAs (postal codes in our installer addresses, plus `CITY_FSAS`), lists every organization serving them, and looks up one Google listing per organization (about 450 Places calls for both countries; ratings are an Enterprise Places field with ~1,000 free calls a month, so never search per city; `--offline` reuses `data/energy-advisor-places.json`). Hand-rejected false matches are in `REJECT`; a branch office (AmeriSpec of Canada (Kingston)) only counts its own branch's listing. US companies are listed for every one of our cities within 80 km of their office. Writes `installers/<prefix>energy-advisor-installers-real.csv` / `<state>-energy-auditor-installers-real.csv` and `data/energy-advisor-directory.json` (every company, matched or not).
- **`scripts/scan_advisor_websites.py`** reads each company's website once and records which services it mentions (EnerGuide, blower door, HERS...) in `data/energy-advisor-sites.json`. Profiles show them as tags; never copy their wording.
- **`scripts/build_advisor_directory.py`** writes `/energy-advisors/` and `/energy-auditors/` (every company, indexable) and one profile per company (`/energy-advisors/<slug>/`, noindex like installer profiles): full profile with rating, address and city ranks when Google matched, basic profile otherwise, plus an "Is this your company?" box. Writes `data/energy-advisor-profiles.json`, which `build_installer_rankings.py` reads to link ranking cards. Run it before the rankings.
- **Ranking pages** `/installers/<region>/<city>/energy-advisor|energy-auditor/` come from `build_installer_rankings.py` (no quote form: the lead router only knows installer trades; a "for advisors" box with embed, sheet and badge; NRCan cost $600 to $1,000 for both evaluations, fact general-22). Lists overlap across a province, so the 90% duplicate rule leaves one indexable page per province or state and the rest noindex; the directory pages carry the search value.
- **`scripts/build_rebate_sheets.py`** writes `/rebate-sheet/<region>/<city>/` (164 printable one-page sheets, noindex) and `/rebate-sheet/` from `calculator/data/<code>.json`, applying each city's calculator `set` answers (utility, north...) so Kelowna gets FortisBC programs. Every installer ranking page links its city sheet.
- Nova Scotia has no NRCan organization (book through Efficiency NS); Newfoundland has too few rated advisors for a ranking. Order after data changes: build_energy_advisor_list, scan_advisor_websites, build_rebate_sheets, build_advisor_directory, build_installer_rankings, post_steps.

## Monthly data release (added 2026-10-22)
