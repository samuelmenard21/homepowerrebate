
## California pages, source registry and link hygiene (added 2026-10-02)

- **Shared builder, hand-written prose.** California city and category pages come from `scripts/build_ca_pages.py` in the same layout as the Ontario and BC pages (hero badge, rebate cards, local sections, installers, claim steps, visible FAQ with schema). The prose for each page is hand-written in `data/ca/pages/<city>/<category>.json` (and `index.json` for the city hub); never a fill-in template (see the `hpr-category-page-localization` and `hpr-no-doorway-pages` skills). The builder refuses a page under 750 words, with fewer than 4 FAQ items or 3 outside references, with more than one em dash, or containing "waitlist", "unclear" or "fully subscribed" (PowerScore reads those as a status). A page without a JSON file is not built; its URL redirects to the city hub. Cities that share one utility and offer identical amounts (Rancho Cordova and Folsom share SMUD with Sacramento) get a hub only. Run `python3 scripts/check_duplicate_content.py --near-duplicate-threshold 0.90` before committing. Add `git add -f data/ca` (data/ is gitignored).
- **Research first.** Read each program page in the browser pane (many utility sites block simple fetchers), put every amount in `data/verified-facts/*.json` with `status`, `verified_on` and `source_url`, then write the page. Re-read the page when you cite it; figures change (SMUD, LADWP and BWP all had stale numbers here).
- **Source registry for the monthly check.** `python3 scripts/build_source_registry.py` writes `data/rebate-sources.json` (every fact source, every page reference, plus extra program pages). Re-run it after adding facts or pages and `git add -f data/rebate-sources.json`. The `monthly-rebate-check` scheduled task (Step 1B) re-opens each entry.
- **Link hygiene after any generator run:** `python3 scripts/fix_redirected_links.py`, `python3 scripts/follow_official_sources.py` (official program sources are normal links, not nofollow), then `python3 scripts/check_internal_links.py` (fails on missing pages, links to redirected URLs, and pages shadowed by a redirect), then `python3 scripts/generate_sitemap.py`.
- **Hub pipeline order for California changes:** build_ca_pages, build_powerscore, build_powerscore_page, build_hub_tops, build_hub_showcase, build_hub_tidy, apply_canonical_nav_footer, check_hub_layout, build_home_powerscore, build_home_feed, then the link steps above. Old generators `build_ca_la.py`, `build_ca_sf.py`, `build_ca_sj.py` and `build_ca_sacramento.py` skip any city that has a folder in `data/ca/pages`.

### SEO clean-up steps (added 2026-10-02)
Run after any generator, before the link steps: `python3 scripts/fix_seo_meta.py` (og:image uses og-image.jpg because social sites do not render SVG; Article author is Sam Menard as a Person with the same @id as the About page; indexable titles lose the " | HomePowerRebate" suffix when over 60 characters; duplicate titles get the state or province; installer profile pages carry no AggregateRating, which Google allows only for reviews collected on our own site) and `python3 scripts/link_orphans.py`. The author byline is Sam Menard, the founder named on /about. Googlebot gets 200 for the external scripts pages actually load (form-handlers*.js); other .js/.json URLs are 403 at Cloudflare, which does not affect rendering today. FAQ rich results are limited to government and health sites, so FAQPage markup keeps visible FAQs useful but will not expand in results.
Installer pages and Google Maps data (2026-10-02): Places ratings and reviews may be displayed with attribution but must not feed LocalBusiness, AggregateRating or Review markup. Profile pages therefore carry only BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, ranking pages say "Ratings and review counts from Google Maps", and `python3 scripts/fix_installer_places.py` cleans any page the generators wrote earlier.

Blog refresh (2026-10-02): `scripts/refresh_blog_post.py <slug>` rewrites a stale post from `data/blog-refresh/<slug>.json` (title, desc, h1, lead, sections, faq, refs, updated), with Sam Menard byline, Article and FAQPage JSON-LD. Figures must come from facts files or the post's refs. `git add -f data/blog-refresh`.

## BC insulation, windows and water-heater pages (added 2026-10-02)

`scripts/build_bc_pages.py` builds `/ca/bc/<city>/{insulation,windows,water-heater}/` (54 pages) from hand-written `data/bc/pages/<city>/<category>.json`, same layout and quality gate as the California builder (`git add -f data/bc`). Per-city facts come from `data/bc-housing-census2021.json` (Statistics Canada 2021 Census table 98-10-0233-01: households, share built before 1981, owner-occupied detached houses, apartments, renters; pulled with the WDS API by member id) and `city-climate-data.json`. Never write "orchard-era", "many homes have..." or other unsourced local colour. Facts used: bc-pages-8/9/10/21 (re-read 2026-10-02), bc-blog-24 (FortisBC), bc-blog-26 (Nanaimo loan), general-13/14/15. Program rules worth knowing: BC Hydro's Home Renovation Rebate is only for homes heated mainly by electricity (gas homes use FortisBC), condos and apartments are excluded, income-qualified households are told to use CleanBC ESP (which has no insulation or window rebates in 2026), the City of Vancouver is excluded from the window rebate (BC Hydro and FortisBC), Kelowna and Penticton use FortisBC electricity programs. The old BC pages said "up to $11,350" with federal grants and "CleanBC up to $5,500 for insulation"; both were wrong and are gone. Windows, water heater and insulation pages for other regions (ON, AB, MA, NY, PA, CO, VT, NS) still use the old template and are next. `_partials/nav-footer.html` no longer carries the build-note comment (it was stamped on every page).

## Utility and program pages (added 2026-10-02)

`scripts/build_utility_pages.py` builds `/programs/<slug>/` from hand-written `data/utilities/<slug>.json` (cards name a verified fact, so status, date and source come from the facts file) and the `/programs/` index (new utility pages plus the older hand-made program pages). Live: FortisBC, SMUD, LADWP, SCE, Pasadena, Glendale, Burbank, Riverside, San Jose Clean Energy, CleanPowerSF, San Diego Community Power, PG&E (12, with BC Hydro, CleanBC, Peak Saver, Mass Save, NYS Clean Heat, Xcel, PECO, PPL, Alberta and Efficiency NS already there). Same quality gate as the city builders (700 words, 4 FAQs, 3 outside links). Every California and BC city page now links to its utility page (`UTILITY_PAGE` in `build_ca_pages.py`; Kelowna and Penticton use FortisBC in `build_bc_pages.py`). To add a utility: read its pages, add facts, write the JSON, run the builder, `git add -f data/utilities`, regenerate the registry and sitemap. Not built yet (facts too thin or unverified): SDG&E, Efficiency Vermont (figures came from search excerpts, not the pages), Green Mountain Power, Con Edison and the other NY utilities, Enbridge, Save on Energy, Ava, Moreno Valley Utility.
