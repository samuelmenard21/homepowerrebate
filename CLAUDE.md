
## California pages, source registry and link hygiene (added 2026-10-02)

- **Shared builder, hand-written prose.** California city and category pages come from `scripts/build_ca_pages.py` in the same layout as the Ontario and BC pages (hero badge, rebate cards, local sections, installers, claim steps, visible FAQ with schema). The prose for each page is hand-written in `data/ca/pages/<city>/<category>.json` (and `index.json` for the city hub); never a fill-in template (see the `hpr-category-page-localization` and `hpr-no-doorway-pages` skills). The builder refuses a page under 750 words, with fewer than 4 FAQ items or 3 outside references, with more than one em dash, or containing "waitlist", "unclear" or "fully subscribed" (PowerScore reads those as a status). A page without a JSON file is not built; its URL redirects to the city hub. Cities that share one utility and offer identical amounts (Rancho Cordova and Folsom share SMUD with Sacramento) get a hub only. Run `python3 scripts/check_duplicate_content.py --near-duplicate-threshold 0.90` before committing. Add `git add -f data/ca` (data/ is gitignored).
- **Research first.** Read each program page in the browser pane (many utility sites block simple fetchers), put every amount in `data/verified-facts/*.json` with `status`, `verified_on` and `source_url`, then write the page. Re-read the page when you cite it; figures change (SMUD, LADWP and BWP all had stale numbers here).
- **Source registry for the monthly check.** `python3 scripts/build_source_registry.py` writes `data/rebate-sources.json` (every fact source, every page reference, plus extra program pages). Re-run it after adding facts or pages and `git add -f data/rebate-sources.json`. The `monthly-rebate-check` scheduled task (Step 1B) re-opens each entry.
- **Link hygiene after any generator run:** `python3 scripts/fix_redirected_links.py`, `python3 scripts/follow_official_sources.py` (official program sources are normal links, not nofollow), then `python3 scripts/check_internal_links.py` (fails on missing pages, links to redirected URLs, and pages shadowed by a redirect), then `python3 scripts/generate_sitemap.py`.
- **Hub pipeline order for California changes:** build_ca_pages, build_powerscore, build_powerscore_page, build_hub_tops, build_hub_showcase, build_hub_tidy, apply_canonical_nav_footer, check_hub_layout, build_home_powerscore, build_home_feed, then the link steps above. Old generators `build_ca_la.py`, `build_ca_sf.py`, `build_ca_sj.py` and `build_ca_sacramento.py` skip any city that has a folder in `data/ca/pages`.

Run after any generator, before the link steps: `python3 scripts/fix_seo_meta.py` (og:image uses og-image.jpg because social sites do not render SVG; Article author is Sam Menard as a Person with the same @id as the About page; indexable titles lose the " | HomePowerRebate" suffix when over 60 characters; duplicate titles get the state or province; installer profile pages carry no AggregateRating, which Google allows only for reviews collected on our own site) and `python3 scripts/link_orphans.py`. Ontario insulation (2026-10-03): the 25 `/ca/on/<city>/insulation/` pages are built by the same `scripts/build_on_pages.py` from `data/on/pages/<city>/insulation.json`. Two streams: the attic stream (no assessment; 50% of after-tax cost up to $1,250 attic or $750 cathedral/flat roof; must insulate 100% of the roof assembly; participating insulation contractor; installed by Nov 30 and entered by Dec 31, 2026; facts on-21) and the assessment stream (tables in on-22: attic $1,500/$1,200/$900, walls $1,200/$2,100/$3,600, basement walls $900/$1,500, slab $500, header $300, crawl space and exposed floor amounts, insulation total $7,700, semis and end rows 75% and middle rows 50% of wall and basement maximums, minimum area shares, pro-rated). Ontario heat pump (2026-10-03): the 25 `/ca/on/<city>/heat-pump/` pages come from `scripts/build_on_pages.py` and `data/on/pages/<city>/heat-pump.json`, from the program's May 2025 Heat Pump Stream requirements and its heat pump page (facts on-0/1/2/18/23). Rates by prior heating fuel: electric resistance, oil, propane or wood $1,250/ton up to $7,500 (ground-source $2,000/ton up to $12,000); Enbridge gas or any rental $500/ton up to $2,000 (ground-source $3,000 flat). Tons = rated heating capacity at 8.3C / 12,000 BTU/h. Rules to keep: homes with an existing space-heating heat pump are not eligible; electric, oil and wood homes only qualify with NRCan sizing options C or D; oil tanks must be removed or emptied by a TSSA contractor; up to three outdoor units count toward one rebate; written pre-approval before install; contractor files within 30 days; cheque within 60 days. No end date is stated for this stream. Ontario water heater, solar, battery and the remaining categories still use the old template.

## Solar roof check (added 2026-10-05, NOT LIVE yet)
`/roof-check/` (built by `scripts/build_roof_check.py`, noindex until `data/roof-check/config.json` has a `worker_url`) takes an address and shows panels, yearly output and the verified solar rebates for the province or state. Roof data comes from the Google Solar API and Geocoding API through `roof-check-worker/` (a separate Worker, private in `build_public.py`): it holds the API key as a secret, counts lookups in KV and refuses past `MONTHLY_CAP` (8,000; each lookup is one Geocoding and one Building Insights request and Google's free allowance is 10,000 of each a month), limits each visitor to 6 an hour, and stores nothing from Google (Solar API policy forbids caching). Google budget alerts do not stop spending; the hard stops are the Worker counter and the daily quotas set on each API in Google Cloud (about 250 a day). Rules are in `data/roof-check/regions.json`, each program naming verified facts; regions without a verified rule show roof numbers and say no rebate is verified (never invent one). Required attribution: the words "Google Maps" and "Source: Includes solar data from Google". Coverage (checked 2026-10-05 at city centres against Google's 20260506 coverage maps): no data at Timmins, Fort St. John, Penticton, Prince George and Cape Breton; existing-solar detection covers all MA cities but only 4 of 25 Ontario cities. Deploy steps are in `roof-check-worker/README.md`. Open questions: Google's terms on storing derived results with a lead, and the privacy page should mention the one-hour IP counter.
