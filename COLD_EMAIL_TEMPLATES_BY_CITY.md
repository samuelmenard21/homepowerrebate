## Mail Merge Options

### Option A: Gmail (Fastest for <50 emails)
1. Copy template into a Gmail draft
2. Use Gmail's built-in merge (Tools > Mail merge)
3. Upload CSV with [Name], [City], [Email]
4. Hit send

### Option B: Mailchimp (Best for tracking)
1. Create campaign in Mailchimp
2. Upload `INSTALLER_LIST_FINAL.csv`
3. Add subject line template with `*|FNAME|*` + `*|CITY|*`
4. Send campaign
5. Track opens + clicks in Mailchimp dashboard

### Option C: Manual (If <30 emails)
1. Ctrl+F / Cmd+F to replace [Name] and [City] for each installer
2. Copy email body
3. Paste into Gmail compose
4. Send
5. Record in spreadsheet
