## Messaging Principles (Applied Here)

✅ No "BC-specific" language in headlines or core copy
✅ Regional callouts only in top nav and "expansion" section
✅ Contractions used throughout (you're, don't, it's)
✅ Short sentences (most under 15 words)
✅ Specific examples ("2-3 installers," "$X million," not "many" or "a lot")
✅ Explains what we control vs. what we don't
✅ CTA is specific ("Calculate my rebate" not "Learn more")
✅ Addresses homeowner concerns head-on (sales pressure, gatekeeping)

## A/B Testing Notes

When you're ready to test, consider variations on:
1. **CTA text:** "Calculate my rebate" vs. "See my rebates" vs. "Get matched"
