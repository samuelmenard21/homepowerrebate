## Targeting Strategy

**Where to find HPCN installers:**
1. **HPCN Registry** — Search by city: https://www.energyadvisors.ca/ (official list)
2. **Google Search** — "HPCN heat pump [city]" or "solar installer [city] certified"
3. **BC Hydro Rebate Pages** — Many link to approved installers
4. **Local trade groups** — Ask HVAC suppliers, electrical wholesalers for referrals
5. **LinkedIn** — Search "HPCN" + city name, or "heat pump installer" + city

**Vetting before outreach:**
- ✓ Must be HPCN-certified
- ✓ Must have 4.5+ Google rating (or close)
- ✓ Must have 10+ reviews (shows volume)
- ✓ Must operate in your city (no multi-province networks)

---

## Outreach Cadence

**Week 1:**
- Identify 5-8 HPCN installers per city
- Send initial email (Template #1)
- Wait 3-5 days

**Day 3-5:**
- Follow up via LinkedIn if no response (Template #3)
- Wait 2-3 days

**Day 5-8:**
- Second follow-up email: "Saw your recent [City] projects. Still interested?"
- Stop after second follow-up (respect their time)

**Goal:** 20-30% response rate = 1-2 installers per city willing to talk.

---

## Conversation Flow (When They Reply)

**If "Tell me more":**
1. **Send:** Link to /ca/bc/[city] page + the pitch page (INSTALLER_PARTNERSHIP_PITCH.md)
2. **Wait:** 24 hrs
3. **Follow-up:** "Any questions? Happy to chat 15 min."

**If "How many leads?":**
- Answer honestly: "Right now, we're targeting 60-90 leads/month per city once we build traffic. Early adopters may see fewer. But you're getting a new audience at a fixed cost, not paying per lead or commission. Your conversion rate is up to you."
- Don't oversell. They know their business.

**If "What about competitors?":**
- "You're the only one on this page. No Angi auction, no competing on price. Homeowners see you or nobody."

**If "When do I start getting leads?":**
- "Once you're live (48 hrs after onboarding), leads start within days if we have traffic. Grows as we build."

**If "Can I cancel anytime?":**
- "Yes, month-to-month after the first month. But after you see a few jobs, most installers don't want to leave."

---

## Red Flags / Don't Pursue

- ❌ Less than 4.5 stars on Google (or fewer than 8-10 reviews)
- ❌ Not HPCN-certified (major liability)
- ❌ Multi-province chains (they have their own lead gen)
- ❌ No Google My Business (can't pull ratings)
- ❌ Negative recent reviews or complaints (vet first)

---

## Success Metric

**Goal:** 1 signed installer per city within 4 weeks.  
**Reality:** Expect 30-40% to reply, 10-20% to sign up.  
**Timeline:** First installer onboarded by Week 2, second by Week 4.

Once you have one real installer live with their Google rating + photos, use them as proof to pitch the others. "Look what we built for [City installer]. You could be next."
