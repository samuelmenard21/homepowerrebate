## Section 1: The Problem We Solve

Not all certified installers are created equal.

You can find a list of HPCN-certified heat pump installers anywhere. But certification ≠ trustworthiness. Some certified installers:
- Pressure customers into unnecessary upgrades
- Disappear after installation
- Don't follow through on rebate paperwork
- Have poor workmanship or customer service

We vet for the stuff that actually matters.

## Section 2: Our Universal Requirements (All Regions)

Every installer on HomePowerRebate meets these standards:

### 1. Case Studies Required (2+ Completed Jobs)
- We require 2+ real jobs with before/after data
- You can see what they've actually built, not just promises
- Each case study shows: project type, timeline, costs, rebates received, customer satisfaction

### 2. Insurance & Bonding
- Proof of liability insurance ($1M+ coverage)
- Proof of workers' comp insurance (if applicable)
- We verify this every year

### 3. Response Time SLA
- Quote within 48 hours of request
- No ghosting customers
- Clear communication throughout the process

### 4. Standard Proposal Format
- All installers use a consistent proposal template
- Heat Pump, Solar, Battery installations are itemized identically
- This lets you compare apples-to-apples, not apples-to-oranges

### 5. No Sales Pressure Commitment
- No surprise upgrades mid-project
- No aggressive follow-up sales calls
- No gatekeeping information to force decisions
- We receive complaints and we'll boot installers who violate this

### 6. Expertise Tags (Transparency)
- Each installer clearly lists what they do and don't do
  - ✓ Heat pumps | ✓ Solar | ✓ Battery
  - ✓ New construction | ✓ Retrofits | ✗ Commercial
- No "we do everything" generalist claims

## Section 3: BC-Specific Requirements

On top of universal standards, every BC installer has:

### HPCN Certification
- Heat Pump Certification Network credential required
- Updated annually

### BC Hydro Program Knowledge
- Trained on BC Hydro heat pump rebate eligibility and application
- Familiar with Peak Saver demand response program
- Can explain what BC Hydro controls vs. what they control

### FortisBC Knowledge
- For northern/interior BC regions, FortisBC program knowledge required
- Understand differences between BC Hydro and FortisBC incentives

### Island vs. Mainland Considerations
- For island installers: knowledge of grid limitations and mini-split efficiency requirements
- Can explain why certain heat pump types work better on island grids
