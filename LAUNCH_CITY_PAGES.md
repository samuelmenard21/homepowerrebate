# City Page Launch Strategy

## What You Now Have

1. **CITY_PAGE_STRATEGY.md** — Strategic framework for why and how city pages work
2. **INSTALLER_BRIEF.md** — Ready-to-email template for each installer
3. **14 city landing pages** — Already exist at `/ca/bc/[city]` (need enhancement)

## Quick Execution Plan

### Week 1: Pick Your Lead Installer (1 city)
**Goal:** Prove the concept works before scaling to all 14

**Action:**
1. Choose your strongest city (biggest market, best relationship with installer)
   - Recommendation: **Vancouver** (highest search volume) or **Kelowna** (strong market)
2. Send personalized email with INSTALLER_BRIEF.md
   - Edit the placeholders: [CITY], [Your name], [Your email]
   - Personal note: "This is a new initiative—you'd be our first featured installer"
3. Set deadline: 1-2 weeks for them to respond

**Email template:**
```
Subject: [City Name] Featured Installer Page - Free Marketing for You

Hi [Installer Name],

We're building dedicated pages for each BC city to help homeowners understand rebates. You're our go-to partner for [City], so we'd like to feature you prominently.

This is free for you—no cost, no commitment. Just share info about your recent projects and local insights, and we'll build a page that drives qualified leads to you.

Attached is the brief with everything we need.

Let me know if you have questions.

[Your name]
HomePowerRebate
```

---

### Week 2: Build the City Page

**Once installer responds with info:**

1. **Create page at:** `/ca/bc/[city]/index.html` (probably already exists—enhance it)
2. **Sections to add:**
   - Local weather/sun data (pull from NOAA/weather API)
   - Installer spotlight (their story + credentials)
   - 2-3 case studies (their projects with photos)
   - Local cost reality (typical system costs in that area)
   - Local permitting guide (timeline + requirements)
   - Local FAQ (installer-answered questions)
3. **Test & launch**

**Page should answer:**
- ✓ "Will solar work in MY city?" (local weather data)
- ✓ "Who do I trust?" (installer profile)
- ✓ "What will it cost?" (real numbers)
- ✓ "How long will it take?" (local timeline)
- ✓ "What paperwork do I need?" (local permitting)

---

### Week 3: Measure & Iterate

**Track:**
- Page views (Google Analytics)
- Installer leads (ask them: "Where did you find me?" → track if they say "[City] page")
- Lead quality (do they close deals?)

**Iterate:**
- If installer gets leads → scale to next city
- If low traffic → may need to improve SEO (add more local keywords, get backlinks)
- If leads but no closes → optimize messaging

---

### Week 4+: Scale to All 14

**Once proof-of-concept works:**

1. **Email all 13 remaining installers** (personalized INSTALLER_BRIEF.md for each city)
2. **Batch build pages** (template makes this fast)
3. **Timeline:** 1-2 pages per week = all 14 live within 7-8 weeks

**Parallel work:**
- Build internal installer directory (which installer covers which city)
- Create update cadence (add case studies quarterly)
- Set up local SEO checklist (keywords, local schema, citations)

## What Each City Page Drives

### Search Traffic
- "[City name] + solar rebate"
- "[City name] + heat pump cost"
- "[City name] + energy upgrades"
- "[City name] + solar installer"

### Installer Referrals
- Installers share page with past customers ("Here's the rebate info we used")
- Installers link from their website → you get backlinks + referrals

### Lead Quality
- Homeowners arrive pre-educated on rebates
- They know about your installer before calling
- Lower sales friction = higher close rate
