# Quick Lead Gathering Guide (Kelowna)

## Method 1: Use Eguana Map (Fastest - 10 min)

1. Open the Eguana dealer map
2. Zoom to Kelowna
3. For each purple pin:
   - Click it
   - Get company name, address, phone
   - Open their Google My Business link to grab:
     - Email (usually on GMB or website)
     - Google rating
     - Review count
   - Add to spreadsheet: KELOWNA_INSTALLER_LEADS.csv

**Result:** 6-8 pre-vetted installers with full contact info

---

## Method 2: Google Search (If You Need More)

Use these search queries to find additional installers:

**Search 1:** `"HPCN certified" heat pump installer Kelowna site:energyadvisors.ca`
**Search 2:** `HPCN heat pump Kelowna BC`
**Search 3:** `"solar installer" Kelowna "HPCN certified"`
**Search 4:** Google Maps: "HVAC Kelowna" or "solar installer Kelowna"

For each result:
- Note company name + location
- Click through to Google My Business or website
- Grab phone + email
- Record rating + review count

---

## Method 3: Phone Calls (If You Need Verification)

Once you have 6-8 names:
1. Call each company
2. Ask: "Are you HPCN-certified?"
3. Ask: "Do you install heat pumps and solar in Kelowna?"
4. Add notes to spreadsheet

**This takes 30-45 min for 6-8 calls, but confirms they're legit.**

---

## Priority Order

1. **Eguana map** (primary) — pre-vetted, active dealers
2. **Google search** (secondary) — fill gaps, verify via GMB
3. **Phone calls** (optional) — final confirmation before outreach

## Pro Tips

- **Copy contact info as you go** — don't tab back and forth
- **Check their Google rating NOW** — if below 4.0, might not be worth emailing
- **Look for recent reviews** — shows they're actively working (not stale)
- **Note any specialties** — "solar focus", "heat pump expert", etc. — helps personalize emails

---

## If You Get Stuck

- Can't find email? → Call and ask for email, or use LinkedIn
- No Google My Business? → Google their website or call for contact info
- Don't see HPCN badge? → Call and ask directly ("Are you HPCN certified?")
