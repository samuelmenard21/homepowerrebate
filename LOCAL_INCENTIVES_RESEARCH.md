## How to Complete This Research

### Step 1: City Websites (30 min per city)
For each city, search:
- `[City] solar incentive 2024`
- `[City] energy retrofit program`
- `[City] heat pump rebate`
- `[City] sustainability grant`
- Check: Building Dept, Planning Dept, Sustainability Office pages

### Step 2: City Council Minutes (15 min per city)
Search: `[City] council meeting solar energy retrofit incentive 2024 2025`
- Look for approved programs with dollar amounts
- Find program names + application deadlines
- Note: Programs often get re-approved annually in January/February

### Step 3: Phone Call to Sustainability Officer (10 min per city)
Call City Hall Main Number → Ask for "Energy" or "Sustainability" coordinator
Script:
> "Hi, I'm building a website that connects homeowners with solar and heat pump installers. I want to feature the local incentives your city offers. What programs do you have for solar, heat pumps, or home energy retrofits in 2024-2026?"

Listen for:
- Program names
- Dollar amounts
- Income qualification (if any)
- Application process
- Timeline/deadline

### Step 4: Verify with Installer (once recruited)
When you contact local installers, ask:
> "What local city incentives do your customers use most? Any programs I'm missing?"
> "Do you ever apply for [specific program] on behalf of customers?"
