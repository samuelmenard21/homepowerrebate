## Full Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction

HomePowerRebate ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website (homepowerrebate.com) and services.

Please read this policy carefully. If you don't agree with our practices, please don't use our site.

---

### 2. What Information We Collect

#### A. Home Details (For Rebate Calculation)
When you use our rebate calculator, we collect:
- **Postal code** — to determine eligibility and utility provider
- **Home type** — house, condo, manufactured home
- **Heating system type** — furnace, baseboard, heat pump, other
- **Cooling system** — AC, mini-split, none
- **Solar interest** — interested / not interested
- **Battery interest** — interested / not interested
- **Home ownership** — own, rent, other

**Purpose:** To calculate your rebate eligibility and connect you with installers.

#### B. Contact Information
When you request a quote, we collect:
- **Name** — to personalize communication
- **Email address** — for quotes and follow-ups
- **Phone number** (optional) — so installers can reach you
- **Address** (optional) — for installer service area verification

**Purpose:** To match you with installers and send quotes.

#### C. Expansion Interest ("Request My City" Form)
When you request a city we don't serve yet, we collect:
- **Name, email, city, region** — to know where demand exists
- **Interests** — heat pump / solar / battery / all
- **Timestamp** — to track priority and follow-up timing

**Purpose:** To prioritize which cities to expand to and notify you when we launch.

#### D. Usage Information
We automatically collect:
- **Device type** — desktop, mobile, tablet
- **Browser type and version**
- **Pages visited** — which city pages, which blog posts, which tools
- **Time on page** — how long you spend reading
- **Referral source** — how you found us (organic search, link, direct)
- **IP address** — (anonymized) for security and fraud prevention

**Purpose:** To improve site performance and understand how people use our service.

#### E. Cookies
We use cookies (small files stored on your device) to:
- Remember your rebate calculation (so you don't have to re-enter data)
- Track your session (so you can pick up where you left off)
- Measure site usage (Google Analytics)

**Optional tracking:** We don't use tracking cookies by default. If you're OK with it, we use Google Analytics to see which pages are most helpful.

### 3. How We Use Your Information

#### A. Rebate Calculation & Installer Matching
We use your home details to:
- Calculate rebates you qualify for (BC Hydro, FortisBC, federal, local)
- Recommend appropriate system types for your situation
- Match you with 2-3 vetted installers in your area

#### B. Communication & Quotes
We use your contact info to:
- Send installer proposals (with your permission only)
- Follow up on your interest (1-2 follow-up emails max)
- Answer questions about rebates or installers
- Send rebate update notifications (when amounts change)

#### C. Expansion Planning
We use "Request My City" data to:
- Track demand by city and region
- Prioritize which cities to expand to
- Measure the success of launches (did requesters complete assessment after launch?)
- Notify you when we go live in your requested city

#### D. Service Improvement
We use usage information to:
- Fix bugs on the site
- Make the calculator faster
- Improve installer matching
- Understand which content helps most (city guides vs. blog posts)

#### E. Analytics & Reporting
We use anonymized usage data to:
- Measure how many homeowners we've helped
- Track which regions are most active
- Report on installer performance (quote acceptance, satisfaction)
- Publish regional trends (e.g., "heat pump adoption in Ontario increased 15% this quarter")

**We don't:** Report individual homeowner data. All analytics are aggregated and anonymized.

### 4. Who We Share Your Data With

#### A. Installers
We share your contact info with installers **only when you request a quote.**

At that point, you've explicitly asked to hear from them. They'll receive:
- Your name, email, phone
- Your home details (so they can prepare a proposal)
- Your rebate eligibility (so they can factor it into pricing)

**You control this.** You can choose to contact only 1 of the 3 installers, or none at all.

#### B. Utility Companies & Government Agencies
We may share aggregated, anonymized data (never individual data) with utilities and governments for:
- Impact reporting ("HomePowerRebate users claimed $2M in BC Hydro rebates this year")
- Program feedback (e.g., "HPCN certification program feedback based on installer experiences")

#### C. Service Providers
We use service providers for:
- **Email delivery** — Sendgrid (to send you confirmations and updates)
- **Database hosting** — Supabase (to store your data securely)
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics (to understand site usage)
- **Payment processing** — Stripe (if we ever offer premium services)

These service providers sign agreements to protect your data.

#### D. Legal Requirements
We may share your data if required by law (e.g., subpoena, government request, fraud investigation). We'll notify you when possible.

#### E. What We DON'T Do
- We don't sell your data
- We don't license your data to third parties
- We don't use your info for marketing outside HomePowerRebate
- We don't share with real estate agents, lenders, or other third parties

### 5. How We Protect Your Data

#### Security Measures
- **Encryption in transit** — All data sent to us is encrypted (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at rest** — Your data is encrypted in our database
- **Access controls** — Only authorized employees can access data
- **Annual security audits** — Third-party security reviews
- **No public access** — Your data is never visible to other users

#### Data Retention
- **Active data** — We keep your rebate calculation and installer quotes while you're using our service
- **After you leave** — If you don't log in for 2 years, we delete your data
- **Upon request** — You can request deletion anytime
- **Legal holds** — We keep data if required by law (typically 7 years for tax/compliance)
