# Regional Messaging Guide — HomePowerRebate

**Purpose:** Keep messaging consistent across BC, Ontario, California while adapting to regional specifics. Use this as the style guide for all rewrites.

---

## Core Principle

**Never say "BC" in headers or core copy.** Say "your area," "your region," "your utility." Regional specifics go in data tables, not headlines.

---

## Messaging Hierarchy

### Level 1: Universal (Works Everywhere)

**These appear on every page, regardless of region:**

- "Get matched with trusted installers in your area"
- "Cut through rebate confusion"
- "Compare vetted installers side by side"
- "We help you claim what you're owed"
- "No sales pressure, no gatekeeping"

### Level 2: Regional (Changes by BC/ON/CA)

**These change based on region selector:**

- "Home energy rebates available in [Region]"
- "Your utility offers: [specific rebate name + amount]"
- "Federal incentive: [IRA credit / Canada Greener Homes / other]"
- "Typical payback: [X years for your region]"

### Level 3: Hyper-Local (Changes by City)

**These change by city within the region:**

- "Heat pump rebates in [City], [Province]"
- "[City]'s trusted installers"
- "[City] typical costs after rebates"

---

## Tone Guidelines (Multi-Region)

### ✅ DO Use:
- "Your utility" (instead of "BC Hydro" or "Ontario Hydro One")
- "In your area" (instead of "in BC" or "in Ontario")
- "Home energy rebates" (instead of "BC rebates" or "Ontario incentives")
- Contractions ("you're," "don't," "it's")
- Short sentences
- Specific examples with context
- Regional callouts only in data sections

### ❌ DON'T Use:
- "BC-specific language" in core copy (Peak Saver → "demand response programs")
- Generic placeholders that won't work across regions ("BC Hydro pays")
- Assumptions about weather/climate (use regional data)
- Jargon without explanation (define terms first time)
- Overly formal tone ("homeowners are increasingly adopting")

## Quick Audit Checklist

Before publishing any page:

- [ ] No "BC-specific" language in headline or intro
- [ ] Regional data in tables, not core copy
- [ ] Contractions used (you're, don't, it's)
- [ ] Sentences under 15 words
- [ ] Specific examples (not generic)
- [ ] Explains what you don't control (rebate timing, for example)
- [ ] Installer vetting criteria visible
- [ ] CTA is specific ("Calculate my rebate" not "Learn more")
- [ ] Regional callout only where necessary
