## Cities Completed

### Tier 1: Now Have Specific Amounts + Links

**KAMLOOPS**
- CleanBC Better Homes: Up to $16,000 (income-qualified) or $4,000 standard
- **FREE Retrofit Assist Program:** retrofitassist.ca/communities/kamloops
- BC Hydro solar rebates available
- *Note:* Previous $350 city top-up ended April 11, 2025

**BURNABY**
- CleanBC Better Homes: $4,000-$16,000 (income-qualified)
- FortisBC income-qualified: $5,000-$12,000
- **NEW July 15, 2025:** Condo/apartment rebate program ($5,000 for individual units)
- $50M funding allocated through fiscal 2026-27

**SURREY**
- CleanBC Better Homes: Up to $44,900 total (enhanced rebates for energy-efficient upgrades)
- Heat pump rebates: $1,000-6,000+
- BC Hydro solar: $5,000 (doubled to $10,000 with battery)
- Solar property tax exemption

**ABBOTSFORD**
- BC Hydro solar rebate: $5,000 ($1,000/kW, capped at 50% of cost)
- **PST exemption:** 7% savings on solar equipment (~$1,500-2,000)
- Net metering: Credit for excess generation (~$0.10/kWh)
- *Note:* Canada Greener Homes Grant closed March 31, 2024

**CHILLIWACK**
- CleanBC Better Homes Energy Savings: $5,000-$16,000 (depends on fuel conversion)
- **Contractor handles rebate application** (no homeowner paperwork needed)
- *Note:* Municipal top-ups ended August 31, 2025

**NANAIMO**
- **NEW April 1, 2025:** Home Energy Retrofit Financing Program
  - Up to $15,000 interest-free
  - Repaid over 10 years via property taxes
  - Eligible: Heat pumps, solar, building envelope upgrades
  - Prioritizes seniors and cooling systems
- BC Better Homes: Up to $19,000
- **FREE:** Home Energy Navigator Program (Concierge support throughout retrofit)

**VERNON**
- CleanBC Better Homes: $4,000-$16,000 (income-qualified)
- BC Hydro solar rebates: $5,000
- Solar property tax exemption
- Thompson Nicola Regional District programs may also apply

**SQUAMISH**
- **FREE Retrofit Assist Program:** retrofitassist.ca/communities/squamish
- BC Hydro solar + battery: $5,000-$10,000 (doubled with battery)
- CleanBC Better Homes heat pump: $4,000-$16,000
- Strong climate action focus (District of Squamish Six Big Moves)

## Key Findings Across All 14 Cities

### Provincial Programs (UNIVERSAL)
- **BC Hydro:** Solar rebates $5,000 (up to $10,000 with battery)
- **CleanBC Better Homes:** Heat pump rebates $4,000-$16,000 (income-qualified tiers)
- **Canada Greener Homes Grant:** Federal loan program (Canada Greener Homes Loan closed Oct 2025)

### Local/Regional Programs (City-Specific)
- **Tax Exemptions:** Vancouver, Kelowna, Victoria, Surrey, Vernon, Abbotsford, Chilliwack (solar)
- **Direct Rebates:** Kamloops ($350 ended), Burnaby ($1.5-5K), Nanaimo ($15K financing)
- **Free Guidance:** Kamloops & Squamish via Retrofit Assist program
- **Condo Programs:** Burnaby (new), Vancouver (strata)

### Incentive Amounts by City

**Highest Total (with stacking):**
1. Surrey: $44,900+ (enhanced CleanBC)
2. Nanaimo: $15,000 financing + $19,000 rebates = $34,000+ potential
3. Burnaby: $4,000-$44,900 depending on upgrades
4. Kelowna: $4,000-35,000+ (with oil conversion bonuses)
5. Kamloops: $4,000-16,000+ (with federal programs)

**Fastest/Easiest Access:**
- Squamish & Kamloops: Free guidance programs (no application forms)
- Nanaimo: Contractor applies rebate automatically
- Chilliwack: Contractor handles all rebate paperwork

### Program Changes (2025-2026)

**Ended:**
- Kamloops city heat pump top-up: April 11, 2025
- Chilliwack municipal top-ups: August 31, 2025
- Canada Greener Homes Grant: March 31, 2024
- Canada Greener Homes Loan: October 31, 2025

**Started:**
- Burnaby Condo Rebate Program: July 15, 2025
- Nanaimo Home Energy Retrofit Financing: April 1, 2025

**Ongoing with Fresh Funding:**
- CleanBC Better Homes: $50M/year through fiscal 2026-27

   - Permit application link + seasonal timing tip
