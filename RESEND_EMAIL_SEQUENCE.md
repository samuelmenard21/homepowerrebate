## How to Set Up in Resend Dashboard

Unfortunately, Resend's free tier doesn't support automated drip campaigns yet. **You have two options:**

### Option A: Manual Broadcasts (Simple, 5 min each)
1. Go to Resend → **Broadcasts**
2. Click **Create**
3. For each email above:
   - Paste the HTML body
   - Set subject line
   - Select your "HomePowerRebate Newsletter" audience
   - **Schedule** for the dates (now, +3 days, +7 days)
4. Done

### Option B: Automation Ready (For Future)
When you upgrade to Resend Pro ($20/mo), you can set up:
- **Automations** → New signup trigger
- Send Email 1 immediately
- Send Email 2 after 3 days
- Send Email 3 after 7 days
