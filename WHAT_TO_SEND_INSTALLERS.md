**You say:** "The way it works: homeowners come to our calculator, see exactly what they qualify for — personalized rebate amount, monthly savings, everything. If they want to be matched, they give me their info and they get routed to you exclusively in [City]. You're the only installer they talk to. You get every qualified lead in your city. And we do all the marketing — homepage feature, email to 2,000 subscribers, social spotlights, blog case studies. All you do is install."

**If they say:** "What about leads? How many am I going to get?"

**You say:** "Honest answer: we're early. This week we're signing installers. As we build traffic, leads grow. But here's why first month is free — you can see for yourself. You're not risking anything. And the marketing features are day-one guaranteed. You're getting featured to hundreds of homeowners week one, whether you get 2 leads or 20."

**If they say:** "What if this doesn't work out?"

**You say:** "You cancel anytime. No contract. No penalty. You only pay if you want to keep going. First month is free so you can try it."
