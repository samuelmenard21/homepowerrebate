Who's behind this — and why it's independent

HomePowerRebate is a plain-English guide to Canada's home-energy rebates — built province by province, starting with British Columbia. It isn't run by an installer, a manufacturer, or a utility. Here's the honest version of who built it and how it makes money.

HomePowerRebate was built because BC homeowners told us the same story: they wanted to claim BC Hydro's solar + battery rebate, but every comparison site they tried sold their info to a dozen installers. They got buried in calls. The big rebate cheque felt out of reach because the process was hostile.

We took the opposite approach. Vetted installers in each city, picked for HPCN certification and customer reviews. You choose who to contact. No auctions, no bundles, no data sales. Independent of BC Hydro, but transparent about every official source we cite.

Today we serve 18 BC cities. In 2026 we'll be in more. In 2027 we'll expand to other provinces and states. Same model the whole way.

Why HomePowerRebate?

As a homeowner myself, I understand how overwhelming it can be to figure out rebates and incentives for major household projects. When I was researching heat pumps for my own home, I realized how broken the process was—confusing websites, contradictory information, and self-interested comparisons that sell your info to installers instead of helping you.

HomePowerRebate came from that frustration. I built this as a homeowner, for homeowners. The mission is simple: take the confusion out of rebates, help you understand what you actually qualify for, and connect you with vetted installers who'll work on your terms. My goal is to help other homeowners save money on major household expenses without the pressure or the runaround.

What "independent" actually means

  • Not a utility. HomePowerRebate is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by BC Hydro or FortisBC. We just explain their programs and link to them.
  • Not an installer or manufacturer. We don't sell heat pumps, solar panels, or batteries, so we have no reason to steer you toward one product over another.
  • You choose who to contact. We show you all vetted installers in your city, and you pick who you want to connect with — no high-pressure sales.

What we're not

We're a starting point, not a substitute for official confirmation. Rebate amounts, eligibility, and deadlines change, and your exact numbers depend on your home, your income, and an EnerGuide evaluation. Always confirm your specifics with the official program and a licensed professional before you sign anything. When a program has closed or a number has changed, we update the guide and show you the date.
