# HomePowerRebate × Eguana — BC channel partnership

**A one-page proposal for a low-lift, mutual-referral partnership in British Columbia.**

---

## Who we are
HomePowerRebate is an independent BC home-energy guide and homeowner-matching service. We help BC homeowners understand every rebate they qualify for — heat pumps, solar, **batteries**, water heaters, EV chargers — then match each homeowner with **one trusted local installer** (no lead auctions, no data selling). We're live across 14 BC cities with detailed, accurate rebate pages and a growing homeowner audience.

## Why this matters for Eguana right now
BC's battery rebate hinges on one thing most homeowners don't understand: **the battery must be on BC Hydro's approved list** to earn anything. The most famous battery (Tesla Powerwall) earns **$0**. The Eguana Evolve is **on the list**, is Peak-Saver-eligible, and has BC Hydro's dedicated enrollment support — but homeowners rarely know this at the moment they're choosing.

**We already close that gap in your favour.** Our content educates BC homeowners on exactly why an approved battery like Evolve is the right call — honestly, not as an ad. Every battery-interested homeowner we reach arrives pre-educated on the approved-list advantage.

## What we bring you
- **Qualified BC demand** — homeowners actively researching solar + storage, already primed on the approved-battery/rebate logic that favours Evolve.
- **Honest third-party credibility** — an independent guide recommending Evolve carries more weight than a spec sheet.
- **BC search presence** — dedicated Evolve explainer, an approved-battery guide, and battery/solar sections on all 14 city pages (see below).
- **A homeowner-matching channel into installers** — we route matched homeowners to local installers, which is exactly where your product gets sold.

## What we're asking for
1. **Introductions to your BC installer network.** We're signing one installer per city; if they carry Evolve, the whole chain aligns — homeowner → us → your installer → Evolve battery.
2. **A two-way referral relationship.** You point BC homeowner inquiries (e.g. from your BC Hydro landing page) to us for local matching; we route battery-ready homeowners to Evolve-carrying installers.
3. **A success-based referral fee** — paid only on an installed system, formalised once we've proven volume. Nothing upfront from either side.

## Proposed pilot (1 city, ~60–90 days)
Start in **one strong-fit market** — e.g. Kelowna (top-tier solar) or Victoria/Nanaimo (storm-season resilience demand). One Evolve installer, our lead flow, a shared success metric (matched homeowners → site visits → installs). Expand city-by-city on what works.
