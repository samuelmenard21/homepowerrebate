## Step 4: Calls & Conversation (Tuesday–Friday)

**What to expect:**
- 30–40% will reply to Email 1
- 50% of those will book a call

**Call structure (15 min):**

1. **Intro (2 min)**
   - "Thanks for your time. I'm Sam. I'm building a directory of vetted installers for BC homeowners seeking rebates."

2. **Show the page (3 min)**
   - Pull up https://homepowerrebate.com/ca/bc/kelowna
   - Scroll to the installer card: "This is where you'd appear. Your Google rating, photos, video."

3. **Explain the model (5 min)**
   - "You get a new audience — homeowners actively searching for rebates. Fixed $300/month. No commission. You own the customer."
   - "We handle traffic, lead gen, the technical side. You close the sale and file the rebate."
   - "First 10 lock in $300/month forever."

4. **Handle objections (3 min)**
   - "How many leads?" → "Right now, we're ramping traffic. Early adopters may see 1-3/week starting, growing to 8-12/week. But you're paying flat, not per lead, so your close rate is what matters."
   - "What if I'm already busy?" → "You control availability. Pause bookings anytime. No lock-in."
   - "Is my rating private?" → "Your real Google rating pulls live. It's the trust signal."

5. **Close (2 min)**
   - If interested: "Great. Here's what's next: I'll send you the onboarding link. Takes 1 hour to connect your Google My Business, upload 3 photos. You're live in 48 hours."
   - If not sure: "No pressure. Sleep on it. I'll check in Monday."

**Take notes:** Name, rating, hesitations, next steps.

## Success Metrics

**Target:** 1 signed installer by end of Week 2

**Reality check:**
- Emails sent: 6–8
- Response rate: 20–30% (1–2 replies)
- Call booking rate: 50–75% (0–1 calls)
- Close rate: 50–100% (0–1 signed)

**If you get 0 replies:**
- Your messaging might be off (tweak subject line, add more specifics)
- Try calling instead of emailing (harder but higher close rate)
- Reach out to local HVAC suppliers for warm referrals

**If you get calls but no closes:**
- They're hesitant about early stage ("prove it works first")
- Offer: First month free, then $300/month if they want to continue
- Or: "I'm signing 2 installers. You're one. Who's next?" (social proof pressure)
