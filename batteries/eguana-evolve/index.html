Eguana Evolve: Specs, Backup Power and Rebates (2026)

What it does, what it can back up, and which rebates it qualifies for in Canada and the US. Specs from Eguana's datasheet. Last checked October 3, 2026.

By Sam Menard

Short answer: The Eguana Evolve stores 14 kWh and delivers up to 5 kW of continuous power per unit. It's AC coupled, so it pairs with new or existing solar, and you can add battery modules up to 28 kWh. It needs a spot that stays above freezing. Battery programs in BC, Ontario, Massachusetts and parts of California may cover it; each keeps its own list of eligible models, so check before you sign.

How it works

Eguana Evolve is an AC-coupled battery system built in Calgary. It sits beside your solar system rather than replacing its inverter, so it works with new or existing solar. The base system stores 14 kWh and can grow to 28 kWh by adding battery modules, which suits homes that want longer backup through multi-day outages.

What can it back up?

Two numbers matter. kWh is how much energy it stores (how long it lasts). kW is how much it can power at once (how many things run together).

  • One unit stores 14 kWh; two store 28 kWh.
  • One unit can run 5 kW at once. A fridge, lights, internet and furnace fan together use about 1 kW. Central AC can use 3 to 5 kW while running, and more for a moment when it starts.

Who it's best for

  • Adding a battery to solar you already have, with any inverter brand.
  • Homes that want more storage (up to 28 kWh) for long outages.
  • Buyers who want a Canadian-made battery.

Watch out for

  • It's rated down to 0°C only, so it needs a spot that stays above freezing.
  • 5 kVA of power is less than Powerwall 3 or FranklinWH, so you may not run central AC and other big loads at once.
  • The warranty guarantees 60% capacity after 10 years, lower than some rivals.

Common questions

How long will a Evolve power my home in an outage?

It depends on what you run. A fridge, lights, internet and a furnace fan might use about 1 kW, so one 14 kWh unit could last around 13 hours with no solar (allowing for losses). Central AC or electric heat drains it much faster. With solar, the battery refills during the day.

Does the Eguana Evolve work in the cold?

Not below freezing. The Evolve LFP is rated to operate from 0°C to 50°C (best at 15°C to 30°C), so in Canadian winters it belongs indoors, in a heated garage or basement.

Can I get a rebate for the Eguana Evolve in BC?

BC Hydro only pays rebates on batteries on its qualified battery list, up to $5,000 with Peak Saver. Confirm the exact Evolve model is on the current list with your installer before you sign.
