Kelowna vs Kamloops: Which BC Interior City Is Best for Solar?

BC's two sunniest cities go head-to-head on solar potential, heat pump efficiency, rebates, and 10-year ROI. We compare climate, grid, and savings.

Interior BC's solar twins

Kelowna and Kamloops are basically tied for BC's sunniest, best climates for solar. Both get 300+ sunny days per year. Both have dry winters that love heat pumps. But they're not identical—the markets, utilities, and installer options differ.

If you're trying to decide between the two, here's what actually matters.

Solar Resource: Kelowna vs Kamloops

Factor Kelowna Kamloops
Annual sunny days 310+ days 300+ days
Peak sun hours 4.8–5.2 hrs/day 4.6–5.0 hrs/day
Solar production rating Excellent (4.9/5) Excellent (4.8/5)
Typical system size 5–7 kW (residential) 5–7 kW (residential)
Annual system output 7,500–8,500 kWh/year 7,200–8,200 kWh/year

Winner: Kelowna (slight edge) — 310+ sunny days and marginally higher peak sun hours mean Kelowna systems produce ~3–5% more annual output. Over 20 years, that's $1,500–$2,500 more in electricity generation.

Heat Pump Climate: Kelowna vs Kamloops

Both cities are ideal for heat pumps. Winter temperatures are nearly identical, but precipitation patterns differ slightly:

  • Kelowna: Average winter low: -5°C, very dry (rain instead of snow). Heat pump efficiency: 350%+
  • Kamloops: Average winter low: -5°C, slightly wetter (some snow). Heat pump efficiency: 350%+

Winner: Tie — Both cities favor heat pump performance. Kelowna's drier winters might give a 2–3% efficiency edge, but it's negligible.

10-Year Heat Pump + Solar + Battery ROI

Metric Kelowna Kamloops
Upfront cost (after rebate) $2,000–$5,000 $2,000–$5,000
Annual heating savings $300–$400 $300–$400
Annual solar/battery savings $2,000–$2,500 $1,900–$2,400
Peak Saver rewards (10 years) $3,000–$5,000 $3,000–$5,000
10-year total value $25,000–$30,000 $24,000–$29,000

Winner: Kelowna (by ~$1,000–$1,500 over 10 years)

The difference is marginal: Kelowna's slight solar edge (~3–5% higher production) and fractionally drier climate (2–3% heat pump efficiency gain) compound to ~5% better total ROI. Over 10 years on a $25K+ investment, that's $1,000–$1,500.

Installers & market vibe

Kelowna: Mature market. More installers, faster growth, more options.

Kamloops: Still growing. Fewer installers, but the ones there are sharp and motivated.

If you want pick from multiple installers, Kelowna's your place. If you want fast turnaround with less competition for your project, Kamloops wins.

Real talk

Living in Kelowna? You've got a slight edge on solar (310+ days), more installers, and $1K more over 10 years. Go for it.

Living in Kamloops? Don't sweat it. You're within 5% of Kelowna's savings. The climate is basically the same. The installers here are good. Go solar + heat pump + battery if it makes sense for your home—the difference to Kelowna is noise.
