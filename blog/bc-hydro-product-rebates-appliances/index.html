BC Hydro Rebates 2026: Thermostat, AC, Furnace and Appliances

By Sam Menard · Updated September 28, 2026

Short answer: BC Hydro's smart thermostat rebate is for baseboard heat only: from October 2026 you can get up to five free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats. There is no BC Hydro rebate for an air conditioner, a furnace, or fridges and washers right now. The biggest open rebates are up to $4,000 for a heat pump that replaces electric heat (which also cools your home), and up to $10,000 for solar plus a battery.

Here for a furnace or air conditioner? BC Hydro doesn't rebate either one. See our BC furnace and AC rebate guide for what FortisBC and CleanBC do offer, and when a heat pump is the smarter buy.

Doing two or more renovation upgrades (like a heat pump plus insulation) can add a BC Hydro bonus of up to $2,000.

BC Hydro smart thermostat rebate (Mysa and Sinopé)

BC Hydro's thermostat offers are for homes heated by electric baseboards. That means line-voltage thermostats like Mysa and Sinopé. There are two ways in:

Your situationWhat you get
Baseboards, no smart thermostat yetUp to 5 free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats from October 2026, already enrolled in Peak Saver. Then $50 each winter ($100 if income-qualified).
You already own a Mysa or Sinopé baseboard thermostatEnroll it in Peak Saver: $100 one-time bill credit + $50 each winter. That's the "$150" you may have seen.
Ecobee or Nest on a furnace or heat pumpNo BC Hydro thermostat rebate.
FortisBC electric customer (Kelowna, Penticton)BC Hydro offers don't apply. FortisBC has its own connected-thermostat rebate (up to $150, electric central heat only).

Peak Saver events run November to March and last up to four hours. BC Hydro may lower your heat a couple of degrees. You can opt out of any event, but if you skip more than half, you may lose that season's reward. Mysa vs Ecobee vs Nest: which fits your heat · Peak Saver, explained.

BC Hydro rebates for air conditioners: what to do instead

BC Hydro's air conditioner offer has ended. If you want cooling, a heat pump is the better buy. It cools in summer and heats in winter. If it replaces electric heat, BC Hydro pays up to $4,000, plus up to $1,000 more for installs finished by October 31, 2026.

BC Hydro furnace rebate: there isn't one

BC Hydro doesn't rebate furnaces, gas or electric. If you have a gas furnace, the only heat pump money right now is the income-qualified CleanBC program (up to $13,000 at Income Level 1, less at Levels 2 and 3). Our BC furnace rebate page covers FortisBC and CleanBC options in full, so we won't repeat it here. For heat pumps, see the BC heat pump rebate guide.

BC Hydro appliance rebates: fridges, washers, dryers

When we checked BC Hydro's product rebates page on September 28, 2026, no appliance discounts were listed. BC Hydro has run seasonal in-store discounts before, so check again before you buy. If you're a FortisBC electric customer (for example Kelowna or Penticton), FortisBC pays up to $100 on an efficient fridge and up to $250 on a washer or dryer.

Honest take: don't replace a working fridge just for a rebate. A new one rarely pays back fast. Replace it when it breaks, and pick an ENERGY STAR model then.

BC Hydro heat pump rebate application

You apply after the install, within 6 months of your invoice. The installer must be a Home Performance Contractor Network (HPCN) member, and the heat pump must be on BC Hydro's eligible list. For the full $4,000, it must meet 100% of your heating at -5°C and replace electric heat. Not on BC Hydro?

If FortisBC is your electric utility, BC Hydro's programs don't apply. FortisBC has its own versions. See BC Hydro vs FortisBC rebates.

Questions people ask

Does BC Hydro have a smart thermostat rebate?

Yes, for baseboard heat. From October 2026, BC Hydro customers with electric baseboards can get up to five free Mysa or Sinopé line-voltage thermostats, pre-enrolled in Peak Saver. If you already own one, enrolling it in Peak Saver pays $100 plus $50 each winter. Ecobee and Nest don't qualify.

Does BC Hydro give a rebate for an air conditioner?

Not right now. BC Hydro's product rebates page says its air conditioner offer has ended. If you want cooling, a heat pump is the rebated option: up to $4,000 if it replaces electric heat. Income-qualified people referred by a health authority program can apply for a free portable AC.

Is there a BC Hydro furnace rebate?

No. BC Hydro does not rebate furnaces. It rebates heat pumps that replace electric heat (up to $4,000). FortisBC and CleanBC handle gas-related offers.

Are there BC Hydro rebates on fridges, washers or dryers right now?

When we checked on September 28, 2026, BC Hydro listed no active appliance discounts. BC Hydro has run seasonal in-store discounts before, so check its product rebates page before you shop. FortisBC electric customers (Kelowna, Penticton and others) can get up to $100 on a fridge and up to $250 on a washer or dryer.

Does BC Hydro give a free Mysa thermostat?

Yes, starting October 2026, for BC Hydro customers with electric baseboard heat. You can order up to five free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats. They come already enrolled in Peak Saver.

What's the biggest BC Hydro rebate for a house?

Solar plus a battery with Peak Saver can reach $10,000. For heating, the heat pump rebate is up to $4,000, plus up to $1,000 extra for installs done Aug 1 to Oct 31, 2026.
