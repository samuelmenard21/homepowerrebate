Full Rebate Breakdown

Refrigerators

BC Hydro reimburses $150–$300 on Energy Star fridges. The rebate depends on size and model efficiency.

  • Qualification: Must be Energy Star certified (check the yellow ENERGY GUIDE label)
  • What qualifies: Standard fridges, French-door, side-by-side, all sizes
  • What doesn't: Mini fridges, wine coolers, commercial units
  • Application: Online at BC Hydro website within 90 days of purchase
  • Timeline: 4–6 weeks after BC Hydro receives your application

Washers & Dryers

Up to $500 rebate for efficient washer-dryer combos. This is the biggest appliance rebate.

  • Qualification: Must show ENERGY GUIDE label and efficiency rating
  • Washer alone: $200–$300
  • Dryer alone: $150–$250
  • Combo (washer + dryer): Up to $500
  • What qualifies: Front-load or top-load washers, electric or gas dryers, all-in-one combos
  • Popular brands: LG, Samsung, Bosch, Whirlpool, Maytag

Induction Cooktops

$200–$400 rebate. Induction is more efficient than gas and electric coil stoves.

  • What qualifies: Built-in or portable induction cooktops, 4+ burners recommended
  • Eligible brands: Bosch, Electrolux, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Bertazzoni
  • Installation: Must be properly installed. BC Hydro may require photo proof.
  • Cost context: Typical induction cooktop costs $400–$1,200. Rebate covers 20–50% of cost.

Dishwashers

$150–$250 rebate on Energy Star dishwashers.

  • Qualification: Energy Star certified only
  • Size: Standard (24-inch) and compact (18-inch) models qualify
  • Brands: LG, Bosch, Whirlpool, Miele, GE all have Energy Star models

Smart Thermostats

$50–$150 rebate. Smaller rebate but easier to apply than appliances.

  • What qualifies: WiFi-enabled, works with electric baseboard or gas furnace
  • Popular models: Ecobee, Mysa, Honeywell, Nest (some models)
  • Installation: DIY or professional. BC Hydro sometimes covers installation costs separately.
  • Note: Overlap with HPCN-installer rebates. Don't double-dip—apply for one, not both.

Heat Pump Water Heaters

$300–$500 rebate when replacing an electric tank water heater.

  • Qualification: Must replace an existing electric tank (not gas)
  • Installation: HPCN-certified installer required for full rebate
  • Cost context: Heat pump water heaters cost $3K–$5K installed. Rebate covers 10–15% of cost.
  • Combined savings: Can stack with CleanBC water heater rebates for up to $1,500 total

How to Apply: Step by Step

  1. Buy the appliance → Keep the receipt and ENERGY GUIDE label (usually on the side of the appliance at the store)
  2. Take a photo → Photograph the appliance model number and ENERGY GUIDE label (required for most applications)
  3. Visit BC Hydro's rebate portal → homepowerrebate.com or bchydro.com/rebates
  4. Select your appliance type → Fridge, washer, dryer, cooktop, dishwasher, etc.
  5. Upload your proof → Receipt + photo of ENERGY GUIDE label
  6. Submit within 90 days → Application window closes 90 days after purchase
  7. Wait 4–6 weeks → BC Hydro processes and sends rebate via cheque or direct deposit

Income-Qualified vs. Universal

Most BC Hydro product rebates are universal (everyone qualifies). Some are income-qualified:

Income qualification threshold: BC Hydro considers households under ~$75K household income as income-qualified. You'll need to self-certify this when you apply.

Common Questions

Can I apply for multiple appliance rebates at once?
Yes. If you're doing a kitchen renovation and buying a fridge, dishwasher, and cooktop, you can apply for all three. There's no limit on the number of appliance rebates per household per year.
Do I need to be a BC Hydro customer to qualify?
Yes, you must have an active BC Hydro account. FortisBC customers have a separate program with different rebates.
Can I get the rebate if I buy used/refurbished appliances?
No. BC Hydro only rebates new appliances with valid ENERGY GUIDE labels. Used appliances don't qualify.
What if I buy an appliance at Costco, Amazon, or Home Depot?
Doesn't matter where you buy it—as long as the receipt and ENERGY GUIDE label are valid, the rebate applies. BC Hydro doesn't partner with specific retailers.
What if I miss the 90-day application deadline?
The rebate window closes. BC Hydro doesn't process late applications. Set a reminder when you buy the appliance.
How do I know if an appliance is Energy Star certified?
Look for the blue Energy Star label on the appliance or box. Every qualifying appliance will have this visible. If it's not there, it probably doesn't qualify.
Can I stack product rebates with heat pump rebates or solar rebates?
Yes! Product rebates are separate from system rebates. You can claim a fridge rebate + heat pump rebate + solar rebate in the same year.
What if BC Hydro denies my application?
Most denials happen because the ENERGY GUIDE label is missing or illegible. Contact BC Hydro with your receipt. If you have proof the appliance was Energy Star certified at purchase, they may override the decision.

The Bottom Line

BC Hydro product rebates aren't flashy, but they're real savings when you're already planning to replace an appliance. $150–$500 off a fridge or washer matters. Just remember to apply within 90 days and keep your receipt + ENERGY GUIDE label.

Next step: If you're planning a renovation or appliance upgrade, check BC Hydro's full rebate list to see if you qualify for anything else. Stacking rebates (fridge + heat pump + insulation) turns one big project into $5K–$10K in total savings.
