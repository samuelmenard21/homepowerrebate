BC Hydro Rebate Deadlines 2026: Key Dates

By Sam Menard · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer: The next date that matters: October 31, 2026, the last day to finish a heat pump install for BC Hydro's bonus of up to $1,000 (first come, first served). Also: apply for most BC Hydro renovation rebates within six months of your invoice, and enroll a battery in Peak Saver within 14 days of in-service.

Deadlines that repeat

  • 6 months: apply for BC Hydro heat pump, insulation, window and water heater rebates within six months of the invoice.
  • 14 days: enroll a new battery in Peak Saver within 14 calendar days of its in-service date for the $5,000 cap.
  • Before install: solar and battery need an accepted self-generation application before you install.
  • 180 days: EV charger rebate applications.

Contractor rules

BC Hydro's heat pump, insulation, solar and battery rebates all require a Home Performance Contractor Network (HPCN) member. Ask for their membership before you sign.

What to do next

  1. Check who your electric utility is.
  2. Check whether your income qualifies for CleanBC.
  3. Get quotes from qualified contractors and apply on time.

Questions people ask

When does the BC Hydro heat pump bonus end?

Installs must be completed by October 31, 2026. It's up to $1,000, first come, first served.

How long do I have to apply for a BC Hydro rebate?

Six months from the invoice date for most home renovation rebates. For EV chargers, 180 days.
