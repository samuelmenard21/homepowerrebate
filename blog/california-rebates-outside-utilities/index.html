You're Not on a Big Utility. You Still Get These.

You looked up your utility and it wasn't LADWP, SMUD, PG&E, SDG&E, or SCE. Don't worry — these programs don't care who your utility is.

You Don't Need a Big Utility to Get Rebates

Most of our coverage focuses on California's five biggest utilities. But if you're served by a smaller municipal utility or electric co-op, you still qualify for real money — it's just organized at the state and federal level instead of through your utility bill.

Programs Open to Every California Homeowner

1. TECH Clean CA

This is a statewide heat pump incentive program, not tied to a specific utility. Your contractor applies for it directly. Coverage and amounts can vary by region, so confirm with your installer whether your address qualifies.

2. HEEHRA (federal, state-administered)

The High-Efficiency Electric Home Rebate program is a federal program, but in California it's run through the California Energy Commission. It's income-qualified and can cover a large share of electrification costs — heat pumps, electrical panel upgrades, water heaters, and more — regardless of which utility delivers your power.

3. Federal tax credits

Separate from HEEHRA, federal tax credits for qualifying heat pumps, insulation, and other upgrades are claimed on your tax return and have nothing to do with your utility.

Where to Check Official, Current Details

Because state-level programs adjust their funding and rules during the year, always check the source directly before assuming you qualify: California Energy Commission — IRA Residential Energy Rebate Programs.

What To Do Next

  1. Find a contractor enrolled in TECH Clean CA — they'll know how to apply on your behalf.
  2. Check your household income against HEEHRA's qualifying limits.
  3. Ask your tax preparer about federal credits for your specific upgrade.

Even without a big utility rebate, these three layers can add up to meaningful savings. If you're near San Diego, also see our San Diego hub for nearby examples of how the stack works in practice.
