CleanBC Income Qualified Program 2026: Do You Qualify for 100% Free Heat Pump?

BC's most generous rebate program covers the complete cost of a heat pump installation for eligible homeowners—no money down, no loans, no repayment. Here's who qualifies and how to apply.

💰 The Offer: CleanBC's Income Qualified program pays 100% of heat pump equipment + professional installation for eligible low- and moderate-income BC homeowners. You can have a brand-new, fully installed heat pump system at zero out-of-pocket cost.

Who Qualifies for 100% Free Heat Pump?

CleanBC Income Qualified is available to homeowners whose household income falls below specific thresholds. These thresholds change annually and vary by family size:

Family Size Approximate Income Limit (2026)
1 person $50,000–$60,000
2 people $70,000–$80,000
3 people $85,000–$100,000
4 people $100,000–$120,000
5+ people Add $15,000–$20,000 per person

Note: These are 2026 estimates based on provincial cost-of-living. Exact thresholds are updated each year. To confirm your eligibility, contact Service BC or visit the CleanBC website directly.

What's Included in the Free Heat Pump Program?

If you qualify, CleanBC covers:

  • High-efficiency heat pump equipment (outdoor and indoor units)
  • Professional installation (labor, permits, electrical work)
  • Removal of old heating system (furnace, boiler, or baseboard heaters)
  • Ductwork upgrades (if needed for heat pump compatibility)

You pay $0 upfront, $0 during installation, and $0 at completion. No loans, no rebate applications, no waiting for a check. The program pays the contractor directly.

What You Need to Qualify

✓ Eligibility Checklist

  • Own your primary residence in BC (or have landlord consent)
  • Household income below the 2026 threshold for your family size
  • Your home currently heats with fossil fuels (oil, natural gas, propane) OR inefficient electric resistance
  • Your home is deemed "suitable" for heat pump installation (most homes qualify)
  • No outstanding liens or property tax arrears

How to Apply: Step-by-Step

Step 1: Check Eligibility (5 min)

Verify your household income qualifies using the CleanBC Income Qualified calculator on the provincial website.

Step 2: Request an Energy Audit (Free)

Contact CleanBC or an approved program administrator. They'll schedule a free in-home energy assessment to confirm your home is suitable for a heat pump. Takes 1–2 weeks to book.

Step 3: Complete the Application (30 min)

Submit your income verification (T4s, NOAs, pay stubs) and property information. Most applicants approve within 2–4 weeks.

Step 4: Get Pre-Approved (1–2 weeks)

CleanBC reviews your documents. Once approved, you're cleared to schedule installation with an approved contractor.

Step 5: Installation (3–5 days)

A CleanBC-approved installer removes your old heating system and installs the new heat pump. Installation is completely free to you.

Step 6: System Activation & Training (1 day)

The installer shows you how to use your new heat pump and thermostat. You're done—and the system is fully yours.

Total Timeline: 4–6 Weeks

From initial application to having a working heat pump installed, the process takes approximately 4–6 weeks if you qualify. Most delays happen during the energy audit scheduling phase, not approval.

What If You're Slightly Over the Income Limit?

If your household income is slightly above the CleanBC Income Qualified threshold, you may still have options:

  • BC Hydro Heat Pump Rebate: Up to $8,000 rebate (covers 40–80% of costs for moderate incomes)
  • Canada Greener Homes Grant: Federal program covering up to $5,000 (similar income limits to CleanBC)
  • FortisBC Energy Efficiency Rebate: Up to $4,500 depending on location

Stacking these rebates can cover most or all of your heat pump costs even if you don't qualify for the 100% free program.

Frequently Asked Questions

📱 Can I apply online?
Yes. You can start your application on the CleanBC website or call Service BC (1-844-397-2299). Most steps can be completed online, though you'll need to schedule an in-home energy audit.
🏠 What if I rent, not own?
Unfortunately, you don't qualify as a renter. The homeowner (your landlord) would need to apply. Encourage your landlord to apply—they'll get a free heat pump upgrade and can raise your rent down the line if they choose. Some landlords see this as a win-win.
💰 Do I need to repay the program?
No. This is a grant program, not a loan. You receive a fully installed heat pump at no cost and owe nothing back. Your only obligation is to maintain the system.
⚡ What if my home doesn't have ductwork?
CleanBC will install a ductless mini-split heat pump system instead. This is often BETTER than ducted systems—easier to install, quieter, more efficient. It costs the same (covered by CleanBC).
🔧 Can I choose which contractor installs my system?
You can request a contractor, but final assignment depends on CleanBC's network and availability in your area. All approved contractors are vetted and trained. Quality is consistent across the program.
❓ What if I'm self-employed or have variable income?
CleanBC accepts self-employed income (T1 General forms, business tax returns). If your income varies, they typically use a 2-year average. Contact Service BC to discuss your situation.
🌡️ Will the heat pump heat my entire home?
Yes. A properly sized heat pump (designed during your energy audit) will heat your entire home even in BC winters. Ductless systems work just as well as ducted systems for whole-home heating.

Why This Program Exists

CleanBC Income Qualified was created to address climate goals AND equity. Heat pumps are highly efficient (they use 1/3 the energy of oil furnaces), but the $12,000–$15,000 upfront cost puts them out of reach for many households. This program removes that barrier.

BC's goal: transition low-income homeowners away from fossil fuels while lowering their heating costs long-term. You benefit from lower energy bills; BC benefits from lower emissions.
