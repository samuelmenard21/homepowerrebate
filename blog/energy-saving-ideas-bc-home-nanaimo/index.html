Cut Your Power Bill in Nanaimo: Free and Cheap Ideas

Free habits first, cheap fixes next, then the rebates that help pay for the big stuff.

By Sam Menard · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer: Start with free habits (thermostat set-backs, cold-water laundry, blinds), then seal drafts for a few dollars. If you have baseboard heat, BC Hydro is giving out up to five free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats from October 2026.

Nanaimo is on central Vancouver Island, with mild, wet winters that are close to ideal for heat pumps. That shapes where your energy goes: heating in winter. Many Nanaimo homes use baseboards, oil or older gas furnaces. The island's mild winters mean a well-sized heat pump can carry nearly the whole heating load.

Free: do these this week

  • Turn the heat down when you're asleep or out. Lower heat means less energy used.
  • Use your blinds. Open south-facing ones on sunny winter days; close them at night.
  • Wash in cold water and run full loads. Heating water is most of a washer's energy.
  • Close doors and turn off baseboards in rooms you don't use.
  • Clean or change filters on your furnace or heat pump so it doesn't work harder than it needs to.

Cheap: a few dollars each

  • Weatherstripping around doors and windows.
  • Foam gaskets behind outlet covers on outside walls.
  • Pipe insulation on the first few feet of hot water pipe.
  • LED bulbs in the lights you use most.

To find drafts, hold your hand near window and door frames on a windy day.

Free smart thermostats and Peak Saver

If your home has electric baseboards and you're a BC Hydro customer, you can get up to five free Mysa or Sinopé line-voltage thermostats starting in October 2026. They come pre-enrolled in Peak Saver, which pays a $50 reward each winter ($100 if income-qualified). Peak Saver events run November to March, last up to four hours, and you can opt out. Ecobee and Nest do not qualify, because they don't control baseboards.

Bigger upgrades, when you're ready

  • Insulation: BC Hydro pays by area and R-value added, up to $900 for an attic and $1,200 for walls or basement (for electrically heated homes).
  • Heat pump: up to $4,000 from BC Hydro if you replace electric heat.
  • Heat pump water heater: up to $1,000 from BC Hydro when replacing an electric tank.
  • Income-qualified? ECAP gives a free home energy evaluation and free upgrades.

What's different in Nanaimo

  • City of Nanaimo Home Energy Retrofit Financing: zero-interest loans up to $15,000, paid back over 10 years on your property tax bill. It covers heat pumps, insulation, windows and doors, and solar. Applications are open Sept 14 to Oct 31, 2026, and you must register with the free Home Energy Navigator.
  • The Regional District of Nanaimo adds $150 to BC Hydro's home EV charger rebate for homes in its electoral areas (a separate application).

My tip for Nanaimo: Nanaimo is one of the few cities in our list with its own low-cost financing. If the up-front cost is what's stopping you, look at the city loan below before you give up.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest way to cut my heating bill?

Turning the heat down at night and when you're out costs nothing. Sealing drafts costs a few dollars. After that, insulation usually gives the most back.

Do Ecobee or Nest thermostats get a BC Hydro rebate?

No. BC Hydro's free thermostats and Peak Saver thermostat rewards are for line-voltage baseboard thermostats like Mysa and Sinopé.

What is Nanaimo's zero-interest retrofit loan?

The City of Nanaimo lends up to $15,000 at 0% for heat pumps, insulation, windows and doors, or solar. You repay it over 10 years on your property tax bill. The 2026 intake runs Sept 14 to Oct 31.
