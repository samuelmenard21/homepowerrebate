Free Heat Pump in Burnaby? Income-Qualified Rebates 2026

If your income qualifies, it can be. Here are the 2026 rules.

By Sam Menard · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer: Sometimes. If your household income is low enough, you can get a heat pump installed for little or nothing in Burnaby. There are two routes: ECAP, where the utility installs upgrades for free, and the CleanBC Energy Savings Program, which pays 100% of eligible costs up to a limit. The limit is up to $13,000 if you're switching from gas, oil or propane at Income Level 1.

Burnaby is in Metro Vancouver, with mild, rainy winters that suit heat pumps very well. Burnaby has many condos and townhomes as well as older houses with baseboard heat. For a house on baseboards, a ductless multi-split is the usual path to BC Hydro's whole-home rebate.

Who qualifies? The 2026 income limits

It's based on how many people live in your home and the combined pre-tax income of the adults. These are the CleanBC limits for invoices from July 6, 2026:

These are maximums. CleanBC pays 100% of eligible costs up to the limit, so a modest system can cost you nothing. The rates changed on July 6, 2026. If you see "$16,000" elsewhere, that's the old amount.

ECAP: the truly free option

The Energy Conservation Assistance Program is run by BC Hydro and FortisBC. An energy evaluator visits for free, and if you qualify, they install upgrades for free. Some homes get a heat pump or insulation; others get smaller items. You don't choose the upgrades; the evaluator does.

What's different in Burnaby

  • The City of Burnaby's climate-friendly home upgrades page points residents to provincial rebates and the free CleanBC Energy Coach. We did not find a separate city cash top-up.

My tip for Burnaby: Many Burnaby homes are strata. If you live in a condo or townhouse, check your strata bylaws before you book an install, because outdoor units usually need approval.

What to do next

  1. Count the people in your home and add up the adults' income. Compare to the table.
  2. Pre-register with the CleanBC Energy Savings Program before any work starts, or apply to ECAP through your utility.
  3. Use a program-registered contractor. Self-installs are not eligible.
  4. If you don't qualify, check the regular rebates in our heat pump guide.

Questions people ask

Is the heat pump really free?

It can be. CleanBC pays 100% of eligible costs up to the limit, and ECAP installs upgrades at no charge. If your system costs more than the limit, you pay the difference.

Can I get the income-qualified rebate if I already have electric heat?

Only at Income Level 1, up to $5,000. The Level 2 electric stream was discontinued in July 2026. BC Hydro's regular rebate (up to $4,000) is open to any income.

Can I get a heat pump rebate for a Burnaby condo?

BC Hydro runs separate offers for condos and apartments. The house rebates on this page are for single-family homes, row homes and similar. Check BC Hydro's condo page and your strata rules first.
