Can you get a free heat pump in Fort St. John, BC?

If your household income qualifies, the answer is often yes — installed at no cost. Here's how it works in Fort St. John in 2026.

By Sam Menard · Updated September 2026 · 3 min read

This program doesn't apply to every household, but for the ones that qualify in Fort St. John, it's the biggest rebate in BC: a heat pump, insulation and weatherization, installed for free. No rebate paperwork, no upfront cost.

The program: ECAP

It's called the Energy Conservation Assistance Program (ECAP), run through BC Hydro, Fort St. John's electricity utility. If you qualify by income, it sends an energy coach to your home and installs energy-saving upgrades at no charge.

What "free" actually includes

Depending on your home, ECAP can cover a free air-source heat pump, insulation, draftproofing, ventilation, and a home energy assessment — all installed, not reimbursed. The federal Greener Homes Affordability money now flows through this same channel, which is part of why it's expanded.

Who qualifies in 2026

Eligibility is based on household income and size. Thresholds were recently raised — for example, a family of four earning up to about $57,200 can now qualify, up from roughly $44,000 before. Rough guide (exact numbers depend on your household and are confirmed on application):

Household sizeApprox. income ceiling
1 person~$28,000–$35,000
2 people~$35,000–$43,000
3 people~$43,000–$53,000
4 peopleup to ~$57,200

These are approximate and vary by household — treat them as "worth checking," not a hard cutoff. Renters and homeowners can both be eligible depending on the upgrade.

The Fort St. John angle

Fort St. John sits north of 100 Mile House, where the province adds up to $6,000 extra for switching off oil heat and up to $8,000 extra for switching off gas or propane — on top of the standard stack.

Not quite qualifying? You're still in great shape

If your income is above the ECAP line, you're not paying full price either — the regular CleanBC rebates in Fort St. John are large:

  • Heat pump: $4,000–$16,000 depending on income tier
  • Insulation: up to $5,500 · windows/doors: up to $2,000
  • Heat pump water heater: $1,000–$3,000

See the full local breakdown, including Fort St. John's own rebate stack, on the Fort St. John rebate page.

How to start in Fort St. John

  1. Check your rough eligibility with the assessment tool (it asks your income tier).
  2. Apply to the income-qualified program through BC Hydro's savings-based-on-income application.
  3. An energy coach assesses your home and arranges the free installs.
  4. Once you're set up, browse verified installers in Fort St. John for anything ECAP doesn't cover.
