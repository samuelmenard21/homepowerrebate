How We Built BC Coverage, City by City

Eighteen cities. Eight rebate categories each. No shortcuts on the research.

By Sam Menard · Updated August 2026 · 5 min read

Why we started with BC

British Columbia has the most coherent home energy rebate program in Canada: CleanBC. One heat pump rebate, one solar rebate, one insulation program — a homeowner can understand the whole system in an afternoon. That made it the right place to prove our model before expanding anywhere else.

What "complete" actually means to us

Every BC city page — from Vancouver down to Fort St. John — covers all 8 standard rebate categories: heat pump, solar, battery, insulation, water heater, windows and doors, EV charger, and smart thermostat. Not just the heat pump number that gets all the attention. A homeowner comparing what to upgrade first needs the full picture, and most rebate sites don't give it to them.

What we learned building 18 cities

Rebates vary more locally than most homeowners expect. Vancouver stacks CleanBC, BC Hydro, and a city-level top-up on top of each other. Kelowna runs on FortisBC gas instead of BC Hydro electricity, which changes the math entirely. Getting this right meant researching each city's actual utility territory, not assuming one province-wide number applies everywhere.

Where we are now: full coverage across 18 BC cities, and the model has expanded to Ontario, Alberta, Nova Scotia, and Massachusetts — same standard, same honesty about what's real versus outdated.
