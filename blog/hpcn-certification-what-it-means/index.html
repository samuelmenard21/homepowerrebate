HPCN: What It Means for BC Rebates

By Sam Menard · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer: HPCN is the Home Performance Contractor Network: a searchable list of BC heat pump, insulation, and window and door contractors who meet training and business standards. BC Hydro requires an HPCN member for its heat pump and insulation rebates. It's a good floor, not a guarantee of great work, so still check reviews and quotes.

What HPCN is

The HPCN is managed by the Home Performance Stakeholder Council, an independent not-for-profit. It isn't a government licence. Contractors don't pay a fee to be listed.

What members must meet

  • Extra training in the house-as-a-system, heat loss and heat gain calculations, and ventilation
  • At least three years in business in BC
  • Best-practice and rebate program training
  • WorkSafeBC compliance, a valid business licence, general liability insurance and standard warranties
  • A commitment to ethical business practices

Which rebates need an HPCN contractor

  • BC Hydro heat pump rebate (up to $4,000 when replacing electric heat)
  • BC Hydro insulation rebates
  • CleanBC Energy Savings Program (income-qualified) uses its own registered-contractor list. Check Better Homes BC.

If your contractor isn't eligible, you can lose the rebate. Check before you sign, not after.

How to check a contractor

  1. Search the Better Homes BC contractor tool or BC Hydro's contractor search.
  2. Filter by your area, the upgrade and the rebate program.
  3. Make sure the company name on your quote matches the listing.

What HPCN doesn't guarantee

  • Quality: read reviews and ask for references.
  • Price: get more than one quote for the same scope.
  • Sizing: ask for a written heat-loss calculation.

FAQ

What is HPCN in BC?

HPCN is the Home Performance Contractor Network, a searchable list of BC home-upgrade contractors (heat pump, insulation, and window and door contractors) managed by the Home Performance Stakeholder Council, an independent not-for-profit.

Do I need an HPCN contractor to get a rebate in BC?

For BC Hydro's heat pump and insulation rebates, yes. The contractor must be an HPCN member. CleanBC's Energy Savings Program uses its own list of registered contractors. Always check the program's rules before you sign.

How do I check if a contractor is in the HPCN?

Search the contractor tool on Better Homes BC or BC Hydro's HPCN search. You can filter by location, upgrade type and rebate program. If the company isn't listed, ask them why before you sign.

Does HPCN membership guarantee good work?

No. It means the contractor met training and business standards, such as three years in business in BC, WorkSafeBC compliance, liability insurance and program training. You still need to check reviews, references and warranty terms.
