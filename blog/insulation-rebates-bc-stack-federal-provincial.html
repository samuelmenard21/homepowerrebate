BC Insulation Rebates 2026: Stack Federal + Provincial Grants for Up to $11,350

BC homeowners can combine federal, provincial, and utility rebates on the same insulation project. Here's exactly how much you can claim—and why the stack matters.

The Stack: How Much You Can Claim

A single attic insulation project can qualify for rebates from three separate programs. They don't compete—they combine.

Program Insulation Rebate Eligibility
CleanBC (Provincial) $1,500–$5,500 All BC homeowners
BC Hydro (Utility) $900 BC Hydro customers
FortisBC (Utility) $500–$2,000 FortisBC gas customers; higher if income-qualified
Total Possible Stack Up to $11,350 Income-qualified homeowners, dual utility service

Why this matters: A typical $3,000 attic insulation project nets $2,000–$3,000 in rebates under a full stack. Your out-of-pocket cost drops to $0–$1,000. That's a payback period of under 2 years from heating savings alone.

Breaking Down Each Rebate

CleanBC: The Big One ($1,500–$5,500)

This is the largest rebate and applies to almost all BC homeowners. Income doesn't matter—you qualify based on the upgrade you're making.

  • Attic insulation: Add $500 for every R-value increase up to $2,500 (R-50 max payout)
  • Wall insulation: Up to $1,500
  • Crawlspace or basement: Up to $1,500
  • Combined ceiling/wall/floor: Up to $5,500 total per home per year

Key detail: You must use a BetterHomes BC contractor (HPCN member). DIY installations don't qualify.

BC Hydro: $900 Flat (Electricity Customers)

Applies to any insulation project. Doesn't stack with other insulation rebates from BC Hydro, but does stack with CleanBC and FortisBC.

  • Attic: $900 (regardless of R-value added)
  • Walls or basement: $900
  • Combined work: Still just $900 (one rebate per address per year)

Key detail: Must be HPCN-certified contractor. Invoice required within 6 months of completion.

FortisBC: $500–$2,000 (Gas Customers)

The wildcard. Non-income-qualified homeowners get $500; income-qualified get up to $2,000.

  • Basic rebate: $500 attic, $500 wall, $500 basement
  • Income-qualified: Up to $2,000 if you earn under ~$75K household income

Key detail: Application window is 90 days after installation. Invoice must show HPCN contractor. Processing takes 4–6 weeks.

Application Timeline: Step by Step

Week 1–2: Get quotes

Contact 2–3 BetterHomes BC contractors. Tell them you want to stack all available rebates—they'll know the requirements.

Week 3–4: Installation

Contractor handles the work. Keep your invoice and any before/after photos. Ask contractor for HPCN certification number.

Week 5–6: Submit to CleanBC + BC Hydro

Contractor usually submits CleanBC rebate on your behalf. You submit BC Hydro rebate separately (takes 15 minutes online).

Week 7–10: Submit to FortisBC (if applicable)

Application window is 90 days after completion. Submit invoice + HPCN cert. Processing takes 4–6 weeks.

Week 12–16: Rebate checks arrive

CleanBC (4–8 weeks) → BC Hydro (4–6 weeks) → FortisBC (4–6 weeks). Stagger submissions for cash flow.

Pro tip: Submit applications in this order: CleanBC first (largest, longest wait), then BC Hydro, then FortisBC. This spreads the rebate checks across 3–4 months and keeps your cash flow smooth.

Who Qualifies: Income & Utility Breakdown

CleanBC (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3)

All BC homeowners qualify for at least Tier 1. Higher income = lower rebate, but you still get something.

  • Tier 1 (Under $110K): $1,500 rebate on attic insulation
  • Tier 2 ($110K–$165K): $750 rebate on attic insulation
  • Tier 3 (Over $165K): $500 rebate on attic insulation

BC Hydro

All BC Hydro customers qualify. No income requirement.

FortisBC

All FortisBC gas customers qualify for basic rebate. Income-qualified (under ~$75K) get the higher tier.

If You Have Dual Service (Both BC Hydro + FortisBC)

You qualify for CleanBC + BC Hydro + FortisBC rebates on the same project. This is rare but possible in some border regions. Stack everything.

Common Questions

Can I DIY insulation and still get rebates?
No. All three programs require HPCN-certified contractors. DIY installations disqualify you from every rebate. The contractor cost is worth it for the rebate stack.
What if I already got a furnace rebate—can I still stack insulation rebates?
Yes. Furnace rebates are separate from insulation rebates. You can claim both in the same year from the same utility.
Do I need a home energy audit?
CleanBC requires an EnerGuide pre-upgrade assessment (not an audit—just a quick inspection). BC Hydro and FortisBC do not. Your contractor handles this.
What's the timeline from completion to rebate check?
CleanBC: 4–8 weeks. BC Hydro: 4–6 weeks. FortisBC: 4–6 weeks after submission (which can be up to 90 days after completion). Total: 2–5 months depending on submission order.
What if I have oil or propane heat instead of gas?
CleanBC and BC Hydro still apply. FortisBC doesn't (they only service gas customers). You get up to $2,400 instead of $2,900+.
Can I claim the rebate if I already applied years ago?
Each program has a one-per-address-per-year rule. If you claimed insulation rebates in 2025, you're ineligible for 2026. Mark your calendar for next year.
