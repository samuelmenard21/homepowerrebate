Nova Scotia Had Canada's Best Heat Pump Rebate. It Quietly Vanished.

Search "Nova Scotia heat pump rebate" today and a lot of what comes back is describing a program that no longer exists.

By Sam Menard · Updated August 2026 · 6 min read

What it used to be

For years, Nova Scotia's home heating rebates had a real claim to being the most generous in Canada — a byproduct of the province's high electricity rates and unusually large share of homes still heated with oil. Stackable grants, interest-free loans, and a dedicated Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program (OHPA) top-up made the province a genuine leader.

What changed, and when

  • December 31, 2025: The core Home Heating System Rebates Program closed to new applications.
  • July 2, 2026: The federal OHPA program — which added up to $15,000 combined for oil-to-heat-pump conversions — closed to new applicants after available funding was fully allocated.

Nova Scotia moved first here, ahead of most other provinces making similar cuts. That timing matters: a lot of "current" rebate content published in 2024 and 2025 is now describing a program that simply doesn't exist anymore, and it hasn't been updated.

What's actually left in 2026

Efficiency Nova Scotia still offers up to $5,000 to any homeowner, up to $15,000 for moderate-income households, and free installation for income-qualified homes through HomeWarming. Insulation, air sealing, and smart thermostats remain free. Solar still pays $0.60/watt. Halifax adds its own Solar City financing program on top.

It's a real program. It's just not the program most content online is still describing.

If you're planning around Nova Scotia numbers: verify directly with Efficiency Nova Scotia before budgeting a project. A lot of installer and blog content quoting "$15,000+ stackable" rebates is describing the pre-Dec-2025 program.
