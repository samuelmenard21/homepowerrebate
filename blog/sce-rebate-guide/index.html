What Does SCE Actually Pay for a Heat Pump?

You live in Riverside, San Bernardino, Ontario, or Moreno Valley. SCE is your electric company. Here's the real rebate number, not a guess.

You Just Want a Straight Answer

You're looking at a broken furnace or a sky-high summer AC bill. You live in Southern California Edison (SCE) territory — that's Riverside, San Bernardino, Ontario, and Moreno Valley, all part of the Inland Empire. You want to know: what will SCE actually pay me for a heat pump?

Short answer: around $3,100 through a program called TECH Clean CA, and possibly more if you live in a lower-income or "hard-to-reach" area.

The Main Program: TECH Clean CA

SCE doesn't hand out heat pump rebates directly through its own website in most cases. Instead, it funds a statewide program called TECH Clean CA. Your installer applies for this rebate on your behalf — you don't fill out separate SCE paperwork.

  • Base rebate: about $3,100 for a qualifying heat pump system
  • Disadvantaged Community (DAC) bonus: higher rebate if your neighborhood is flagged as a DAC by the state
  • Hard-to-reach bonus: extra money if you're in a rural or under-served part of the SCE service area

Check the current rebate table on SCE's own rebate marketplace at sce.com before you sign a contract — rebate amounts get updated during the year.

What Else You Can Stack

1. Federal HEEHRA rebate

The federal High-Efficiency Electric Home Rebate program can add thousands more if your household income qualifies. This stacks on top of TECH Clean CA — it doesn't replace it.

2. EV charger rebate: $500

If you also install a home EV charger, SCE offers around $500 through the same marketplace program.

3. Smart thermostat rebate: $50

A small but easy add-on. Ask your contractor to include it on the same paperwork.

What It Actually Costs

Across California, a typical heat pump install runs around $19,844 before any rebate. With the SCE/TECH Clean CA rebate of $3,100, plus federal credits if you qualify, your real bill drops significantly. Get two or three contractor quotes — prices vary a lot by installer.

How to Apply

  1. Pick a contractor enrolled in TECH Clean CA (ask them directly — if they don't know the program, keep calling).
  2. Get your quote in writing before work starts.
  3. Your contractor submits the rebate paperwork — you usually don't have to.
  4. Rebate is typically applied as a discount on your invoice, not a mail-in check.
