Your Full SDG&E Rebate Picture

San Diego, Chula Vista, and Escondido homeowners: here's everything SDG&E offers, in one place.

What You Actually Get as an SDG&E Customer

You want the whole list, not scattered pages. Here's what San Diego Gas & Electric offers homeowners in San Diego, Chula Vista, and Escondido right now.

The Rebates

1. Heat pump rebate: $250–$500

This is a modest rebate. San Diego's mild climate means less heating demand than colder parts of the state, which is part of why the number is smaller — more on that in our deeper explainer.

2. Battery rebate (SGIP): $1,000+

SGIP, the Self-Generation Incentive Program, pays toward home battery storage. In SDG&E territory this is one of the stronger programs available — often worth more attention than the heat pump rebate alone.

3. EV charger rebate: $500

A flat rebate for installing a home EV charger.

4. Smart thermostat rebate: $50

Small, but stack it onto any other project for free extra savings.

Why Heating Isn't the Big Story Here

San Diego's climate is mild. In technical terms, the area has around 1,100–1,200 heating degree days a year — a measure of how much heating a home typically needs. That's low compared to most of the state. A heat pump still helps, especially for cooling, but it won't save as much on heating bills as it would in a colder city.

Where the Real Money Is: Solar + Battery

If you're deciding where to put your budget, SGIP battery incentives combined with solar tend to pencil out better for most SDG&E households than a heat pump alone. See our full breakdown: Heat Pump or Solar in San Diego?

How to Apply

  1. Check current rebate amounts at sdge.com/rebates before signing any contract.
  2. Confirm your contractor is enrolled in the relevant SDG&E or SGIP program.
  3. Apply before installation starts wherever the program requires pre-approval.
