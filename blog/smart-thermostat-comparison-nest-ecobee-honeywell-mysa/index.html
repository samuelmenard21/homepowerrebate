Quick quiz: would you keep your rebate?

Five questions. Tap an answer to see why.

1. You have a gas furnace in BC. Does BC Hydro's heat pump rebate apply?

BC Hydro's rebate is for replacing electric heat. Gas homes should look at other programs, such as FortisBC for its own customers.

2. In Ontario's heat pump stream, what comes first?

Written pre-approval comes before the install.

3. What does a BC Hydro battery need to reach the $5,000 rebate?

Peak Saver enrollment, within 14 days of the in-service date. Tesla battery models are excluded.

4. A solar system under an Ontario net metering agreement is eligible for the Home Renovation Savings stream.

False. The stream's requirements say systems under net metering are not eligible.

5. When should you check a product against the qualified list?

Before. Swapping equipment after the fact is the hardest fix.

Mysa vs Ecobee vs Nest: Which Smart Thermostat Fits Your Heat?

The right thermostat depends on one thing: how your home is heated. Here's how to pick in 30 seconds, what each one costs, and which rebates each one actually gets.

By Sam Menard · Updated October 16, 2026 · 8 min read

Deadline coming up

  • October 31, 2026: FortisBC instant in-store rebates on connected thermostats end October 31. Official source

All upcoming deadlines

Short answer: Mysa vs Ecobee comes down to your heat. Electric baseboards? Get Mysa (or Sinopé). Ecobee and Nest can't run baseboards at all. Furnace or ducted heat pump? Ecobee or Nest are the usual picks, and Mysa's Central HVAC model is a cheaper option. Ductless mini-split? Mysa for Mini-Split or Cielo. In BC, baseboard homes can get up to five Mysa or Sinopé thermostats free from BC Hydro starting October 2026.

Safety note: Never wire a 24V thermostat like Ecobee or Nest to a 240V baseboard heater. It won't work, and it can damage the thermostat. Thick wires and a "240V" label mean line voltage. If you're not sure, turn off the breaker and ask an electrician.

Mysa vs Ecobee: which is better?

Verdict: Mysa for electric baseboards, Ecobee for a furnace or ducted heat pump.

This is the question most people search. The honest answer: they rarely compete for the same home. Mysa started as a baseboard thermostat. Ecobee is built for furnaces and ducted heat pumps. Here's how they line up.

  • Pick Mysa if you have baseboards. Ecobee can't control them. A 4-room baseboard home needs about four Mysas, so price it per room.
  • Pick Ecobee if you have a furnace or ducted heat pump and want room sensors to even out hot and cold spots. It needs a C-wire, and Ecobee includes an adapter if you don't have one.
  • Furnace, but on a budget? Mysa for Central HVAC lists for less than any Ecobee. Then it's a true head-to-head: app, looks and price.

Same story as Ecobee. Nest is a 24V thermostat for furnaces and ducted systems. It can't run baseboards.

BC Hydro and Mysa: free thermostats this fall

Free from BC Hydro: BC Hydro customers with electric baseboard heat can now order up to five free smart thermostats from Mysa or Sinopé, valued at $600, with free shipping. You enroll in Peak Saver at checkout. Order on BC Hydro's page, or see every BC thermostat rebate.

  • Peak Saver pays you each winter. $50 a season, or $100 if you're income-qualified. During peak events (four hours max, November to March), BC Hydro may lower your heat a couple of degrees. You can override any event. Skip more than half and you may lose that season's reward.
  • Already own a Mysa? Enrolling an eligible baseboard model in Peak Saver earns a $100 one-time credit plus the $50 seasonal reward. That's the "up to $150" you may have seen. It's a bill credit, not money off the price.
  • Furnace or ducted heat pump? An Ecobee or Nest doesn't qualify with BC Hydro. Pick on features, not rebates.
  • FortisBC area? If FortisBC sends your electric bill, BC Hydro programs don't apply. Use FortisBC's offers instead.

Mysa vs Cielo (mini-splits)

Verdict: Mysa if you want a clean look and Apple Home. Cielo if you want a thermostat with buttons. Check your unit's own Wi-Fi adapter first.

Both control a ductless heat pump by sending the same signals as its remote.

  • Looks: Mysa is a minimal white unit. Cielo's Breez Max looks more like a classic wall thermostat with buttons.
  • Smart home: Mysa works with Apple Home, Alexa, Google and SmartThings, and its app has no subscription.
  • Check first: Many heat pump brands sell their own Wi-Fi adapter. It talks to the unit directly and can show more detail. Ask your installer before you buy either.

Mysa vs Hilo (Quebec)

This isn't really a choice between two thermostats. Hilo is a service from Hydro-Québec, and Mysa works with it. Hilo pairs with Hydro-Québec's Rate Flex D. You pay less for power most of the winter, but more during peak demand events. During those events, Hilo turns your connected devices down for you.

  • Who can use it: Hydro-Québec customers only. Outside Quebec, Hilo doesn't apply.
  • Hardware: it starts with the Hilo hub. You can use Hilo's own products or connect compatible Mysa, Sinopé or Honeywell Home thermostats.
  • The bonus: Hilo pays up to $210 per connected device, enough to cover up to the full price after manufacturer discounts. You must connect all your eligible devices and agree to 12 months of automatic peak-event control. It's paid once per device, the number of devices is limited, and the offer can change without notice.
  • Savings: Hilo says its customers save $150 per winter on average, and up to 20% of the winter bill.

So if you're in Quebec with baseboards and want Mysa, check Hilo's compatible devices list before you buy. Buying a listed Mysa model and connecting it to Hilo can earn the bonus. Checked October 3, 2026.

Mysa vs Hoot (baseboards)

Verdict: Mysa in BC, because Hoot is not on BC Hydro's Peak Saver thermostat list.

KING's Hoot is a Wi-Fi baseboard thermostat for 120V, 208V and 240V heaters. Check your wiring against the model's specs before you buy. In BC there's one big difference: Hoot isn't on BC Hydro's Peak Saver thermostat list. Mysa and Sinopé are. That list is what earns the $100 sign-up credit and $50 yearly reward.

When a smart thermostat isn't worth it

BC Hydro says smart thermostats cut the electricity used for space heating by about 9% on average, and up to $150 a year when they run baseboards well. That's real money. But be honest with yourself:

  • You already keep the heat low and steady. A thermostat saves money by turning heat down when you're away or asleep. If you already do that by hand, savings are small.
  • You're about to replace baseboards with a heat pump. Don't buy five baseboard thermostats you'll pull off the wall next year. In BC, take the free ones instead.
  • You rent. Ask your landlord first. Rebates usually need the account holder.

The bigger win for baseboard homes is a ductless heat pump. It often uses half the electricity or less for the same heat, and adds air conditioning. Common questions

Which is better, Mysa or Ecobee?

It depends on your heating. Mysa's baseboard thermostats run electric baseboard heaters, which Ecobee can't control. Ecobee is a 24V thermostat for furnaces and ducted heat pumps. If you have baseboards, choose Mysa or Sinopé. If you have a furnace or ducted heat pump, choose Ecobee, Nest or Mysa for Central HVAC.

Is Mysa or Nest better?

Mysa is better for electric baseboards, because Nest can't control them. Nest is a strong pick for a furnace or ducted heat pump, especially if you use Google Home. Mysa also makes a Central HVAC model for furnaces.

Is there an ecobee rebate?

It depends on your utility. Ontario's Home Renovation Savings program pays $125 on listed ecobee models. FortisBC pays up to $150 for connected thermostats on electric central heat. Red Deer pays $50 for ENERGY STAR models. BC Hydro does not rebate ecobee, because its offers only cover baseboard thermostats.

Can Nest or Ecobee control electric baseboard heaters?

No. Nest and Ecobee are low-voltage (24V) thermostats. Electric baseboards run on 120V or 240V line voltage and need a line-voltage thermostat such as Mysa, Sinopé or KING Hoot. Never wire a 24V thermostat to a baseboard heater.

Can I get a free Mysa thermostat from BC Hydro?

Yes, if you are a BC Hydro residential customer with electric baseboard heat. You can now order up to five free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats, which BC Hydro values at $600. You enroll in Peak Saver when you order, which pays $50 per household each winter.

Is Mysa or Hoot better for baseboard heaters?

In BC, Mysa. Hoot is not on BC Hydro's Peak Saver thermostat list, and Mysa and Sinopé are. Outside BC, both run 120V, 208V and 240V baseboards, so check wiring and your utility's list before you buy.

Is Mysa or Cielo better for a mini-split heat pump?

Both control a ductless mini-split by mimicking its remote. Mysa has a minimal design, works with Apple Home, Alexa, Google and SmartThings, and has a subscription-free app. Cielo's Breez Max looks more like a classic wall thermostat. Also ask your installer about your heat pump brand's own Wi-Fi adapter.

Should I get Mysa or Hilo in Quebec?

You can have both. Hilo is a Hydro-Québec service, not a rival thermostat, and Mysa thermostats can connect to it. Connecting a compatible Mysa to Hilo can earn a bonus of up to $210 per device if you agree to 12 months of automatic control during peak events.

How much does a smart thermostat save?

BC Hydro says smart thermostats cut the electricity used for space heating by about 9% on average, and up to $150 a year when used to control baseboard heating efficiently.
