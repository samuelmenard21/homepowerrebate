When you pair a solar system with a smart thermostat and battery, you unlock something most homeowners miss: **automatic Peak Saver optimization**. Your thermostat learns when peak demand hits (4–9 PM in winter) and automatically defers your heating to off-peak hours.

Result: Lower energy bills + higher Peak Saver rewards.

## The Peak Saver magic

During winter peak demand hours (4–9 PM), BC Hydro pays you to *not* use electricity. A smart thermostat lets your battery discharge to power heating while your heat pump runs at off-peak times (late night, early morning) to recharge.

Result: You get paid twice:
1. Peak Saver rewards (from BC Hydro)
2. Lower total heating bills (from shifting demand)

## How it works with solar + battery

**Scenario: 5 PM on a cold winter day**
- Peak demand hits (grid is stressed)
- Your smart thermostat notices it's peak time
- Battery automatically discharges to heat your home
- Your solar + battery system powers your house
- BC Hydro credits you as Peak Saver participant
- Off-peak (11 PM): heat pump recharges battery for next morning

Without the smart thermostat? You'd still benefit from solar + battery, but you're leaving Peak Saver rewards on the table.

## Pairing with heat pumps

If you're installing a heat pump alongside solar + battery:

1. **Smart thermostat** controls the heat pump
2. **Heat pump** uses Peak Saver scheduling to shift heating load
3. **Battery** provides backup during outages
4. **Solar** charges the battery during the day

This is the *full* optimization: you're not just heating efficiently, you're heating *strategically* based on grid demand.

## One thing to watch: Interoperability

Not every smart thermostat works perfectly with every battery system. Before you buy:

1. **Ask your installer** which thermostat they recommend for your specific inverter/battery combo
2. **Check the manufacturer website** for Peak Saver compatibility
3. **Confirm the app integration** works with your phone/home automation

99% of the time it's seamless. But it's worth 5 minutes of verification before you install.
