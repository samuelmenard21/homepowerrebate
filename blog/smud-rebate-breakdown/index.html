Every SMUD Rebate, Broken Down

Sacramento, Folsom, Roseville, Rancho Cordova: SMUD's rebates are some of the best in California. Here's the full list.

Why SMUD Is Different

SMUD (Sacramento Municipal Utility District) is a public, locally-run utility — not an investor-owned company like PG&E or SCE. That structure is a big part of why its rebates run higher than most of the state. If you're in Sacramento, Folsom, Roseville, or Rancho Cordova, here's every program, listed out.

The Full List

1. Heat pump rebate: $3,000–$4,000 per ton

This is one of the strongest heat pump rebates in the state's utility landscape. A typical 3-ton system could see $9,000–$12,000 in rebates alone.

2. Panel upgrade rebate: $2,000

Older homes often need an electrical panel upgrade before they can support a heat pump or EV charger. SMUD helps cover that cost.

3. Battery rebate (including Tesla Powerwall): $5,400

A meaningful incentive toward home battery storage.

4. Heat pump water heater rebate: $4,000

Separate from the space-heating heat pump rebate above — this is for your hot water tank.

5. Solar rebate: $2,000

An incentive for new solar installations.

6. My Energy Optimizer + storage enrollment bonus: up to $10,000

SMUD's demand-response program pays homeowners who enroll their battery storage to help balance the grid. This is separate, ongoing money on top of the upfront rebates above.

7. EV charger rebate: $600

For installing a home Level 2 EV charger.

8. Smart thermostat rebate: $50

The easiest one to claim — add it to any other project.

How Much This Can Add Up To

Stack a 3-ton heat pump ($9,000–$12,000), a heat pump water heater ($4,000), and a battery ($5,400), and you're looking at $18,000+ in rebates before federal credits. Always confirm current figures — SMUD updates its programs regularly.
