Window & Door Replacement Rebates in Surrey, BC

By Sam Menard · Updated September 2026 · 4 min read

Surrey homeowners get the standard provincial window rebate, no extra municipal layer to track.

The short version for Surrey: ENERGY STAR window and door replacement is rebated $500-$2,000 through BC Hydro and CleanBC, and there's no extra municipal top-up on top of the provincial and utility programs.

How much rebate you get in Surrey

Surrey's window and door rebate runs $500-$2,000, delivered through BC Hydro and the CleanBC Better Homes program, for replacing old windows and doors with ENERGY STAR-certified models. It's typically claimed as part of a whole-home retrofit; a standalone window swap usually lands toward the lower end of that range.

Who qualifies

  • You own your home in Surrey
  • You're replacing operable windows or exterior doors with ENERGY STAR-certified models
  • Windows must have a U-value of 1.72 or lower (typically triple-pane, low-E coating)
  • Doors must be ENERGY STAR-certified for your climate zone
  • Income-qualified households get higher rebates
  • Work done by a licensed installer may be required

The Surrey angle

Surrey follows the standard provincial stack for windows and doors, with no separate municipal program layered on top. The number above is the complete picture — no hidden extra layer to track down.

Rebate programs in Surrey

CleanBC Retrofit Program (Primary)

Covers windows and doors as part of a whole-home retrofit package, often bundled with insulation, heat pumps, or other upgrades. Window rebates in Surrey range within $500-$2,000 depending on the number of openings and your income tier.

Energy Conservation Assistance Program (ECAP)

For low-income Surrey households, delivered through BC Hydro. Provides deeper rebates on windows and doors, sometimes at no cost to homeowners. Income limits apply; check eligibility with BC Hydro directly.

  • Full-home window replacement: $8,000–$15,000 (10–15 windows + doors)
  • Rebate coverage: $500-$2,000 of total project cost
  • Energy savings: $400–$800/year in heating costs (varies by home size and current windows)

How to claim the rebate in Surrey

  1. Get a home energy audit (required before work starts; often included in retrofit package)
  2. Get quotes from verified Surrey installers familiar with BC Hydro and CleanBC programs
  3. Confirm your windows/doors meet ENERGY STAR requirements and U-value specs
  4. Work is completed by a licensed contractor
  5. Installer files the rebate claim with CleanBC or BC Hydro
  6. Rebate arrives in 4–8 weeks (sometimes longer for ECAP)

Tips for maximizing your rebate

  • Bundle with other upgrades: Whole-home retrofits (windows + insulation + heat pump) often qualify for higher total rebates than standalone window work — see the Surrey heat pump rebate guide for how the two stack together.
  • Check income thresholds: Low-to-median income households qualify for deeper rebates; ECAP offers the best deals for eligible applicants.
  • Verify ENERGY STAR certification: Ask the installer for proof that windows/doors are certified; non-certified models don't qualify.
  • Compare installers: Licensed, experienced contractors are more likely to navigate rebate paperwork correctly and avoid delays.
  • Plan for air sealing: Window replacement is often combined with air sealing (caulking, weatherstripping) which can boost total rebate eligibility.
