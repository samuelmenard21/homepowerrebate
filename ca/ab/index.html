Alberta Home Energy Rebates 2026

Alberta has no province-wide home energy rebate. Most help comes as financing through your city, plus a few small local rebates. Here's what's actually open, city by city.

What changed recently in Alberta

Checked September 28, 2026. See every change.

  • September 2026: Calgary Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) (paused). Calgary's CEIP financing (up to $50,000 repaid on your property tax) is closed to new applications until winter 2026/2027. Source
  • September 2026: Lethbridge and St. Albert CEIP (paused). Lethbridge's CEIP is at capacity and closed to new pre-qualification. St. Albert is taking waitlist applications only. Source
  • July 2026: Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (Alberta) (ended). The federal grant of up to $10,000 to switch from oil to a heat pump closed to Albertans on July 31, 2026. Source
  • December 2025: Canada Greener Homes Grant (ended). The last day to submit documents was December 31, 2025. The Greener Homes Loan stopped taking applications October 1, 2025. Source

Program guides: Alberta energy rebates

Short answer: There is no Alberta government rebate for heat pumps, furnaces, insulation or solar in 2026. The federal Greener Homes Grant and Loan are closed, and the federal oil-to-heat-pump grant closed to Albertans on July 31, 2026. What's left is CEIP, a city program that lends you up to $50,000 for upgrades and lets you pay it back on your property tax bill. But several cities' CEIP programs are full right now. A few cities add small cash: Lethbridge ($1,350), St. Albert ($1,400) and Red Deer ($50 thermostat rebate).

Is there a heat pump or furnace rebate in Alberta?

Not from the province. We also couldn't find a current published heat pump or furnace rebate from ENMAX, ATCO or EPCOR in September 2026. Utility offers can appear and disappear, so call yours before you buy. Your real options are CEIP financing (if your city has room) and, for new mortgages, CMHC Eco Plus, a partial refund of mortgage insurance premiums for energy-efficient homes.

Common questions

Does Alberta have an energy efficient home rebate in 2026?

No province-wide one. Alberta's main tool is CEIP, a city-run loan repaid on your property tax bill. Lethbridge and St. Albert add small cash incentives, and Red Deer has a $50 thermostat rebate.

Are there furnace rebates in Alberta?

We found none from the province or from ENMAX, ATCO or EPCOR in September 2026. CEIP may finance a qualifying heating upgrade if your city's program is open.

Is the oil to heat pump grant open in Alberta?

No. July 31, 2026 was the last day for Albertans to apply.
