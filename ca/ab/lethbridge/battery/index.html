Battery Storage Rebates in Lethbridge

Here's exactly what Lethbridge homeowners can get for battery storage, plus what to actually do about it.

How much you get

No cash rebate or grant exists for home battery storage in Alberta in 2026 — not from the province, and not from Fortis Alberta, Lethbridge's electricity utility. What exists is financing, and Lethbridge's version of it pays better than most of the province.

Lethbridge participates in CEIP but sets its own rate and its own top-up: as of 2026 the city runs a 2.83% fixed interest rate and a $1,350 rebate — up from $800 — applied directly against your financed balance, on top of the standard $50,000 cap and up to 25-year repayment through your property tax bill. That covers an eligible battery, usually paired with solar. The city raised the rebate after the federal Canada Greener Homes Grant was cancelled, to offset some of that loss. You'll need at least $3,000 of eligible project cost to qualify. (See CEIP's renewable energy upgrades page →)

Don't confuse this with Lethbridge's separate Environmental Incentive Program — that one only covers rain barrels and low-flow toilets, credited to your water utility account. It won't help pay for a battery.

The federal Canada Greener Homes Loan — which used to offer up to $40,000 interest-free financing that could cover battery storage — closed to new applicants on October 1, 2025, and is no longer an option for 2026 applications. (Confirm current status →)

Bottom line: if you want a home battery in Lethbridge, CEIP financing at the city's 2.83% rate plus the $1,350 balance rebate is the real path — not a cash rebate, but a better deal than the province-wide default. Apply and get approved before signing a contract with an installer.

How to claim it

  1. Apply for CEIP financing and get approved before signing a contract or starting work
  2. Sign a Clean Energy Improvement Agreement covering the battery (and solar, if paired)
  3. Have your system installed by a qualified contractor
  4. Repayment is added to your property tax bill, not a separate loan payment

Common questions

How much is the battery storage rebate or incentive in Lethbridge?

There isn't a cash rebate for home battery storage in Alberta. Alberta's CEIP program will finance an eligible battery system (usually paired with solar) through property-tax repayment, up to $50,000 — no grant currently exists. Lethbridge does apply its own 2.83% CEIP rate and a $1,350 rebate against the financed balance, which is better than most other Alberta cities get.
