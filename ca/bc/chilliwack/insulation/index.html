A Chilliwack rancher

A 1,400 square foot attic at R-20 topped to R-50 adds R-30. That pays 0.02 x 30 x 1,400 = $840. Add R-10 to 700 square feet of crawlspace wall for 0.09 x 10 x 700 = $630. The total is $1,470 by our arithmetic.

A city of detached houses

The 2021 Census counted 35,760 households in Chilliwack. Single-detached houses make up 54% of them, and 72% of households own their home. Of the households, 12,080 (34%) live in homes built before 1981, and 6,495 are owner-occupied houses from that era. Only 21% live in apartments.

Farms and outbuildings

Chilliwack has a lot of farmland. The rebate covers the home only, not barns, garages, workshops or other outbuildings, and the home must be a year-round primary residence at least 12 months old. A secondary suite in a detached house can qualify if it has its own meter.

The weather

Chilliwack's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was 0.2°C and the coldest day was -17.5°C. Winter is wet more than snowy, and cold outflow winds can drive the lows down. Insulation helps comfort and moisture control as much as the heating bill.

Which insulation to choose

BC Hydro accepts batts, loose fill, board and spray foam. A Chilliwack rancher with an open attic usually gets loose fill, which adds depth over the old layer. Closed walls are often filled by blowing material through small holes. Rigid board and spray foam suit basement walls and rim joists. Compare quotes by finished R-value, not by thickness, since each material carries a different R-value per inch.

The rebate cannot be more than the invoice shows you paid.

What 'Home Performance Contractor Network' means

BC Hydro requires an HPCN member for insulation. The network is run by an independent non-profit, the Home Performance Stakeholder Council. Members need three years in business in BC, training, WorkSafeBC and insurance, and homeowners pay no fee to use one. Check the current registry before you sign.

If your income is lower

BC Hydro sends income-qualified customers to the CleanBC Better Homes Energy Savings Program, and the two cannot be combined. That 2026 program document has no insulation or window rebates. The free Energy Conservation Assistance Program may include insulation, so ask there first.

If the BC Hydro account is held by a parent or a landlord, they must sign a consent form before you can apply.

Rules to check

  • An HPCN member must install the insulation.
  • Insulation must follow the program's best-practice guide for air sealing.
  • The rebate cannot exceed what the invoice shows you paid.
  • Apply within six months.

How to claim in Chilliwack

  1. Confirm the home is electric-heated, a primary residence and at least 12 months old.
  2. Collect quotes listing the R-value added for each area.
  3. Keep the final invoice.
  4. Apply online within six months.
  5. Combine with another upgrade to check the bonus.

Common questions

Does a barn or shop qualify?

No. Only the home qualifies, not outbuildings.

Can a house with a rental suite claim?

Yes, if both are individually metered.

How cold does Chilliwack get?

The average January low was 0.2°C and the coldest day in 2015 to 2024 was -17.5°C.

How much can an attic earn?

Up to $900, with at least R12 added.

What if I heat with gas?

Look at FortisBC, which lists the same formula for gas customers.
