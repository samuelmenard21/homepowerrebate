Solar and Battery Rebates in Coquitlam

BC Hydro pays up to $5,000 for solar panels and up to $5,000 for a home battery in Coquitlam. A battery can keep your lights and fridge on in an outage; the rebate makes it cheaper.

How much sun Coquitlam gets

Coquitlam averaged about 3.21 peak sun hours a day from 2020 to 2024. Hillside homes facing south can do better than the average; homes on north slopes or under tall trees can do much worse. As a rough guide, a 5 kW system here would make about 4,687 kWh a year, using 3.21 sun hours times 365 days and assuming 80% of the panel rating reaches your panel after losses. Your installer's design for your own roof will be more accurate.

Homes in Coquitlam

The 2021 Census counted 55,945 households in Coquitlam. 35% of Coquitlam households live in a detached house (19,700 homes), and about 69% own. Ask the installer for a shade report for your own roof before you sign.

Shade is the real limit

With 3.21 peak sun hours, Coquitlam's average is fine. Trees, hills and next-door buildings are what decide how much your own roof makes. A good quote includes a shade report with month-by-month output. If a third of the roof is shaded in winter, expect the yearly total to come in under the rough guide above.

Battery first: outage backup

A battery stores power for when the grid goes down. The rebate is $500 per kWh, so a 10 kWh battery that cost at least $10,000 can reach $5,000 with Peak Saver. Without Peak Saver, the battery must be paired with solar and the rebate stops at $1,500.

The 50% cap and the order of steps

Every rebate is limited to 50% of the installed cost. You must hire a contractor in the Home Performance Contractor Network and submit a self-generation application to BC Hydro before you install. Wait for technical acceptance. After install, send the final inspection documents and paid invoices. BC Hydro pays within 60 days of approval.

Tesla Powerwall is not eligible

Tesla's Powerwall 2 and 3, Tesla inverters and Tesla chargers are not eligible for BC Hydro or CleanBC rebates. The only exception is an application made before March 12, 2025.

How to claim it

  1. Confirm your home is on BC Hydro's grid.
  2. Hire a Home Performance Contractor Network member.
  3. Submit the self-generation application before you install, and wait for technical acceptance.
  4. Install. Enroll the battery in Peak Saver within 14 days of the in-service date if you want the $5,000 battery rebate.
  5. Send the final inspection documents and paid invoices.

Common questions

How much sun does Coquitlam get?

About 3.21 peak sun hours a day on average from 2020 to 2024, from the Open-Meteo historical archive.

What does Peak Saver ask of my battery?

BC Hydro can draw on it during a few winter peak events, November to March, for up to four hours. You can opt out of events, but opting out of more than half may cost you the seasonal reward.

Can I get the battery rebate without solar?

Yes, but only if you enroll the battery in Peak Saver. Then the battery-only rebate can reach $5,000.

Is the Tesla Powerwall eligible?

No. Tesla batteries get $0 from BC Hydro and CleanBC.

Who can apply?

Owners of single-family homes, duplexes, townhomes and mobile homes connected to BC Hydro's grid.
