Who in Fort St. John can use this

The 2021 Census counted 8,775 households in Fort St. John. About 58% own, 50% live in detached houses and 42% rent. Of the households, 2,035 own a detached house built before 1981. Fort St. John's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was -15.0°C, and the coldest day was -42.2°C.

Gas tank or electric tank?

The rebate is only for replacing an electric water heater. If your tank runs on natural gas, BC Hydro and FortisBC do not pay for a switch to a heat pump model. Lower-income households can use the CleanBC Energy Savings Program, which pays up to $3,500 for Level 1 households converting from a fossil fuel. Only one primary water heating rebate is allowed per home across programs.

Choose from the NEEA list

BC Hydro pays only for models that sit in Tier 2 or higher on NEEA's Advanced Water Heater Specification list for heat pump water heaters. Ask the installer for the exact model number and check it against the list before you sign. Put the model on the invoice. A tank that is not on the list will not be paid, no matter how efficient it looks.

Documents and deadline

You have six months from the invoice date to apply. The only supporting document is the invoice, and BC Hydro publishes a sample showing what it must list. The installer must be a licensed contractor with a GST number and a valid BC business licence for the trade. Self-installation is not eligible, and the new tank must not have been used in another home.

Where the tank goes

A heat pump water heater needs air, and a very cold garage or crawlspace is a hard place for one. Ask the installer for a written note on how the model performs at your coldest room temperature and what backup heat it uses. The program does not set a temperature rule, so the answer comes from the model's specifications.

How to claim in Fort St. John

  1. Check that the tank you are replacing is electric and is your primary water heater.
  2. Pick a model from NEEA's Tier 2 or higher list and get it on the quote.
  3. Have a licensed contractor install it and keep the invoice.
  4. Apply online within six months of the invoice date.
  5. If your income is lower, ask about the CleanBC program before paying.

Common questions

Does a cold room matter?

The program does not set a temperature rule. Ask the installer how the model works in your space.

Can renters claim?

The account holder applies, or signs a consent form.

What if my tank is gas?

BC Hydro and FortisBC do not pay for a gas switch.

Is there a bonus?

BC Hydro lists a bonus of up to $2,000 for more than one eligible upgrade.

Which model must I choose?

One listed in Tier 2 or higher on NEEA's heat pump water heater list.
