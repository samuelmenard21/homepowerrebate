BC Hydro and CleanBC Rebates 2026

Up to $10,000 in rebates for solar + battery, plus heat pump incentives. Find your city below and compare top-rated local installers.

Short answer: BC Hydro pays up to $4,000 for a heat pump that replaces electric heat, up to $10,000 for solar plus a battery, and free Mysa or Sinopé thermostats for baseboard homes. CleanBC pays income-qualified homes up to $13,000 to switch from gas, oil or propane to a heat pump. FortisBC customers have their own rebates instead.

What changed recently in BC

Checked September 29, 2026. See every change.

  • October 2026: BC Hydro heat pump bonus (coming). The extra bonus of up to $1,000 on BC Hydro heat pump rebates only covers installs completed by October 31, 2026. Source
  • July 2026: CleanBC Energy Savings Program (income-qualified) (cut). New rules for invoices from July 6, 2026: heat pump rebates for switching from gas, oil or propane are now $13,000, $7,000 or $3,500 by income level, plus a $3,000 northern top-up for levels 1 and 2. Source
  • July 2026: BC Hydro net metering (changed). Net metering closed to new customers. New solar owners get the self-generation rate, which pays 10 cents per kWh for power you export. Source
  • December 2025: Canada Greener Homes Grant (ended). The last day to submit documents was December 31, 2025. The Greener Homes Loan stopped taking applications October 1, 2025. Source

Common questions

What you should know about Power Smart 2.0.

Read the questions and answers

What is the BC Hydro Power Smart 2.0 rebate?

Power Smart 2.0 is BC Hydro's $1.1 billion program offering up to $10,000 for solar + battery systems, plus $4,000 for heat pumps, with additional incentives for insulation, windows, and energy-efficient upgrades. It's available to BC Hydro residential customers.

How much does a solar + battery system cost in BC?

A typical residential solar + battery system costs $25,000 to $40,000 depending on size and location. With the BC Hydro rebate (up to $10,000), Peak Saver rewards (~$3,000 over 10 years), and lower power bills ($5,000 to $9,000 over a decade), your net cost is dramatically reduced. Payback periods range from 6 to 12 years depending on the city and home size.

Which BC cities are covered by HomePowerRebate?

We currently serve 18 BC cities: Abbotsford, Burnaby, Chilliwack, Coquitlam, Fort St. John, Kamloops, Kelowna, Langley, Maple Ridge, Nanaimo, Penticton, Prince George, Richmond, Squamish, Surrey, Vancouver, Vernon, and Victoria. Expanding to more BC cities through 2026.

Is FortisBC eligible for the Power Smart 2.0 rebate?

No. The Power Smart 2.0 rebate is only for BC Hydro customers. FortisBC has its own rebate programs. Check with your utility directly.

Can I use a Tesla Powerwall with the BC Hydro rebate?

No. Tesla products are not on BC Hydro's approved battery list. Installing a Powerwall means zero rebate — a $5,000+ mistake. We recommend approved alternatives like Eguana Evolve, Enphase IQ, LG Home, SolarEdge Home, Panasonic EverVolt, and Generac PWRcell.

What about heat pump rebates?

BC Hydro offers up to $4,000 for whole-home heat pumps (80%+ of conditioned space) and up to $1,500 for partial heat pumps (50–79% coverage). Income-qualified households can reach up to $13,000 through CleanBC. Compare top-rated local heat pump installers (4+ stars, 10+ reviews minimum) as part of the Power Smart 2.0 program.

Is this really free for homeowners?

Yes. You don't pay HomePowerRebate anything. Installers don't pay us either, and no one can pay to rank higher.

Can I get multiple quotes?

By design, you see the top-rated installers in your city, not just one. Browse ratings, reviews, and specialties, then pick who you trust. We don't auction your info out to the highest bidder.

What if I'm not in one of these 18 cities?

We're expanding through 2026. Join the waitlist on the homepage and we'll email you when we come to your area.

How long does installation take?

Typically 4 to 8 weeks from signing the contract, including BC Hydro pre-approval, equipment delivery, installation (1–3 days), and final inspection. Rebate cheques arrive 30–45 business days after the system is connected.
