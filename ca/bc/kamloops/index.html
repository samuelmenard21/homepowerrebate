What Kamloops homeowners can claim

In 2026, Kamloops homeowners can get up to $4,000 from BC Hydro for a whole-home heat pump when it replaces electric heat (plus up to $1,000 more for installs finished by October 31, 2026). Income-qualified households switching from gas, oil or propane can get up to $13,000 through CleanBC. BC Hydro also pays up to $5,000 for solar panels, up to $5,500 for insulation, up to $1,000 for a heat-pump water heater and up to $550 for an EV charger.

Kamloops runs on BC Hydro electricity and FortisBC gas, and it's one of only a handful of BC towns with free RetrofitAssist guidance. With ~4.2 peak sun hours it's also better for solar than the coast. You stack CleanBC, both utilities, and federal money for lower-income households.

Replacing Your Furnace in Kamloops?

If your gas furnace is aging, check CleanBC's income-qualified program first: up to $13,000 (Level 1), $7,000 (Level 2) or $3,500 (Level 3) to switch to a heat pump. BC Hydro's $4,000 rebate is only for replacing electric heat. Above the income limits there's no provincial rebate for a gas-to-heat-pump switch right now, so compare quotes and local financing. You also gain air conditioning.

Honest math for Kamloops

If you heat with electricity, expect up to $4,000 for a whole-home heat pump (plus up to $1,000 if it's finished by October 31, 2026), and add insulation (up to $5,500), windows (up to $2,000) and solar ($5,000) where they fit. If you heat with gas, the provincial heat pump money is income-qualified: up to $13,000 at Level 1.

The old federal "Greener Homes" $40,000 grant and loan are both closed as of late 2025. Any site still advertising them is out of date. The federal money that remains flows to lower-income households through the CleanBC programs below.

Provincial & utility programs — what each one is

These are the backbone. They're the same whether you're in Kamloops or anywhere else in BC — what changes is which utility runs them for you. Here's each program and how it works.

Through CleanBC & BetterHomes BC

Provincial

Heat pump rebates

$1,500–$13,000

BC Hydro pays up to $4,000 ($1,500 partial) when a heat pump replaces electric heat, plus up to $1,000 more for installs finished Aug 1 to Oct 31, 2026. CleanBC's income-qualified program pays up to $13,000 / $7,000 / $3,500 (income levels 1 / 2 / 3) to switch from gas, oil or propane.

How it works: A registered contractor (HPCN member) installs it and usually handles the paperwork. For CleanBC's income-qualified program you register before work starts; BC Hydro needs no pre-approval. Apply within six months of the invoice. BC Hydro rules · CleanBC
Provincial

Insulation, windows & doors rebates

up to $5,500 + $2,000

Up to $5,500 for insulation (attic, walls, crawlspace) and up to $2,000 for ENERGY STAR windows and exterior doors. This is the "building envelope" — sealing the house before you size heating. See our full insulation rebates breakdown →

How it works: Same BetterHomes BC application as the heat pump. Do these first or at the same time — a tighter house lets you install a smaller, cheaper heat pump and cuts bills year-round.
Provincial

Heat pump water heater rebate

up to $3,500

A heat pump water heater uses ~⅓ the energy of a standard electric tank.

How it works: BC Hydro pays up to $1,000 when it replaces an electric tank. Income-qualified CleanBC households switching from gas, oil or propane can get up to $3,500. Pairs naturally with a space-heating heat pump on the same visit.

Through BC Hydro (your electricity utility)

BC Hydro

Home solar (self-generation) rebate

$5,000

A rebate toward rooftop solar. Excess power you generate is credited back on your bill.

How it works: As of June 1, 2026 the install must be done by a Home Performance Contractor Network member to qualify. New self-generation customers export surplus power at 10¢/kWh from July 1, 2026 — so sizing the system to your own use matters more than it used to.
BC Hydro

Home EV charger rebate

up to $550

50% of cost up to $350 for a Level 2 home charger, plus $200 for an EV power-management device that lets you charge without an expensive panel upgrade.

How it works: Apply through your utility after install. On BC Hydro, enrolling a smart charger in Peak Saver adds a $250 credit plus $50 each season you stay in. EV rebate details →

BC Hydro

Smart thermostats & in-store rebates

Free

Homes with electric-baseboard heating can get a free smart thermostat (a ~$350 value) starting fall 2026. Comparing models? See Mysa vs. Nest vs. Ecobee →

How it works: Enrolling a smart thermostat in Peak Saver adds a $100 bonus plus $50 seasonal rewards for letting your utility trim usage at peak times — you keep control and can opt out of any event.
Peak Saver

Peak Saver device enrollment

$100–$500 + seasonal

Once an eligible smart device is installed, enrolling it in Peak Saver pays a one-time bonus plus a reward every winter season — and for a home battery it's what unlocks the higher self-generation rebate.

What enrolls & pays: home battery (Eguana, SolarEdge) $500 + $250/season · EV charger $250 + $50/season · smart thermostat (Mysa, Sinopé) $100 + $50/season · water heater $100. You keep control and can opt out of any event. Enroll your devices →

Free

Free energy-saving kit

$0

A box of LED bulbs, a high-efficiency showerhead and other quick wins, mailed to your door to install yourself. Open to income-qualifying households.

How it works: Apply online through the income-based programs; the kit ships free. No contractor needed. Income-qualified programs →

Free

Free install + energy coaching (ECAP)

$0

The Energy Conservation Assistance Program sends an energy coach to your home and installs upgrades at no cost — which for qualifying homes can include a free heat pump, insulation, weatherization and ventilation.

How it works: Income-based. Thresholds were recently raised — a family of four earning up to ~$94,900 can now qualify. This is the single biggest opportunity for lower-income Kamloops homeowners; the federal Affordability money flows through here.

Through FortisBC (your natural-gas utility)

FortisBC

FortisBC heat pump & water heater rebates

up to $12,000

Since most homes here also have a FortisBC gas account, you can tap Fortis rebates too: up to $4,000 for a heat pump ($1,500 partial), or up to $12,000 income-qualified, plus $5,000 for a dual-fuel (heat pump + high-efficiency gas furnace) system.

How it works: Income-qualified customers can also get up to $3,000 on a gas ENERGY STAR water heater and up to $2,000 on insulation. Apply within six months of your install invoice. Your contractor coordinates the two utilities so you don't double-claim the same measure. FortisBC rebates →
Federal

Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program

grant (income-based)

The replacement for the closed Greener Homes grant/loan. An $800M federal program for low-to-median income households, delivered in BC through the provincial CleanBC channels — covering insulation, heat pumps, windows and solar.

How it works: You don't apply separately — in BC it's folded into the income-qualified BetterHomes/ECAP streams above. If you qualify by income, it's already part of what your evaluation checks.
How the income tiers work

Why heat pump rebates vary so much

The heat pump rebate isn't one number — it scales with household income. CleanBC's income limits depend on household size. For a family of four (pre-tax, July 6, 2026 rules): Level 1 up to $94,900, Level 2 up to $118,600, Level 3 up to $189,800. The assessment tool estimates your tier in a minute.

FAQ for Kamloops residents

Will solar actually work in Kamloops?

Absolutely — Kamloops is one of the better solar cities in BC at about 4.2 peak sun hours. An 8–10 kW system offsets a large share of a typical bill, and the sunnier climate means a noticeably faster payback than the coast.

What's the real cost of a solar system here?

An 8 kW system usually runs $25,000–$30,000 before rebates. After the $5,000 BC Hydro rebate your net is roughly $20,000–$25,000, paying back in about 8–12 years — quicker here thanks to the sunshine.

Why do heat pump rebates matter so much in Kamloops specifically?

Kamloops' hot summers and cold winters make a heat pump ideal — one machine covers both. BC Hydro pays up to $4,000 when you replace electric heat, and CleanBC up to $13,000 if your income qualifies, and RetrofitAssist gives you free expert help lining up every rebate.

What if my income is lower — is there more help?

Considerably more. The Energy Conservation Assistance Program can install a heat pump, insulation and weatherization for free, and the federal Affordability money flows through it. A family of four earning up to ~$94,900 can qualify. Start with the assessment or ask us and we'll point you to the income-qualified stream.

Can I do this if I rent or live in a condo?

Renters can't claim the structural rebates (your landlord would), but the free energy-saving kit and smart-thermostat offers are open to you. In a condo, in-unit upgrades like a heat pump water heater or windows may qualify with your building's approval; whole-building work goes through the condo board and a separate multi-unit program.

When should I apply?

Get quotes now and apply as soon as you've chosen an installer — the pre-upgrade evaluation has to happen before work starts, so late applications forfeit the rebate.
