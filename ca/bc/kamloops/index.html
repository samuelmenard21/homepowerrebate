What you can claim in Kamloops in 2026

Amounts come from the program pages we re-read on October 2, 2026. Each card is a maximum, and most depend on how your home is heated.

Open

Whole-home heat pump

Up to $4,000

Must replace electric heat such as baseboards or an electric furnace, meet 100% of the heating load at -5C and be installed by a Home Performance Contractor Network member.

Open

Partial heat pump

$1,500

For a heat pump that covers at least half of the heating load while replacing electric heat.

Open

Limited-time bonus

Up to $1,000

Extra money on heat pump installs completed between August 1 and October 31, 2026.

Open

Income-qualified switch from gas, oil or propane

$3,500 to $13,000

CleanBC pays by income level (Level 3 / Level 2 / Level 1) for a central or three-head heat pump, for invoices from July 6, 2026.

Open

Insulation

Up to $5,500

Attic, wall and basement or crawlspace work, paid per R-value added per square foot. Homes heated mainly by electricity only.

Open

Windows and doors

Up to $2,000

$100 per opening, with a U-factor of 1.22 or lower.

Open

Heat pump water heater

Up to $1,000

Only when it replaces an electric water heater.

Open

Solar panels

Up to $5,000

$1,000 per kW, capped at half the cost, from an HPCN member installer.

Open

Battery storage

$1,500 or $5,000

Up to $1,500 on its own with solar, or up to $5,000 with Peak Saver enrolment.

Open

EV charger

Up to $350

50% of a new wall-mounted Level 2 charger, plus $200 for a power-management device.

The older-house pool

The 2021 Census counted 9,895 owner-occupied detached houses built before 1981 in Kamloops, with 70% of households owning overall and 50% living in detached houses. About 5.1% reported needing major repairs. These houses are the target for BC Hydro's insulation rebate of up to $5,500 and its window rebate of up to $2,000, both for homes heated mainly by electricity.

A colder interior winter

The January average low in our climate file is -6.1°C and the coldest day in 2015 to 2024 was -34.3°C. BC Hydro's heat pump rule asks the system to cover 100% of the heating load at -5°C, which is a much easier test on the coast than here, so ask an installer what the model delivers at -20°C and whether a backup is needed. Solar gets about 3.64 peak sun hours a day, a good number in this group.

Electric versus gas homes

Kamloops electricity is BC Hydro. A house on baseboards can claim up to $4,000 for a whole-home heat pump. A house heated by natural gas from FortisBC cannot claim the BC Hydro heat pump rebate, and an income-qualified household can instead use CleanBC at $3,500, $7,000 or $13,000. Above those income limits, insulation is the sound first step because it pays off whatever you burn.

Sequence matters

Insulate first, then size the heat pump. A tighter house needs less equipment, and the heat pump load calculation should use the post-insulation numbers if the work will be done within a year.

Where to start in Kamloops

  1. Check your heating fuel.
  2. Measure the attic and ask for an insulation quote that lists R-value added.
  3. Ask a heat pump installer what the model does at -20C.
  4. Check CleanBC if income is lower.
  5. Apply within six months.
