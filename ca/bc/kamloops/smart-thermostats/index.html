Smart Thermostat Rebates in Kamloops

Kamloops runs hot, dry summers and genuinely cold winters — the kind of swing where a scheduled thermostat earns its keep — and BC Hydro's new free thermostat program is built for the city's many older baseboard-heated homes.

How it works

Kamloops sits in a semi-arid valley with a wider temperature swing than most of the province, and neighbourhoods like North Kamloops still carry a lot of older housing stock on electric baseboards. BC Hydro's new program ships up to five free Mysa or Sinopé smart thermostats to eligible baseboard homes starting this fall, about $350 of hardware at no cost, pre-enrolled in Peak Saver for an annual reward.

Who qualifies

  • Electric baseboards heat the Kamloops home
  • You hold the BC Hydro account at the service address
  • The circuit doesn't already have a compatible smart thermostat

What it doesn't cover

Kamloops homes on central forced-air, especially newer builds up on Aberdeen and Batchelor Heights that often came with heat pumps installed, fall outside this baseboard-specific offer. A retail smart thermostat still works there, it's just not the free-shipped version.

How to claim it

  1. Confirm your Kamloops home's heat source, then check eligibility for everything else with the free rebate assessment
  2. Sign up through BC Hydro's program once it opens
  3. Install the thermostat yourself, or have your HVAC contractor set it up during another visit
  4. Set your schedule — lower temperatures overnight and while you're out are where the real savings come from

Common questions

Why does a smart thermostat matter more in Kamloops than on the coast?

Kamloops sees a much wider temperature swing than coastal BC, hot, dry summers and genuinely cold winters, so there's more heating load to trim with good scheduling, especially in older North Kamloops homes still running baseboards.

Does the free BC Hydro thermostat work on a Kamloops rental?

Yes, as long as the tenant holds the BC Hydro account and the unit heats with electric baseboards. There's no wiring change involved, so landlord sign-off generally isn't required for the thermostat swap itself.
