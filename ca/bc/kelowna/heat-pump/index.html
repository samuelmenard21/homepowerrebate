A Kelowna example

A Kelowna house on FortisBC electricity, heated by baseboards, gets a NEEP cold-climate multi-split. FortisBC pays $4,000. Adding attic insulation can add the $300 two-upgrade bonus.

Winter in Kelowna

Kelowna's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was -3.2°C, and the coldest reading was -24.0°C. FortisBC asks for a heat pump sized to keep 22°C at the local design temperature, not a fixed -5°C. For Kelowna, that is a colder target than BC Hydro's, so expect a slightly larger unit.

Homes in Kelowna

The 2021 Census counted 62,210 households in Kelowna. About 65% own and 43% live in a detached house (26,450 homes). Of all households, 8,205 own a detached house built before 1981.

BC Hydro or FortisBC: check your bill

Look at who sends your power bill. If it is FortisBC, use FortisBC's heat pump rebate: $4,000 whole home, $1,500 partial, and a $300 bonus for adding a second upgrade. BC Hydro's limited-time bonus does not apply. If your bill comes from BC Hydro, use BC Hydro's rules instead. FortisBC also pays income-qualified electric customers up to $12,000 for a central ducted whole-home system.

Only for homes replacing electric heat

FortisBC's electric heat pump rebate is for homes that are heated by electricity today. If you heat with natural gas, it does not cover the switch. The heat pump must be NEEP cold-climate certified, and a single mini-split only qualifies in homes of 1,200 square feet or less. The contractor must do a CSA F280-12 heat load calculation.

Whole home or partial

For the $4,000 whole-home rebate, the system must keep 22°C at your local design temperature. If it covers part of the home, the partial rebate is $1,500. Add another eligible home renovation upgrade and you may get a $300 two-upgrade bonus.

How to claim it

  1. Confirm your electricity account is with FortisBC or a listed municipal utility.
  2. Choose a NEEP cold-climate certified heat pump and a qualified contractor.
  3. Get a CSA F280-12 heat load calculation in the quote.
  4. Install and pay the invoice.
  5. Apply online through FortisBC.

Common questions

Is the BC Hydro bonus open to Kelowna?

Only to BC Hydro customers. FortisBC electric customers use FortisBC's rebate, which has a $300 two-upgrade bonus instead.

I heat with FortisBC gas in Kelowna. What can I get?

FortisBC's electric heat pump rebate is for replacing electric heat. If your income qualifies, the CleanBC Energy Savings Program pays for gas-to-heat-pump switches.

Does FortisBC need a special contractor?

The contractor must do a CSA F280-12 heat load calculation and install a NEEP cold-climate certified unit. Check FortisBC's page for the current contractor rules.

Can I get a rebate if I heat with gas?

Not from the utility rebate. It is only for replacing electric heat. Income-qualified households can use the CleanBC Energy Savings Program, which pays up to $13,000 at Level 1 for central or 3+ head systems.

How long do I have to apply?

Apply through FortisBC after the install is paid. Check FortisBC's page for the current deadline.
