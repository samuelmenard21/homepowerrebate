How much rebate you get

Windows and doors qualify for up to $2,000 back through the CleanBC Better Homes program in Langley — $100 for each window or door you replace, capped at 20 openings. It's a smaller number than Langley's heat pump rebate, but for a home that just needs glass (not a full HVAC swap) it's the most direct dollar-for-dollar program available.

Whether your account is with BC Hydro or FortisBC, the application runs through the same CleanBC portal — the utility only determines which one processes your paperwork.

What it actually costs

Full-home replacement in Langley runs the same $8,000–$15,000 range as the rest of the Fraser Valley; expect $6,000–$13,000 after the rebate.

Who qualifies

  • You own your home in Langley
  • Your current windows are single-pane or older double-pane
  • Two tiers apply: a lower rebate for windows rated U-factor 1.40 or better, and the full $100/window rate for U-factor 1.22 or better. Ask installers quoting Langley jobs to price both tiers — the price difference for the better glass is often smaller than the rebate gap.
  • An HPCN-registered installer files the rebate claim on your behalf

Worth knowing locally

Langley's township runs its own top-up program for home heat pump installs (up to $2,000 on top of CleanBC), which tells you the City is actively subsidizing retrofits here — it's worth calling the Township directly to ask whether any current program extends to window replacement, since municipal offers change year to year and this page tracks the provincial rebate, not municipal ones.

How to claim it

  1. Get quotes from installers registered with the CleanBC Better Homes program in Langley
  2. Confirm the quoted windows meet at least the Tier 1 U-factor (1.40 W/m²·K)
  3. Ask your installer to price Tier 2 glass (U-factor 1.22 or lower) — the rebate difference often outweighs the cost difference
  4. Work is installed and inspected
  5. Installer files the rebate claim through CleanBC
  6. Rebate arrives in 4–8 weeks
