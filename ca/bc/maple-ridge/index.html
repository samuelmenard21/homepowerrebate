Maple Ridge Home Energy Rebates

Maple Ridge has 33,110 households, 79% of them owners and 53% in detached houses. Few BC cities combine that much ownership with that many houses, which is exactly who the home rebates are written for.

What you can claim in Maple Ridge in 2026

Amounts come from the program pages we re-read on October 2, 2026. Each card is a maximum, and most depend on how your home is heated.

Open

Whole-home heat pump

Up to $4,000

Must replace electric heat such as baseboards or an electric furnace, meet 100% of the heating load at -5C and be installed by a Home Performance Contractor Network member.

Open

Partial heat pump

$1,500

For a heat pump that covers at least half of the heating load while replacing electric heat.

Open

Limited-time bonus

Up to $1,000

Extra money on heat pump installs completed between August 1 and October 31, 2026.

Open

Community top-ups (select postal codes)

+$4,000 heat pump

Select Maple Ridge postal codes only. Heat pump top-up, plus $1,750 more on a Level 2 EV charger and double appliance rebates.

Open

Income-qualified switch from gas, oil or propane

$3,500 to $13,000

CleanBC pays by income level (Level 3 / Level 2 / Level 1) for a central or three-head heat pump, for invoices from July 6, 2026.

Insulation

Up to $5,500

Attic, wall and basement or crawlspace work, paid per R-value added per square foot. Homes heated mainly by electricity only.

Open

Windows and doors

Up to $2,000

$100 per opening, with a U-factor of 1.22 or lower.

Open

Heat pump water heater

Up to $1,000

Only when it replaces an electric water heater.

Open

Solar panels

Up to $5,000

$1,000 per kW, capped at half the cost, from an HPCN member installer.

Open

$1,500 or $5,000

Up to $1,500 on its own with solar, or up to $5,000 with Peak Saver enrolment.

A house-and-owner city

In the 2021 Census, 29% of Maple Ridge households lived in homes built before 1981, and 5,160 of those homes are owner-occupied detached houses. Major repairs were reported by 4.8%. Maple Ridge has the second-highest owner share in the group after the Township of Langley. For an electric-heated house the BC Hydro rebates up to $4,000 (heat pump), $5,500 (insulation), $2,000 (windows) and $1,000 (water heater) can all apply to the same project.

Maple Ridge top-ups by postal code

Maple Ridge is on BC Hydro's list of community-based offers, but only select postal codes qualify. In those areas, a heat pump replacing electric heat earns $4,000 more for a whole home or $1,500 more for a partial system. Also, a Level 2 EV charger at a single-family home gets a $1,750 top-up (up to the full cost) on top of the $350 rebate, if you apply between August 4 and December 31, 2026 and enroll the charger in Peak Saver for its $250 bonus. And select ENERGY STAR fridges, washers and dryers get double the regular rebate (see our BC appliance rebates page). None of this helps a house on propane or oil, since the heat pump top-up follows the same electric-heat rule as the standard rebate. Check your address on BC Hydro's postal code checker.

Rural lots and fuel

Where homes sit away from gas lines, propane or oil is common, and BC Hydro will not rebate a heat pump that replaces those fuels. CleanBC pays $3,500, $7,000 or $13,000 for income-qualified households who switch to a central or three-head heat pump. We cannot say how many Maple Ridge homes use each fuel, so check your own bills.

Colder than the rest of the Lower Mainland

The 2015 to 2024 record low was -19.6°C, which is lower than Burnaby, Richmond or Surrey, although the January average low is a mild 0.1°C. Size the heat pump to BC Hydro's -5°C rule and ask the installer what the unit does at -15°C.

Solar and battery

Solar here gets about 3.19 peak sun hours a day, the lowest figure in our data. Rebates up to $5,000 for solar and up to $5,000 for a battery with Peak Saver are available but depend on funding.

Where to start in Maple Ridge

  1. Check your heating fuel.
  2. Measure insulation depth in the attic and ask for R-value quotes.
  3. Get two HPCN quotes for the heat pump.
  4. Ask about CleanBC if income is lower.
  5. Apply within six months.

Common questions

Is Maple Ridge mostly owners?

Yes, 79% of households own according to the 2021 Census.

What is the coldest it gets?

The record low in 2015 to 2024 was -19.6 degrees Celsius.

Do I get both insulation and window rebates?

Yes, they are separate programs with separate caps, and a bonus of up to $2,000 applies to multiple upgrades.

Does gas disqualify me?

From BC Hydro, yes. CleanBC may still apply on income.
