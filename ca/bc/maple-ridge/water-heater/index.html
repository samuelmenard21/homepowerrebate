Heat Pump Water Heater Rebates in Maple Ridge

Maple Ridge has one of the highest ownership rates in Metro Vancouver and many houses with a basement suite. A heat pump water heater replacing an electric tank can earn up to $1,000.

What heat pump water heater rebates pay in Maple Ridge

We re-read BC Hydro's heat pump water heater page on October 2, 2026. BC Hydro and FortisBC pay only when you replace an electric water heater. Replacing a gas tank earns nothing from them, and the CleanBC amounts below are for income-qualified households only.

Up to $1,000

For replacing an electric primary water heater with a heat pump model on NEEA's Tier 2 or higher list.

More than one upgrade

Bonus up to $2,000

BC Hydro adds a bonus when you finish more than one eligible upgrade.

A house with a suite

The main house replaces an electric tank and earns up to $1,000. A suite with its own meter and its own electric tank might earn a second rebate. Check with BC Hydro first. If the house also gets 1,000 square feet of wall insulation at R-12, that line pays 0.09 x 12 x 1,000 = $1,080.

Who in Maple Ridge can use this

The 2021 Census counted 33,110 households in Maple Ridge. About 79% own, 53% live in detached houses and only 17% in apartments. Of the households, 5,160 own a detached house built before 1981. Maple Ridge's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was 0.1°C.

Gas tank or electric tank?

The rebate is only for replacing an electric water heater. If your tank runs on natural gas, BC Hydro and FortisBC do not pay for a switch to a heat pump model. Lower-income households can use the CleanBC Energy Savings Program, which pays up to $3,500 for Level 1 households converting from a fossil fuel. Only one primary water heating rebate is allowed per home across programs.

Questions to ask the installer

A heat pump water heater pulls warmth from the air around it, so where it sits matters. Ask the installer how much open space the model needs, how loud it runs, where the condensate drains, and what happens in a cold utility room. These are not program rules, but they decide whether you like the tank after the first winter. Get the answers in the quote.

Documents and deadline

You have six months from the invoice date to apply. The only supporting document is the invoice, and BC Hydro publishes a sample showing what it must list. The installer must be a licensed contractor with a GST number and a valid BC business licence for the trade. Self-installation is not eligible, and the new tank must not have been used in another home.

Houses with suites

BC Hydro accepts a detached house with a secondary suite when each is individually metered. If the suite has its own electric tank, ask BC Hydro whether it counts as its own home for the rebate, and how it treats a shared tank, before you buy.

How to claim in Maple Ridge

  1. Check that the tank you are replacing is electric and is your primary water heater.
  2. Pick a model from NEEA's Tier 2 or higher list and get it on the quote.
  3. Have a licensed contractor install it and keep the invoice.
  4. Apply online within six months of the invoice date.
  5. If your income is lower, ask about the CleanBC program before paying.

Common questions

Can both a house and its suite claim?

Possibly, if each is individually metered and has its own electric tank. BC Hydro's page does not say how it treats a suite, so confirm with BC Hydro before you buy.

What if my tank is gas?

BC Hydro and FortisBC do not pay for a gas switch.

Is there a bonus?

BC Hydro lists a bonus of up to $2,000 for more than one eligible upgrade.

Do I need a special contractor?

A licensed contractor with a GST number and a BC business licence.

How cold does Maple Ridge get?

The average January low was 0.1°C, and the coldest day in 2015 to 2024 was -19.6°C.
