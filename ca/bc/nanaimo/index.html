Nanaimo Home Energy Rebates

Nanaimo's own Home Energy Retrofit Financing offers a zero-interest loan of up to $15,000 repaid on your property tax over ten years. The application window runs from September 14 to October 31, 2026.

What you can claim in Nanaimo in 2026

Amounts come from the program pages we re-read on October 2, 2026. Each card is a maximum, and most depend on how your home is heated.

Open

Whole-home heat pump

Up to $4,000

Must replace electric heat such as baseboards or an electric furnace, meet 100% of the heating load at -5C and be installed by a Home Performance Contractor Network member.

Open

Partial heat pump

$1,500

For a heat pump that covers at least half of the heating load while replacing electric heat.

Open

Limited-time bonus

Up to $1,000

Extra money on heat pump installs completed between August 1 and October 31, 2026.

Open

Income-qualified switch from gas, oil or propane

$3,500 to $13,000

CleanBC pays by income level (Level 3 / Level 2 / Level 1) for a central or three-head heat pump, for invoices from July 6, 2026.

Open

Insulation

Up to $5,500

Attic, wall and basement or crawlspace work, paid per R-value added per square foot. Homes heated mainly by electricity only.

Open

Windows and doors

Up to $2,000

$100 per opening, with a U-factor of 1.22 or lower.

Open

Heat pump water heater

Up to $1,000

Only when it replaces an electric water heater.

Open

Solar panels

Up to $5,000

$1,000 per kW, capped at half the cost, from an HPCN member installer.

Open

Battery storage

$1,500 or $5,000

Up to $1,500 on its own with solar, or up to $5,000 with Peak Saver enrolment.

Open

EV charger

Up to $350

50% of a new wall-mounted Level 2 charger, plus $200 for a power-management device.

The City loan

Nanaimo homeowners with taxes in good standing can register with the free Home Energy Navigator and apply for the loan. It covers heat pumps, insulation, windows and doors and solar. It is a loan, not a rebate, so it sits alongside BC Hydro's rebates rather than replacing them.

Provincial rebates

Nanaimo electricity is BC Hydro. An electric-heated house can claim up to $4,000 for a heat pump, up to $5,500 for insulation, up to $2,000 for windows and up to $1,000 for a water heater. Gas, oil and propane homes use CleanBC for income-qualified help.

Nanaimo's housing

The 2021 Census counted 43,165 households. 39% live in homes built before 1981, 51% in detached houses and 67% own. 7,880 are owner-occupied detached houses built before 1981.

Using the loan and the rebates together

The City loan pays for work up front and you repay it through property tax over ten years. BC Hydro's rebates then come back to you after the invoice. Ask the City whether early repayment is allowed if you want to use a rebate to pay the loan down. The loan covers heat pumps, insulation, windows and doors, and solar, so a bundled project is possible.

The window to apply closes October 31, 2026, so if you want this year's round, register with the Home Energy Navigator now.

Gas customers

Some Nanaimo homes burn natural gas, oil or propane. BC Hydro's rebates do not cover those homes, but the City's loan does, because it is open to Nanaimo homeowners generally. For income-qualified households CleanBC pays $3,500, $7,000 or $13,000 by level for a central or three-head heat pump.

Mild climate

The January average low is 2.2°C and the record low in 2015 to 2024 was -10.2°C, the mildest record low of the 18 cities we cover. Solar gets about 3.44 peak sun hours a day.

Where to start in Nanaimo

  1. Register with the Home Energy Navigator.
  2. Apply for the City loan before October 31, 2026.
  3. Take HPCN quotes.
  4. Check CleanBC if income is lower.
  5. Apply for BC Hydro rebates within six months.

Common questions

How big is the Nanaimo loan?

Up to $15,000 at zero interest, repaid on property tax over ten years.

When can I apply?

September 14 to October 31, 2026.

Does it replace the rebates?

No. It is a loan, so it can cover the cost while rebates are paid.

Is Nanaimo mild?

Yes, the record low in 2015 to 2024 was -10.2 degrees Celsius.
