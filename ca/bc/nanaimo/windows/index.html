How much rebate you get

BC Hydro and CleanBC programs offer up to $2,000 for windows and doors in Nanaimo. The exact amount depends on your income and the scope of your project.

Who qualifies

  • You own your home in Nanaimo
  • Your windows are single or old double-pane (replacement candidates)
  • New windows must be ENERGY STAR-certified with U-value ≤ 1.72
  • Your HPCN-certified installer files the rebate claim
  • Income-qualified households see higher rebates

How to claim it

  1. Get a home energy audit (required before work starts)
  2. Get quotes from installers familiar with CleanBC in Nanaimo
  3. Confirm windows/doors meet ENERGY STAR requirements
  4. Work is installed
  5. Installer files rebate claim with BC Hydro
  6. Rebate arrives in 4–8 weeks
