The northern top-up

Homes at or north of 100 Mile House on BC Hydro service get an extra $3,000 for a central, multi-split or two mini-split heat pump, or $1,500 for a single head, on CleanBC Level 1 and 2 only. Level 1 at $13,000 plus $3,000 makes $16,000 for a fossil-fuel home. Prince George and Fort St. John qualify.

Housing in numbers

The 2021 Census counted 31,795 households. 62% live in homes built before 1981, the second-highest share in the group, and 58% live in detached houses, the highest. 68% own. 10,470 are owner-occupied detached houses from before 1981. Major repairs were reported by 6.5%.

Extreme cold

The January average low is -9.7°C and the record in 2015 to 2024 was -42.4°C. A heat pump in this climate needs to be a cold-climate model and the installer should state its output at -25°C or below. Insulation first, then equipment.

Electric homes

For electric-heated houses BC Hydro pays up to $4,000 for a heat pump, up to $5,500 for insulation, up to $2,000 for windows and up to $1,000 for a water heater. Solar gets about 3.21 peak sun hours a day.
