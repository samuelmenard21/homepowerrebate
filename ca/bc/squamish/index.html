Squamish Home Energy Rebates

Squamish is the smallest BC hub in our group by households (9,185), yet it recorded -28.1°C between 2015 and 2024, colder than any Lower Mainland city.

What you can claim in Squamish in 2026

Amounts come from the program pages we re-read on October 2, 2026. Each card is a maximum, and most depend on how your home is heated.

Open

Whole-home heat pump

Up to $4,000

Must replace electric heat such as baseboards or an electric furnace, meet 100% of the heating load at -5C and be installed by a Home Performance Contractor Network member.

Open

Partial heat pump

$1,500

For a heat pump that covers at least half of the heating load while replacing electric heat.

Open

Limited-time bonus

Up to $1,000

Extra money on heat pump installs completed between August 1 and October 31, 2026.

Open

Income-qualified switch from gas, oil or propane

$3,500 to $13,000

CleanBC pays by income level (Level 3 / Level 2 / Level 1) for a central or three-head heat pump, for invoices from July 6, 2026.

Open

Insulation

Up to $5,500

Attic, wall and basement or crawlspace work, paid per R-value added per square foot. Homes heated mainly by electricity only.

Open

Windows and doors

Up to $2,000

$100 per opening, with a U-factor of 1.22 or lower.

Open

Heat pump water heater

Up to $1,000

Only when it replaces an electric water heater.

Open

Solar panels

Up to $5,000

$1,000 per kW, capped at half the cost, from an HPCN member installer.

Open

Battery storage

$1,500 or $5,000

Up to $1,500 on its own with solar, or up to $5,000 with Peak Saver enrolment.

Open

EV charger

Up to $350

50% of a new wall-mounted Level 2 charger, plus $200 for a power-management device.

A small town in a cold pocket

The January average low is 0.2°C, much like Abbotsford, but the coldest day was -28.1°C. The gap between average and extreme is why the heat pump needs a real load calculation. BC Hydro asks for equipment that covers 100% of the load at -5°C, and in Squamish a cold snap goes well past that.

Squamish's housing

The 2021 Census counted 9,185 households. 34% live in homes built before 1981, 41% in detached houses and 70% own. 1,645 are owner-occupied detached houses from before 1981. Major repairs were reported by 5.4%.

Electric versus gas

Squamish electricity is BC Hydro, so electric-heated houses can claim up to $4,000 for a heat pump, up to $5,500 for insulation, up to $2,000 for windows and up to $1,000 for a water heater. A house on gas, oil or propane uses CleanBC income help at $3,500, $7,000 or $13,000.

Sun

Solar gets about 3.27 peak sun hours a day. Rebates of up to $5,000 for solar are available but depend on funding.
