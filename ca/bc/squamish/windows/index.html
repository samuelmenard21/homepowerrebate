A Squamish family home

A house with 13 windows, a front door and a back door is 15 openings. That pays $1,500. The home must be at least 12 months old, so a brand-new build cannot claim for its original windows.

Squamish's homes in numbers

The 2021 Census counted 9,185 households in Squamish. About 3,115 (34%) live in homes built before 1981, and 1,645 are owner-occupied detached houses from that period. About 41% live in detached houses, 25% in apartments, and 70% own.

A mild average, a cold tail

Squamish's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was 0.2°C, but the coldest day in that record was -28.1°C. Our other coastal cities saw -10°C to -17°C. A window that is fine on an average night can fail on the coldest ones.

Who can claim

The home must be mainly electric-heated, at least 12 months old and a primary residence. Row homes and townhouses qualify when each unit has its own meter and the account is not held by a strata corporation. Condos and apartments use a separate program.

Counting openings and certification

Each rough opening counts as one. A bay window is one. Skylights are excluded. Units need a U-factor of 1.22 W/m2-K or lower and a listing from CSA, Intertek, Labtest, QAI or NFRC.

Reading a U-factor label

The rebate needs a U-factor of 1.22 W/m2-K or lower, and lower means less heat loss. Many window labels show the older imperial scale instead. To convert, multiply the imperial number by 5.678. A label that says U-0.30 equals 1.70 W/m2-K, which is too high. A label that says U-0.21 equals about 1.19 W/m2-K and passes. Ask the contractor to quote the metric value for each model so you do not have to convert.

Timing and paperwork

You have six months from the invoice date to apply, and the application needs the invoice and a photo of the manufacturer label on each unit. BC Hydro publishes a window and door checklist and a sample invoice. Ask the contractor to fill in the invoice the way the sample does, listing each window or door separately. A vague invoice is the usual reason a claim is sent back.

If your income is lower

BC Hydro sends income-qualified customers to the CleanBC Better Homes Energy Savings Program instead, and the two cannot be combined. The 2026 program document lists no insulation or window rebates, so the free Energy Conservation Assistance Program is the route to ask about.

How to claim in Squamish

  1. Check the home is electric-heated and at least 12 months old.
  2. Get quotes that list the U-factor of each unit.
  3. Photograph each label on installation day.
  4. Keep the invoice and apply within six months.
  5. Add insulation to the same application to check the bonus.
