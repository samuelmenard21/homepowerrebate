EV Charger Rebates in Surrey

Complete guide to ev charger rebates for Surrey homeowners in 2026—eligibility, amounts, and exactly how to claim them.

What to expect

Surrey covers a large, still-growing BC Hydro service area, and occasional winter storm outages happen across it — nothing unusual for the region, but worth planning around if your electrician is scheduling a panel inspection alongside the charger install. The rebate itself: 50% of cost up to $350 through CleanBC Go Electric, plus $200 for a power-management device instead of a panel upgrade.

Eligibility

  • BC Hydro residential customer in a detached home, duplex, or row home in Surrey
  • Eligible Level 2 charger and power-management device (if used)
  • Approved electrical permit
  • HPCN-certified installer files the claim

Choosing a home EV charger

With Surrey's grid seeing occasional winter storm outages across such a large service area, a smart charger that supports BC Hydro's Peak Saver program is worth the extra look: enrollment earns a $250 bill credit plus $50 every season after, on top of the CleanBC purchase rebate, and demand-response scheduling helps the grid handle peak load during exactly the conditions that cause storm strain. Confirm the model supports Peak Saver's signal before buying, since not all smart chargers do.

On the panel side, ask your electrician to confirm capacity before committing to a full 40-60A Level 2 circuit; a load-managing charger (Emporia, or ChargePoint's power-sharing units) can often avoid a $1,500-plus upgrade. And check the connector: most 2025+ EVs use Tesla's NACS plug, while J1772 still covers older cars and most third-party chargers.

Car and Driver's "EV Charging Levels Explained" lays out the Level 1/2/3 distinction clearly: caranddriver.com. BC Hydro's home charger options page, with a short video specific to this program, is the most relevant follow-up: bchydro.com.

Where to buy a home EV charger

For a lot of Surrey homeowners, the electrician scheduling the panel inspection and install can also supply the charger, which keeps the whole project on one timeline. Buying direct from the manufacturer or an electrical distributor is the other dependable option once you know which model fits.

If you shop general online retail, confirm the seller is the real registered brand, not a reseller or counterfeit, and confirm the unit is new, not used or refurbished, since a used unit can disqualify the rebate. In-store stock at home-improvement or auto retailers across Surrey's large service area is still limited, so call ahead. And check with the dealership if a new EV purchase is part of the plan; a free Level 1 or Level 2 charger is increasingly bundled in.

How to claim it

  1. Apply for pre-approval before purchasing anything, about 15 minutes
  2. Confirmation number, approval usually within 30 days
  3. Licensed Surrey electrician installs the charger
  4. Final approval within 9 months of pre-approval (as of July 2026)
