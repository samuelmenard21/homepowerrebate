How much rebate you get

Most Surrey homes heat with natural gas, and older gas furnaces paired with original single-pane or early double-pane windows lose a lot of that heat straight through the glass. The CleanBC Better Homes rebate pays up to $100 per window or door replaced, capped at $2,000 per home, and Surrey's older detached housing stock — Newton, Whalley, and Guildford in particular have a lot of 1970s–90s builds — tends to qualify easily.

FortisBC bills the gas side for most Surrey homes; BC Hydro covers the electricity. The window rebate application goes through CleanBC regardless of which one is on your bill.

What it actually costs

A full-home job in Surrey runs $8,000–$15,000 before the rebate; with the $2,000 maximum applied, most owners land around $6,000–$13,000.

Who qualifies

  • You own your home in Surrey
  • Your current windows are single-pane or older double-pane
  • Qualifying windows need a U-factor of 1.40 W/m²·K or better for the base tier, or 1.22 or better for the full per-window rate — the tier difference on a 15-window Surrey home can be worth several hundred extra dollars, so it's worth specifying up front with your contractor.
  • An HPCN-registered installer files the rebate claim on your behalf

How to claim it

  1. Get quotes from installers registered with the CleanBC Better Homes program in Surrey
  2. Confirm the quoted windows meet at least the Tier 1 U-factor (1.40 W/m²·K)
  3. Ask your installer to price Tier 2 glass (U-factor 1.22 or lower) — the rebate difference often outweighs the cost difference
  4. Work is installed and inspected
  5. Installer files the rebate claim through CleanBC
  6. Rebate arrives in 4–8 weeks
