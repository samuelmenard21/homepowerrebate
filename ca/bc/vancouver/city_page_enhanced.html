FAQ for Vancouver Residents

Will solar work in Vancouver?

Yes. Vancouver gets ~3.5 peak sun hours per day on average. It's cloudier than the interior, but solar still makes sense. Winter production is lower, but summer production is strong. Combined with heat pump and battery, you can achieve significant energy independence.

What's the real cost of solar in Vancouver?

An 8 kW system typically costs $26,000–$30,000 before rebates. After the $5,000 BC Hydro rebate + $500-1,000 from expedited permitting and tax exemption, net cost is usually $20,000–$24,000. Pays for itself in 9–13 years through energy savings.

Do I need a heat pump in Vancouver? It's mild.

Yes—even mild winters add up in heating costs. Heat pumps work down to -15°C and are increasingly popular in Vancouver. They cut heating costs by 50-70% and work great with the city's moderate climate.

What if I live in a condo?

Vancouver has special strata retrofit programs. You can install solar even in condos (on common roof space or your balcony, with strata approval). Heat pumps and water heaters can often be done unit-by-unit. Ask your installer about condo-specific strategies.

When should I apply for rebates?

Apply immediately after getting quotes. Vancouver's permitting is fast (2-3 weeks), but BC Hydro interconnection takes 4-8 weeks. Starting early means your rebate cheque arrives sooner.
