EV Charger Rebates in Vancouver

A Vancouver house can get back half the cost of a home charger, up to $350, from BC Hydro. The catch is who lives in Vancouver. Most households rent or live in an apartment, and this rebate is not for them. Here is who qualifies, what you can add on top, and what to do if you live in a strata.

What EV charger rebates pay in Vancouver

We re-read BC Hydro's home charger and Peak Saver pages on October 3, 2026. We found no extra top-up from the City of Vancouver. BC Hydro lists local top-ups for Kamloops, Squamish and parts of the Regional District of Nanaimo only.

Up to $350

50% of the cost of buying and installing a Level 2 charger at a single-family home, row home or duplex.

A Vancouver example

A charger and install cost $1,200. BC Hydro pays 50%, which would be $600, but the cap is $350. If an electrician also fits a $400 power management device to avoid a panel upgrade, BC Hydro adds $200. Total incentives can never be more than you paid.

Most Vancouver households are not eligible

Statistics Canada's 2021 Census counted 305,335 households in Vancouver. About 55% of them rent, and about 62% live in apartments. Only about 44,710 households, roughly 15%, live in a single-detached house. BC Hydro's charger rebate is for single-family homes, row homes and duplexes. If you rent, ask your landlord whether they would add a charger. If you own in a strata, the building has to apply for the multi-unit rebates, which are separate. The honest answer for most Vancouver households is that this page is for the minority who own a house.

If you own a house

The fit is good. A house usually has a garage, a carport or a lane where a charger can go, and you control the wiring. About 18,275 Vancouver households own a single-detached house built before 1981. Those homes often have smaller electrical panels, and a 40 or 50 amp charger may not fit without help. A power management device is the cheaper fix, and BC Hydro adds $200 when you claim one.

Ask your electrician to do a load calculation first. It tells you whether your panel has room, which charger size makes sense, and whether you need the device at all.

Charging in Vancouver's mild winters

Vancouver's average January low from 2015 to 2024 was 1.7°C, and the coldest reading was -14.1°C. That is mild for Canada, so cold slows your charging far less than in Calgary or Prince George. A garage or covered spot still helps on the few very cold mornings, and it keeps the charger out of the rain.

Peak Saver and winter evenings

Peak Saver pays you for letting BC Hydro briefly slow a smart charger when the grid is busy. BC Hydro says events run from November to March, last up to four hours, and you can opt out. The enrollment credit is $250 and the seasonal reward is $50. If you opt out of more than half the events, you may lose the seasonal reward. Pick a charger that BC Hydro lists as Peak Saver eligible if you want this.

If you live in a strata or rent

You cannot claim the home charger rebate for a condo parking stall, and a landlord or strata council has to agree to any wiring. BC Hydro runs separate rebates for multi-unit buildings, and the council usually leads that process. Start by asking your strata manager whether the building has an EV charging plan. A written plan makes any later request easier.

Apply within 180 days

Send BC Hydro your proof of purchase and proof of install within 180 days of the install. Keep the itemized invoice and the electrical permit. Do not buy a Tesla Wall Connector if you want the rebate.

How to claim it

  1. Check that you live in a house, row home or duplex.
  2. Pick an eligible Level 2 charger that is not a Tesla.
  3. Have a licensed electrician install it, with a permit.
  4. Apply to BC Hydro within 180 days with the invoice and proof of install.
  5. Enroll the charger in Peak Saver if it is eligible.

Common questions

How much is the EV charger rebate in Vancouver?

BC Hydro pays 50% of the cost of a Level 2 charger and install, up to $350. A power management device adds $200, and Peak Saver adds a $250 credit if your charger qualifies.

Does the City of Vancouver add a top-up?

We found none. BC Hydro's local top-ups are for Kamloops, Squamish and parts of the Regional District of Nanaimo.

Can I claim it if I live in a condo or strata?

Not this rebate. It is for single-family homes, row homes and duplexes. BC Hydro has separate rebates for multi-unit buildings.

Is a Tesla Wall Connector eligible?

No. BC Hydro's list of ineligible products includes Tesla chargers.

Do I need an electrician?

Yes. Have a licensed electrician install the charger and ask about the permit. You will need the itemized invoice for your claim.

How long do I have to apply?

180 days from the install date.
