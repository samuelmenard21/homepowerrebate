What you can claim in Vancouver in 2026

Amounts come from the program pages we re-read on October 2, 2026. Each card is a maximum, and most depend on how your home is heated.

Open

Whole-home heat pump

Up to $4,000

Must replace electric heat such as baseboards or an electric furnace, meet 100% of the heating load at -5C and be installed by a Home Performance Contractor Network member.

Open

Partial heat pump

$1,500

For a heat pump that covers at least half of the heating load while replacing electric heat.

Open

Limited-time bonus

Up to $1,000

Extra money on heat pump installs completed between August 1 and October 31, 2026.

Open

Community top-ups (select postal codes)

+$4,000 heat pump

Select neighbourhoods west of Cambie only. Heat pump top-up, plus $1,750 more on a Level 2 EV charger and double appliance rebates.

Open

Income-qualified switch from gas, oil or propane

$3,500 to $13,000

CleanBC pays by income level (Level 3 / Level 2 / Level 1) for a central or three-head heat pump, for invoices from July 6, 2026.

Open

Insulation

Up to $5,500

Attic, wall and basement or crawlspace work, paid per R-value added per square foot. Homes heated mainly by electricity only.

Open

Windows and doors

Up to $2,000

$100 per opening, with a U-factor of 1.22 or lower.

Open

Heat pump water heater

Up to $1,000

Only when it replaces an electric water heater.

Open

Solar panels

Up to $5,000

$1,000 per kW, capped at half the cost, from an HPCN member installer.

Open

Battery storage

$1,500 or $5,000

Up to $1,500 on its own with solar, or up to $5,000 with Peak Saver enrolment.

Open

EV charger

Up to $350

50% of a new wall-mounted Level 2 charger, plus $200 for a power-management device.

The housing mix decides everything

The 2021 Census puts 43% of Vancouver households in homes built before 1981 and 45% as owners. Just 15% live in detached houses. There are 18,275 owner-occupied detached houses from before 1981, the largest pool among the 18 cities we cover. For those homes BC Hydro's rebates apply if the house is electric, and for the rest of the city the question is whether you own a strata unit, rent, or own a house on gas.

West of Cambie: extra BC Hydro offers

BC Hydro's community-based offers cover select neighbourhoods in Vancouver west of Cambie. In those postal codes, a house heated by electricity earns $4,000 more for a whole-home heat pump or $1,500 more for a partial one. Also, a Level 2 EV charger at a single-family home gets a $1,750 top-up (up to the full cost) on top of the $350 rebate, if you apply between August 4 and December 31, 2026 and enroll the charger in Peak Saver for its $250 bonus. And select ENERGY STAR fridges, washers and dryers get double the regular rebate (see our BC appliance rebates page). Condos and apartments still cannot use the renovation rebates, top-up or not. Check your address on BC Hydro's postal code checker.

The window rebate is not available in the City of Vancouver

BC Hydro and FortisBC both exclude the City of Vancouver from their window and door rebates. Insulation, heat pump and heat pump water heater rebates still apply. If you are comparing Vancouver with Burnaby or Richmond, expect that difference.

Strata and rental

Strata owners use BC Hydro's condo program, and the strata guide covers the amounts we verified. A tenant cannot claim a house rebate for a building they do not own, but low-income renters can ask about free upgrades. Of Vancouver households, 6.3% reported major repairs needed.

Climate

The January average low is 1.7°C, the record low in 2015 to 2024 was -14.1°C, and solar yields about 3.41 peak sun hours a day. A cold-climate heat pump rarely strains in this climate.

Where to start in Vancouver

  1. Work out if you own a house, own a strata unit or rent.
  2. If you own an electric-heated house, price heat pump and insulation.
  3. If you own a strata unit, start with the strata guide.
  4. Skip the window rebate in the City of Vancouver.
  5. Apply within six months.

Common questions

Why can't I claim windows in Vancouver?

BC Hydro and FortisBC exclude the City of Vancouver from the window and door rebate.

Is Vancouver mostly renters?

Yes. About 55% of households rent according to the 2021 Census.

How big is the older-house pool?

18,275 owner-occupied detached houses were built before 1981.

Do apartments qualify for home rebates?

No. BC Hydro has a separate condo and apartment program.
