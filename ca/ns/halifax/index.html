Home Energy Rebates in Halifax

🔥 Heat Pump

Up to $5,000 (all) / $15,000 (moderate income)

The big stackable Home Heating System Rebates Program closed Dec 31, 2025, but Efficiency Nova Scotia still offers up to $5,000 to any homeowner, up to $15,000 for moderate-income households, and free installation for income-qualified homes through HomeWarming.

☀️ Solar

$0.60/watt + Solar City

The provincial $0.60/watt rebate nets roughly $6,000 on a 10kW system. Halifax also runs its own Solar City program — property-assessed financing at 4.75% fixed over 10 years, no upfront cost, repaid through your property tax bill via a Local Improvement Charge. It's financing, not a rebate, but it removes the cash-flow barrier that stops a lot of homeowners from going solar.

Renting or living in a condo?

If you rent

The heat pump, insulation, and water heater rebates above go to whoever owns the equipment, so you can't claim them directly as a tenant — that's your landlord's decision. Point them to this page, especially the free HomeWarming upgrades and Efficiency Nova Scotia's heat pump rebate, since neither requires upfront cash from the landlord beyond the project cost.

If you own a condo unit

In-unit upgrades you're personally responsible for — a smart thermostat, an in-suite heat pump, a water heater — can generally still qualify for the Efficiency Nova Scotia rebates above, subject to your condo board's approval for anything touching shared systems or the exterior. Solar City's property-tax financing is tied to the property title, so confirm with Halifax's Solar City program whether an individually-titled unit can enroll on its own.

If it's a whole-building retrofit

Honestly: this page doesn't confirm a dedicated multi-unit or condo-corporation program for a building-wide heat pump or solar retrofit. A condo board considering one should contact Efficiency Nova Scotia and the Solar City program directly to find out what, if anything, applies at the corporation level, rather than assume the individual-homeowner numbers above scale up automatically.
