Program In Transition

Nova Scotia Home Energy Rebates 2026

Nova Scotia used to run some of the most generous stackable heat pump grants in the country. That program closed at the end of 2025. Here's exactly what's still real in 2026, and what to expect if you're switching off oil or electric heat.

What changed

If you've seen Nova Scotia described online as having "the most generous heat pump rebate in Canada," that's now out of date. The core Home Heating System Rebates Program closed to new applications on December 31, 2025. The federal top-up, the Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program (OHPA), closed to new applicants on July 2, 2026. Nova Scotia moved first on this compared to most other provinces.

What's left is real but more targeted. A new federal program is reportedly returning to serve more than 35,000 low- and median-income Nova Scotia households with no-cost heat pump and insulation upgrades — worth watching if you fall into that income band.

The federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program closed to new applicants on July 2, 2026, and the core provincial heat pump program closed December 31, 2025 — both dates are reflected below, not the older, more generous numbers still floating around online.

Heat pump

Efficiency Nova Scotia heat pump rebate

Up to $5,000 (up to $15,000, moderate income)

The $10,000-federal + $5,000-provincial Oil to Heat Pump Affordability Program closed to new applicants July 2, 2026 (funding fully allocated) — if you already applied, you have until January 31, 2027 to finish the work. What's still open to any homeowner: up to $5,000 through Efficiency Nova Scotia's Home Energy Assessment pathway, and up to $15,000 for moderate-income households; income-qualified homes may also get free installation through HomeWarming.

Source: /ca/ns/halifax · Efficiency Nova Scotia
Insulation & air sealing

HomeWarming free upgrade

Free

Still active as a no-cost upgrade for income-eligible households through HomeWarming — no purchase, this is a direct install, not a rebate you claim after paying.

Solar

Provincial solar rebate

$0.60/watt

Roughly $6,000 on a typical 10kW system. Halifax homeowners can also use the city's separate Solar City financing (4.75% fixed, repaid through the property tax bill) to cover the rest with no upfront cash.

Source: /ca/ns/halifax · Efficiency Nova Scotia
