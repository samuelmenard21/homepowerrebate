What this means practically

Unlike Toronto (HELP loan) or Ottawa (Better Homes Ottawa), there's currently no confirmed local financing program covering EV chargers here. Check with Alectra Utilities directly for any current EV charger incentive, as utility-specific programs can exist separately from the provincial HRSP.

How to claim it

  1. Check with Alectra Utilities directly for any current EV charger incentive
  2. Purchase and install a Level 2 charger from a certified installer
