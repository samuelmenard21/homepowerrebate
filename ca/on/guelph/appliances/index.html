What Ontario pays for new appliances in Guelph

We re-read the program's appliance page on October 4, 2026. The rebates are province-wide, so the amounts are the same in every city, and the rules about what you replace and which model you buy are what decide a claim.

Ontario grid customers

Energy Affordability Program

The appliance page states no end date, so confirm it is still open before you buy.

Tenants and the appliance page

With 34% of Guelph households renting, tenants may ask whether they can claim. The page asks for a residential customer on the Ontario grid and an existing electric appliance in need of replacing. It does not say owners only. A tenant buying their own washer, for example, has to decide whether it stays when they leave.

Alectra Utilities

Alectra Utilities supplies power and Guelph postal codes begin with N, which the Energy Affordability Program lists. The program can provide energy-efficient fridges and freezers to households under the income limits.

Adding up the six

The six rebates are $200 for a heat pump dryer, $150 for an induction cooktop or range, $75 for a washer, $50 each for a fridge and a freezer and $25 for a dishwasher. A household that replaced all six would receive $550, though few Guelph homes will need to swap everything at once. The rebate is flat, so it takes the same amount off an entry model and a premium one.

Most Efficient, not just ENERGY STAR

The rebate is for ENERGY STAR Most Efficient models, which the page describes as going beyond standard ENERGY STAR requirements. A fridge with the ordinary ENERGY STAR mark may not qualify. Each appliance has its own product list linked from the program page, so find the model number there before you pay, whether you buy in Guelph or online.

When replacing makes sense

About 44% of Guelph's 56,480 households live in homes built before 1981. Age of the house tells you little about the age of the fridge, and a working appliance rarely pays back a swap through a $25 to $200 rebate alone. The rebate suits a replacement you already need to make.

What kind of home

Unlike the thermostat rebate, the appliance page lists no home type. It asks for a residential customer on the Ontario grid. It does not address condos or apartments directly, so a Guelph condo owner or tenant should confirm with the program. 34% of Guelph households rent, and a tenant should ask the landlord before swapping a fridge or range that came with the unit.

How to claim in Guelph

  1. Check that the appliance you are replacing is electric and that you are on the Ontario grid.
  2. Find a new model on the program's ENERGY STAR Most Efficient list for that appliance.
  3. Buy it new from any retailer and keep the receipt.
  4. Upload the receipt on the program page within 60 days of purchase.
  5. If your household income is under the limits, call 1-844-770-3148 about free fridges and freezers first.

Common questions

Does Alectra give an appliance rebate in Guelph?

We did not find one.

Can students claim?

If they are residential customers on the grid and replace an existing electric appliance, the page does not exclude them.

Is the induction rebate for a range or a cooktop?

Both are included at $150.

What does Most Efficient mean?

A level above standard ENERGY STAR, as the page describes it.
