Ontario works differently than BC. Instead of one heat pump rebate tiered by household income, Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program tiers the amount by which fuel you're currently heating with — and cities like Toronto add their own low-interest loan on top, rather than a cash top-up. Each city page explains its own local utility and any city-run financing program.
