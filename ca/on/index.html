Common questions

What you should know about the HRSP.

What is Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program?

The Home Renovation Savings Program (HRSP) is Ontario's main home-energy rebate program, run through participating utilities. It covers heat pumps ($2,000-$12,000 depending on your current fuel), solar (up to $5,000), battery storage (up to $5,000), insulation, windows, water heaters, and smart thermostats. Some upgrades need no home energy assessment; others require one plus at least two qualifying measures.

How much does a heat pump rebate cover in Ontario?

It depends on what you're switching from. Gas customers get up to $2,000 for an air-source heat pump or $3,000 for ground-source. Switching from electric, oil, propane, or wood gets up to $7,500 (air-source) or up to $12,000 (ground-source).

Which Ontario cities are covered by HomePowerRebate?

We currently serve 19 Ontario cities and regions: Toronto, Ottawa, Mississauga, Brampton, Hamilton, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Barrie, London, Kitchener, Windsor, Oakville, Oshawa, Whitby, Burlington, Cambridge, Greater Sudbury, and Durham Region.

Is there an EV charger rebate in Ontario?

There's no direct HRSP cash rebate for a home EV charger. In Toronto, a Level 2 charger install is an eligible expense under the City's HELP loan program instead.

What happened to the federal Greener Homes loan?

The federal Canada Greener Homes Loan closed to new applicants on October 1, 2025. It's not coming back in its old form. The province-wide HRSP rebates above are what's actually available in 2026 — they're grants, not loans, so there's nothing to repay.

Is this really free for homeowners?

Yes. You don't pay HomePowerRebate anything. We earn a small referral fee from the installer only when you book a job.

Can I get multiple quotes?

By design, you see every vetted installer in your city, not just one. Browse ratings, reviews, and specialties, then pick who you trust. We don't auction your info out to the highest bidder.
