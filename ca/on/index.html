Most Common Question

Should I get a heat pump or solar first?

Short answer: Heat pump wins. A heat pump pays for itself in 6–8 years. Solar takes 9–17 years.

Heat Pump Path:

You pay about $8,000 after the Ontario rebate. You save $1,200 per year on heating. It's paid for in about 7 years. After that, free heating for 20+ years.

Solar Path:

You pay about $20,000 after the rebate. You save $1,400 per year on electricity. It's paid for in about 14 years. After that, free electricity for 20+ years.

Quick Questions

Will a heat pump heat my Ontario home in winter?
Yes. Ontario winters are similar to BC's. New heat pumps work down to -20°C. Most winters don't get that cold. When they do, backup heat kicks in. Read more about heat pump performance in Ontario winters.
The federal loan closed. What now?
Ontario's new Home Renovation Savings program gives up to $7,500 back. It's a rebate, not a loan—no repayment required. Learn what's available now.
What if my rebate gets rejected?
Common reasons: Your contractor isn't approved, you made too much money, or you applied after the work was done. See all rejection reasons and how to avoid them.
How long until I get my rebate?
About 5–6 months total from application to payment, similar to BC. Application approval takes 2–3 weeks. Work gets done in 1–5 days. Final inspection takes 2–3 weeks. Rebate arrives in 6–12 weeks.
