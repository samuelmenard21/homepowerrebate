What Ontario pays for a smart thermostat in London

We re-read the three program pages on October 4, 2026. The rebate is province-wide, so the amount is the same in every city, and the page that matters is the one that says whether your home and heating qualify.

Open

Smart thermostat

$125 back

One rebate on an eligible smart thermostat, claimed with a code at checkout or with a receipt uploaded within 60 days of buying.

Open

Who is eligible

Gas heat or electric heat or cooling

You must be an Enbridge Gas customer heating mainly with a gas furnace or boiler, or be on the Ontario grid and use electric heat or cooling, and live in a house, semi, row, townhome or mobile home on a permanent foundation.

Open

One per household, ever

No earlier thermostat offer

A smart thermostat rebate, discount or free device from Enbridge, Save on Energy or Home Renovation Savings in the past rules you out.

Open

Peak Perks

$75, then $20 a year

A prepaid card for joining the summer demand response program with central air or a heat pump controlled by an eligible Wi-Fi thermostat.

Open

Energy Affordability Program

Free for income-qualified homes

Households under the income limits get a programmable or smart thermostat, depending on the home, with other upgrades at no charge.

Open

Cornwall excluded

Hydro-Quebec grid

Cornwall Electric customers cannot join the rebate or Peak Perks.

The rebate page states no end date, so confirm it is still open before you buy.

Eligible houses, tall apartments

66% of London households live in a house, semi, row or townhome, and 42% rent. A resident of one of the 21% in tall buildings is outside the rebate, so the free Energy Affordability Program is the question to put to the landlord or to the program.

London Hydro

London Hydro supplies electricity, and London postal codes begin with N, which is on the program's list. Peak Perks is open to its customers with central air and an eligible Wi-Fi thermostat. London's average January low is -7.5°C.

Who here can claim the $125

The program pays for single detached, semi-detached, row, townhome and mobile homes on a permanent foundation. In London about 66% of 174,655 households live in one of those. If yours is an apartment or a duplex unit, this rebate is not written for you, and the Energy Affordability Program is the route to ask about.

Six brands, specific models

The rebate covers ecobee, Google Nest, Honeywell Home, Copeland Sensi, Mysa and Sinopé, and only the model numbers the program page lists for each. A thermostat from the same brand that is not on the list earns nothing, so match the box to the page before you pay. Check that your furnace or heat pump works with the model as well, which the maker's compatibility checker will tell you.

The size of the saving

The rebate is a flat $125, so it takes the same amount off a cheap thermostat and an expensive one. It does not guarantee a lower bill. How much a smart thermostat changes your energy use depends on your schedule, your system and how you set it, and we have no program figure to quote for that.

How to claim in London

  1. Check that your home type, heating and cooling match the eligibility card, and that you have not had a thermostat offer before.
  2. Pick one of the listed models from ecobee, Google Nest, Honeywell Home, Copeland Sensi, Mysa or Sinopé.
  3. Get a code from the program page and use it at checkout, or buy first and upload the receipt within 60 days.
  4. If you have central air, enroll the same thermostat in Peak Perks. London Hydro is your local distributor, but Peak Perks is run by Save on Energy.
  5. If your household income is under the program limits, call 1-844-770-3148 before buying, since a free thermostat may be available.

Common questions

Does London Hydro have its own program?

We did not find one on the pages we read.

Can I get Peak Perks and the $125?

Yes, as separate programs. Peak Perks needs central air.

Is a duplex unit eligible?

The page lists single and semi-detached homes, rows, townhomes and mobile homes. Ask the program about a duplex.

What is the claim window?

60 days from purchase for the receipt route.
