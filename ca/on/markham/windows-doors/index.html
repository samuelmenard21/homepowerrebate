Windows & Doors Rebates in Markham

The breakdown

Why this one needs an assessment

Windows and doors bundle into the assessment-required path alongside insulation, water heater, and air sealing. You need at least 2 of those upgrades — completing 3+ earns an extra $500 bonus.

How to claim it

  1. Book an initial home energy assessment with a registered energy advisor ($600, refunded later)
  2. Get your custom report
  3. Complete the window/door replacement plus at least one other qualifying upgrade
  4. Book a follow-up assessment
  5. Rebate arrives by mail within 60 days

Common questions

Are window and door rebates available in Markham?

Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program covers ENERGY STAR-rated window and door replacement as part of a broader retrofit, usually alongside insulation.

Do all new windows qualify for the rebate in Markham?

No. They need to meet ENERGY STAR's rating for your climate zone. A standard double-pane replacement usually won't qualify on its own.
