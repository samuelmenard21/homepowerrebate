What Ontario pays for new appliances in Niagara Falls

We re-read the program's appliance page on October 4, 2026. The rebates are province-wide, so the amounts are the same in every city, and the rules about what you replace and which model you buy are what decide a claim.

Who can claim

Ontario grid customers

A residential customer on the Ontario grid with an existing electric appliance in need of replacing. Cornwall Electric customers are excluded.

Open

Free route

Energy Affordability Program

Income-qualified households can get energy-efficient fridges and freezers at no charge, along with other upgrades.

The appliance page states no end date, so confirm it is still open before you buy.

Mostly houses

70% of households own and only 4% live in tall buildings. The appliance rebate fits a house where one appliance fails at a time. 61% of the city's homes were built before 1981, though the age of the house says little about the age of its fridge.

Niagara Peninsula Energy

Niagara Peninsula Energy is the distributor, and Niagara Falls postal codes begin with L, which the Energy Affordability Program lists. January lows average -6.1°C, the mildest of the 25 cities.

Buy new, then upload the receipt

Buy a new appliance from any retailer, then upload the receipt on the program page within 60 days of purchase. Used and refurbished units do not qualify. Keep the receipt, the model number and the delivery date together. Niagara Falls households who buy during a sale should still confirm the model is on the list at the time they check out.

Replace, do not add

The program page asks for an existing electric appliance in need of replacing. A new fridge to go in a spare corner, or an induction range where the kitchen had no range, is not a replacement. If you are switching from gas to an electric appliance, the page does not say whether that counts, so ask the program before you buy. Niagara Falls households that already cook on an electric range are the clearest fit for the induction rebate.

Fridges and freezers at no cost

The Energy Affordability Program lists energy-efficient refrigerators and freezers among the items that comprehensive support can provide, along with LED bulbs, dehumidifiers and window air conditioners. It does not list washers or dryers, and its saving kits include no major appliances. Income limits run from $48,220 for one person to $96,439 for four, and Niagara Falls postal codes are among those it covers. Call 1-844-770-3148 to ask.

What kind of home

Unlike the thermostat rebate, the appliance page lists no home type. It asks for a residential customer on the Ontario grid. It does not address condos or apartments directly, so a Niagara Falls condo owner or tenant should confirm with the program. 30% of Niagara Falls households rent, and a tenant should ask the landlord before swapping a fridge or range that came with the unit.

How to claim in Niagara Falls

  1. Check that the appliance you are replacing is electric and that you are on the Ontario grid.
  2. Find a new model on the program's ENERGY STAR Most Efficient list for that appliance.
  3. Buy it new from any retailer and keep the receipt.
  4. Upload the receipt on the program page within 60 days of purchase.
  5. If your household income is under the limits, call 1-844-770-3148 about free fridges and freezers first.
