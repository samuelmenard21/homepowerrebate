Home EV charger rebate

None via HRSP

The provincial Home Renovation Savings Program does not include a cash rebate for home EV charger installs.

We're not going to invent a rebate that doesn't exist. Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program covers heat pumps, solar, battery storage, insulation, windows/doors, water heaters, thermostats, and appliances — but not home EV chargers. If you're planning an EV charger install in Niagara Falls, your best next step is checking directly with Niagara Peninsula Energy Inc. (NPEI), your local electricity utility, for a separate off-peak charging incentive; we could not independently confirm specific dollar figures for any NPEI EV program, so we're not listing numbers here.

No local top-up in Niagara Falls either

We checked the City of Niagara Falls directly and found no municipal EV charger rebate or loan program. If you install one, the cost currently comes out of pocket, offset only by whatever NPEI can confirm for you directly.
