What Ontario pays for new appliances in Oakville

We re-read the program's appliance page on October 4, 2026. The rebates are province-wide, so the amounts are the same in every city, and the rules about what you replace and which model you buy are what decide a claim.

Who can claim

Ontario grid customers

A residential customer on the Ontario grid with an existing electric appliance in need of replacing. Cornwall Electric customers are excluded.

Open

Free route

Energy Affordability Program

Income-qualified households can get energy-efficient fridges and freezers at no charge, along with other upgrades.

The appliance page states no end date, so confirm it is still open before you buy.

Owners replacing one at a time

59% of Oakville households live in detached houses. For an owner the practical question is which appliance fails first, since the page states no per-household limit and each claim has its own 60-day window.

Oakville Hydro

Oakville Hydro distributes power and Oakville postal codes begin with L, which the Energy Affordability Program lists. The page's only named exclusion is Cornwall Electric customers. The average January low here is -6.8°C.

Adding up the six

The six rebates are $200 for a heat pump dryer, $150 for an induction cooktop or range, $75 for a washer, $50 each for a fridge and a freezer and $25 for a dishwasher. A household that replaced all six would receive $550, though few Oakville homes will need to swap everything at once. The rebate is flat, so it takes the same amount off an entry model and a premium one.

Most Efficient, not just ENERGY STAR

The rebate is for ENERGY STAR Most Efficient models, which the page describes as going beyond standard ENERGY STAR requirements. A fridge with the ordinary ENERGY STAR mark may not qualify. Each appliance has its own product list linked from the program page, so find the model number there before you pay, whether you buy in Oakville or online.

When replacing makes sense

About 27% of Oakville's 73,560 households live in homes built before 1981. Age of the house tells you little about the age of the fridge, and a working appliance rarely pays back a swap through a $25 to $200 rebate alone. The rebate suits a replacement you already need to make.

What kind of home

Unlike the thermostat rebate, the appliance page lists no home type. It asks for a residential customer on the Ontario grid. It does not address condos or apartments directly, so a Oakville condo owner or tenant should confirm with the program. 22% of Oakville households rent, and a tenant should ask the landlord before swapping a fridge or range that came with the unit.

How to claim in Oakville

  1. Check that the appliance you are replacing is electric and that you are on the Ontario grid.
  2. Find a new model on the program's ENERGY STAR Most Efficient list for that appliance.
  3. Buy it new from any retailer and keep the receipt.
  4. Upload the receipt on the program page within 60 days of purchase.
  5. If your household income is under the limits, call 1-844-770-3148 about free fridges and freezers first.
