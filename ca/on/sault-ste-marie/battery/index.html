What the battery storage rebate pays in Sault Ste. Marie

Sault Ste. Marie homeowners adding home battery storage can claim $300 per kWh of installed capacity, capped at 50% of total project cost, up to a maximum of $5,000 on its own. The real value shows up when you pair it with solar: the two rebates stack for up to $10,000 combined, one of the biggest single-project totals on the whole Sault Ste. Marie page.

How to claim it

  1. Complete the eligibility form on the Save on Energy portal
  2. Select a participating installer
  3. Get mandatory pre-installation approval before work starts
  4. Installation is completed
  5. Installer submits the post-installation application
