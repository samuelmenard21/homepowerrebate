Windows & Doors Rebates in Timmins

The Home Renovation Savings Program pays $100 per rough opening replaced with an eligible ENERGY STAR window or door, as part of the assessment-required tier — the same rate across Ontario.

Part of the bigger retrofit tier

Windows and doors sit alongside exterior wall, foundation, crawlspace, and water heater rebates in the assessment-required tier on the main Timmins page — you need a home energy assessment (~$600, refunded as a rebate) and at least two qualifying upgrades, with a $500 bonus for completing three or more.

How to claim it

  1. Book a home energy assessment (~$600, refunded as a rebate)
  2. Replace windows/doors with eligible ENERGY STAR products, alongside at least one other qualifying measure
  3. Submit the Save on Energy application with assessment and contractor documentation
  4. Rebate cheque arrives after post-installation approval

Common questions

How much is the windows and doors rebate in Timmins?

$100 per rough opening replaced with an eligible ENERGY STAR window or door. It's a per-opening rebate, so a project replacing several windows adds up quickly, and it's part of the assessment-required tier of the province's Home Renovation Savings Program.

Do I need a home energy assessment for the windows rebate?

Yes. Like the exterior wall, foundation, and water heater rebates on the main Timmins page, windows and doors sit in the assessment-required tier, which needs a home energy assessment (~$600, refunded as a rebate) and at least two qualifying upgrades.

Why do windows matter more in a city like Timmins?

Timmins' older mining-era housing stock and much colder winters than southern Ontario put more strain on older, less efficient windows and doors than in a typical southern city. The rebate rate is identical everywhere in Ontario, but the case for doing the work is stronger here.
