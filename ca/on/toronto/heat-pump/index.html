How much rebate you get

Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program pays $500/ton (up to $2,000) for Enbridge Gas customers switching from gas, or $1,250/ton (up to $7,500) for households switching from electric, oil, propane, or wood.

Estimated value: $1,500–$7,500. The exact amount depends on your current heating fuel, system size, and which programs you combine.

Who qualifies

  • You own your home in Toronto
  • Your current system matches the upgrade type (e.g., a gas furnace for a heat pump upgrade)
  • A participating contractor files the rebate application on your behalf
  • For the Toronto HELP loan: the loan is approved before you hire a contractor, not after

How to claim it

  1. Get quotes from participating contractors in Toronto
  2. If financing with the Toronto HELP loan, apply and get approved first
  3. Work is installed
  4. Contractor files the HRSP rebate application through Save on Energy
  5. Rebate arrives in 30–60 days
