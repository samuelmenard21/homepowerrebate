How to claim it

  1. Complete the eligibility form on the Save on Energy portal
  2. Select a participating HVAC contractor
  3. Get mandatory pre-installation approval before work starts
  4. Installation is completed
  5. Contractor submits the post-installation application
  6. Rebate cheque arrives within 60 days of approval

Common questions

How much is the heat pump rebate in Whitby?

It depends on your current heating fuel. Enbridge Gas customers get up to $2,000 for an air-source heat pump or $3,000 for ground-source. If you heat with electric, oil, propane, or wood, you get up to $7,500 (air-source) or $12,000 (ground-source). This is the same structure across all of Ontario.

Is a heat pump worth it in Whitby's winters?

Yes, as long as you get a cold-climate model, which the rebate requires anyway. Ontario winters regularly dip below -15°C, but modern cold-climate heat pumps keep working well past -25°C.
