HRV & Ventilation Rebates in Whitby

The honest answer: no dedicated HRV rebate in Whitby

Ontario's provincial Home Renovation Savings Program (HRSP), delivered through Enbridge Gas and local electric utilities like Elexicon Energy in Whitby, covers heat pumps, insulation, windows and doors, air sealing, solar, smart thermostats, and a handful of appliances. Heat recovery ventilators and energy recovery ventilators are not on that eligible-measures list, whether installed on their own or alongside another upgrade. This isn't a technicality or a paperwork gap — we checked the program's own eligible-upgrades documentation directly, and ventilation equipment simply doesn't appear in either its assessment-required or no-assessment-required tiers.

Whitby's blend of established neighbourhoods and newer Durham-region subdivisions means some homes already have a builder-installed HRV and others don't — but neither group has a rebate to fall back on.

What we checked

  • The Home Renovation Savings Program's own eligible-upgrades list (homerenovationsavings.ca) — no ventilation category in either the assessment-required or no-assessment-required tiers
  • Enbridge Gas's ERV/HRV incentive page — confirmed it applies to multi-family, commercial, and industrial buildings, not single-family homes like most Whitby properties
  • The federal Canada Greener Homes Grant, which formerly counted HRV/ERV toward its retrofit grant — closed to new applicants since February 2024, with the final document-submission deadline passed as of December 31, 2025
  • The Canada Greener Homes Affordability Program, the federal replacement for income-qualified households — as of 2026-09-02, it operates in Manitoba, BC, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and PEI, but has not launched in Ontario

Why this matters if you're sealing up your Whitby home

Air sealing, insulation, and window upgrades — all rebate-eligible under HRSP — make a home tighter, which is good for energy bills but can trap stale air, moisture, and indoor pollutants that used to leak out on their own. An HRV or ERV solves that by swapping stale indoor air for fresh outdoor air while recovering most of the heat. It's a real need for a well-sealed Whitby home; Ontario just doesn't currently pay toward it the way British Columbia does with its income-qualified CleanBC top-up. Common questions

Is there an HRV or ERV rebate in Whitby?

No. Ontario's Home Renovation Savings Program (HRSP) does not list heat recovery ventilators (HRV) or energy recovery ventilators (ERV) as an eligible upgrade category for homeowners in Whitby or anywhere else in the province. The program covers heat pumps, insulation, windows/doors, air sealing, solar, smart thermostats, and select appliances — ventilation equipment is not on that list.

Does Enbridge Gas offer an HRV rebate to Whitby homeowners?

Enbridge Gas does run an ERV/HRV incentive, but it's restricted to commercial, industrial, and multi-family residential buildings — not single-family or freehold homes. A single-family Whitby homeowner installing an HRV in their own house does not qualify for it.
