Good news! Condos and apartments ARE eligible for some rebates.

  • Heat pump water heater: up to $3,500
  • Free smart thermostats: 5 units, free, if you have electric baseboard heat (Oct 2026)
  • Free energy saving kit: LED bulbs, weather stripping, etc. (income-qualified)
