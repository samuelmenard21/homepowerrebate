California Rebate Calculator 2026: What Your Utility Pays

California's rebates depend on your electric utility. Pick yours and see what is open, what is on a waitlist and what has ended.

By Sam Menard · Rules last verified October 2, 2026

Short answer: California's statewide heat pump programs (TECH Clean California and the federal HEEHRA) are fully reserved and on waitlists. What still pays is local: SMUD pays up to $3,000 for a heat pump and up to $4,000 for a heat pump water heater, LADWP pays up to $2,500 per ton, Glendale pays $1,000 per ton (up to $5,000) when you replace a gas furnace, and Burbank and Pasadena have their own rebates.

How we work it out

  • Every amount comes from the program's own page, linked on each result.
  • We count only programs that are open today. Programs that start later or have a waitlist are listed but not added.
  • Your answers stay in your browser. We only get your email if you ask us to notify you.
  • Nobody pays to be included, and installers cannot change what you see.

Common questions

Is there a statewide heat pump rebate in California?

Not open right now. TECH Clean California single-family incentives were fully reserved on November 14, 2025 and take waitlist applications only. The federal HEEHRA program was fully reserved on February 24, 2026. The federal 25C and 25D tax credits also ended for 2026 installs.

How much does SMUD pay for a heat pump?

Up to $3,000 for a two-stage or variable-stage heat pump with a participating contractor, plus up to $2,000 more in Go Electric bonuses when you replace a gas furnace or water heater. A heat pump water heater pays up to $4,000.

What does LADWP pay?

Up to $2,500 per ton for a heat pump, up to $2,500 for a heat pump water heater, and other rebates such as $2.00 per square foot for ENERGY STAR windows. Apply within 12 months after the work.

What if my utility is PG&E or SDG&E?

Choose Another utility. We have not verified a current PG&E or SDG&E home rebate on a program page, so the calculator shows only the statewide programs and their status.
