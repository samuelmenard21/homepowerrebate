Minnesota Rebate Calculator 2026: What Your Utilities Pay for Your Home

Tell us which companies send your bills and see the rebates that fit your home, with a source for every number.

By Sam Menard · Rules last verified October 6, 2026

Short answer: Minnesota rebates come from your gas and electric companies. CenterPoint pays $1,100 for a hybrid heat pump, Minnesota Power pays $1,000 for a cold-climate heat pump, Rochester Public Utilities pays up to $2,200, Xcel Energy pays up to $3,000 for insulation, and Minneapolis adds up to $5,000. The state's federal rebates have not launched, so they are not counted.

How we work it out

  • Every amount comes from the program's own page, linked on each result.
  • We count only programs that are open today. Programs that start later or have a waitlist are listed but not added.
  • Your answers stay in your browser. We only get your email if you ask us to notify you.
  • Nobody pays to be included, and installers cannot change what you see.

Common questions

Which rebates does the calculator add up?

Only rebates that are open and that we confirmed on the program's own page. Xcel's heat pump and thermostat amounts are shown but not counted, because we could not confirm them on a current page.

Why does it ask who sends my gas and electric bills?

Minnesota has no single statewide utility rebate. Each company has its own program, and some pay for different things, so the same upgrade can earn money from two companies.

Is the state HOMES or HEAR rebate included?

No. The state says that program has not launched and has no launch date.

Are federal tax credits included?

No. We could not confirm that any federal home credit is open for 2026 installs.
