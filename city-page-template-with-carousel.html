Frequently Asked Questions

Will your heat pump work in [CITY]'s winters?

Yes. Heat pumps work down to about -15°C. [CITY]'s average winter low is around [X]°C. Cold-climate heat pumps (which these installers specialize in) handle our weather fine.

What's Peak Saver and should I enroll?

Peak Saver is BC Hydro's demand response program (island cities only). You pre-heat your home at off-peak times, and during peak demand, your system temporarily dials back. You get paid $200 upfront plus $15–30/month. Enrollment is automatic, but these installers can explain how to optimize.

Does a heat pump replace my furnace completely?

Yes. Heat pumps are a complete replacement—they handle heating, cooling, and hot water. You remove the old furnace and install the heat pump.

How much can I really save per year?

Depends on your current heating system and habits. Furnace users typically save $250–400/year. Baseboard heater users save even more ($600+/year).

Is the rebate guaranteed?

BC Hydro honors the rebates if your system is installed by a certified installer (all three here are) and passes inspection. These installers know how to make sure the paperwork gets done right.
