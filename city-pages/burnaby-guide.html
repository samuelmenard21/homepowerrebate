Common Questions Burnaby Homeowners Ask

Will a heat pump work for my 2,000+ sq ft Burnaby home?

Yes. Modern heat pumps handle large homes efficiently. For 2,000 sq ft, you may need a dual-zone system (two indoor units). BC Hydro rebate covers either configuration. Efficiency actually improves with multiple zones.

Can I install solar on my Burnaby roof if it has shade?

Depends on the shade pattern. Burnaby homes vary significantly. Our installers assess roof orientation and shade trees during the quote. Many Burnaby homes get 4–6 peak sun hours, which is solid for solar ROI.

Does my homeowners insurance cover battery backup?

Usually. Most insurance treats home batteries like other electrical upgrades—no extra premium. Confirm with your insurer. Battery actually reduces insurance risk (fewer outage-related claims).
