Why Kamloops Homeowners Are Choosing Heat Pumps

Kamloops gets 300+ sunny days per year—highest solar resource in BC. Winters are dry and moderate (average -5 to 0°C). This combination makes heat pump + solar + battery an excellent investment for Kamloops homeowners.

  • Highest solar resource in BC: 300+ sunny days per year means maximum solar production and battery charging
  • Dry interior winters: Heat pumps achieve peak efficiency (~350%) in Kamloops's dry, cold air
  • Summer cooling: Kamloops summers are hot (25–30°C). Heat pump cooling is essential
  • Annual heating + cooling savings: $300–$500/year vs furnace + AC

The Eguana Battery: Why It Matters for Kamloops

Kamloops is the ideal market for solar + battery. Eguana batteries qualify for $5,000 BC Hydro rebate when paired with solar:

  • Solar synergy: High solar production (300+ days/year) means battery charges efficiently every day
  • Peak Saver enrollment: $300–$500/year for sharing battery power during peak demand. Without enrollment, rebate drops to $1,500
  • 10-year payback: Battery saves $3K–$5K through avoided peak charges + Peak Saver rewards
Kamloops advantage: You have the best solar resource in BC + ideal heat pump climate. Heat pump + solar + battery is a home run financially here more than anywhere else in BC.
