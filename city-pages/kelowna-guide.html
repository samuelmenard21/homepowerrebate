Why Kelowna Homeowners Are Choosing Heat Pumps

Kelowna's dry interior winters are perfect for heat pump efficiency. Winters are crisp (average -5°C) but predictable—no fog or ice like coastal BC. Heat pumps work at ~350% efficiency in Kelowna's climate, meaning you save $200–$400/year on heating vs gas furnace.

  • Dry climate advantage: Heat pumps achieve peak efficiency in dry, cold air. Kelowna winters are ideal
  • Summer cooling: Kelowna summers are hot (25–30°C). Heat pump cooling is essential, not optional
  • Solar resource: Kelowna gets 300+ sunny days per year—best solar potential in BC. Pair with battery for peak production
  • Upfront cost: $7K–$11K installed, minus $4K–$8K rebate = often free

Common Questions Kelowna Homeowners Ask

Do heat pumps work in Kelowna winters?

Yes, perfectly. Kelowna's dry, crisp winters are ideal for heat pump efficiency. At -5°C, a heat pump still has plenty of ambient heat to extract and move into your home. You'll save $200–$400/year vs gas furnace.

Is the Eguana battery worth it if I already have solar?

Yes. Battery + solar + Peak Saver enrollment gives you $5K rebate, $300–$500/year, and backup power for 4–6 hours. Payback is 12–15 years, after which you're saving $3K–$5K per decade indefinitely.

What about in Kelowna summer?

Heat pump cools in summer. Kelowna summers are hot, and AC-equivalent cooling is essential. Heat pump does both heating and cooling with one unit.
