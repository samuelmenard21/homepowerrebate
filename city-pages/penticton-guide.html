Why Penticton Homeowners Are Choosing Heat Pumps

Penticton's wine-country luxury market is seeing strong heat pump adoption. Affluent homeowners value both efficiency and comfort. Heat pumps deliver both while cutting heating costs by 30–50%.

  • Affluent market advantage: Penticton's upscale demographic values quality and sustainability
  • Dry interior climate: Heat pumps work at peak efficiency in Penticton's dry, moderate winters

The Eguana Battery: Why It Matters for Penticton

Eguana batteries are built in Canada and qualify for $5,000 BC Hydro rebate when paired with solar. For affluent Penticton homeowners:

Penticton premium: Heat pump + solar + battery appeals to Penticton's quality-conscious homeowners. Complete energy retrofit positions homes at market premium.
