Common Questions Squamish Homeowners Ask

Will a heat pump work in Squamish's rainy winters?

Yes. Heat pumps work in any climate; they extract ambient heat even in rain. Squamish's mild winters (3–5°C average) are actually ideal for heat pump efficiency.

How much backup power will my battery provide?

A 5kWh battery provides 4–6 hours of backup power for essential loads: fridge, lights, water heater, heat pump, well pump (if applicable). Not whole-house backup, but covers critical needs during coastal outages.
