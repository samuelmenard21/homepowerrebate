What changed in home energy rebates: October 2026

410 new programs, 5 status changes and 25 amount changes, with the official source for every row.

This is our October 2026 data release: what changed in the home energy rebate programs we track, in one place, with a source for every row. It is built from our verified program facts, not from news. Cite it freely and link back.

410new programs and facts
5status changes
25amount changes
479open programs now

Since 2026-09-30, we added 410 programs and facts, changed the status of 5 and changed the amount on 25. We now track 651 programs across 27 regions and funding sources, and 479 of them are open (it was 196 on 2026-09-30). 16 start soon, 42 are closed and 25 need their status confirmed.

How we made this

We compare our verified program facts today with the same facts in the last version saved before 2026-09-30. A fact is a program, its status, its amount, its rules and the official page it came from, with the date we last read that page. We change a fact only after reading the official page. The monthly rebate tracker lists dated news; this release lists what changed in our data. Past releases are on the data release page.

Common questions

What does a status change mean?

A program moved between open, starting soon, waitlist, paused, closed or confirm status because its own page said so. We re-read the page before we change it.

Why do so many new programs appear?

We added new provinces, states and cities. Each new region brings its own programs, each with a source and a checked date.

Can I reuse this data?

Yes. Please cite HomePowerRebate and link to this page. The CSV buttons give you the rows.

Are these amounts what I will get?

No. They are the maximums programs publish. Your amount depends on your home, utility and income. Use the rebate calculator for your own numbers.
