Subject: October rebate update: BC bonus ends Oct 31, Xcel and Nova Scotia open Oct 1
Preview: One deadline this month, and two programs opening.

[CLOSING SOON] BC Hydro heat pump bonus ends October 31
If you are replacing electric heat in BC Hydro territory, the extra bonus of up to $1,000 only covers installs finished by October 31, 2026. Book now if you want it. Amounts stack on top of the up-to-$4,000 base rebate.
https://homepowerrebate.com/calculator/bc/

[OPENING] Colorado: Xcel Energy heat pump rebates start October 1
$750 per heating ton, or $1,500 per ton for homes heated with Xcel natural gas. A heat pump water heater pays $750, or $1,500 with the gas bonus.
https://homepowerrebate.com/calculator/co/

[OPENING] Nova Scotia: heating assistance applications open October 1
HARP paid $400 last winter and has opened on October 1 in past years. The 2026-27 amount has not been posted yet.
https://homepowerrebate.com/ca/ns/

[CHANGED] Alberta's oil-to-heat-pump grant closed July 31
The federal grant of up to $10,000 is closed to Albertans. Calgary's clean energy loan intake is also closed until winter 2026/2027.
https://homepowerrebate.com/calculator/ab/

[CHANGED] Nova Scotia's Moderate Income Rebate was cut to $5,000
Stacked with the Home Energy Assessment rebates, the most you can now get is $10,000.
https://homepowerrebate.com/calculator/ns/

[RAISED] Ontario raised two Home Renovation Savings rebates
The smart thermostat rebate is now $125 (from $100) and no-assessment attic insulation pays up to $1,250 (from $1,000).
https://homepowerrebate.com/calculator/on/

[NEW] Solar pilots and loans
Pasadena Water and Power pays $0.60 per watt for rooftop solar ($1.00 if income-qualified) and up to $550 per kWh for batteries, while funds last. Boston has a 0% solar loan of up to $25,000. Waterloo Region launched RetrofitWR with loans up to $40,000 at 2.5% for GrandBridge Energy customers.
https://homepowerrebate.com/rebate-tracker/
