# Heat pump: the plan that earns backlinks (2026-11-04)

Companion to `docs/seo/heat-pump-city-plan.md` (waves) and `docs/seo/awards-2026-plan.md` (the December awards). Goal: links from real sites to the heat pump city pages, the `/installers/heat-pump/` hub and the city ratings page, earned by being useful. Nothing here is paid, traded or required for a listing.

## Rules we keep
- Money never changes a ranking, and nobody has to link to us to be listed or to win.
- First note to a company asks for nothing. A link is only mentioned after they reply.
- Natural anchors (company or city name, "HomePowerRebate"), never keyword-stuffed anchors in badges.
- Real names, real numbers from our data, sources shown. Suppression list and blocklist always respected (CASL).
- Sam approves every list and every wording before anything is sent.

## Where links come from, best first
| Source | Asset we give them | Who | The ask (when) | Realistic yield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Installers (1,559 companies) | their own free website and Google review, then an award and a seal | top 3 per city, wave by wave | seal that links to their city page (only after they reply, after the award notice) | the biggest pool; plan for 10 to 15% of winners and finalists |
| 2. Local news | city numbers from the ratings page and the awards | 10 reporters, one per top city | "you may use the numbers"; a link is the natural credit | 3 to 8 |
| 3. Energy advisors and auditors (887 companies) | calculator embed, printable city rebate sheet | already in daily outreach | link the sheet or calculator on their site | 10 to 20 |
| 4. Chambers, municipal sustainability pages, community energy groups | a free, neutral "find a heat pump installer in <city>" resource | 3 to 5 per wave-1 city, one email each | add us to their resources page | 5 to 10 |
| 5. Trade and homeowner groups | the federal partner kit and rebate alert page | associations, landlord and condo groups | share the kit; link the guide | 3 to 6 |
| 6. Utilities and programs | a month of `official_click` numbers (homeowners we sent them) | BC Hydro, Mass Save, Enbridge, Better Homes BC | a "find a contractor" resource link | 1 to 3, low odds, January |
| 7. Reclaim | any mention without a link | found by search | a polite "could you link the source?" | a few |

These are ranges to plan with, not promises. Reply rates are unknown (the October intro emails drew about 1%), which is why value comes first and links come last.

## The ladder for one wave-1 city (example: Hamilton)
1. **Measure:** page position, impressions, clicks (weekly check already runs).
2. **Give:** personal note to the top 3 companies with their rank, 1 or 2 fixes and the one-page review. No ask.
3. **Recognise:** award notice Dec 3 to 5 with their seal. No ask.
4. **Local proof:** a pitch to the local paper with Hamilton's numbers; a note to the Hamilton chamber and the city's sustainability office with the page.
5. **After a reply:** offer the seal snippet that links to their Hamilton page, and thank them. One follow-up at most.
6. **Record:** new links go in the weekly report (below).

## Timeline
- **Nov 4 to 10:** measure the baseline (referring domains from Search Console and Bing, `domain-rating`, `link-evidence`); approve the 10 installer notes and the reporter list.
- **Nov 9 to 30:** wave 1 notes (about 30, top 3 in 10 cities); research chamber, municipal and local energy group targets for wave 1; advisor outreach continues.
- **Dec 1:** refresh ratings; build winners; gate review of wave 1 pages.
- **Dec 3 to 8:** private winner notices, then public launch and press release.
- **Dec 9 to 20:** seals after replies; congratulate; reclaim mentions.
- **January:** wave 2 (30 cities) runs the same ladder; utilities pitch with a month of click data; second-wave categories.
- **February to March:** "State of heat pump installers" annual report; tie to the federal rebate launch.

## Measuring
- Weekly report adds: referring domains (new, lost), links by source type, links to each wave-1 page, and replies by segment.
- Targets to plan against (not forecasts): 10 referring domains by Dec 1, 30 by Jan 15, 60 by Mar 1. Set after the baseline is measured.

## Risks
- Low reply rates; refresh Google ratings first or winners can be disputed; the badge must not look like pay to play; a seal widget copied across many sites with keyword anchors can look like a link scheme, so use brand or city anchors only; award disputes (same-day removal on request).
