Eguana Evolve FAQ

Is Eguana a real company or a startup? +

Eguana is a publicly traded Canadian company (TSX: EUG) based in Calgary, established in 1998. It has 25+ years of experience in grid-edge power electronics. This is not a startup betting on unproven tech—it's an established manufacturer with decades of proven cold-climate performance.

How much does an Eguana Evolve battery cost? +

System costs range $12,000–$25,000+ depending on capacity and installation. The BC Hydro rebate of $5,000 applies when paired with solar. Your installer should provide an itemized quote and explain the rebate pathway.

Does Evolve work with my existing solar panels? +

Yes. Evolve is a DC-coupled battery system, meaning it works with most modern solar inverters. Your installer assesses your setup and confirms compatibility. If you're installing solar + Evolve together, it's straightforward.

What if my power goes out? +

When the grid goes down, Evolve switches to backup mode automatically. It powers your solar-charged battery to run essential loads—lights, fridge, heat pump—for as long as the battery stores energy. With solar recharging during the day, a typical system can sustain critical loads for 24–48 hours depending on usage.

What happens to my battery in winter when there's less sun? +

In BC winters, solar production drops but doesn't stop—even overcast days generate 40–50% of summer output. Evolve can store that power. For longer outages or cloudy stretches, you'll be limited by stored energy. Pair Evolve with a cold-climate heat pump for year-round resilience.

Is Peak Saver worth it? +

Absolutely. Peak Saver adds roughly $3,000 over 10 years in seasonal rewards, and your battery actually helps the grid during winter stress. More importantly, Peak Saver enrollment unlocks the full $5,000 BC Hydro rebate—missing the 14-day enrollment window costs you $3,500. Evolve makes Peak Saver simple.

Why Eguana instead of LG, Enphase, or other brands? +

All of these brands are on BC Hydro's approved list and qualify for the $5,000 rebate. Eguana's advantage is cold-climate heritage and Peak Saver integration from the ground up. If you're in BC and prioritize proven winter performance + simple Peak Saver enrollment, Eguana is the logical choice. Other brands work too—Evolve is just purpose-built for BC.
