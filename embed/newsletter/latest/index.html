November rebate update: Heat pump water heater deadline: November 30 and 10 more

Here is what changed since last month, and what is closing soon. Every figure is read from the program's own page.

CLOSING SOON · New York
Heat pump water heater deadline: November 30

Con Edison pays $1,500 (instead of $1,300) on applications sent by November 30.

CLOSING SOON · Ontario
Attic insulation deadline: November 30

Book your post-audit by November 30. It must be done by December 31.

PSE's higher washer and dryer rebates end November 30. Buy and install by December 31 for the regular amounts.

ENDED · Massachusetts
Eversource home EV charging rebate (2026 year)

Eversource's page says the 2026 application deadline was November 1, 2026. It is waiting for state approval of the 2027 program. Watch Eversource for the 2027 program before you buy a charger.

NEW · Canada
National Heat Pump Rebate

Ottawa announced a $2 billion rebate for electric heat pumps: up to $10,000 for households below the median income and $2,000 for other eligible homeowners. Launch is expected in early 2027, and installs from November 1, 2026 can apply once it opens. Income line, eligible homes and fuels are not published yet; Ottawa says details come on November 1, 2026. Use the provincial rebates that are open now and keep your invoices. We list every confirmed detail on one page.

See how it affects your home · Official source

ENDED · British Columbia
BC Hydro heat pump bonus

The extra bonus of up to $1,000 on BC Hydro heat pump rebates ended. Installs had to be finished and paid by October 31, 2026. You can still apply by November 30, 2026 if your install was done in the window. If your install was finished by October 31, send your application by November 30. Otherwise the standard rebate still applies.

See how it affects your home · Official source

COMING UP · Nova Scotia
Nova Scotia Heating Assistance Rebate (HARP)

HARP helps lower-income households with winter heating bills. It paid $400 last winter and has opened on October 1 in past years. Confirm the 2026-27 date and amount with the Province. Check your eligibility and apply as soon as the new season opens.
