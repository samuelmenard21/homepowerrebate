Registered Energy Advisors in Canada

Every NRCan-licensed company, with phone numbers and Google ratings. Updated October 2026.

Many rebate programs in Canada pay only after an EnerGuide home evaluation by a registered energy advisor, one visit before any work and one after. Every registered advisor books through a service organization licensed by Natural Resources Canada. This page lists all 127 of them, by province, plus 1 advisor team that told us they are registered, with the 61 we could match to a rated Google Maps listing ranked first.

NRCan says the two evaluations together average $600 to $1,000, depending on the provider and province, and are usually paid after the work. Service organizations never sell door to door or by unsolicited calls or emails.

Not sure when to call one? Read when to call an energy advisor: what an evaluation and a heat load calculation are, and which rebates ask for them first.

Guides by province: British Columbia, Ontario, Nova Scotia, Newfoundland and Labrador, and how to find an advisor anywhere in Canada.
