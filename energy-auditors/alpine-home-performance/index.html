What a home energy auditor does

An auditor tests your house and writes a report that ranks the fixes. These are the standard services. Ask this company which ones it offers.

Home energy audit

Looks at insulation, windows, heating and cooling equipment, and finds where the house wastes energy.

You get: a report that puts the fixes in order.

Blower door test

A fan pulls air through the house to measure how leaky it is.

You get: a measure of how airtight the house is.

HERS rating

RESNET lists HERS rating companies. A HERS rating scores how efficient a home is.

You get: a rating you can compare between homes.

Questions people ask

Do I need a home energy auditor?

Not for every project. Start with your utility, since some offer their own assessment at low or no cost, then decide whether you need an independent rater.

What is a HERS rating company?

RESNET lists the companies that can give a HERS rating. See the RESNET list.

What does it cost?

Prices vary by company and home. Ask for the price of every visit up front, and whether the report counts for the rebate program you plan to use.
