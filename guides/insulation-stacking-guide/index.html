BC Insulation Rebates: Stacking Guide 2026

How to combine federal, provincial, and utility insulation rebates for up to $5,500+. Which ones stack? Which don't? How to claim them all.

The Stacking Opportunity

Insulation is the most cost-effective upgrade, and it has the best rebate stacking.

You can claim:

  • BC CleanBC insulation rebates (provincial) — up to $5,500
  • BC Hydro insulation bonus (utility) — up to $900
  • FortisBC insulation rebates (utility) — up to $2,000
  • Income-qualified bonuses — extra $500–$1,500

Example: $4,000 attic insulation project → Claim $3,500 in rebates → Net cost: $500

What Counts as "Insulation" for Rebates?

Type Max Rebate What Qualifies
Attic $2,500 (CleanBC) Any increase to R-50 or higher. $500 per R-value increase.
Walls $1,500 (CleanBC) Interior or exterior. Blown-in or rigid foam.
Basement/Crawlspace $1,500 (CleanBC) Rim joist, rim band, band board insulation.
Combined (all three) $5,500 (CleanBC) Claim all three in one year. Total cap is $5,500.

Stacking Rules (What Combines, What Doesn't)

✓ THESE STACK (Combine them)

CleanBC + BC Hydro
Both can be claimed on the same project. They're separate programs.

CleanBC + FortisBC
Both can be claimed if you have gas service from FortisBC. Different programs, different requirements.

Attic + Walls + Basement (all in one year)
All three can be done and claimed together. Total cap: $5,500 from CleanBC.

Income-qualified bonuses
If you earn under ~$75K household income, add $500–$1,500 extra to each program.

✗ THESE DON'T STACK (Choose one)

Two BC Hydro rebates
You can only claim BC Hydro insulation rebates once per address per year. If you do attic insulation, you can't claim a separate basement rebate from BC Hydro (but you can from FortisBC).

Two FortisBC rebates
Same rule: one FortisBC rebate per address per year. Choose your project wisely.

Real Example: Maximum Stack

Project: Attic + walls + basement in one year

Total project cost: $8,000

Rebates claimed:

  • CleanBC (all three): $5,500
  • BC Hydro (attic bonus): $900
  • FortisBC (basement): $500
  • Income-qualified bonus (if under $75K): +$1,000

Total rebates: $7,900

Your out-of-pocket: $100

This is the absolute maximum. Most homes don't do all three sections in one year.

Important: HPCN Contractor Required

All three programs (CleanBC, BC Hydro, FortisBC) require HPCN-certified contractors.

DIY insulation doesn't qualify for ANY rebates.

This is non-negotiable. The contractor fee (~15–20% of project cost) is worth it because the rebates cover it.

Example: $4,000 insulation job with contractor fee = ~$4,800 total. After $3,500 in rebates, you pay $1,300. Net savings from lower heating bill: $400–$600/year. Payback: 2–3 years.

If You Can Only Do One: Priority Order

1. Attic insulation first — Best payback, biggest rebate, highest priority for energy savings

2. Basement/rim joist second — Heat loss, but smaller than attic. Still worth doing.

3. Wall insulation last — Most expensive, hardest to do, smallest impact per dollar spent

Pro tip: If your budget is $4,000, do attic-only. If $8,000+, do attic + basement. Skip walls unless you're replacing siding anyway.

Income-Qualified Bonuses

If your household income is under ~$75,000, you get extra rebates:

Program Standard Income-Qualified Extra Bonus
CleanBC Up to $5,500 Up to $7,000 +$1,500
BC Hydro $900 $1,200 +$300
FortisBC $500 $2,000 +$1,500

How to qualify: You self-certify on the application. No income verification needed. Completely confidential.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Waiting to apply after 90 days. The rebate window closes. Submit immediately.
  • ❌ Forgetting to keep copies of everything. Invoice, photos, rebate confirmations — keep them for 7 years (CRA audit).
  • ❌ Not getting a pre-upgrade assessment. CleanBC requires it. Your contractor arranges it; it's free.
  • ❌ Doing DIY thinking you'll apply anyway. You won't qualify. HPCN contractor is required.
  • ❌ Mixing up the three programs. Each has different timelines, requirements, amounts. Read carefully.
