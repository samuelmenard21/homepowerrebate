How We Choose Trusted Installers

Here's exactly what it takes to join HomePowerRebate's network. We're transparent so you know who you're working with.

The Problem We Solve

Not all certified installers are created equal.

You can find a list of HPCN-certified heat pump installers anywhere. But certification doesn't guarantee trustworthiness. Some certified installers pressure customers into unnecessary upgrades, disappear after installation, or don't follow through on rebate paperwork.

We vet for the stuff that actually matters.

Universal Requirements (All Regions)

Every installer on HomePowerRebate meets these standards:

1. Case Studies Required

We require 2+ real completed jobs with before/after data. You can see what they've actually built, not just promises. Each case study shows: project type, timeline, costs, rebates received, and customer satisfaction.

2. Insurance & Bonding

Proof of liability insurance ($1M+ coverage). Proof of workers' comp insurance (if applicable). We verify this every year.

3. Response Time SLA

Quote within 48 hours of request. No ghosting customers. Clear communication throughout the process.

4. Standard Proposal Format

All installers use a consistent proposal template. Heat pump, solar, and battery installations are itemized identically. This lets you compare apples-to-apples, not apples-to-oranges.

5. No Sales Pressure Commitment

No surprise upgrades mid-project. No aggressive follow-up sales calls. No gatekeeping information to force decisions. We receive complaints and we'll boot installers who violate this.

6. Expertise Tags

Each installer clearly lists what they do and don't do:
✓ Heat pumps | ✓ Solar | ✓ Battery
✓ New construction | ✓ Retrofits | ✗ Commercial
No "we do everything" generalist claims.

BC-Specific Requirements

On top of universal standards, every BC installer has:

  • HPCN Certification — Heat Pump Certification Network credential required. Updated annually.
  • BC Hydro Program Knowledge — Trained on heat pump rebate eligibility and application. Familiar with Peak Saver demand response program.
  • FortisBC Knowledge — For northern/interior BC regions, FortisBC program knowledge required. Understand differences between BC Hydro and FortisBC incentives.
  • Island vs. Mainland Considerations — For island installers: knowledge of grid limitations and mini-split efficiency requirements.

How We Enforce This

We're not just checking a box once.

  • Annual re-vetting. Every installer goes through criteria verification yearly.
  • Customer complaints. If we get consistent complaints, we investigate. Violation = removal.
  • Case study audits. We spot-check references to make sure case studies are real.
  • Program updates. When utility programs change, we re-train installers.

What Gets You Booted

We remove installers who:

  • Disappear mid-project or post-installation
  • Violate the "no sales pressure" rule (we hear about it, they're out)

  • Miss response time SLAs consistently
  • Fail insurance/bonding verification
