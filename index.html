What changed

Rebates are opening and closing

Closing soon: BC Hydro heat pump bonus ends 2026-10-31. Details

Every item is checked against the program's own page. Last checked 2026-10-01.

  • Coming up
    Nova Scotia Heating Assistance Rebate (HARP) Nova Scotia · 2026-10-01

    HARP helps lower-income households with winter heating bills. It paid $400 last winter and has opened on October 1 in past years. Confirm the 2026-27 date and amount with the Province.

  • Coming up
    BC Hydro heat pump bonus British Columbia · 2026-10-31

    The extra bonus of up to $1,000 on BC Hydro heat pump rebates only covers installs completed by October 31, 2026.

  • Paused
    Calgary Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) Alberta · 2026-09

    Calgary's CEIP financing (up to $50,000 repaid on your property tax) is closed to new applications until winter 2026/2027.

  • Paused
    Lethbridge and St. Albert CEIP Alberta · 2026-09

    Lethbridge's CEIP is at capacity and closed to new pre-qualification. St. Albert is taking waitlist applications only.

  • Ended
    Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (Alberta) Alberta · 2026-07-31

    The federal grant of up to $10,000 to switch from oil to a heat pump closed to Albertans on July 31, 2026.

  • Cut
    CleanBC Energy Savings Program (income-qualified) British Columbia · 2026-07-06

    New rules for invoices from July 6, 2026: heat pump rebates for switching from gas, oil or propane are now $13,000, $7,000 or $3,500 by income level, plus a $3,000 northern top-up for levels 1 and 2.

  • Ended
    Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (Nova Scotia) Nova Scotia · 2026-07-02

    Closed to new applicants on July 2, 2026 after funding ran out. A standby list is reviewed only if money frees up.
