What changed lately

Programs open, change and close all year. We check each one against its own page. Last full check: Oct 1, 2026.

  • Coming up
    Nova Scotia Heating Assistance Rebate (HARP)Nova Scotia · 2026-10-01

    HARP helps lower-income households with winter heating bills. It paid $400 last winter and has opened on October 1 in past years. Confirm the 2026-27 date and amount with the Province.

  • Coming up
    BC Hydro heat pump bonusBritish Columbia · 2026-10-31

    The extra bonus of up to $1,000 on BC Hydro heat pump rebates only covers installs completed by October 31, 2026.

  • New
    National Heat Pump RebateCanada · 2026-10-08

    Ottawa announced a $2 billion rebate for electric heat pumps: up to $10,000 for households below the median income and $2,000 for other eligible homeowners. Launch is expected in early 2027. Income line, eligible homes and retroactivity are not published yet.

  • Paused
    Calgary Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP)Alberta · 2026-09

    Calgary's CEIP financing (up to $50,000 repaid on your property tax) is closed to new applications until winter 2026/2027.

  • Paused
    Lethbridge and St. Albert CEIPAlberta · 2026-09

    Lethbridge's CEIP is at capacity and closed to new pre-qualification. St. Albert is taking waitlist applications only.

  • Ended
    Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (Alberta)Alberta · 2026-07-31

    The federal grant of up to $10,000 to switch from oil to a heat pump closed to Albertans on July 31, 2026.

  • Cut
    CleanBC Energy Savings Program (income-qualified)British Columbia · 2026-07-06

    New rules for invoices from July 6, 2026: heat pump rebates for switching from gas, oil or propane are now $13,000, $7,000 or $3,500 by income level, plus a $3,000 northern top-up for levels 1 and 2.

Questions people ask us

Is HomePowerRebate free?

Yes. It is free for homeowners. Installers do not pay to be listed or to rank higher, and we do not charge referral fees right now.

Are you part of the government or a utility?

No. We are independent. We read each program's official page and link to it next to every amount, so you can check it yourself.

How do you know the amounts are right?

We keep 651 rebate amounts and rules, each with its source page and the date we read it, and we re-read them every month. When a program closes or pauses, we mark it that way.

Can I get Canada's new federal heat pump rebate now?

Not yet. Ottawa plans up to $10,000 for households below the median income and $2,000 for other eligible homeowners, starting in early 2027. The income line is not published yet. You can sign up above to get an email when it opens.

Is there still a US federal tax credit for heat pumps or solar?

No. The 25C and 25D tax credits ended on December 31, 2025, and only cover work finished before then. State, city and utility rebates are still open in many places.

Which places do you cover?

161 cities in 23 provinces and states. In Canada: Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Nova Scotia, Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, New Brunswick, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Quebec, Yukon and Northwest Territories. In the US: California, Massachusetts, New York, Pennsylvania, Colorado, Vermont, Michigan, New Jersey, Illinois, Washington and Minnesota.

How do you rank installers?

By Google rating and number of reviews, so lots of good reviews count more than a few. Companies cannot pay to move up or to hide reviews.
