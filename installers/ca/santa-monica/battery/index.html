Which battery should you ask about?

Most home installers work with a few brands. Compare usable storage (kWh), how much power it can deliver at once (kW), whether it works in an unheated garage, and the warranty. Our side-by-side guides cover the Tesla Powerwall 3, Enphase IQ Battery 5P, Eguana Evolve and FranklinWH aPower 2. Compare home batteries.

Before you hire a home battery installer

  1. Ask for the usable storage (kWh) and the continuous power (kW), and which circuits it will keep running in an outage.
  2. Ask whether the battery is on your rebate program's approved list. Brands not on the list get no rebate.
  3. Ask where it will go. Some batteries lose capacity or stop charging in a cold garage.
  4. Ask about the warranty: years, and how much capacity it keeps by the end.
