What Makes an Installer HPCN-Certified?

Every installer in this directory has:

HPCN Membership

Active membership in the Home Performance Contractor Network, BC's leading trade association for energy retrofit professionals.

Google Verification

Live Google Business Profile with 4+ star rating and at least 10 reviews from verified customers.

Service Coverage

Proven work in heat pump, solar, battery storage, or other BC rebate-eligible services in their local market.

Insurance & Licensing

We verify current liability insurance and relevant electrical/gas licensing before listing.
