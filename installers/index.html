How We Screen This List

What Gets an Installer Onto This Directory?

Every installer here passed this automated screen — not a personal vetting call, and we say so plainly:

Google Verification

Live Google Business Profile with a 4+ star rating and at least 10 reviews from verified customers.

Real Trade Match

Filtered to actual heat pump, HVAC, or solar businesses — adjacent trades (duct cleaning, general handyman, supply-only) are excluded by name and category.

Correct City

Assigned to the city in their actual business address, not just wherever they showed up in a search.

BC: HPCN Program

In BC specifically, HPCN (Home Performance Contractor Network) membership is what CleanBC actually requires for the rebate to apply — confirm it directly with the installer, we don't verify it automatically. Ontario's HRSP program has no equivalent membership requirement.

We have not personally called, insured-checked, or licence-verified any installer on this list — the criteria above are all automated (rating, review count, trade-type match). Always confirm current licensing, insurance, and (for BC) HPCN status directly with any installer before signing a contract. See our full vetting checklist →
