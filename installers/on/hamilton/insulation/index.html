Before you hire a insulation installer

  1. Ask whether they will air-seal before insulating, and what they will seal (attic hatch, pot lights, pipes, top plates).
  2. Ask for the R-value before and after for each area, and the material and depth they will install.
  3. Ask whether your rebate needs an energy assessment before and after the work, and who books it.
  4. Ask how they handle ventilation and any old insulation that could contain vermiculite (asbestos risk).
