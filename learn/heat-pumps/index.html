Heat pumps, explained

A heat pump heats your home in winter and cools it in summer. Here is what to check before you buy, and what each open program pays.

By Sam Menard · Rebate amounts checked October 21, 2026

Quick quiz

Myth or true? The 5-question quiz

Tap an answer to see why.

1. BC Hydro's whole-home heat pump rebate asks the heat pump to meet 100% of your heating at what temperature?

-5°C, according to the BC Hydro rebate page.

2. True or false: a bigger heat pump always gets a bigger rebate.

False. Programs pay by rules and rated capacity, and oversized units can cost more without helping.

3. A BC home with a gas furnace applies for the BC Hydro heat pump rebate. What happens?

BC Hydro's rebate is for replacing electric heat.

4. In Ontario's heat pump stream, when do you need pre-approval?

Before. Pre-approval must be written and in hand before the work starts.

5. Can a heat pump also cool your home?

Yes. It reverses to move heat out of the home in summer.

A heat pump moves heat instead of burning fuel to make it. In winter it pulls heat from outside air and brings it indoors. In summer it runs in reverse, like an air conditioner. That is why one machine can do both jobs.

Cold-climate models are built for cold winters, and most rebate programs ask for them. Many programs also care about three things you can check early: what heats your home today, which installer does the work, and which model goes on the invoice. The table below shows what each open program pays, but the rules behind each amount are different, so read the official page before you sign.

What to check before you buy

  • What heats your home now. Some programs only pay when you replace electric heat. Others pay more for oil, propane or wood than for gas.
  • The installer. Some rebates need a program-registered contractor, and some need approval in writing before work starts. Ask first.
  • The exact model. Programs pay for models on their own lists. Get the model numbers on the quote and check them yourself.
  • The size. Programs size by how much heat your home loses, not by the size of your old furnace. Ask to see the math.
  • The deadline. Some programs give you months after the invoice. Others close on a date. Check the date on the official page.

Common questions

Do heat pumps work in cold weather?

Cold-climate models are made for it, and rebate programs ask for them. Ask your installer to show you the numbers for your coldest days.

Can I stack rebates?

Sometimes. Programs set their own rules, and some limit the total. Our calculator adds up only the open programs that apply to the upgrade you pick.

Do I need to keep my old furnace?

It depends on your home and the program. Ask your installer what they advise, and check the program page for any rule about backup heat.

Where do I find a good installer?

Start with our ranked lists for your city. They are ranked by Google reviews, and where a program publishes a registered contractor list we show a badge.
