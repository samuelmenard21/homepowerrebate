## Post 1: federal alert (Mon)

Ottawa says its new heat pump rebate is up to $10,000 for households below the median income and $2,000 for everyone else. Applications open in early 2027.

What Ottawa has not said yet: where the income line is, which homes qualify, or the exact start date. A lot of sites are guessing. We are not.

We built a free alert. You get one email the day applications open, and one when Ottawa publishes who gets the $10,000.

https://homepowerrebate.com/heat-pump-rebate-alert/?src=linkedin

## Post 3: a rebate change (Fri)

Newfoundland and Labrador raised its Home Heating Supplement. It is now $400 to $1,000 (it used to be $200 to $500). Applications close Jan 15, 2027.

We check every program against the official page and date it, so you can see what changed and when.

https://homepowerrebate.com/ca/nl/heating-assistance/?src=linkedin
