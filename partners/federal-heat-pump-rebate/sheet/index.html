The federal heat pump rebate: what we know

Information checked October 9, 2026. Applications are not open yet.

Prepared for you by

Up to $10,000Households below the median income
$2,000Other eligible homeowners

What Ottawa has said

  • Up to $10,000 for households below the median income, and $2,000 for other eligible homeowners.
  • Applications are expected to open in early 2027. They are not open yet.
  • One rebate per person, and the heat pump must be on the Eligible Products List.
  • It can be combined with provincial programs. Each program has its own rules.

Ottawa's plan, as reported by CBC from the government document: apply in an online portal, get approval within 24 hours, buy and install an approved heat pump, upload receipts, payment within five days. No energy audit before or after.

What Ottawa has not said yet

  • The income line that decides $10,000 or $2,000
  • Which home types and heating fuels qualify, including natural gas
  • The exact launch date, and whether purchases made before launch count

What to do now

  • Find out what heats your home today and what your heat pump options are.
  • If your system fails before launch, keep every invoice. Ottawa has not said whether earlier purchases count, so do not count on the rebate.
  • Get the launch alert and check your home at homepowerrebate.com/programs/national-heat-pump-rebate/do-i-qualify/

Independent information from HomePowerRebate (homepowerrebate.com/programs/national-heat-pump-rebate/). This is not from the Government of Canada: check canada.ca for official news. Amounts are the most a program pays; your amount depends on your home, utility and income.
