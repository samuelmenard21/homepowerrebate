Straight answers

What installers ask us first.

Is the territory really exclusive?

Yes. One installer per city, per service. While you're our partner for Kelowna solar + battery, every Kelowna solar + battery lead goes to you and only you. We don't run auctions and we never will — that promise is the whole reason homeowners trust us.

Why $300/month instead of per-lead pricing?

Simpler. Fairer. You know your cost upfront. We're betting on quality, not volume. If leads aren't converting for you, that's feedback we take seriously. Per-lead models incentivize us to send any warm body; flat monthly incentivizes us to send only the right fit.

How good are the leads?

Every homeowner has run our calculator, seen their personalized rebate amount and monthly bill impact, and chosen to be matched with you. They give a real name, phone, city, home type, heating fuel, and their full rebate plan. They are not idle browsers — they raised their hand, educated themselves, and chose your city.

Do I have to install a specific battery?

We steer homeowners toward BC Hydro-approved systems (we favour the Eguana Evolve for its cold-climate performance and guaranteed rebate eligibility) because a rebate that falls through kills the deal for both of us. If you have a preferred approved product, let's talk — the priority is that the homeowner qualifies.

Do I need to be HPCN certified?

BC Hydro requires Home Performance Contractor Network certification for these rebates as of June 1, 2026. To be a partner you need to be HPCN certified (or actively enrolling). We can point you to the process.

Do you connect our Google My Business?

Yes. If you have one, we link it to your profile so homeowners see your real rating, reviews, and location. If you don't have a GMB yet, we show you how to set one up. Your reviews and rating are your best sales tool — we make sure homeowners see them before they call.

What about videos and photos?

We feature them. Send us before/after photos of your best projects, and ideally a 30–60 second intro video (optional). We post them on your profile, in our email newsletter, and on our social channels. This is thousands of dollars worth of marketing exposure if you were paying for it on Facebook or Google Ads.

How many leads will I get?

We're early. Honest answer: leads grow as our traffic grows. But here's the deal — first month is free so you can prove it works yourself. And the marketing features are guaranteed day one: you're featured on our homepage, sent to 2,000+ email subscribers, spotlighted on social, and positioned for quarterly blog case studies. That marketing alone is worth thousands if you were buying ads.
