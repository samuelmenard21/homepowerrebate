Design Specs for Canva/Figma

Type 1: Blog Post

  • Size: 1000×1500px
  • Top 30%: Blog image (auto-pulled)
  • Middle 50%: Headline text (22pt, bold) + stat (16pt)
  • Bottom 20%: Logo + "Read full guide"
  • Colors: Amber accent, dark text on cream bg
  • Font: Fraunces (title), Inter (body)

Type 2: Tips/Hacks

  • Size: 1000×1500px
  • Background: Gradient (dark teal)
  • Headline: 60pt, all caps, white, bold
  • Icon: Large emoji (48pt, centered)
  • Subtitle: 18pt white, secondary info
  • Style: High contrast, minimal text

Type 3: City Guide

  • Size: 1000×1500px
  • City Name: 80pt, bold, centered
  • Rebate Amounts: $4K–$16K Heat Pump, $5K Solar, etc.
  • Icons: Emoji (💡, ☀️, 🔋)
  • CTA Button: Amber, "See All Rebates for [City]"
  • Background: Cream gradient
