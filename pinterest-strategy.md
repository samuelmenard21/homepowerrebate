## Pin Design Specs

### Type 1: Blog Post Pins (40% of posts)

**Dimensions:** 1000 × 1500px (standard vertical Pinterest)
**Layout:**
- Top 30%: Blog featured image (scaled)
- Middle 50%: Headline text + key stat
- Bottom 20%: "HomePowerRebate" logo + "Read full guide"

**Text Examples:**
- "BC Heat Pump Rebates 2026: Get $4K–$16K Back"
- "Tesla Powerwall Doesn't Qualify for BC Hydro ($5K Impact)"
- "CleanBC Income Qualified: 100% Free Heat Pump"

**Colors:** 
- Accent: #d4751c (amber)
- Text: #0a2a2e (ink, dark)
- Background: #faf7f2 (paper)

### Type 2: Tips/Hacks Pins (35% of posts)

**Dimensions:** 1000 × 1500px
**Layout:**
- Bold headline (60pt, all caps)
- Icon or callout (centered)
- Subtitle (secondary info)
- "homepowerrebate.com" footer

**Text Examples:**
- "5 Heat Pump Rebates BC Homeowners Forget"
- "Peak Saver Battery: $5K vs $1.5K (This One Decision)"
- "Installer Red Flags: Payment Before Install = STOP"

**Design:** Bold, high-contrast, minimal text

### Type 3: City Guide Pins (25% of posts)

**Dimensions:** 1000 × 1500px
**Layout:**
- Top: City name (bold, 80pt)
- Middle: Key rebate amounts ($4K–$16K Heat Pump, $5K Solar, etc.)
- Bottom: CTA ("See all rebates for [City]") + logo

**Text Examples:**
- "Vancouver Homeowners: Get $10K in Rebates (Heat Pump + Solar)"

**Design:** Clean, scannable, city-focused

## Posting Schedule (Optimal for 500+ clicks/month)

**Frequency:** 3-4 pins/day (21-28 pins/week)
**Best Times (US Pacific, adjust for BC timezone):**
- Morning: 6-9 AM (early planners)
- Midday: 12-1 PM (lunch break scrollers)
- Evening: 6-8 PM (after-work research)

**Weekly Schedule:**
- Monday-Friday: 4 pins/day (20 pins/week)
- Saturday: 2 pins/day (weekday content planning)
- Sunday: 2 pins/day (weekend homeowner research)
- **Total: 24 pins/week = ~100 pins/month**

**Life Expectancy:**
- Each pin lives 3-6 months actively
- Average engagement: 20-30 clicks per pin over lifetime
- 100 pins/month × 25 clicks avg = 2,500 clicks/month potential
- Conservative: 500+ clicks/month target (20% engagement rate)

## Link Strategy

### Pin Linking Rules

**Type 1: Blog Post Pins**
- Link to: Full blog post URL
- Example: `https://homepowerrebate.com/blog/bc-hydro-peak-saver-battery-rebate-5000-vs-1500.html`
- UTM tracking: `?source=pinterest&medium=pin&campaign=peak-saver`

**Type 2: Tips/Hacks Pins**
- Link to: Relevant blog post OR city page
- Example: Tips pin → `/blog/not-all-installers-trustworthy.html`
- Example: Peak Saver pin → `/blog/bc-hydro-peak-saver-battery-rebate-5000-vs-1500.html`

**Type 3: City Guide Pins**
- Link to: City landing page
- Example: `https://homepowerrebate.com/ca/bc/vancouver/`
- UTM tracking: `?source=pinterest&medium=pin&campaign=city-[city]`

**All links include UTM params for analytics:**
- `?source=pinterest&medium=pin&campaign=[topic]`
