Frequently asked questions

Which city has the better overall PowerScore, Vancouver or Coquitlam?

Vancouver has the higher overall PowerScore at 89.7/100, compared to Coquitlam's 79.5/100. PowerScore is a 0-100 average of 8 rebate categories built from real published rebate amounts, program status, and how many programs stack.

Coquitlam scores higher on solar, 92.5 vs 88.8 out of 100. Vancouver's strongest category overall is heat pump at 92.5, while Coquitlam's strongest is heat pump at 92.5.
