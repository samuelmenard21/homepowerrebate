    What the numbers show

    Three things we learned scoring 149 cities

    Most money on the table

    US$40,000

    Burlington, Vermont has more open rebate money than any of the 149 cities: up to US$40,000 across 7 upgrades, more than New York City or Vancouver. It has its own city-owned power company, Burlington Electric, which runs its own heat pump and EV charger rebates.

    Minneapolis vs Duluth

    21× more

    In Minnesota, homes in Minneapolis can find up to US$29,000 in open rebates. In Duluth, it is US$1,350. Same state. The local utility and city programs make the difference.

    Best of Alberta

    CA$50

    Calgary is the best-scoring city in Alberta, and the only rebate open there today is CA$50 for a smart thermostat. Most Alberta programs are closed or limited.

    Method

    How PowerScore works

    Each city gets a score from 0 to 100 for each of 8 upgrades. The overall PowerScore is the plain average of those 8. Every number comes from the rebate amounts on our own city pages, which we check against official program pages.

    60% money25% open15%
    • 60% Money. The top rebate for that upgrade, in US dollars, compared with a common yardstick: what the best-funded tenth of cities offer. Matching the yardstick earns full marks.
    • 25% Open today. Open programs get full credit. Funding limited or unclear programs get half. Closed or missing programs get zero.
    • 15% Stacking. How many separate programs apply (federal, provincial or state, utility, city). More layers score higher, up to four.
    How reliable is this ranking?
    • One yardstick for every city. A city's money score compares its top rebate with what the best-funded tenth of cities offer, not with the best city in its own province or state.
    • Currency is approximate. Canadian amounts are converted at 0.73 US dollars per Canadian dollar, only to put both countries on one scale.
    • Open rebate money is not a promise. It adds the top open rebate in each upgrade. Most homes will not qualify for all of them, and some programs cannot be combined.
    • Ties are real. Only 66 different overall scores exist across 149 cities, because most rebate programs cover a whole province or state.
    • Not every region has been verified. Regions marked "Not yet" (none) have not had each program checked against its official page.
    • Data last rebuilt October 16, 2026. Amounts change. The rebate tracker lists what has ended or moved.

    About the energy bill estimate

    The city breakdown shows an estimated yearly electricity and heating fuel cost for a 2,500 sq ft home, based on utility rate schedules and typical regional usage. It is not a quoted bill and plays no part in the PowerScore. Nova Scotia and Massachusetts show a range because many homes there heat with oil, which costs more. Figures marked with an asterisk (*) use a neighbouring utility's rates where we could not find a local one.
