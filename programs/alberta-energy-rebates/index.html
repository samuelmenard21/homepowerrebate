Alberta Energy Rebates 2026: What's Really Available

Every amount links to the official program. Last checked October 1, 2026.

By Sam Menard

Short answer: Alberta has few home energy rebates in 2026. We found no current ENMAX, ATCO, EPCOR or FortisAlberta heat pump or solar rebate on any official site. What exists: Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) financing repaid on your property tax (Calgary's intake reopens winter 2026/27), a $50 smart thermostat rebate in Red Deer, EPCOR's Peak Rewards pilot, and free upgrades for income-qualified Calgarians.

How to apply

  1. Check whether your town offers CEIP and when intake opens.
  2. If a quote mentions a 'utility rebate', ask the installer for the official program link before you count on it.
  3. In Red Deer, keep your thermostat receipt and apply in the same calendar year.

Recent changes

  • 2026-09: Calgary Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP). Calgary's CEIP financing (up to $50,000 repaid on your property tax) is closed to new applications until winter 2026/2027. Source
  • 2026-09: Lethbridge and St. Albert CEIP. Lethbridge's CEIP is at capacity and closed to new pre-qualification. St. Albert is taking waitlist applications only. Source
  • 2026-07-31: Federal Oil to Heat Pump Affordability (Alberta). The federal grant of up to $10,000 to switch from oil to a heat pump closed to Albertans on July 31, 2026. Source

Common questions

Does ENMAX have a heat pump rebate?

Not that we could find. As of our last check, no official ENMAX, ATCO, EPCOR or FortisAlberta page offers a heat pump or solar rebate. Ask for the program link if a quote lists one.

Is there a smart thermostat rebate in Alberta?

Only locally: Red Deer pays $50, and EPCOR's Peak Rewards pilot pays $50 plus $25 a season in select Edmonton neighbourhoods.

Can I still get the Oil to Heat Pump grant in Alberta?

No. The last day for Alberta residents to apply was July 31, 2026.
