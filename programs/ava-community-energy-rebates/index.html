Ava Community Energy Rebates in 2026

Ava Community Energy supplies electricity to 16 cities and unincorporated Alameda and San Joaquin Counties, but it is not the company that delivers it, and most big home rebates in the area come from other programs. This page says what Ava does offer, what we could not confirm and where to look for the rest.

What Ava offers in 2026

We read Ava's pages on October 5, 2026. Ava's pages describe programs but, for heat pump water heaters, publish no dollar amount, so we show none.

Rate discounts and bill help

Ava lists rate discounts for qualified customers, help with overdue bills and an auto-enrolled solar discount. Its default plan is stated to be 0.5% lower than PG&E, with 100% renewable plans also offered.

Battery storage and induction

The City of Oakland lists Ava rebates for battery storage and induction stoves. We did not read the amounts, so check Ava's incentive finder.

What Ava is

Ava is a community choice aggregator. It buys the electricity for its customers, while PG&E still owns the wires and sends the bill. That split explains why you may see an Ava rebate, a PG&E rebate and a BayREN rebate for the same house. Each has its own application and its own rules, and none of them is a single program.

Where heat pump money comes from

We found no Ava cash rebate for a heat pump. Money for heat pumps in the area is more likely to come through BayREN, PG&E programs and state programs, which are covered on our PG&E page and the Bay Area city pages. Ava's heat pump water heater page says homes are eligible for rebates but gives no amount and points to its incentive finder, so run that tool with your address.

The incentive finder

Ava runs an online finder that returns federal, state and local incentives and tailors them to income, location and whether you own or rent. It is the right first step because the right answer depends on your household. Treat any amount it shows as something to confirm on the program's own page before you sign a contract.

Weatherization comes first

BayREN's EASE Home pays 80% of weatherization costs, which can cut the size of the system you need. Because a tighter house needs less equipment, many households sequence the work: sealing and insulation first, then the heat pump. Ask your contractor to quote both ways so you can compare the total.

Solar and batteries

Ava's pages cover residential solar, battery storage and net energy metering. New solar customers in PG&E territory are on the Net Billing Tariff, which pays far less for exports than the old net metering, so a battery matters more than it once did. Check Ava's page for any battery rebate and its rules.

How to claim help in Ava territory

  1. Check your bill for the Ava name to confirm you are an Ava customer.
  2. Run Ava's incentive finder with your address, income band and whether you own or rent.
  3. Check BayREN's EASE Home if you plan weatherization.
  4. Ask PG&E programs about heat pump rebates and income-qualified help.
  5. Confirm every amount on the program's own page before you sign.

Common questions

What is Ava Community Energy?

A community choice aggregator that supplies electricity to 16 cities and unincorporated Alameda and San Joaquin Counties. PG&E still delivers it.

Does Ava pay a heat pump rebate?

We found no Ava cash rebate for heat pumps. Its heat pump water heater page gives no amount and points to its incentive finder.

Is Ava cheaper than PG&E?

Ava states its default plan is 0.5% lower than PG&E. Compare your own bill before assuming a saving.

What is BayREN's EASE Home?

A program that pays 80% of weatherization costs and up to $2,000 for knob-and-tube wiring. It is not run by Ava.

Is the federal solar credit available?

No. The 25D credit ended for installs after December 31, 2025.
