Consumers Energy Home Rebates

Consumers Energy supplies gas and electricity to many Michigan homes, including most of Grand Rapids, and gas for many homes in Lansing. Its Home Solutions program pays for high efficiency furnaces, insulation, windows and a few heat pump cases, and it offers a free home energy visit. We read its pages and its 2026 chart on October 6, 2026.

What Consumers Energy offers in 2026

Each card names the fact we keep on file. Consumers says its 2026 chart is valid for installs from January 1 to December 31, 2026, that only participating contractors can file, and that funds are first come, first served.

Who Consumers serves

Consumers Energy supplies electricity, natural gas or both across much of Michigan outside southeast Michigan. We cover it for Grand Rapids and for Lansing's gas. Your bill shows the company name. Lansing's electric company is the Board of Water & Light, which has its own rebates, and southeast Michigan is mostly DTE territory.

Rules in the 2026 chart

Consumers' chart says rebates are for separately metered single-family homes or buildings with fewer than three units. New construction and commercial buildings are not eligible. The application must be filed by a participating contractor within 30 days of finishing. Consumers also says details can change or end without notice, so read the chart on the day you sign.

Heat pump rules are narrow

The Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020 to 2024) says utility gas heats 82.5% of Grand Rapids homes and 74.6% of Lansing homes. Consumers' heat pump rebates do not fit a gas home switching to a heat pump, since the chart lists them as replacements for an existing heat pump or electric heat. Gas homes are better served by the furnace and insulation rebates.

Two documents, two thermostat amounts

Consumers' appliance rebate page lists a $75 Wi-Fi thermostat rebate, and its 2026 chart lists $50, or $100 for homes with both gas and electric service. We show the range and mark it as needing a check, rather than pick one.

Free help

The free home energy analysis has no income test listed. For free work, Michigan's weatherization program covers households at or below 200% of the federal poverty level through local agencies.

How to claim Consumers Energy rebates

  1. Confirm Consumers is your company for the service you are claiming.
  2. Pick a contractor from Consumers' participating list.
  3. Ask what the contractor will file and when. The deadline is 30 days after finishing.
  4. Keep a copy of the application and the invoice.
  5. Expect a check in six to eight weeks.

Common questions

Does Consumers Energy pay for a heat pump?

A little. $200 to $350, but air source and ground source are for replacing an existing heat pump, and a mini-split must replace electric heat.

How much does Consumers pay for a furnace?

$100 for 95% to 96.99% efficiency and $300 for 97% or better.

Is the home energy analysis free?

Yes. Consumers' page says it is free, with no income test listed.

Who files the application?

A participating Consumers contractor, within 30 days of finishing.

Is there a federal tax credit?

No. It ended for work finished after December 31, 2025.
