Efficiency Vermont Home Rebates

Efficiency Vermont is the statewide program, and your electric utility decides how much extra you get. We re-read its pages on October 3, 2026. Weatherization now pays up to $3,000, or $7,000 for income-eligible homes, which is lower than the figure in its January announcement.

What Efficiency Vermont pays in 2026

Most amounts are instant discounts taken at purchase through a participating contractor or retailer.

Open

Ductless heat pump

$375 to $475, plus up to $600

$375 for up to 2 tons and $475 over 2 tons, effective January 1, 2026. Integrated controls add up to $600. Ducted systems pay $1,200 up to 2 tons, $1,700 over 2 and under 4 tons and $2,200 at 4 tons or more.

Open

Income bonus on heat pumps

$200 to $2,200

Green Mountain Power low-income customers receive $2,200, VPPSA municipal utility customers $1,000 and all others $200. Rental owners and Burlington Electric customers are not eligible for this bonus.

Open

Heat pump water heater

$600, up to $1,000

$400 more for income-eligible households. Effective August 16, 2025.

Open

Home Performance with ENERGY STAR

Up to $3,000 ($7,000 income-eligible)

Covers 75% of insulation and air-sealing cost. Needs a participating contractor and at least a 10% blower-door improvement. Effective July 1, 2026, while funding lasts.

Open

Ground-source heat pump

Up to $2,100 per ton

Plus $500 for income-eligible Vermonters. New systems up to 10 tons.

Open

Thermostats and appliances

$25 to $400

Smart thermostat up to $100, dryers up to $400, window air conditioners $150, wood or pellet stoves $400 and dehumidifiers from $25.

Burlington Electric and Vermont Gas customers are paid by their own utility for weatherization, up to $9,500 for a single-family home.

Why the weatherization figure fell

The January 2026 announcement quoted weatherization rebates of up to $4,000 and $9,500. Efficiency Vermont's page now lists $3,000 and $7,000 for offers effective July 1, 2026. The $9,500 figure survives only for Burlington Electric and Vermont Gas customers. We use the page, not the announcement, because the page is what a contractor submits against. If a contractor quotes $9,500 and you are a Green Mountain Power customer, ask for the program page to be shown.

How your utility changes the number

The same heat pump earns a different total depending on who sends your electric bill. A Green Mountain Power low-income customer collects the $2,200 bonus. A customer of a VPPSA municipal utility gets $1,000 at moderate or low income, and everyone else gets $200. Burlington Electric customers sit outside the bonus and instead use Burlington Electric's own post-purchase rebates of up to $2,900 on ductless and $8,750 on ducted systems.

Other utilities add smaller offers: Stowe Electric $250, Vermont Electric Co-op $150, and VPPSA municipalities $200 on weatherization and water heaters.

Ducted or ductless

Ductless mini-splits are cheaper and simpler in a home with no ductwork. Ducted systems pay more because they serve the whole house. Size drives the ducted amount, so a large home with a 4-ton system reaches $2,200 while a 2-ton system gets $1,200. Ask for a load calculation before choosing tonnage.

Financing the rest

Efficiency Vermont lists a Home Energy Loan with rates starting at 0% interest, and free products for income-qualified Vermonters. Weatherization first can reduce the heat pump size you need.

How to claim Efficiency Vermont rebates

  1. Find your electric utility on your bill: Green Mountain Power, Burlington Electric, a municipal utility or a co-op.
  2. Pick an Efficiency Excellence Network contractor or a participating retailer.
  3. For weatherization, have the contractor submit the project for pre-approval and complete your income attestation.
  4. Complete the work, including blower-door testing, and confirm where to send any rebate.
  5. Keep the invoice. Rebates are typically mailed within 60 days of final review.

Common questions

Are Efficiency Vermont rebates instant discounts?

Many are, taken off the price at purchase through a contractor or retailer.

How much does weatherization pay now?

75% of cost up to $3,000, or up to $7,000 for income-eligible households.

Does Burlington Electric get the same bonus?

No. Burlington Electric customers use Burlington Electric's own rebates.

What does a heat pump water heater get?

$600, or up to $1,000 for income-eligible households.

Do rental owners qualify?

For the heat pump income bonus, no. They can use Home Performance with ENERGY STAR for rental property owners on one-to-four-unit buildings.
