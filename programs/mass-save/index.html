Mass Save Rebates 2026: What You Can Get

Every amount links to the official program. Last checked October 1, 2026.

By Sam Menard

Short answer: Mass Save pays up to $8,500 for an air-source heat pump, or up to $16,000 (sometimes no cost) for income-qualified homes. Insulation is 75% to 100% off after a free home energy assessment, heat pump water heaters get $750, and the 0% HEAT Loan covers up to $25,000. It's for Eversource, National Grid, Unitil and Cape Light Compact customers, not municipal light plant towns.

How to apply

  1. Check your electric utility: Mass Save covers Eversource, National Grid, Unitil and Cape Light Compact, not municipal light plant towns.
  2. Book the free home energy assessment; it unlocks insulation discounts and the HEAT Loan.
  3. Use an installer who works with Mass Save, and apply by February 28, 2027 for 2026 installs.

Recent changes

  • 2026: Boston Solar Loan Pilot. A 0% loan of up to $25,000 for rooftop solar, fully forgiven after 20 years of living in the home. Source
  • 2025-02-28: Mass Save 2025-2027 budget. Regulators cut the three-year Mass Save budget by $500 million. Residential budgets fell about 25% for gas and 15% for electric. Source

Common questions

Why did my town not qualify for Mass Save?

Towns with a municipal light plant (like Belmont, Concord or Holyoke) aren't in Mass Save. They have their own programs and the state's MLP Z-Loan.

Did Mass Save cut its rebates?

Regulators cut the 2025-2027 Mass Save budget by $500 million in February 2025. Heat pump rebates of up to $8,500 are still offered.
