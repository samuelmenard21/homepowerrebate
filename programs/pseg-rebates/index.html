PSE&G Rebates for Homeowners

PSE&G is New Jersey's largest utility, serving cities such as Newark and Jersey City. It runs a large set of rebates through its HVAC Instant Rebates Program, its appliance store, a home battery program and free assessments. Amounts change by what you replace and how much you earn. These are the 2026 figures from PSE&G's own pages, read on October 6, 2026.

Who PSE&G serves

PSE&G's official territory list shows Newark in Essex County and Jersey City in Hudson County for both electric and gas. JCP&L serves much of the central and shore area, and Atlantic City Electric serves southern New Jersey, so check the name on your electric bill. If your bill says something else, see the JCP&L page or the Atlantic City Electric page.

Rules that catch people out

The heat pump programs require you to own the house where the work happens. They cover single-family and attached homes of one to four units, and they exclude new construction and commercial property. Rebates are credited as a deduction on the contractor's invoice, not on your bill. The large replacement rebates apply to gas or delivered fuel customers who take the old system out of service, which means a form and sometimes photos. Heat pumps must be sized to the home with a written heating load calculation.

Paying over time

The repayment option lets qualified customers pay for work in equal parts on the PSE&G bill with no interest. The limit is $25,000 for an HVAC project, $50,000 for a stand-alone decarbonization project and $75,000 when the two are combined. You need 12 months of on-time payment history, though a letter from a previous utility can substitute. PSE&G says it does not run a credit check. Terms are 84 months, or 84 or 120 months for income-qualified customers on projects over $10,000.

What PSE&G does not pay

We did not find a PSE&G rebate for rooftop solar, insulation as a stand-alone rebate (outside the free programs) or windows. State solar credits and net metering still apply to solar owners. The federal credits ended after 2025, and the state's federal-funded home rebates have no launch we could find.

How to claim a PSE&G rebate

  1. Confirm your electric or gas bill says PSE&G.
  2. For heating, cooling and water heaters, choose a participating contractor from PSE&G's program page or call 1-855-846-2895.
  3. Ask for a quote that shows each rebate line and any removal work.
  4. For appliances, buy the model and apply online within 120 days with the receipt.
  5. Keep invoices, model numbers and photos.

Common questions

How much does PSE&G pay for a heat pump?

$300 to $600 for a standard heat pump, and up to $10,000 (or $12,000 for income-qualified homes) to replace a gas, oil or propane heating system.

Do I have to own my home?

Yes. The HVAC and decarbonization programs require the owner of the house where the work is done.

Does PSE&G pay for a battery?

Yes. GridSmart pays up to $5,000, for new installs of three approved batteries, in return for letting PSE&G use the battery up to 100 times a year.

Is there help paying upfront?

Yes. Interest-free on-bill repayment of up to $25,000, $50,000 or $75,000 depending on the project.

Can the rebate go on my bill?

No. PSE&G credits HVAC and battery rebates only as a deduction on the contractor's invoice.
