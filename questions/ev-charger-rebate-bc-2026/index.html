Is there a rebate for installing an EV charger in BC?

BC offers modest EV charging incentives (usually under $500) through BC Hydro and provincial programs. These are separate from the main heat pump/solar rebates. Federal tax credits may also apply if you purchase an eligible vehicle. Check BC Hydro's website for current EV incentive programs.
