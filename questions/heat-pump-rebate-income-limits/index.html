Heat pump rebate income limits

Most base heat pump rebates have NO income limit. You can earn any amount and still claim them. However, some programs offer extra rebates if your household income is below a certain threshold.

Canadian Programs

Province Program Base Rebate Income Limit for Extra?
BC BC Hydro Power Smart 2.0 Up to $4,000 No income limit
BC CleanBC Income-Qualified Top-Up +$2,000–$8,000 ~$110K household income
Ontario Provincial rebate (varies by utility) $1,500–$3,000 No income limit (varies)
Alberta Provincial/municipal programs $1,000–$2,000 Check local program
Federal Canada Greener Homes (closed) N/A (closed to new applicants) N/A

U.S. Programs (Sample)

State Program Rebate Income Limit?
California Title 24 / Local utility $300–$1,500 Varies by utility
New York Con Edison / PSEG rebates $500–$1,500 Some programs income-qualified
Massachusetts MassSave / Utility programs $300–$1,200 Generally no limit, but check

Key Takeaway

Apply anyway. Most people qualify for the base rebate regardless of income. Even if you don't meet an income-qualified top-up threshold, you'll still get the main rebate. The worst outcome is "no," and you'll know to apply elsewhere.

Income limits are set by household annual income—usually total household before taxes. Each program defines it slightly differently (some include spouse, some don't). When you apply, they'll ask for proof of income (tax return, pay stub).
