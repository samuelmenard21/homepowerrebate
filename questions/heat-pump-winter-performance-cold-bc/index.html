Will a heat pump work during BC's cold winters?

Yes. Modern cold-climate heat pumps are designed to work down to -20°C. BC rarely drops below -15°C except in the far north (Fort St. John, Prince George). A heat pump with proper sizing and a backup electric element keeps your home warm all winter. Most BC homeowners report 30–50% heating bill reductions.
