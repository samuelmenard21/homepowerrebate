Is Tesla Powerwall eligible for BC Hydro's home battery rebate?

No. Tesla Powerwall is not on BC Hydro's approved battery list, so it does not qualify for the $5,000 rebate. BC Hydro's rebate requires the battery to integrate with their Peak Saver program, which Powerwall does not. Approved alternatives include Eguana Evolve, LG Chem RESU, and Enphase IQ Battery—all of which do qualify and work with Peak Saver.
