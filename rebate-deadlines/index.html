Home Rebate Deadlines Coming Up

Heat pump, insulation, appliance and EV charger rebates that end soon, soonest first. Updated October 23, 2026.

Short answer: 44 home energy rebate deadlines are coming up in Canada and the US. The next one is October 31, 2026: BC Hydro heat pump bonus; BC Hydro Condo and Apartment Rebate: limited-time heat pump bonus; FortisBC instant rebates on energy-efficient products. Many year-end offers need the work done, not just signed, by December 31.

Read each deadline closely. Some mean the install must be finished. Others mean the form must be in. A contractor who is booked up may not finish in time, so ask for a firm date in writing.
