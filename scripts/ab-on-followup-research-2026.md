## 1. Lethbridge, Alberta

**Question asked:** Does Lethbridge Electric Utility (the city's own municipally-owned electric utility, distinct from ENMAX/ATCO/EPCOR used by other AB cities) run its own rebate program?

**Finding: No dedicated Lethbridge Electric Utility rebate program was found.** Searches for "Lethbridge Electric Utility rebate" and similar returned nothing — no evidence the city's electric utility itself runs a residential energy-efficiency rebate program, distinct from the city's general environmental/CEIP programs. This negative is consistent with (does not contradict) the previous pass's inconclusive finding — it's now a more deliberately searched negative, though still not 100% exhaustive.

**However, two genuine city-run programs were found, one of which is a real, currently-live, upgraded local top-up:**

### 1a. CEIP rebate increase to $1,350 (GENUINE FINDING — HIGH CONFIDENCE, ADDED TO SITE)

The City of Lethbridge increased its Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) rebate from $800 to **$1,350**, applied directly against the homeowner's Clean Energy Improvement Tax (i.e., reducing the amount financed) — described as a response to the federal Canada Greener Homes Grant ending. The city's CEIP also carries its own fixed interest rate, confirmed directly from the official page as **2.83%**, with terms up to 25 years, no prepayment penalty, repaid via property tax bill. Minimum project cost $3,000, max $50,000 eligible costs per property.

- [lethbridge.ca — Clean Energy Improvement Program (CEIP) official program page](https://www.lethbridge.ca/property-taxes/clean-energy-improvement-program-ceip/) — official, direct fetch, states $1,350 rebate + 2.83% fixed rate
- [lethbridge.ca news — "Clean Energy Improvement Program still taking applications, rebate increasing"](https://www.lethbridge.ca/news/posts/clean-energy-improvement-program-still-taking-applications-rebate-increasing/) — official, direct fetch, confirms $1,350 (up from $800) and the reason for the increase
- [CTV News Calgary — "City of Lethbridge increases rebates for Clean Energy Improvement Program"](https://calgary.ctvnews.ca/city-of-lethbridge-increases-rebates-for-clean-energy-improvement-program-1.6828274) — third-party, consistent
- [mylethbridgenow.com — "City increases rebate for Clean Energy Improvement Program"](https://www.mylethbridgenow.com/39296/news/city-increases-rebate-for-clean-energy-improvement-program-applications-still-open/) — third-party, consistent

4 sources, 2 official/primary (lethbridge.ca itself), 2 independent local news, all consistent on both the $1,350 figure and the 2.83% rate. **Confidence: High.**

**Action taken:** Added a Lethbridge-specific CEIP rebate callout ($1,350 rebate, 2.83% fixed rate) to `ca/ab/lethbridge/index.html`, replacing the generic "5–10% incentive" CEIP framing inherited from the sitewide AB template with Lethbridge's actual confirmed numbers. See Section 3.

### 1b. Environmental Incentive Program (found, NOT added — out of scope for this site)

The City of Lethbridge also runs an **Environmental Incentive Program** (April 22 – December 31, 2026, or until funds deplete): 50% rebate up to $100 per item, for **rain barrels and low-flow toilet replacements only**, max $400/household, applied as a credit to the water utility account.

- [lethbridge.ca — Environmental Incentive Program](https://www.lethbridge.ca/news/posts/environmental-incentive-program/) — official, direct fetch

**Confidence: High** (single official source, but plainly stated with no ambiguity). **Not added to the site** — this is a water-conservation rebate (rain barrels, toilets), not an energy-efficiency category (heat pump/solar/insulation/water heater in the site's energy sense) that fits this page's scope. Documented here for completeness only.

## 2. Ontario: Ottawa / Hamilton / Mississauga-Brampton Financing — Re-Verification

**Finding: All three pages' existing content was independently re-confirmed as accurate. No edits were needed or made.**

### Ottawa — Better Homes Ottawa Loan Program (CONFIRMED, matches published content exactly)

Re-verified: $10,000–$125,000 (or 10% of assessed value, whichever is lower), fixed **4.33%** rate, 20-year term, 4% administrative fee, repaid via property tax bill (PACE/Local Improvement Charge model), natural-gas upgrades excluded. This exactly matches what's already published on `ca/on/ottawa/index.html` (FAQ schema + body copy), including the specific 4.33% rate and $125,000 cap.

- [ottawa.ca — Better Homes Ottawa Loan Program official page](https://ottawa.ca/en/city-hall/budget-finance-and-corporate-planning/funding/environmental-funding/better-homes-ottawa/what-better-homes-ottawa-loan-program) — official (direct fetch returned an empty body this pass, so treat as cited-but-not-freshly-fetched; the URL itself was confirmed live via search)
- [betterhomesottawa.ca — Better Homes Ottawa Loan Program](https://betterhomesottawa.ca/rebate-and-incentive-programs/better-homes-loan-program/) — official program site
- [rbheating.com — "Ottawa Heat Pump Financing at 4.33% | Better Homes Loan"](https://rbheating.com/blog/ottawa-heat-pump-financing/) — third-party, confirms 4.33% rate
- [airzonehvac.ca — "Ottawa HVAC Rebates 2026: The New Roadmap"](https://www.airzonehvac.ca/the-greener-homes-loan-is-gone-heres-the-2026-roadmap-for-ottawa-rebates/) — third-party, consistent

4 sources, 2 official (ottawa.ca + betterhomesottawa.ca), consistent on rate, cap, and term. **Confidence: High. No change needed — already accurate on the live site.**

### Hamilton — Better Homes Hamilton (CONFIRMED as still genuinely unresolved; site's hedged framing is correct and should stay as-is)

Re-verified: up to $20,000, 0% introductory rate, 10–15 year term, Local Improvement Charge via property tax bill; covers assessments/permits, envelope work, mechanical systems (heat pumps), supporting electrical/ductwork. Launched as a pilot (~50 homes, $1M pool). **Current 2026 application status still could not be confirmed** — the official Engage Hamilton consultation page shows the consultation phase as "concluded" / discussions "closed," with no clear statement of whether a live intake currently exists. This matches — almost word for word — the hedge already published on `ca/on/hamilton/index.html` ("we could not confirm from official sources whether it's still accepting applications — contact the City of Hamilton directly").

- [engage.hamilton.ca/betterhomeshamilton](https://engage.hamilton.ca/betterhomeshamilton) — official, direct fetch, consultation shown as concluded, no clear current-intake statement
- [solar-x.ca — "Better Homes Hamilton energy retrofit program 2026"](https://solar-x.ca/blog/hamilton-better-homes-solar-financing-2026) — third-party
- [resilientcolleges.ca — "Better Homes Hamilton" announcement](https://resilientcolleges.ca/news-and-stories/better-homes-hamilton-accessing-energy-efficiency-retrofits-to-be-made-easier-for-hamilton-homeowners/) — third-party/NGO
- [hamilton.insauga.com — "Hamilton's energy retrofit program"](https://hamilton.insauga.com/hamiltons-new-energy-retrofit-program-could-help-lower-income-residents-upgrade-their-homes/) — local news

4 sources, 1 official (direct fetch), consistent on program mechanics; **application-status ambiguity itself is the confirmed finding** — genuinely still unconfirmed whether new applications are open, not just under-researched. **Confidence: High on program terms; the "status unconfirmed" hedge is itself the correct, verified conclusion — not a gap to close.** No change needed; the existing hedged copy is accurate and should NOT be upgraded to a confident "currently open" claim.

### Mississauga & Brampton — Peel Residential Energy Program / CCET (CONFIRMED: still no loan/rebate program, just coaching pilot)

Re-verified: Mississauga, Brampton, and Caledon jointly run the Peel Residential Energy Program (PREP) via the Centre for Community Energy Transformation (CCET), launched March 2024. It is **free energy coaching only** (phone/video consultations), explicitly described as still "exploring financing options" rather than offering one. No loan or rebate program exists for either city at the municipal level. This matches the published pages exactly (`ca/on/mississauga/index.html` and `ca/on/brampton/index.html` both state "doesn't have its own loan or rebate program at this time... a small energy-coaching pilot... rather than a rebate or loan").

- [mississauga.ca — Centre for Community Energy Transformation](https://www.mississauga.ca/projects-and-strategies/city-projects/peel-residential-energy-program/) — official
- [council.cleanairpartnership.org — Community Retrofit Program / CCET](https://council.cleanairpartnership.org/sector/community-retrofit-program/) — third-party/NGO
- [insauga.com — "Save money on energy bills with Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon home energy retrofits program"](https://www.insauga.com/save-money-on-energy-bills-with-mississauga-brampton-caledon-home-energy-retrofits-program/) — local news
- [brampton.ca — Grow Green / Conserving Energy at Home](https://www.brampton.ca/EN/residents/GrowGreen/Pages/Conserving-Energy-at-Home.aspx) — official (Brampton's own page, no loan/rebate program mentioned)

4 sources, 2 official (mississauga.ca + brampton.ca), consistent negative finding. **Confidence: High. No change needed — the "no program yet, coaching pilot only" framing on both pages is accurate and current.**
