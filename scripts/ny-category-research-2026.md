# NY Category Research 2026 — Non-Heat-Pump Rebate Programs by Utility Territory + City

Research date: August 22, 2026. Scope: insulation/air sealing, solar, battery storage, EV chargers (Level 2 home), windows/doors, smart thermostats — for National Grid (upstate: Buffalo, Albany, Syracuse, Rochester), Con Edison (NYC/Westchester: Yonkers, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, White Plains, New York City), PSEG Long Island (Huntington, Smithtown, Islip, Babylon, Oyster Bay, Southampton, Brookhaven), and Central Hudson (Poughkeepsie, Newburgh, Kingston, Beacon, Saugerties). NYS Clean Heat and federal HEAR (heat pump programs) are already documented elsewhere on the site and are NOT re-researched here.

**Methodology note / limitation:** This pass was done via web search + targeted page fetches, not a manual crawl of every linked PDF on every utility site. Most claims below have 2 sources; where a third independent source was found it is listed. Where I could not find a third corroborating source, that is stated explicitly rather than implied. Dollar figures should be spot-checked against the live utility page before publishing, since several utility rebate blocks (e.g., NY-Sun Megawatt Block) close and reopen on a rolling basis.

## Per-City Findings

For all 21 cities, the **utility-territory table above is the baseline** — every city inherits its territory's programs identically, since none of these are city-administered utility rebates. Below are ONLY city/county-specific additions found, or an explicit "nothing found" where a real search was done and returned no local top-up.

### National Grid territory

- **Buffalo** — Local weatherization capacity exists via Neighborhood Housing Service of South Buffalo and Lt. Col. Matt Urban Human Services, with city/county WAP funding up to $5,000 per structure for income-eligible households; this is the standard county-administered WAP delivery mechanism, not a city-only top-up rebate beyond the state program. [buffalony.gov Weatherization Assistance Program](https://www.buffalony.gov/391/Buffalo-Weatherization-Assistance-Progra), [Erie County HEAP Weatherization](https://www3.erie.gov/heap/weatherization-assistance-programs) — 2 sources. No city-funded rebate beyond WAP/Comfort Home found.
- **Albany** — City of Albany's Office of Energy & Sustainability (albanysustainability.org) and Albany County's "Incentives for Green Homes" page both function as referral/navigation hubs pointing residents to NYSERDA and utility programs, not as sources of a separate city-funded dollar rebate. [City of Albany Office of Energy & Sustainability](https://albanysustainability.org/energy), [Albany County Incentives for Green Homes](https://www.albanycountyny.gov/government/team-green/incentives-for-green-homes) — 2 sources. No standalone city rebate found.
- **Syracuse** — Weatherization delivered locally through P.E.A.C.E., Inc. (Onondaga County's WAP provider); no Syracuse-specific top-up beyond state WAP/EmPower+ found. [peace-caa.org Department of Energy and Housing Services](https://peace-caa.org/programs/energyhousing/) — 1 source; explicit negative finding, no local top-up located.
- **Rochester** — Rochester has a genuinely distinct local layer: the city-affiliated **RENEW (Rochester ENergy Efficiency and Weatherization)** program and the **Energy Conservation Program (ECP)**, both targeting income-qualified city residents, administered alongside the city's own "Energy Smart Rochester" initiative (cityofrochester.gov). These function as city-branded delivery channels for WAP-equivalent free weatherization rather than an additional cash rebate stacked on top of the state program. [City of Rochester Energy Smart Rochester](https://www.cityofrochester.gov/departments/department-environmental-services-des/energy-smart-rochester), [RACF Rochester Energy Efficiency and Weatherization](https://www.racf.org/collaborations/rochester-energy-efficiency-and-weatherization/) — 2 sources. This is the closest thing found to a "city program" in National Grid territory, though it is a delivery channel, not extra money on top of the state/utility baseline.

### Con Edison territory

- **New York City** — Genuine city-level top-up confirmed: NYC's residential solar property tax abatement (via NYC Department of Buildings/HPD) gives a 4-year abatement of 7.5%/year of installed solar cost (30% total), capped at $62,500/year or $250,000 total, stacked on top of the NY-Sun incentive. This is a real, distinct city-administered benefit, not just a repeat of the state program. [NYC HPD Solar Incentive Matrix PDF](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/hpd/downloads/pdfs/services/solar-incentive-detailed-matrix.pdf), [Anern NY Solar Incentives 2026](https://www.anern.com/new-york-solar-incentives-2026-tax-credits-rebates-property--net-metering-rules.html) — 2 sources; did not find a third independent confirmation of the exact $62,500/$250,000 caps — recommend verifying against nyc.gov before publishing those specific numbers.
- **Yonkers** — WestCOP operates the local WAP delivery office (540 Palmer Rd, Yonkers); no Yonkers-specific rebate beyond state EmPower+/Comfort Home found. [WestCOP Weatherization Program](https://westcop.org/programs/weatherization/) — 1 source; negative finding on a city-specific top-up.
- **Mount Vernon** — Westchester Community Opportunity Program's Mount Vernon Community Action Group delivers WAP locally; Sustainable Westchester also runs an "Energize U Mount Vernon" workshop series, which is educational/navigational rather than a cash program. No Mount Vernon-specific rebate dollar amount found. [Sustainable Westchester Energize U Mount Vernon](https://sustainablewestchester.org/energize-u-mount-vernon-workshop/) — 1 source; negative finding on a local cash top-up.
- **New Rochelle** — No New Rochelle-specific program found beyond standard county WAP delivery and the Westchester-wide EmPower+/Comfort Home stack ($2,500–$3,000 air sealing/insulation, +$2,000 for ENERGY STAR window upgrades, per the state program — not a New Rochelle-only figure). Negative finding, stated explicitly — no local top-up located in this pass.
- **White Plains** — The Weatherization Referral Assistance Program (WRAP, 148 Martine Ave) is a genuine local program, but is restricted to residents age 60+, making it narrower than a general homeowner rebate. The White Plains Community Development Rehabilitation Program also offers home-improvement loans locally. Both are real local layers, though age/income-restricted rather than open to all homeowners. [TheGrantMap White Plains WRAP/Con Ed reference](https://www.thegrantmap.com/city/white-plains/white-plains-conedison-insulation-rebate) — 1 source; recommend direct confirmation with the City of White Plains before publishing WRAP's terms.

### PSEG Long Island territory

See the "Local/municipal layer" note at the end of Section 3 above for Huntington, Brookhaven, Oyster Bay, Islip, Southampton (explicit negative — town page just redirects to NYSERDA), and Smithtown/Babylon (explicit negative — no local top-up found).

### Central Hudson territory

- **Poughkeepsie** — Community Action Partnership for Dutchess County (CAP-DC) is the local WAP/EmPower+ delivery provider; Town of Poughkeepsie's own site hosts a "Central Hudson Assistance" referral page rather than a separate town-funded rebate. No Poughkeepsie-specific top-up dollar figure found. [Town of Poughkeepsie Central Hudson Assistance](https://www.townofpoughkeepsie-ny.gov/416/Central-Hudson-Assistance"), [Dutchess CAP Weatherization](https://www.dutchesscap.org/what-we-do/energy-conservation-programs/weatherization.html) — 2 sources; negative finding on a local cash top-up.
- **Kingston** — Ulster County Community Action Committee (UCCAC) delivers WAP locally; a 2026 Hudson Valley One article confirms Ulster County residents can access "myriad" financial incentives, but these are the same state/NYSERDA/HEAP/WAP programs already covered at the territory level, not a distinct Kingston-only rebate. [Hudson Valley One, March 2026](https://hudsonvalleyone.com/2026/03/15/ulster-county-households-can-tap-myriad-financial-incentives-for-repairs-and-energy-saving-upgrades/), [UCCAC Energy Services](https://uccac.org/programs/energy-services/) — 2 sources; negative finding on a Kingston-specific top-up.
- **Newburgh** — RECAP (Regional Economic Community Action Program, Middletown) delivers WAP/EmPower+/HEAP/HERR for Orange County including Newburgh. No Newburgh-specific rebate found. [RECAP/Mid-Hudson Home Repair](https://midhudsonenergychoices.org/home-repair) — 1 source; negative finding.
- **Beacon** — Genuine local program found: **Energize NY**, active in the City of Beacon plus the Towns of Red Hook and Hyde Park, has helped Westchester/Hudson Valley homeowners secure financing and maximize state incentives, with claimed aggregate savings over $2,000,000/year across 14 communities. This is a real local energy-financing nonprofit program layered on the state baseline, though it primarily helps access financing/state incentives rather than providing its own separate cash rebate. [Patch: Energize NY launches in Mid-Hudson](https://patch.com/new-york/midhudsonvalley/energize-ny-energy-saving-services-homeowners-launches-three-mid-hudson) — only 1 source found; could not independently corroborate the $2M/year aggregate figure — flag as needing a second source before publishing that specific number.
- **Saugerties** — No Saugerties-specific program found in this pass; one search explicitly returned no results for Saugerties specifically (only Beacon). Stated as an explicit negative finding rather than left blank — Saugerties likely relies on Ulster County's UCCAC (same provider as Kingston) for WAP delivery, but this was not independently confirmed for Saugerties by name.

## New/Additional Categories Discovered (outside the original 6)

These surfaced repeatedly during research and don't cleanly fit the existing 6-category taxonomy — flagging for possible category expansion:

1. **Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP)** — a distinct, heavily-documented statewide program (100% free for ≤60% state median income households) that is broader than "insulation" alone (covers heating system repair/replacement, appliance safety issues, hot water tank/pipe insulation). It appears in nearly every city search as the dominant local delivery mechanism. Given how consistently it surfaced, it may deserve its own category or at least a prominent sub-section under insulation, distinct from the market-rate insulation rebates.
2. **EmPower+ program** — income-qualified (≤80% AMI) whole-home efficiency program that can cover up to 100% of insulation/air sealing AND includes heat pump water heaters and other equipment — overlaps insulation but is broader; also appeared repeatedly as the "if income-eligible, start here" umbrella.
3. **Heat pump water heaters** — Central Hudson has a standalone $1,250 rebate on ENERGY STAR heat pump water heaters, separate from space-heating heat pumps (which are already covered by NYS Clean Heat) and separate from the general "insulation" or "windows" buckets. This is a genuinely distinct equipment category not currently in the 6-category taxonomy. [Central Hudson Residential Incentives](https://www.cenhud.com/en/my-energy/save-energy-money/residential-incentives/) — 1 source found; recommend a second-source check before publishing the $1,250 figure.
4. **Property tax abatements/exemptions for solar** — distinct from a cash rebate; NYC's 4-year 30% abatement and the 15-year Huntington property tax exemption on solar system value are both real, but neither fits neatly into a "rebate amount" cell — may need a "tax treatment" column or note added to the solar category going forward.
5. **EV time-of-use/off-peak charging credits** — Con Edison's SmartCharge (~$400/year potential) and Central Hudson's EV ChargeSmart/EV Time-of-Use Rate are behavioral/usage incentives, structurally different from a one-time charger-purchase rebate. If the site's EV charger category is meant to cover "the charger," these usage-based programs may need their own sub-category or at least a clear "this is not a hardware rebate" flag so it isn't confused with PSEG LI's actual $100–$400 hardware rebate.
6. **PACE-style/on-bill financing (Energize NY)** — not a rebate, but a financing mechanism that surfaced independently across Westchester and Hudson Valley cities; worth a category if the site starts covering financing options alongside outright rebates.
