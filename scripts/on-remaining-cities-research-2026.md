## Per-City Findings (fresh WebSearch, cross-checked against currently published `ca/on/{city}/index.html`)

- **London** — **Real, high-confidence, currently published.** BetterHomes London: $6.66M program, low-interest loans up to $40,000 (2.5% fixed, 10–20 year term via property tax bill, transferable on sale), plus up to $10,000 in additional incentives based on energy savings achieved. Launching spring 2026. 3 sources: [london.ca official launch announcement](https://london.ca/newsroom/city-london-launches-betterhomes-program-support-energy-efficient-home-upgrades), [betterhomeslondon.ca](https://betterhomeslondon.ca/), [solar-x.ca](https://solar-x.ca/blog/betterhomes-london-2026-solar-financing). **Matches the site's existing `id="local"` BetterHomes London section exactly** — no changes needed.

- **Whitby** — **Real, high-confidence, currently published.** Covered by the regional Durham Greener Homes program (Windfall Ecology Centre): free energy coaching, Deep Retrofit Rebate up to $10,000 base, plus a Whitby-specific additional $5,000 for deeper retrofits not available in other Durham municipalities. 3 sources: [whitby.ca official](https://www.whitby.ca/en/news/cash-back-incentives-available-for-durham-greener-homes-participants.aspx), [durhamgreenerhomes.ca](https://durhamgreenerhomes.ca/financial/rebates/whitby/), [cmisolar.ca](https://www.cmisolar.ca/2024/08/08/durhams-deep-retrofit-rebate/). **Matches the site's existing section exactly** — no changes needed.

- **Oshawa** — **Real, high-confidence, currently published.** Also covered by Durham Greener Homes (regional, same program as Whitby, without the Whitby-specific $5,000 bonus). Sources: [durham.ca](https://www.durham.ca/en/news/durham-s-home-retrofit-program-pays-off-for-residents.aspx) plus the same Durham Greener Homes sources above. **Matches the site's existing section exactly** — no changes needed. (Note: Oshawa was not one of the 12 originally flagged as unresearched by name in the prior verification doc's list, but was checked here since it shares Whitby's regional program — confirmed correct.)

- **Cambridge** — **Real, high-confidence, currently published.** RetrofitWR (Region of Waterloo + GrandBridge Energy): up to $40,000 at 2.5% fixed over 10–15 years, repaid via electricity bill, free Energy Coach, launching spring 2026 for GrandBridge customers in Cambridge and North Dumfries. 3 sources: [cambridge.ca official](https://www.cambridge.ca/news/posts/upgrade-your-home-with-retrofitwr/), [regionofwaterloo.ca](https://www.regionofwaterloo.ca/programs-and-services/climate-and-environment/climate-action/retrofit-waterloo-region/), [retrofitwr.ca](https://retrofitwr.ca/). **Matches the site's existing section exactly** — no changes needed.

- **Windsor** — **Real but not-yet-live program, correctly framed as such on the site.** R-DEER (Residential Deep Energy Efficiency Retrofit): would offer $10,000–$40,000 in loans depending on home value, funded partly via FCM's Green Municipal Fund. Confirmed still in development, not accepting applications. Fresh search adds one nuance not on the page: Windsor city council reportedly scrapped/paused the full $200M/10-year rollout in 2025 over cost concerns, though the letstalk.citywindsor.ca portal (the site's own cited source) still frames it as an active engagement process rather than a cancelled program. 3 sources: [letstalk.citywindsor.ca](https://letstalk.citywindsor.ca/residential-deep-energy-retrofit-program), [windsorlawcities.ca](https://windsorlawcities.ca/the-city-of-windsors-residential-deep-energy-efficiency-retrofit-r-deer-program-a-model-for-municipal-retrofit-programs/), [CBC News coverage of the cost pushback](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/windsor/windsor-s-key-strategy-to-reduce-emissions-too-costly-without-financial-help-1.7492899). **Site's existing "in development, not yet accepting applications" framing is accurate and appropriately hedged — no change needed**, though a future pass could add a one-line note about the 2025 cost pushback for extra transparency (optional, low priority).

- **Oakville** — **Real, currently published, utility-run not city-run.** Oakville Hydro Retrofit Program: up to 50% of eligible project costs, requires application/pre-approval; plus a $600 post-assessment rebate and an Energy Affordability Program for income-eligible households. 2 sources this pass: [oakvillehydro.com Save on Energy](https://www.oakvillehydro.com/saveonenergy/homeowners), [oakville.ca Home Energy Retrofits 101](https://www.oakville.ca/home-environment/building-renovations/home-energy-retrofits-101/). **Matches the site's existing section exactly** — no changes needed.

- **Burlington** — **Real historical program, correctly marked closed on the site.** Better Homes Burlington pilot (Sept 2023–Jan 2024 application window): zero-interest loans up to $10,000 for air-source heat pumps, capped at 20 loans total. Confirmed the application phase concluded; only previously-approved applicants can still complete projects. 3 sources: [burlington.ca program page](https://www.burlington.ca/en/home-property-and-environment/better-homes-burlington-home-upgrades.aspx), [burlington.ca launch announcement](https://www.burlington.ca/en/news/better-homes-burlington-launching-to-help-homeowners-recover-energy-efficiency-upgrade-costs.aspx), [burlingtontoday.com](https://www.burlingtontoday.com/local-news/burlingtons-heat-pump-incentive-program-what-you-need-to-know-7952930). **Site's "closed to new applicants" framing is accurate** — no change needed.

- **Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Barrie, Greater Sudbury, Markham** — **Confirmed no dedicated municipal loan/rebate program**, consistent with what the site already publishes ("No major municipal loan program... (yet)"). Greater Sudbury has a coaching/concierge service (CHIRP — Community Home Improvement and Resiliency Program) that connects residents to existing incentives rather than offering its own cash top-up, and it's currently at capacity with a waitlist; this matches the site's "feasibility study underway" / no-live-program framing and the established pattern (per the earlier verification doc) of not presenting coaching-only services as cash rebates. Sources: WebSearch results referencing each city's own site plus [sudbury.com](https://www.sudbury.com/spotlight/green-living-improve-your-home-and-reduce-the-risk-of-damage-from-weather-events-with-home-retrofitting-12189733) for CHIRP. No changes needed to any of these five pages.
