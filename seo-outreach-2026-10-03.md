**Title:** HARP heating rebate is open again: $400, apply by March 31 (how to check your status)

The Heating Assistance Rebate opened Oct 1. Quick summary from the Province's pages:

- $400 per household for 2026-27
- Income limits: $30,000 net if you live alone, $45,000 combined if you live with kids or other adults (2025 tax year). GIS and Income Assistance recipients also qualify
- Renters qualify if they pay their own heat (landlord form needed)
- Status check works only if you applied online: confirmation number + postal code
- Takes 8 to 10 weeks. Include a heating bill if it's your first time or you moved, or it stalls
- Service NS will never text you a link to "deposit" your rebate. That's a scam

Official page: https://www.novascotia.ca/apply-heating-assistance-rebate-heating-assistance-rebate-program
