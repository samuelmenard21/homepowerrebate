FAQ for London homeowners

How much can a London homeowner get for heat pump + solar together?

Combining heat pump ($12,000) and solar ($5,000) in London adds up to $17,000 based on the open programs we track, before adding any other category.

What's the maximum rebate stack available in London?

Adding up every open, quantified rebate category we track for London comes to $30,825 across 7 of the 8 categories.

Are all these London rebates available at the same time?

They come from different programs (federal, provincial or state, utility, and municipal), so most homeowners can combine several in the same renovation project. Always confirm current stacking rules with each program before you apply, since eligibility and funding caps can change.

Which rebate categories are NOT currently available in London?

Based on our data, London does not currently have an open rebate with a published dollar amount for: EV Charger. This can change as programs launch or funding resets, so we recheck city pages regularly.

How this stack is calculated

Every dollar figure above comes straight from the London rebate guide's published program amounts — the same numbers used to compute London's PowerScore of 59/100. We only count a category toward the combined total when it's an open program with a real published dollar value; categories with no open program, or no published amount, are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
