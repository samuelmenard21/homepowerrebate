FAQ for Burbank homeowners

How much can a Burbank homeowner get for heat pump + solar together?

Combining heat pump ($2,500) and solar in Burbank adds up to $2,500 based on the open programs we track, before adding any other category.

What's the maximum rebate stack available in Burbank?

Adding up every open, quantified rebate category we track for Burbank comes to $11,475 across 5 of the 8 categories.

Are all these Burbank rebates available at the same time?

They come from different programs (federal, provincial or state, utility, and municipal), so most homeowners can combine several in the same renovation project. Always confirm current stacking rules with each program before you apply, since eligibility and funding caps can change.

Which rebate categories are NOT currently available in Burbank?

Based on our data, Burbank does not currently have an open rebate with a published dollar amount for: Insulation, Solar, Windows & Doors. This can change as programs launch or funding resets, so we recheck city pages regularly.

How this stack is calculated

Every dollar figure above comes straight from the Burbank rebate guide's published program amounts — the same numbers used to compute Burbank's PowerScore of 61/100. We only count a category toward the combined total when it's an open program with a real published dollar value; categories with no open program, or no published amount, are shown as unavailable rather than estimated.
