FAQ for Lowell homeowners

How much can a Lowell homeowner get for heat pump + solar together?

Combining heat pump ($9,500) and solar in Lowell adds up to $9,500 based on the open programs we track, before adding any other category.

What's the maximum rebate stack available in Lowell?

Adding up every open, quantified rebate category we track for Lowell comes to $10,775 across 6 of the 8 categories.

Are all these Lowell rebates available at the same time?

They come from different programs (federal, provincial or state, utility, and municipal), so most homeowners can combine several in the same renovation project. Always confirm current stacking rules with each program before you apply, since eligibility and funding caps can change.

Which rebate categories are NOT currently available in Lowell?

Based on our data, Lowell does not currently have an open rebate with a published dollar amount for: Insulation, Solar. This can change as programs launch or funding resets, so we recheck city pages regularly.

How this stack is calculated
