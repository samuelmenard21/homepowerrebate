Home Energy Rebates in Bakersfield

Wondering what a heat pump actually costs you in Bakersfield after rebates? Here's the real math for SCE customers in Kern County in 2026.

Kern County, served by Southern California Edison (SCE).

What Bakersfield homeowners can claim

Bakersfield sits in SCE territory, but it's part of Kern County — not the Inland Empire region. The rebates below come from SCE and the statewide TECH Clean CA program. All amounts are in US dollars.

Heat Pump

$3,100

TECH Clean CA rebate for a 3–4 ton system. Typical installed cost runs $13,000–$22,000 before rebate.

Solar

$0 utility rebate

No dedicated SCE solar rebate. A 6kW system runs $15,000–$18,000, saving around $1,000/year.

Battery Storage

$0 utility rebate

A 13.5kWh battery costs around $12,000 installed, with no dedicated SCE rebate at this time.

Water Heater

$500

Heat pump water heater rebate.

EV Charger

$500

Toward a Level 2 home charger.

Smart Thermostat

$50

For a qualifying connected thermostat.

Why Bakersfield is different

Bakersfield's climate is hot and dry, with about 1,400 heating degree days a year — much lower than most of California. That means heating costs less here than it does up north, but cooling matters more. A heat pump still pays for itself since it handles both heating and cooling, but the payback period is longer: around 20 years for a heat pump and about 18 years for solar, based on typical local costs and savings.

Honest math for Bakersfield: A heat pump saves an estimated $700 a year on energy bills here. With the $3,100 rebate, that's a payback of roughly 20 years — slower than in cooler parts of the state, but the equipment still needs replacing eventually, so it's worth comparing against a standard AC + furnace swap. How to claim it

  1. Get SCE and TECH Clean CA pre-approval where required
  2. Choose a program-approved contractor
  3. Work is completed
  4. Submit rebate documentation
  5. Rebate processed (typically several weeks)

Bakersfield / Kern County programs

Bakersfield does not have its own dedicated regional rebate hub on this site, since it isn't part of the Inland Empire or Central Valley groupings we track elsewhere. Rebates here come from SCE and statewide programs like TECH Clean CA. Check with Kern County or the City of Bakersfield directly for any local financing programs, since we could not confirm city-specific rebates as of this writing.
