Home Energy Rebates in Fresno

Thinking about a heat pump or solar in Fresno? Here's what PG&E actually pays in the Central Valley in 2026 — and why it's less than the Bay Area.

Central Valley, served by Pacific Gas & Electric (PG&E). No SMUD access here.

What Fresno homeowners can claim

Fresno is in PG&E territory, but its rebate amounts run lower than the Bay Area PG&E cities we cover. All amounts below are in US dollars.

Heat Pump

$250–$500

PG&E's standard heat pump rebate — modest, in line with other PG&E territory. A 3-4 ton system costs $13,000–$22,000 installed before rebate.

Solar

$0 utility rebate

No dedicated PG&E rebate. NEM 3.0 has reduced the value of exported solar power. A 6kW system runs $15,000–$18,000.

Battery Storage

$0 utility rebate

A 13.5kWh battery costs about $12,000 installed, with no dedicated PG&E rebate at this time.

Insulation

$1,600

Available toward qualifying insulation upgrades.

Water Heater

$500

Heat pump water heater rebate.

EV Charger

$500

Toward a Level 2 home charger.

Smart Thermostat

$50

For a qualifying connected thermostat.

Why the numbers look different here

Fresno has about 2,500 heating degree days a year — moderate, more than Bakersfield but less than the Bay Area. A heat pump here saves an estimated $1,000 a year, and with a 6kW solar system saving around $1,200 a year, solar actually pays back faster (roughly 13 years) than the heat pump (about 15 years) once you account for the modest PG&E heat pump rebate.

Be upfront about the number: Fresno's heat pump rebate is small compared to other parts of California. The real savings come from lower energy bills over time, not the rebate itself. Run your free assessment →

How to claim it

  1. Get PG&E pre-approval (required before work begins)
  2. Choose a PG&E-approved contractor
  3. Work is installed
  4. Contractor or you files paperwork with PG&E
  5. Rebate applied as bill credit or check within 4–8 weeks

Fresno City Programs

We could not confirm a Fresno-specific municipal rebate program as of this writing. Most rebates for Fresno homeowners come through PG&E and statewide programs. Check with the City of Fresno directly for any local financing options.
