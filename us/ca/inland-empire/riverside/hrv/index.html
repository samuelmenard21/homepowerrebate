HRV & ERV Rebates in Riverside

SCE doesn't fund a heat recovery ventilator or energy recovery ventilator rebate in Riverside. Here's what actually applies in 2026, and why Riverside's heat and dust-driven need for tighter building envelopes is the real reason to consider one anyway.

How much you get

There's no flat dollar rebate for a heat recovery ventilator (HRV) or energy recovery ventilator (ERV) from SCE in Riverside the way there is for a heat pump or water heater. Ventilation equipment simply isn't a category SCE funds in its residential rebate catalog right now.

Riverside's summer heat and dust push a lot of homeowners toward sealing up their homes tighter, and a tighter home is exactly when mechanical ventilation stops being optional and starts being necessary for indoor air quality.

It's also worth ruling out the two programs homeowners sometimes assume cover this. TECH Clean California funds heat pump HVAC and heat pump water heater installs, not ventilation equipment — and as of late 2025 its single-family heat pump incentives were fully reserved anyway. And the federal Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (25C), which used to cover 30% of some efficiency upgrades, expired December 31, 2025; even while it was active, it never listed HRVs or ERVs among qualifying equipment. So for now, an HRV or ERV in Riverside is an out-of-pocket upgrade.

Source: SCE, TECH Clean California, IRS Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (25C)

Common questions

Does SCE offer an HRV rebate in Riverside?

No. SCE's residential rebate catalog covers heat pumps and a handful of other categories, but not ventilation equipment.

What should a Riverside homeowner do instead of waiting for an HRV rebate?

Treat it as a straight equipment-and-install cost, price a few local installers, and fold it into a broader retrofit plan rather than delaying for a rebate that doesn't currently exist. See our general guide to HRV and ERV systems for how the equipment works and what it costs.
