Heat Pump & Solar Rebates for Burbank Homeowners

Burbank is served by Burbank Water and Power (BWP), the city's own utility — not LADWP. BWP's “Electrify Your Home” rebates pay when you swap gas equipment for electric. Here's what applies, checked against BWP's own pages.

Updated September 28, 2026 · Independent guide. We don't sell your info. Amounts are from Burbank Water and Power's own pages and state program sites.

Most Burbank homes are 1940s–60s single-story houses on older panels, so the panel rebate matters here as much as the equipment rebate.

Short answer: Burbank Water and Power pays $1,000 per ton, up to $2,500, for a heat pump that replaces gas heating (up to $5,000 for BUSS or Lifeline customers), $1,500 for a heat pump water heater replacing gas, and up to $1,250 for a smart EV charger plus panel ($1,500 in disadvantaged areas). BWP rebates cover equipment and panels, not labor.

Is Burbank in LADWP territory?

No. Burbank Water and Power is Burbank's own city utility, and SoCalGas supplies gas. Even though Burbank borders Los Angeles, LADWP's rebates don't apply here.

What does BWP pay to switch from gas to electric?

BWP's rebates are built around one idea: replace a gas appliance. A heat pump that replaces a gas furnace earns $1,000 per ton, capped at $2,500 — so a 3-ton system hits the cap. If you're enrolled in BUSS (Burbank Utility Service Subsidy) or Lifeline, the cap rises to $5,000.

One catch many people miss: BWP rebates pay for equipment and panel upgrades only, not installation labor. Make sure your invoice breaks those costs out. BWP Electrify Your Home →

How do Burbank's EV charger rebates work?

BWP pays $200 for a basic Level 2 charger or $500 for a smart one, plus up to $750 toward a panel upgrade. In Burbank's Disadvantaged Community areas it's $300 or $600, plus up to $900. Renters qualify too. You must switch to BWP's time-of-use rate. BWP EV charger rebate →

What does an all-electric Burbank home stack up to?

Example for a typical homeowner (not low-income): heat pump $2,500 + heat pump water heater $1,500 + panel $750 + induction range $500 = $5,250 from BWP. Low-income customers can get much more — up to $5,000 for the heat pump and $2,000 for induction cooking.

What statewide programs apply in Burbank?

  • TECH Clean California: single-family heat pump money is fully reserved; waitlist only.
  • HEEHRA (income-qualified heat pump and appliance rebates): fully reserved statewide since Feb 24, 2026.
  • Federal 25C and 25D tax credits: ended for equipment placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.
  • SGIP battery incentives: mainly for income-qualified or high-fire-risk homes, and often waitlisted.

What should I do next?

  1. Check your bill: it should say Burbank Water and Power. If it does, the amounts above apply.
  2. Pick one upgrade and read its guide from the table.
  3. Get two or three written quotes that list the model number and efficiency ratings.
  4. Confirm the rebate is still open on the program page before you sign.
  5. Keep your invoice and permit — you'll need both to apply.

Common questions about Burbank rebates

Does LADWP serve Burbank?

No. Burbank Water and Power (BWP) is Burbank's own utility, and SoCalGas provides gas. LADWP rebates don't apply.

How much is the Burbank heat pump rebate?

BWP pays $1,000 per ton, up to $2,500, when a central or mini-split heat pump replaces gas heating. BUSS and Lifeline customers can get up to $5,000.

Does the BWP rebate cover installation?

No. BWP rebates cover qualifying equipment and panel upgrades only, not labor.

How much is the BWP EV charger rebate?

$200 for a standard or $500 for a smart Level 2 charger, plus up to $750 for a panel upgrade. Disadvantaged-community customers get $300 or $600, plus up to $900. You must be on BWP's time-of-use rate.

Can I get a federal tax credit?

No. The federal 25C and 25D credits ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025.
