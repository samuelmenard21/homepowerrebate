Heat Pump & Solar Rebates for Glendale Homeowners

Glendale is served by Glendale Water & Power (GWP), the city's own utility — not LADWP. GWP raised many rebates on November 1, 2025. Here's what a Glendale homeowner can actually get, checked against GWP's rebate page.

Updated September 28, 2026 · Independent guide. We don't sell your info. Amounts are from Glendale Water & Power's own pages and state program sites.

From Brand Boulevard apartments to homes up in the Verdugos, GWP's rebates reward one thing most: switching gas equipment to electric.

Short answer: Glendale Water & Power pays $1,000 per ton, up to $5,000, when a heat pump replaces a gas furnace, and $500 per ton, up to $2,500, when it replaces an old heat pump. Swapping a gas water heater for a heat pump model pays $4,000 for a limited time, and a needed panel upgrade adds $1,000. These are among the biggest city-utility rebates in LA County.

Is Glendale in LADWP territory?

No. Glendale Water & Power is the city's own utility, and SoCalGas supplies gas. Glendale is not Burbank either — the two city utilities sit side by side but run very different rebate lists.

Why does GWP pay two different heat pump amounts?

You may have seen both “$1,000 per ton” and “$500 per ton” for Glendale. Both are real. GWP pays the higher amount when you get rid of gas:

  • Replacing a gas furnace (or gas-and-electric system) with an ENERGY STAR heat pump: $1,000/ton, up to $5,000, paid by check.
  • Replacing an existing heat pump with a newer one: $500/ton, up to $2,500, as a bill credit.

For a 4-ton system replacing a gas furnace, that's $4,000. Add the $1,000 panel rebate if your panel needs an upgrade. GWP rebate page →

What's the smartest order to do upgrades in Glendale?

Because GWP's panel rebate only pays when paired with a heat pump or heat pump water heater, many families do these together. A gas-to-heat-pump water heater swap ($4,000) plus a 3-ton heat pump ($3,000) plus a panel ($1,000) is $8,000 in GWP rebates. The $4,000 water heater amount is marked “limited time,” so check it's still open before you book.

All GWP rebates must be filed within 12 months of purchase, and they're first come, first served until funds run out.

What about solar in Glendale?

GWP's current rebate list doesn't include solar panels or batteries. As a city utility, GWP sets its own net metering rules — it isn't on the state's NEM 3.0 rules that apply to SCE. Ask GWP for its current solar export terms before you compare quotes, and remember the 30% federal credit ended December 31, 2025.

What statewide programs apply in Glendale?

  • TECH Clean California: single-family heat pump money is fully reserved; waitlist only.
  • HEEHRA (income-qualified heat pump and appliance rebates): fully reserved statewide since Feb 24, 2026.
  • Federal 25C and 25D tax credits: ended for equipment placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.
  • SGIP battery incentives: mainly for income-qualified or high-fire-risk homes, and often waitlisted.

What should I do next?

  1. Check your bill: it should say Glendale Water & Power. If it does, the amounts above apply.
  2. Pick one upgrade and read its guide from the table.
  3. Get two or three written quotes that list the model number and efficiency ratings.
  4. Confirm the rebate is still open on the program page before you sign.
  5. Keep your invoice and permit — you'll need both to apply.

Common questions about Glendale rebates

Does LADWP serve Glendale?

No. Glendale Water & Power (GWP) is Glendale's own utility, and SoCalGas provides gas. LADWP rebates don't apply.

How much is the Glendale heat pump rebate?

$1,000 per ton, up to $5,000, if the heat pump replaces gas heating. $500 per ton, up to $2,500, if it replaces an existing heat pump. Units need 15.2 SEER2 and 7.8 HSPF2.

Is the $4,000 GWP water heater rebate real?

Yes, as of September 2026. GWP pays $4,000 for an ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater that replaces a natural gas water heater. It's a limited-time amount and does not apply to replacing an electric tank.

Does GWP give a smart thermostat or EV charger rebate?

Neither appears on GWP's current rebate list. Call GWP at 855-550-4497 to ask before you buy.

How long do I have to apply?

GWP rebates must be submitted within 12 months of purchase.
