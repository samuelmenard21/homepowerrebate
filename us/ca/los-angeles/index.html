Quick Questions

Is LADWP rebate better than PG&E?
Yes, significantly. LADWP pays $2,500/ton. PG&E pays $1,500–$2,000/ton. LADWP also stacks multiple programs. A 3.5-ton system in LA nets $9,170–$10,470. The same system in PG&E territory nets $5,250–$7,000.
How much does heat pump installation cost in LA?
Typically $10,000–$15,000 for a whole-home heat pump system. After LADWP's $10,000+ rebate, your out-of-pocket is $0–$5,000. Add the federal IRA tax credit (up to $3,200) and you might get money back.
Do I need pre-approval?
Yes. LADWP requires you to apply before work starts. Your contractor can help you submit. Approval takes 1–2 weeks. Once approved, you're guaranteed the rebate amount.
Can I do LADWP + federal IRA?
Yes. They stack. LADWP rebate reduces your cost. Federal IRA credit (up to $3,200) is claimed on your taxes. Together they can nearly cover the full installation.
What if my LADWP rebate gets rejected?
Common reasons: Contractor isn't LADWP-approved, system specs don't match the form, or the home isn't LADWP territory. Check your address first at ladwp.com. Make sure your contractor is on LADWP's approved list.
