LADWP Heat Pump Rebate (Los Angeles)

Los Angeles is served by LADWP (Los Angeles Department of Water and Power). LADWP pays $2,500 per ton of cooling capacity. A typical residential heat pump is 3–4 tons.

  • 3-ton system: $2,500 × 3 = $7,500 rebate
  • 3.5-ton system: $2,500 × 3.5 = $8,750 rebate
  • 4-ton system: $2,500 × 4 = $10,000 rebate

Plus: Smart thermostat rebate ($145) + SoCalGas heat pump rebate ($75) = $220 extra.

How to Claim Your Rebate

  1. Call LADWP at 1-800-342-5397 or visit ladwp.com/rebates
  2. Confirm your address is LADWP territory
  3. Get quotes from LADWP-approved contractors
  4. Apply before work starts
  5. Get approval (1–2 weeks)
  6. Contractor installs the system
  7. LADWP inspects and verifies
  8. Rebate applied (6–8 weeks after completion)
