Smart Thermostat Rebates in Los Angeles

LADWP pays you to let it nudge your thermostat on the hottest summer afternoons. Power Savers gives a $55 prepaid card when you are accepted and up to $90 more each year, and there is a separate rebate of up to $100 when you buy an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat through LADWP's marketplace.

What LADWP pays

These figures come from LADWP's Demand Response Power Savers page, which we read on October 2, 2026. The 2026 season runs June 1 to October 31. You need an approved connected thermostat tied to a central air conditioner.

Open

Enrollment

$55

A prepaid card, one per household, after you are accepted into the program.

Open

High participation

$90 a year

For taking part in more than 75% of the season's events.

Open

$40 a year

For taking part in 25% to 75% of events. Under 25% earns no annual incentive.

Open

New thermostat

Up to $100

A rebate on an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat bought through LADWP's Efficient Product Marketplace.

The most it can pay

$55 + $90 = $145 in the first season if you take part in most events, then up to $90 a year after that. Add up to $100 for buying a qualifying thermostat and the first year can reach $245. You can opt out of any event from your phone, a browser or the thermostat, so the incentive is optional but the discipline is yours.

What an event feels like

LADWP can raise your thermostat setting by up to 4°F during a high-demand window of 1 p.m. to 10 p.m., for up to 4 hours at a time and up to 30 times a season, and it excludes holidays. It may pre-cool the house by up to 3°F before an event so you stay comfortable. Your thermostat returns to its earlier setting once the event ends. If you work from home on hot afternoons, decide whether you can live with it before you enroll.

Which thermostats work

LADWP publishes an approved list by provider, including ecobee, Sensi, Honeywell Home, Google Nest, Lux, Alarm.com and Vivint models. It must be configured for central air conditioning, and LADWP allows one thermostat per household or business. Check your model against the list before you enroll or buy. Baseboard and mini-split systems are not what this program describes, so confirm yours is a central system.

Enrollment takes patience

LADWP warns of a delay of up to 10 weeks before it can confirm your participation. Enroll early in the year rather than in September, since the 2026 season ends October 31 and the annual incentive depends on how many events you join.

Your utility: LADWP

LADWP's Power Savers pays more than a flat check: up to $145 a season against Burbank's up to $75 (Burbank) and Pasadena's $50 (Pasadena). It is also a trade-off, because it asks something of you. Los Angeles's average January low over 2015 to 2024 was 7.9°C, so the value here is in summer cooling.

How to join Power Savers

  1. Check that your thermostat is on LADWP's approved list and connected to a central air conditioner.
  2. If you want a new one, buy an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat through LADWP's Efficient Product Marketplace for up to $100 back.
  3. Enroll on the Power Savers page.
  4. Wait for LADWP to confirm your participation, which can take up to 10 weeks.
  5. Receive the $55 prepaid card once you are accepted.
  6. Join most events during the June to October season for the larger annual incentive.

Common questions

How much does Power Savers pay?

A $55 prepaid card on acceptance, plus $40 or $90 a year depending on how many events you join, for up to $145 in the first season.

How much can I get for buying a thermostat?

Up to $100 on an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat bought through LADWP's Efficient Product Marketplace.

How often will LADWP adjust my thermostat?

Up to 30 events a season, in a 1 p.m. to 10 p.m. window, up to 4 hours each and up to 4°F. You can opt out of any event.

Which thermostats qualify?

Models on LADWP's approved list from providers such as ecobee, Sensi, Honeywell Home, Google Nest, Lux, Alarm.com and Vivint.

When does the season run?

June 1 to October 31, 2026.
