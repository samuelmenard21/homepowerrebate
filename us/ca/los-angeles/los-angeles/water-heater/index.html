Heat Pump Water Heater Rebates in Los Angeles

LADWP pays between $1,500 and $2,500 for a heat pump water heater, and the amount depends on the tank size and the unit's efficiency (UEF). It is a rebate you can plan the purchase around, because the tiers are published.

Choosing a size

A typical family tank of 50 gallons at 3.5 UEF earns $1,750. A larger 80-gallon tank at the same UEF earns $2,250, but a bigger tank costs more and takes more space. Size the tank for your household and let the rebate follow.

Does the old heater have to be gas?

LADWP's rebate table is keyed to the pre-existing gas water heater's capacity and UEF, which suggests the program is built around gas replacement. The page does not state in so many words that an electric tank is excluded, so ask LADWP before you buy if your old heater is electric. This is a difference from Glendale, where the heater must replace gas (Glendale).

Compared with the neighbors

Glendale pays $4,000, Burbank $1,500 and Pasadena $500 (Burbank, Pasadena). LADWP's tiers range from $1,500 to $2,500, so a large, efficient tank can pay more than Burbank's flat amount. Each utility sets its own list, so the one on your electric bill decides.

Permits and where it goes

LADWP asks for a final approved permit from the Los Angeles Department of Building and Safety. A heat pump water heater needs room to draw air and a drain for condensate, and it is taller than most gas tanks. With Los Angeles's average January low of 7.9°C over 2015 to 2024, a garage is rarely too cold. Very hot or tight spaces are the more common problem, so ask the installer for the model's allowed temperature range.

Your utility: LADWP

LADWP is the city's own utility and runs this rebate. Applications go in online through your LADWP account or by mail, within 12 months of the purchase date, and LADWP can inspect before it pays. The program line is (866) 484-0433.

Other money

The federal 25C and 25D tax credits ended for spending after December 31, 2025, even where LADWP's page still mentions a federal credit. Statewide help from TECH Clean California and the federal HEEHRA program is fully reserved and takes new requests on hold only.

How to claim the LADWP rebate

  1. Check that LADWP is your electric utility, and that this is not new construction, an addition or an ADU.
  2. Choose a unit with a UEF of at least 3.3 and a capacity of at least 30 gallons.
  3. Install it under a Building and Safety permit and get the permit finaled.
  4. Collect the paid itemized invoice, the manufacturer's specification sheet and the final permit.
  5. Apply through your LADWP account or by mail within 12 months of the purchase date.
  6. LADWP may inspect before it sends the check.

Common questions

How much is the LADWP water heater rebate?

$1,500 to $2,500, depending on tank size and UEF.

What is the minimum efficiency?

A UEF of at least 3.3 and a capacity of at least 30 gallons.

Is a permit required?

Yes, a final approved Building and Safety permit.

Can I claim more than one?

The limit is one per residence, though LADWP says homes or multi-residential buildings may claim more if they apply at one time.

Does it apply to new construction?

No. New construction, additions and ADUs are not eligible.
