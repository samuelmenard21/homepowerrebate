Short answer: In 2026, Pasadena Water and Power pays $170 per ton for a heat pump (about $510 on a 3-ton system), $500 for a heat pump water heater, and $600 for a Wi-Fi Level 2 EV charger. Is Pasadena in LADWP territory?

No. Pasadena has run its own city-owned electric utility, Pasadena Water and Power, for over a century. Your gas comes from SoCalGas. That means LADWP's big per-ton heat pump rebate, which you'll see on lots of LA pages, does not apply to a Pasadena address.

PWP's rebates are smaller per item, but a few have a twist you won't find elsewhere: a small buy-in-Pasadena bonus on heat pumps, water heaters, thermostats and insulation, and a double heat pump rebate ($340/ton) for families on PWP's Bill Payment Assistance program.

Is solar worth it in Pasadena now that the federal credit is gone?

It's closer than it was in 2025. The 30% federal credit ended for systems installed after December 31, 2025. But PWP's new pilot pays $0.60 per watt, so a 6 kW system earns about $3,600 back ($6,000 at the income-qualified $1.00/W rate). PWP also waives its plan-check and inspection fees during the pilot.

PWP is a city utility, so it is not on the state's NEM 3.0 net billing rules that apply to SCE customers. How PWP credits the power you send back is set by the city — ask PWP for the current terms before you trust any installer's payback math. PWP solar and battery pilot →

Should a Pasadena family add a battery?

The January 2025 Eaton Fire burned through Altadena, right on Pasadena's northern edge. Many families here now care about backup power as much as bill savings. PWP's pilot pays up to $550 per kWh of battery, in tiers. On a typical 13.5 kWh battery, the top tier would be about $7,400, but tiers can fill up — confirm which tier is open with PWP before you sign a contract.

How do Pasadena heat pump rebates actually add up?

Here's an honest example for a 3-ton heat pump replacing a gas furnace and old AC:

  • PWP base: 3 × $170 = $510
  • Bought from a Pasadena business: +$60
  • On Bill Payment Assistance: +$510 more

That's $510–$1,080 — real money, but a small share of a full system. The bigger reason to switch in Pasadena is summer: a heat pump is also a high-efficiency air conditioner, and the San Gabriel Valley gets hot. PWP heat pump rebate rules →

What statewide programs apply in Pasadena?

  • TECH Clean California: single-family heat pump money is fully reserved; waitlist only.
  • HEEHRA (income-qualified heat pump and appliance rebates): fully reserved statewide since Feb 24, 2026.
  • Federal 25C and 25D tax credits: ended for equipment placed in service after Dec 31, 2025.
  • SGIP battery incentives: mainly for income-qualified or high-fire-risk homes, and often waitlisted.

Common questions about Pasadena rebates

Does LADWP serve Pasadena?

No. Pasadena Water and Power (PWP) is the city's own electric utility, and SoCalGas provides gas. LADWP rebates do not apply to Pasadena homes.

How much is the Pasadena heat pump rebate?

PWP pays $170 per ton, plus $20 per ton if you buy in Pasadena, plus another $170 per ton if you're on PWP's Bill Payment Assistance program. A 3-ton system gets about $510 at the base rate.

Does Pasadena have a solar rebate in 2026?

Yes. PWP launched a solar and battery pilot in April 2026: $0.60 per watt for rooftop solar ($1.00 per watt for income-qualified customers) and tiered battery incentives up to $550 per kWh, while funds last.

Can I still get the federal tax credit for solar or a heat pump?

No. The federal 25C and 25D credits ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025.

How long do I have to apply for a PWP rebate?

Most PWP rebates must be submitted within 180 days of purchase, after the work is done. You apply through your PWP account.
