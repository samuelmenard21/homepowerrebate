Appliance Rebates in Santa Monica

Appliance rebates in Santa Monica 2026: SCE Marketplace deals and free ESA upgrades for income-qualified owners and renters. Not LADWP.

How much you get

Santa Monica is SCE for electricity and SoCalGas for gas. SCE doesn't publish one flat appliance rebate; the SCE Marketplace shows instant deals that change by product and month. SoCalGas lists its own offers on its rebates page.

Most Santa Monica households rent. Renters who are income-qualified can apply to SCE's Energy Savings Assistance program for no-cost upgrades, with the landlord's OK.

Source: SCE rebates & Marketplace (checked Sept 28, 2026)

Choosing appliances for a Santa Monica apartment or condo

Most Santa Monica homes are apartments or condos, so space and venting decide more than price. A ventless heat pump dryer fits where you can't add an outside duct, and it uses about half the energy of a standard dryer. Expect loads to take longer.

Thinking about induction? It only works with magnetic pots (cast iron, most stainless steel). Many older buildings have only a gas line at the stove, so ask your landlord or HOA if a 240-volt circuit can be added before you buy a range. A plug-in single-burner induction cooktop is an easy first step for renters.

Honest tip: if your fridge or washer still works, a rebate rarely makes it worth replacing early.

How to claim it

  1. Confirm your appliance model is on the current qualifying list before you buy: not every ENERGY STAR-labeled unit clears every program's bar
  2. Purchase from a retailer that carries a qualifying model, keeping your dated receipt
  3. File the claim the way SCE requires: some programs apply the discount instantly at checkout, others need an online or mailed application after purchase

Common questions

Are there appliance rebates in Santa Monica?

Yes, but they vary. Check the SCE Marketplace and SoCalGas rebates page before you buy. Income-qualified homes can get free upgrades through SCE ESA.

Can renters in Santa Monica get appliance help?

Yes. Income-qualified renters can apply to SCE Energy Savings Assistance with their landlord's permission.

Why don't LADWP rebates apply in Santa Monica?

Santa Monica gets electricity from Southern California Edison, not LADWP.
