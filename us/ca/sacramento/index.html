Why SMUD Pays More Than Any Other California Utility

SMUD covers more upgrade types with higher per-unit rebates than any other California utility. One Sacramento homeowner stacked rebates across heat pump, heat pump water heater, EV charger, and battery storage.

Here's how one Sacramento homeowner got $9,750+: Heat pump $3,000. Heat pump water heater $2,500. EV charger $1,500. Smart thermostat $300. Battery storage $1,800. Plus federal IRA credits up to $3,200. Total: $12,300 in rebates before federal tax credit. Out-of-pocket after SMUD: nearly $0 for a whole-home heat pump system.

SMUD's advantage: They don't just rebate heat pumps. They rebate everything: heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, solar, battery, insulation, EV chargers, and smart thermostats. Stack them all and you can cover 80–100% of your upgrade costs.

How to Claim Your SMUD Rebate

  1. Visit smud.org or call 1-888-742-7683 to check eligibility
  2. Get quotes from SMUD-certified contractors
  3. Submit rebate application before work begins
  4. Get approval (1–3 weeks)
  5. Contractor installs the upgrades
  6. SMUD inspects and verifies completion
  7. Rebate applied (4–8 weeks after completion)

Quick Questions

Why are SMUD rebates so high?
SMUD is a public utility owned by Sacramento County residents, not shareholders. They reinvest profits into customer benefits. They also cover more upgrade types than most utilities, so your total rebate can add up quickly.
How does SMUD compare to BC Hydro?
SMUD ($3,000 for heat pump) pays less per unit than BC Hydro ($4,000–$5,000). But SMUD covers more upgrade types. If you stack all five upgrades, SMUD's total ($9,100+) is close to BC Hydro's top tier.
When do rebates arrive?
Typically 4–8 weeks after SMUD inspects the completed work. Total timeline from application to cash: 8–14 weeks. Contractor can sometimes apply the rebate at point of sale, reducing your up-front cost.
Can I do SMUD + federal IRA?
Yes. They stack completely. SMUD's $3,000 heat pump rebate reduces your cost. Federal IRA credit (up to $3,200 for heat pump) is claimed on your taxes. Together they can cover most or all of the installation.
What if my SMUD rebate gets rejected?
Common reasons: System specs don't meet SMUD's requirements, contractor isn't SMUD-certified, or your home isn't SMUD territory. Check your address at smud.org. Verify your contractor is SMUD-certified before hiring.
