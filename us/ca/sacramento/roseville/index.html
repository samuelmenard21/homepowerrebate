SMUD Heat Pump Rebates in Roseville

Roseville is served by SMUD. SMUD covers multiple upgrade types with strong per-unit rebates.

  • Heat pump (base): $3,000
  • Heat pump water heater: $2,500
  • EV charger (Level 2): $1,000–$1,500
  • Solar (per kW): up to $1,200
  • Battery storage: $1,000–$1,800
  • Smart thermostat: $300

Stack multiple upgrades to maximize your rebate. A heat pump + water heater combo nets $5,500 in SMUD rebates.

How to Claim Your SMUD Rebate

  1. Call SMUD at 1-888-742-7683 or visit smud.org
  2. Get quotes from SMUD-certified contractors
  3. Submit rebate application before work starts
  4. Get approval (1–3 weeks)
  5. Contractor installs the system
  6. SMUD inspects and verifies
  7. Rebate applied (4–8 weeks after completion)
