How to Claim Your SMUD Rebate

  1. Call SMUD at 1-888-742-7683 or visit smud.org
  2. Confirm your address is SMUD territory
  3. Get quotes from SMUD-certified contractors
  4. Submit rebate application before work starts
  5. Get approval (1–3 weeks)
  6. Contractor installs the system
  7. SMUD inspects and verifies
  8. Rebate applied (4–8 weeks after completion)
