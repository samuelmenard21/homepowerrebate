Smart Thermostat Rewards in San Diego

San Diego Community Power pays $50 for each approved smart thermostat you enroll, up to three, and up to $25 a year for letting it nudge your settings on high-demand days. It is a pilot program that pays you to use a thermostat you own, not a discount on buying one.

What San Diego Community Power pays

These figures come from the Smart Energy Rewards page of San Diego Community Power (SDCP), which we read on October 2, 2026. You must be an SDCP residential customer who already owns an eligible smart device.

Open

Upfront incentive

$50 per thermostat

For each approved smart thermostat you enroll, up to three.

Open

Participation incentive

Up to $25 a year

For taking part in Smart Flex Events. You stop earning it if you unenroll.

Open

Event window

June to October

Events happen between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m., up to five times a week and no more than 20 times a year, not on weekends or federal holidays unless the state calls a Flex Alert.

What it adds up to

Three approved thermostats earn $150 upfront, and up to $75 a year after that. For one thermostat, $50 up front and up to $25 a year. It does not pay for the thermostat itself, so if you do not have an eligible one yet, check the approved model list before you buy.

What an event means for you

When SDCP expects unusually high demand, it calls a Smart Flex Event, and your thermostat warms the house by a few degrees so the air conditioner runs less. It may pre-cool the home up to an hour before. You get a notification when an event is scheduled, and you can override the setting at any time, though overriding often may reduce your participation incentive. If someone at home depends on steady cooling, think about that before enrolling.

Eligibility details

The thermostat must stay connected to the internet and directly control an operational air-conditioning unit or system. You cannot be in another demand response program at the same time, such as the Emergency Load Reduction Program or Demand Side Grid Support. Check SDCP's list of eligible models before you enroll or buy. The program is a pilot, so terms can change.

Your utility: SDCP and SDG&E

SDCP sets this program for customers in the City of San Diego, Chula Vista, Encinitas, Imperial Beach, La Mesa, National City and unincorporated San Diego County. SDG&E's own rebates page lists a $75 coupon for an ENERGY STAR smart thermostat, but says its retail offerings have ended (SDG&E rebates). San Diego's average January low over 2015 to 2024 was 8.8°C, so cooling matters far more than heating here.

Compared with other utilities

LADWP's Power Savers pays up to $145 in the first season (Los Angeles), Burbank pays up to $75 (Burbank) and SMUD gives $50 at its online store (Sacramento). SDCP's $50 is in the same range.

How to join Smart Energy Rewards

  1. Confirm that San Diego Community Power supplies your electricity.
  2. Check that your thermostat is on SDCP's eligible model list and controls a working air conditioner.
  3. Sign up your thermostat on the Smart Energy Rewards page.
  4. Leave it connected to the internet.
  5. Receive $50 for each approved thermostat, up to three.
  6. Earn up to $25 a year by taking part in Smart Flex Events.

Common questions

How much does SDCP pay for a smart thermostat?

$50 upfront for each approved thermostat, up to three, plus up to $25 a year for participating.

Does it pay for the thermostat?

No. You must already own an eligible smart thermostat. The incentive is for enrolling it.

When do events happen?

From June to October, between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m., up to five times a week and no more than 20 times a year.

Can I override an event?

Yes, but frequent overrides may reduce your participation incentive.

Does it work in Escondido?

No. Escondido is not an SDCP member.
