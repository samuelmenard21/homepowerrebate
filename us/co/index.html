Estimate your rebates

Short answer: Xcel Energy pays $750 per heating ton for a cold-climate heat pump, and $1,500 per ton for homes heated with Xcel gas from October 1, 2026, which we could not yet confirm is live. A heat pump water heater gets $750 or $1,500, a smart thermostat $50 and a home EV charger up to $500. Colorado Springs Utilities pays $1,500 to $3,000 for a heat pump, and Fort Collins Utilities pays $500 per ton up to $3,000. Solar has no upfront rebate for most homes in 2026, and the federal credits ended after 2025.

Which utility serves you?

Colorado's rebate landscape splits mainly along utility lines. Xcel Energy is the state's dominant investor-owned utility and covers most of the Front Range, including Denver, Aurora, and Boulder. Two of our five covered cities run their own municipal utilities with a separate rebate menu: Colorado Springs Utilities (Colorado Springs) and Fort Collins Utilities, whose rebates run through the regional Efficiency Works program. Heat pump

Xcel Energy heat pump rebate + state tax credit

Up to $9,000

Xcel Energy pays $2,250 per ton of heating capacity for a qualifying cold-climate heat pump (up to $9,000 on a 4-ton system), plus Colorado's separate $1,000 state heat pump tax credit. Front Range homeowners can also stack DRCOG's Power Ahead Colorado rebate (up to $1,500).

Source: /us/co/denver · Xcel Energy / Colorado Energy Office
Solar

Xcel Energy Solar Rewards

$300/kW, up to $1,500

Xcel Energy's Solar Rewards program pays $300 per kW of installed solar capacity, capped at $1,500 per home. The federal 30% residential solar tax credit expired for installations after December 31, 2025.

Source: /us/co/denver · Xcel Energy
Battery storage

Xcel Renewable Battery Connect + state credit

Up to $5,000 + 10% state credit

Xcel Energy pays $250 per kW (up to $5,000) plus $100 per year, in exchange for letting Xcel dispatch up to 60% of your stored energy during peak demand. Colorado's 10% Innovative Energy Storage state tax credit stacks on top statewide, regardless of utility.

Source: /us/co/denver · Xcel Energy / Colorado Energy Office
Water heater

Xcel Energy heat pump water heater rebate

$2,250 flat

Xcel Energy pays a flat $2,250 rebate for a qualifying heat pump water heater, with no income qualification required for the standard rebate.

Source: /us/co/denver · Xcel Energy
Insulation

Xcel Energy insulation + multi-measure bonus

Up to $600 + 25% bonus

Xcel Energy pays up to $600 for qualifying attic and wall insulation, plus a 25% bonus across all standard efficiency rebates when you install three or more qualifying measures within two years of enrolling.

Source: /us/co/denver · Xcel Energy

Windows & doors has no confirmed dedicated Colorado rebate as of 2026, and the federal 25C credit that used to cover it expired for installs after December 31, 2025. Amounts shown are Xcel Energy's — Colorado Springs Utilities and Fort Collins Utilities run separate programs at different rates.

Quick Questions

Read the questions and answers

Why did Xcel Energy stop taking rebate applications?

Xcel's Colorado rebate budgets are set annually and split into cycles. When a cycle's funding runs out, Xcel has to pause new applications in that category until the next cycle opens or more funding is added — it isn't a sign the rebate is gone for good. This has happened before with high-demand categories in Colorado. As of this writing, Xcel's heat pump rebate applications remain open (through September 30, 2027), but funding availability can shift during the year, so it's worth applying and locking in your rebate before you start work rather than waiting.

Should I install my heat pump before October 1?

If you're set on a cold-climate heat pump and can get it invoiced before October 1, 2026, yes — it's worth the push. Xcel Energy's cold-climate heat pump rebate drops about 33% on that date, from $2,250 per ton of heating capacity down to $1,500 per ton. On a typical 3-ton system that's roughly $6,750 versus $4,500. To lock in the higher amount, the installation needs to be complete and invoiced before October 1, with your application submitted by October 15. If you can't make that timeline, the lower rate still stacks with Colorado's $1,000 state heat pump tax credit — confirm the exact current numbers with Xcel before signing a contract, since utility rebate amounts can change again.

Is my contractor's rebate quote a scam?

It can be, if the promised rebate is doing most of the work to make an inflated price look reasonable. Check the real rebate cap before you sign →
