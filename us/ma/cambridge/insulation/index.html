Insulation Rebates in Cambridge

Two out of three Cambridge households rent, and nearly half the homes are from before 1940. Insulation here is often a landlord or condo association decision. Mass Save pays 75%, and up to 100% when a whole small building joins in.

Working it out

MassCEC's example for a 2,000 square foot home: $4,000 of insulation and air sealing before incentives, $1,000 after the 75% Mass Save discount. If the household qualifies by income, or the home is in a 1-4 unit building where every owner joins in, the cost can drop to zero. The 0% HEAT Loan can spread the rest over up to 7 years.

Cambridge housing at a glance

The US Census Bureau's survey (2020 to 2024) counts 54,840 homes in Cambridge. About 47% were built before 1940. Only 10% are single-family detached, and 29% are in two- to four-unit buildings. About 67% of households rent.

Getting a small building to say yes

MassCEC says the cost can drop sharply when a 1-4 unit building is weatherized as one project with every owner on board. In a Cambridge two- or three-family split into condos, one owner can't insulate the shared attic or walls alone. Book the free assessment for the building, share the report, and agree on the work together.

If you rent, you can still book the assessment and pass the results to your landlord.

How the Mass Save insulation offer works

You don't apply for an insulation rebate on your own. You book a free Home Energy Assessment with Mass Save, by phone at 866-527-7283 or online. An energy specialist looks at your attic, walls and basement, and recommends work. If you go ahead, a Mass Save contractor does it, and the discount comes off the bill. You don't pay full price and wait for a cheque.

Renters and landlords can book an assessment too. A renter can get the visit, but the owner signs off on insulation. Mass Save also has a separate track for buildings with five or more units.

How to get insulation rebates in Cambridge

  1. Book a free Mass Save Home Energy Assessment online or at 866-527-7283.
  2. If you might qualify by income, say so when you book. It can raise the discount to 100%.
  3. If you own a unit in a 1-4 unit building, ask the other owners to join. Doing the whole building together can also reach 100%.
  4. Let the energy specialist recommend the work, then pick a Mass Save contractor.
  5. Pay your share after the discount, or use the 0% HEAT Loan.

Common questions

How much does Mass Save pay for insulation in Cambridge?

75% off insulation, air sealing and weather stripping as standard. Up to 100% for income-qualified households or 1-4 unit buildings where all owners agree to one project.

Can one Cambridge condo owner insulate the attic?

Shared parts of a building, like an attic or outside walls, usually need the association's approval. Mass Save's largest discount for 1-4 unit buildings applies when all owners agree to one project.

Is the Mass Save home energy assessment really free?

Yes. Mass Save offers a no-cost assessment in person or by video, plus a no-cost online assessment.

Can renters get Mass Save insulation?

Renters can book the free assessment. The building owner has to agree to any insulation work.

Does Mass Save help with windows?

Only in one case on MassCEC's list: $75 per window for replacing single-pane windows with triple-pane windows. Insulation and air sealing get the larger discount.
