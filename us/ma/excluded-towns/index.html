Is Your Massachusetts Town Locked Out of Mass Save?

If you live in one of 41 Massachusetts towns, Mass Save can't help you — no heat pump rebate, no insulation discount, nothing. Here's the full list, why it happens, and what you can do instead.

Updated August 2026 · Independent guide.

What a Municipal Light Plant is

Most Massachusetts towns buy electricity from a big company like Eversource or National Grid. Those companies pay into a fund that becomes Mass Save's rebate money.

Some towns never joined that system. Instead, over 100 years ago, they built and still run their own local electric company, owned by the town itself. This is called a Municipal Light Plant, or MLP for short.

Because MLP towns don't pay into the Mass Save fund, Mass Save doesn't offer rebates there. It isn't a punishment — it's just how the funding works.

What to do if your town is on this list

1. Check Mass Save's gas side

If your natural gas comes from a Mass Save gas sponsor (not a town gas department), you may still get a no-cost home energy assessment, weatherization help, and heat pump rebates applied through your gas account. Note: the federal 25C and 25D tax credits ended for work done after December 31, 2025.

2. Check if your MLP runs its own program

Many Municipal Light Plants run smaller rebate programs of their own — usually less generous than Mass Save, but real money. Since there are 40-plus different MLPs, each with its own program details, we can't list every one here. Check your own town's municipal utility website directly for current offers.

3. A statewide zero-interest loan option

Massachusetts also runs a Municipal Light Plant zero-interest loan program, built specifically for homeowners in MLP towns who can't access the standard Mass Save HEAT Loan. The DOER MLP Z-Loan lends $500 to $25,000 at 0% interest for up to 5 years, for heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, insulation and weatherization recommended in a home energy assessment. Apply at myz-loan.com before work starts.

FAQ

Why can't my town use Mass Save?

Mass Save is funded by investor-owned utility companies like Eversource, National Grid, and Unitil. Towns with their own Municipal Light Plant don't pay into that fund, so Mass Save can't offer rebates there.

What is a Municipal Light Plant?

An electric utility owned and run by the town itself instead of a private company. Many were set up over a century ago and have stayed independent ever since.

Can I still get help if my town isn't eligible for Mass Save?

Some. The federal 25C and 25D tax credits ended after December 31, 2025. MLP customers can use the state's 0% MLP Z-Loan and the state solar tax credit. Many MLPs also run their own smaller rebate programs — check your town's municipal utility website directly.
