Massachusetts Home Energy Rebates 2026

Mass Save offers some of the most generous home energy rebates in North America — up to $9,500 for a heat pump, up to $25,000 if you're income-qualified, and 75–100% off insulation. Here's exactly what you get and how to claim it.

What you can claim in Massachusetts

Mass Save is a collaboration between Massachusetts's electric and gas utilities (Eversource, National Grid, Cape Light Compact, and others) that funds nearly all residential energy rebates in the state. Unlike Alberta's fragmented utility-by-utility system, Mass Save is a single, unified program — you don't need to track down five different agencies.

🔥 Heat Pump

$8,500–$9,500

$2,650/ton, capped at $8,500. Add $500 for Manual J sizing + $500 for weatherization. Income-qualified: up to $16,000 (air-source) or $25,000 (ground-source).

🧱 Insulation & Air Sealing

75–100% off

Mass Save covers 75% of attic, wall, basement, and air-sealing costs for everyone. Income-eligible households (under 60% AMI) pay $0.

☀️ Solar (SMART 3.0)

Per-kWh incentive

Locked-in production payment for 10–20 years once enrolled. Rate depends on your utility's current capacity block — earlier enrollment gets a better rate.

🔋 Battery Storage

$100/kWh + ongoing

A 15kWh battery earns a $1,500 capacity rebate, plus SMART storage adder ($0.04–0.06/kWh) and annual ConnectedSolutions demand-response payments.

💰 HEAT Loan

0% interest

Up to $25,000, 0% interest, 0% fees, 7-year term. Covers your out-of-pocket costs after rebates.

📋 Home Energy Assessment

Free

Required first step. An assessor checks your insulation, air sealing, and heating system, then confirms exactly what you qualify for.

How this differs from Canada

If you've used HomePowerRebate for a BC or Ontario home, the model is the same — we explain your rebates, then match you with vetted local installers. Two things are different in Massachusetts:

  • Currency: All amounts on this page are in US dollars (USD), not Canadian dollars.
  • Single program vs. utility-by-utility: Mass Save unifies most rebates under one program, unlike Alberta or Ontario where you sometimes stack federal, provincial, and municipal programs separately.
