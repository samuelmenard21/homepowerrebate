How much you get

Common questions

Does Massachusetts have a solar rebate for Somerville homeowners?

Not a flat rebate — SMART 3.0 pays a locked-in per-kWh production payment for 10 to 20 years once you're enrolled, on top of your own electricity savings.

Is solar worth it in Somerville?

Massachusetts gets decent sun for solar, and SMART 3.0's guaranteed per-kWh payment makes the economics more predictable than states relying on a one-time rebate alone.
