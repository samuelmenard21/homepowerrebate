Insulation Rebates in Grand Rapids

Grand Rapids has an old housing stock and a very high share of gas heat, which makes insulation the best fit. Consumers Energy pays $125 to $200 for attic insulation by how high your R-value goes, $125 for walls and $50 for basement walls and rim joists. We read the chart on October 6, 2026.

Why insulation fits Grand Rapids

The Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020 to 2024) says 75.7% of Grand Rapids homes were built before 1980 and 37.5% before 1940. Utility gas heats 82.5% of homes. When gas heat leaks out through a thin attic, you pay for it all winter. Adding R-value is one of the few upgrades that works with the furnace you already have.

The R-value ladder

Consumers pays more as you aim higher. Going from R-48 to R-60 or more raises the attic rebate from $125 to $200 for a gas customer. That is a $75 difference, which will not decide a job, but it is a good reason to ask your contractor to quote the higher level, since the cost to add more material is often small during the same visit.

Small multi-unit buildings

16.1% of Grand Rapids homes are in buildings of two to four units. Consumers' rules cover separately metered single-family homes and multifamily dwellings with fewer than three units. A duplex qualifies, and a three-unit building does not. Ask Consumers if your building counts.

Plan the work around the weather

Insulation crews can work in most seasons, but an attic is easiest to reach when it is not freezing or hot. Booking in autumn leaves time before the December 31 deadline and before the busiest weeks. Ask the contractor for a start date and a filing date in writing, so a late application does not cost you the rebate.

Free help comes first

The free Consumers home energy analysis shows where heat escapes. If your household income is at or below 200% of the federal poverty level, Michigan's weatherization program installs insulation and air sealing at no cost. The state's MiHER program is paused in the Grand Rapids district for new income-qualified applications.

How to claim insulation rebates in Grand Rapids

  1. Book the free home energy analysis, or get two written insulation quotes with area and R-value.
  2. Use a contractor on Consumers' participating list.
  3. Ask for the highest R-value your attic can hold, to reach the higher rebate row.
  4. Make sure the contractor files the application by December 31.
  5. Keep your copy of the application, since Consumers advises you to.

Common questions

How much does Consumers pay for attic insulation?

$125 to $200 for gas and combination customers, depending on the R-value. Electric-only customers get $50 to $70.

What is the deadline?

Consumers' chart lists December 31 for insulation and window rebates.

Are windows worth it?

The rebate is $15 per window, which is small. Windows are rarely the cheapest way to cut a heating bill.

Is there free help for lower incomes?

Yes. The state weatherization program covers households at or below 200% of the federal poverty level.

Does the federal insulation credit apply?

No. It ended for work finished after December 31, 2025.
