Central Hudson Service Territory

Central Hudson Gas & Electric serves the Mid-Hudson region, including Poughkeepsie (the regional anchor), Newburgh, Kingston, and surrounding Hudson Valley communities. Unlike NYSEG (central NY) or National Grid (upstate), Central Hudson's territory is known for waterfront communities, college towns, and a thriving commercial hub.

Central Hudson + HEAR + State Stacking

Mid-Hudson heat pump economics

The opportunity: Central Hudson homeowners can stack utility rebates with state and federal incentives. Combined, three programs deliver $12K–$18K in rebates for typical Mid-Hudson homes, cutting a $20K heat pump installation to $2K–$8K out of pocket.

Typical Central Hudson + state + federal path:

  • Central Hudson rebate: $3,000–$8,000 (fossil removal gets maximum)
  • NYS Clean Heat: $6,000–$10,000 (income & equipment-qualified)
  • Federal HEAR: $4,000–$8,000 (income-qualified, authorized through 2030 — confirm current funding before applying)

Total potential: $13,000–$26,000. Central Hudson homeowners typically see only $3K–$8K from the utility alone. Adding state + federal more than doubles it.

Your out-of-pocket: $0–$8,000 after stacking (vs. full $18,000–$22,000 equipment cost).

Energy savings: $800–$1,200 per year. Mid-Hudson winters are real—heat pumps work hard here. Paid for in 2–5 years.

Central Hudson Rebate Programs

Central Hudson offers rebates across all major home electrification categories. Below is what's currently available.

Heat Pump

  • Single-family >1,000 sq ft: $8,000 (fossil removal), $5,000 (no removal), $3,000 (adding to existing)
  • Apartments/<1,000 sq ft: $5,000 (fossil removal), $3,000 (no removal), $1,000 (adding to existing)

Heat Pump Water Heater

  • ENERGY STAR eligible: $1,250

Solar

  • NY-Sun Megawatt Block: $0.20–$0.40 per watt installed (varies by block status and region)
  • Affordable Solar (income-qualified): $0.80 per watt

Smart Thermostat

  • ENERGY STAR certified: $50

EV Charger

  • ChargeSmart residential program: $25 incentive after 3 months participation

Battery Storage

  • Residential programs: Up to 100% of eligible costs (research incomplete on specifics)

Insulation

  • Weatherization: Up to 30% savings potential (research incomplete on specific rebate amounts)

Windows

  • Program status: Research incomplete
Note: Natural gas equipment rebates ended January 1, 2026. Central Hudson is focusing on all-electric solutions. Verify all amounts with Central Hudson directly, as programs can change.

Why Mid-Hudson Matters for Heat Pumps

Poughkeepsie and the Mid-Hudson Valley experience genuine winter. Design temperatures drop to 5°F. Homes need serious heating—typically 600–900 kWh per month in winter. This is where heat pumps shine. Cold-climate air-source heat pumps work reliably even at 0°F, and the annual heating load justifies the upfront investment.

Plus, the Mid-Hudson region has Marist College (1,000+ employees) and Vassar College (highly ranked liberal arts school) anchoring the regional economy. That means stable local employment and year-round energy demand—perfect conditions for rebate programs to stay stable.
