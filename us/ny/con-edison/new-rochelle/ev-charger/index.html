EV Charger Rebates in New Rochelle

Here's exactly how the ev charger rebate works in New Rochelle (Con Edison territory), plus local installers to call.

How much you get

Con Edison does not appear to offer a direct purchase/hardware rebate for home Level 2 chargers. What it does offer is SmartCharge New York, an off-peak charging behavioral incentive worth up to roughly $400/year, plus a $0.10/kWh off-peak credit — that's a usage reward, not money toward buying the charger itself.

Con Edison's PowerReady program, which offset some infrastructure costs, stopped accepting new Level 2 residential-adjacent applications as of April 22, 2026.

Sources: Con Edison SmartCharge FAQ, Con Edison PowerReady

Administered by: Con Edison SmartCharge New York / PowerReady

New Rochelle-specific note

No New Rochelle-specific program was found beyond the standard Con Edison/state programs above and county-level Weatherization Assistance Program delivery.
