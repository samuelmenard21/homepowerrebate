Westchester County heat pump rebates for Yonkers homeowners: Con Edison + state + federal + DAC bonuses = $14K–$20K+ stacking.

Real Yonkers Example

Yonkers homeowner, $180K household income, DAC area:

  • Con Edison rebate: $6,000
  • NYS Clean Heat: $8,000
  • Federal HEAR: $4,000
  • DAC bonus: $1,500
  • Total rebates: $19,500

Heat pump cost: $20,000. Your out-of-pocket: $500 (plus taxes/installation variables). Energy savings: $1,200/year. Free heating after first year.

How to claim

  1. Check if your Yonkers address is in a DAC area (use mapping tool)
  2. Get Con Edison pre-approval (2–4 weeks)
  3. Apply to state Clean Heat and federal HEAR simultaneously
  4. Work scheduled after pre-approvals (1–3 days)
  5. Rebates arrive 4–8 weeks post-completion
