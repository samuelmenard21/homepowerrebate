Yonkers Heat Pump Rebates 2026: Con Edison

What Yonkers homeowners can really get in 2026, checked against Con Edison and NYSERDA on September 26, 2026.

Short answer: In Yonkers, the main heat pump rebate is NYS Clean Heat (run by Con Edison): up to $10,000 ($11,000 in a Disadvantaged Community) for a whole-home system, taken off your contractor’s bill. It is one rebate — not several that add up. Lower-income households can get much more through EmPower+, and the federal tax credits ended on December 31, 2025.

Con Edison takes the incentive off your contractor’s invoice, so you never wait for a cheque. Your home must pass a “service adequate” check first.

What makes Yonkers different

Yonkers is in Westchester County, where Con Edison supplies both electricity and gas. Much of the housing is older two- and three-family homes, and Con Edison’s program covers 1–4 family buildings, so an owner-occupied two-family can qualify.

Yonkers has a lot of two- and three-family houses from the early 1900s, many heated by oil or gas boilers with radiators. Ductless mini-splits are the usual fit because there are no ducts to reuse, and each unit may need its own outdoor condenser.

Because the Con Edison program covers 1–4 family buildings, an owner who lives in one unit of a two-family can often do both units in one project. Ask whether your contractor is pricing the single-family or apartment tier for each unit.

Hilly streets and tight side yards are common, so ask where the outdoor units will sit and how loud they are next to your neighbour’s windows.

What does not stack

Clean Heat is the Con Edison rebate, so never add the two together. Clean Heat is capped at 70% of cost (85% in a DAC). There is no separate federal HEAR cheque — New York delivers it through EmPower+. The federal 25C/25D tax credits ended December 31, 2025.

Income-qualified? EmPower+ can cover far more than the utility rebate. Check the state DAC map too — it can raise your amount.

EV chargers in Yonkers

Con Edison has no rebate for buying a home charger. Its SmartCharge New York program pays about $400 a year on average for charging off-peak. PowerReady stopped taking new Level 2 applications on April 22, 2026.

What to do next in Yonkers

  1. If you own a two- or three-family, ask for one quote covering every unit.
  2. Confirm with the contractor that your electrical panel can take the new load.
  3. Get 2–3 quotes and ask each contractor to show the rebate tier on the quote.

Common questions

How much is the heat pump rebate in Yonkers?

NYS Clean Heat (run by Con Edison) pays up to $10,000 ($11,000 in a Disadvantaged Community) for a whole-home heat pump in Yonkers, depending on home size, whether you remove the old system, and DAC status.

Can I do a two-family house in Yonkers?

Yes. Con Edison's residential program covers 1-4 family buildings, so you can usually include every unit. Each unit is priced at the tier that fits it, so ask your contractor to show the math.

Is the federal tax credit still available for heat pumps?

No. The federal 25C and 25D credits ended for anything installed after December 31, 2025.
