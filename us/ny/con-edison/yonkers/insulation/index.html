How much you get

Con Edison advertises up to $4,000 for professionally installed insulation and air sealing under its residential rebate program ("Get up to $4,000 to Make Your Home Weather Ready"). A separate, White Plains-specific source cites a lower ceiling of up to $2,000 for attic, wall, and basement insulation and air sealing.

These two figures disagree ($4,000 vs. $2,000), which may reflect different scopes of work or different program tiers. Confirm the exact ceiling for your project directly with Con Edison before budgeting.

Needs verification: The $4,000 and $2,000 figures conflict across our two sources — reconcile with Con Edison's live page before publishing a specific number.

Local programs: WestCOP operates the local WAP delivery office in Yonkers — no Yonkers-specific rebate beyond state EmPower+/Comfort Home was found.
