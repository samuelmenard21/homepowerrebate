The Stacking Advantage: New York vs. Other States

Why New York wins

Short answer: Federal HEAR is authorized through 2030, but confirm it's still funded before you apply. Most states' HEAR programs died after 2025. New York homeowners can stack three payment sources. That's rare.

Typical New York Path (Con Edison, NYC):

Con Edison rebate ($2,000–$10,000) + NYS Clean Heat ($6,000–$10,000) + Federal HEAR ($4,000–$8,000) + DAC bonus if eligible (+$1,000–$2,000) = $14K–$28K+ total.

Your out-of-pocket: $6,000–$14,000 after stacking (vs. full $18,000–$22,000 cost). Paid back in 4–6 years from energy savings.

States without federal HEAR: Only get utility + state = $8K–$12K total. New York doubles it.

Quick Questions

Which utility do I have?
Check your electric bill. Your utility name is listed at the top or on the account section. Or call 411 and ask. Con Edison covers NYC and most of Westchester. National Grid covers Buffalo, Rochester, Syracuse, and most of upstate. NYSEG covers central NY. If unsure, contact us with your zip code.
Can I really stack all three rebates?
Yes. Stacking rules vary by utility. Con Edison allows state + federal HEAR stacking. National Grid allows stacking. NYSEG changed rules in June 2025, now pushes everything through state Clean Heat first. Verify timing with your utility before applying.
What's the fastest rebate path?
Start with federal HEAR (income-qualified, 4–6 week timeline). Layer state Clean Heat after. Con Edison adds utility rebate separately. Each program has its own application—plan 8–16 weeks total for all three to process.
Am I income-qualified for federal HEAR?
If your household income is under 80% of your area's median income (AMI), you likely qualify. NYC: roughly under $75K single income. Upstate: under $65K. States vary. Run our assessment to check.
Do I qualify for EmPower+ (100% cost coverage)?
EmPower+ is only available in Con Edison territory (NYC and Westchester). Low-income households (<60% AMI) get 100% coverage. Moderate-income (60–80% AMI) get 50%. See NYC details for EmPower+ specifics.
