Buffalo Heat Pump Rebates 2026: National Grid

What Buffalo homeowners can really get in 2026, checked against National Grid and NYSERDA on September 26, 2026.

Short answer: In Buffalo, the main heat pump rebate is NYS Clean Heat (run by National Grid): up to $10,000 ($12,000 in a Disadvantaged Community) for a whole-home system, taken off your contractor’s bill. It is one rebate — not several that add up. Lower-income households can get much more through EmPower+, and the federal tax credits ended on December 31, 2025.

How much is the heat pump rebate in Buffalo?

Amounts for a single-family home. You get one line from this table, not several.

What you installStandardIn a Disadvantaged Community

Apartments and homes under 1,000 sq ft get about half these amounts. Your participating contractor applies for you.

What makes Buffalo different

National Grid supplies electricity in Buffalo, while most homes get gas from National Fuel Gas. Lake-effect winters are long and cold, so pick a heat pump on the cold-climate list and get the attic insulated and air-sealed first — many of Buffalo’s older wood-frame homes leak a lot of heat.

Buffalo’s housing is old: many homes are wood-frame doubles (two-family flats) and bungalows built before 1940, often with little wall insulation. That is why insulation is such a common search here — it usually pays back faster than anything else.

Winters are cold and snowy, so a cold-climate heat pump should be sized with a proper load calculation, and many Buffalo homes keep their gas furnace as backup for the coldest nights. That usually counts as a partial system, which earns the smaller partial-load amount.

Outdoor units need to sit above the snow line and away from roof drip edges, where ice can build up.

Insulation rebates in Buffalo

For insulation and air sealing, Buffalo homeowners have three real options: NYSERDA Comfort Home ($2,500 or $3,000 per package, +$2,000 for windows), EmPower+ if your income qualifies (free or 50% covered), and National Grid’s own efficiency offers — see our Buffalo insulation page. The federal 25C insulation tax credit ended December 31, 2025, so it no longer applies.

What does not stack

Clean Heat is the National Grid rebate, so never add the two together. Clean Heat is capped at 70% of cost (85% in a DAC). There is no separate federal HEAR cheque — New York delivers it through EmPower+. The federal 25C/25D tax credits ended December 31, 2025.

Income-qualified? EmPower+ can cover far more than the utility rebate. Check the state DAC map too — it can raise your amount.

What to do next in Buffalo

  1. Start with attic insulation and air sealing — it makes every heat pump option cheaper.
  2. Decide early if you will keep your gas furnace as backup, because it changes which rebate tier you get.
  3. Get 2–3 quotes and ask each contractor to show the rebate tier on the quote.

Common questions

How much is the heat pump rebate in Buffalo?

NYS Clean Heat (run by National Grid) pays up to $10,000 ($12,000 in a Disadvantaged Community) for a whole-home heat pump in Buffalo, depending on home size, whether you remove the old system, and DAC status.

Is there an insulation rebate in Buffalo?

Yes, through NYSERDA's Comfort Home ($2,500-$3,000 per package, plus $2,000 for windows) and, if your income qualifies, EmPower+. The federal 25C insulation credit ended December 31, 2025.

Is the federal tax credit still available for heat pumps?

No. The federal 25C and 25D credits ended for anything installed after December 31, 2025.
