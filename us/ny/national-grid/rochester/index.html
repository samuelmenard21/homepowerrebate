Rochester Heat Pump Rebates: National Grid Stacking

Upstate New York heat pump rebates for Rochester homeowners: National Grid + state + federal stacking for $11K–$20K+.

Monroe County served by National Grid. Long cold winters = high heat pump savings.

What Rochester homeowners can claim

Rochester is in National Grid territory. Stack utility + state + federal rebates for maximum savings on your heat pump installation.

National Grid Rebate

$1,500–$4,000

Utility-level rebate. Equipment type affects amount.

NYS Clean Heat

$6,000–$10,000

State rebate. Income-qualified. Same statewide.

Federal HEAR

$4,000–$8,000

Federal program authorized through 2030, though funding can change — confirm current status before applying. Income-qualified.

Typical Total

$11,500–$22,000

Combined rebates from all three programs.

Energy Savings

$1,000–$1,400/year

Rochester cold climate maximizes heat pump value.

Payback Period

2–5 years

After rebates applied, system pays for itself quickly.

Real Rochester Example

Rochester homeowner, $130K household income:

  • National Grid rebate: $3,000
  • NYS Clean Heat: $8,000
  • Federal HEAR: $5,000
  • Total rebates: $16,000

Heat pump cost: $19,000. Your out-of-pocket: $3,000. Energy savings: $1,300/year. System pays for itself in 2.3 years.

How to claim

  1. Confirm National Grid serves your address
  2. Apply for National Grid pre-approval (2–4 weeks)
  3. Apply to state Clean Heat and federal HEAR simultaneously
  4. Contractor performs installation (1–3 days)
  5. Rebates arrive 4–8 weeks post-completion
