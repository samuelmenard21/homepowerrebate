How each PSEG rebate works

PSEG Long Island Heat Pump Rebate

$4,000–$7,500 (income-tiered)

Most Babylon households qualify for the full $7,500. Installed cost typically runs $13,000–$22,000.

How it works: Contractor applies through PSEG after installation with proof of purchase and system specs. Confirm your contractor is comfortable filing PSEG paperwork before signing. PSEG heat pump rebates →

Home Battery Storage ($200/kWh)

~$2,700 for 13.5 kWh system

Covers a portion of battery costs against ~$12,000 installed cost. Also provides backup during outages.

How it works: Applied for through PSEG after installation. Confirm your battery model is on PSEG/NYSERDA's qualifying equipment list before purchase.

Heat Pump Water Heater Rebate

$1,200

Replaces gas, electric, or oil water heaters with a heat pump unit.

Renting or living in a condo in Babylon?

If you rent

The PSEG heat pump and battery rebates go to whoever owns and installs the equipment — typically your landlord. No PSEG renter-specific program currently exists. If you want these upgrades, the ask goes to your landlord or property manager.

If you own a condo unit

In-unit upgrades you control (heat pump water heater, smart thermostat) may qualify for rebates with HOA approval, especially for shared wall or electrical concerns. Confirm HOA approval in writing before applying.

FAQ for Babylon residents

How much can a Babylon homeowner get for a heat pump?

PSEG pays $4,000 to $7,500 depending on household income. Most Babylon households qualify for the full $7,500. Installed cost typically runs $13,000–$22,000, so the rebate covers a meaningful share.

Is there a solar rebate here?

No — PSEG Long Island does not currently offer a utility solar rebate. A typical 6 kW system costs roughly $15,000–$18,000 and saves around $1,200 a year, paying back in about 13 years. The federal solar tax credit expired December 31, 2025.

What about a home battery?

PSEG offers $200/kWh through NYSERDA, which works out to roughly $2,700 for a typical 13.5 kWh system. A battery also provides backup during outages, increasingly valuable in Babylon.

Solar

NY-Sun Affordable Solar (income-eligible)

No PSEG rebate; ~$0.40/watt (state)

Confirmed negative finding: PSEG Long Island offers no solar hardware rebate. The only option is NY-Sun's Affordable Solar program (~$0.40/watt) for households at or below ~80% area median income; market-rate homeowners get net metering + the 25% state tax credit only.

Source: /us/ny/pseg/babylon/solar/ · PSEG Long Island
Battery

PSEG Long Island battery storage program

Program exists, amount unverified

Long Island is excluded from NYSERDA's statewide storage block — PSEG runs its own separate incentive, but no current $/kWh figure was confirmed in this research pass.
