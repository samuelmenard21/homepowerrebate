Common questions

What's the heat pump rebate in Allentown?

PPL Electric's Act 129 program offers up to $450 for an air-source heat pump and $400 for a mini-split. Confirm current Phase V amounts with PPL before purchasing.

Do I need to confirm this rebate is still active in Allentown?

Yes. Pennsylvania utilities are transitioning from Act 129 Phase IV (closed May 31, 2026) to Phase V (June 2026–May 2031), and not every utility has published new amounts yet. Confirm current status with PPL Electric before assuming a figure is live.
