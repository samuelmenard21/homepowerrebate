PPL Electric: $400

Water Heater Rebates in Allentown

PPL Electric pays $400 for an ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater, and Allentown has plenty of homes where it fits. If your water heater is electric or oil-fired, this is worth a look. Here is what to check and how to claim it.

What water heater rebates pay in Allentown

We re-read PPL Electric's heat pump water heater page on October 5, 2026. The $400 offer was listed with an Apply for Rebate button.

Open

PPL heat pump water heater

$400

ENERGY STAR certified with a uniform energy factor (UEF) of 3.3 or higher. Applies to PPL Electric customers.

Closed

Federal tax credit (25C)

Ended

Ended for spending after December 31, 2025.

What the $400 covers

It is a flat rebate on the unit, not a share of the cost. You pay the installer, then claim the $400 from PPL. Check Best Offers on PPL's store in case your household qualifies for extra help.

Why it fits many Allentown homes

The US Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020 to 2024) shows 39.5% of Allentown households heat with electricity and 14.1% with fuel oil. Homes like these often heat water the same way. An electric tank swapped for a heat pump model uses much less power. An oil-fired water heater swapped for one means fewer oil deliveries.

Gas homes (44.2% of households) save less, since gas water heating is already fairly cheap. A heat pump model can still make sense if you plan to leave gas over time.

How a heat pump water heater works

A plain electric tank heats water with a metal element, like a kettle. A heat pump water heater works more like a fridge in reverse. A small fan pulls warm air over a coil, a compressor moves that heat into the tank, and the cooled air blows out the side. Because it moves heat instead of making it, it uses far less electricity for the same hot shower.

Most models also have a backup element and a few modes. Heat pump only is the cheapest to run. Hybrid mode uses the element when you need a lot of hot water fast, for example on a busy morning. If your family is large, ask about tank size so you don't lean on the element every day.

Space, drain and power

Allentown's homes are mostly older: 82.1% were built before 1980. Water heaters in these homes usually sit in a basement, which tends to suit a heat pump model. Before you buy, check:

  • Enough open space around the unit for air flow. Read the maker's minimum room size.
  • A floor drain or a spot for a small condensate pump.
  • A 240-volt circuit. Electric tanks usually have one. Gas or oil tanks often don't, so budget for an electrician.

The unit cools the air around it. In a damp basement that is a bonus in summer.

Renters

Most Allentown households rent: 56.8%, per the same Census survey. The rebate goes to whoever buys the unit, which is usually the owner. If you rent and pay your own electric bill, it is worth showing your landlord the $400 offer when the old tank nears the end.

Plan ahead

A water heater usually gets replaced in a hurry, when it leaks. If yours is over ten years old, get a heat pump quote now so you are not stuck with whatever plain tank is in stock on the day it fails.

How to get the PPL water heater rebate

  1. Pick an ENERGY STAR certified heat pump water heater with a UEF of 3.3 or higher.
  2. Check the space, drain and electrical circuit with your plumber.
  3. Install it and keep the receipt and model number.
  4. Apply on PPL's rebate store. The deadline is shown when you apply.

Common questions

How much is PPL's water heater rebate?

$400 for an ENERGY STAR certified heat pump water heater.

Does PPL pay for a regular electric tank?

No. The rebate is for heat pump water heaters only.

Is there a federal credit too?

No. The federal credit ended for spending after December 31, 2025.

Can I replace an oil water heater with one?

Yes, as long as you are a PPL Electric customer and the new unit is ENERGY STAR certified. You will likely need a new electrical circuit.

Will it make my basement cold?

It cools the air around it a little. In most basements that is fine, and it also dries the air.

What efficiency does the water heater need?

PPL asks for an ENERGY STAR certified model with a uniform energy factor of 3.3 or higher. Check the UEF on the label before you buy.
