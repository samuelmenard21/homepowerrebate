Up to 35% / $17,500

Up to 35% / $17,500 — The statewide PA Sunshine Solar Program rebates up to 35% of a residential solar PV project's cost, capped around $17,500 for systems up to 10kW (or $5,000 for solar thermal), awarded first-come first-served. This stacks with the 30% federal solar tax credit.

Common questions

Is there a solar rebate in Erie?

Yes — the statewide PA Sunshine Solar Program can rebate up to 35% of a residential solar project, capped near $17,500 for PV, first-come first-served, stacking with the 30% federal solar credit.

Do I need to confirm this rebate is still active in Erie?

Yes. Pennsylvania utilities are transitioning from Act 129 Phase IV (closed May 31, 2026) to Phase V (June 2026–May 2031), and not every utility has published new amounts yet. Confirm current status with Penelec (FirstEnergy) before assuming a figure is live.
