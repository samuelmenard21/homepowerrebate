Do I need to confirm this rebate is still active in Pittsburgh?

Yes. Pennsylvania utilities are transitioning from Act 129 Phase IV (closed May 31, 2026) to Phase V (June 2026–May 2031), and not every utility has published new amounts yet. Confirm current status with Duquesne Light before assuming a figure is live.
