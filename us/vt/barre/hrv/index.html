How much you get

We couldn't confirm a standalone HRV or ERV rebate in Vermont for 2026. Efficiency Vermont's residential rebate catalog, which Green Mountain Power customers in Barre draw from, lists rebates for heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, and whole-home weatherization, but as of 2026 there's no separate line item for a heat recovery ventilator or energy recovery ventilator.

Ventilation work may still be covered as part of a broader Home Performance with ENERGY STAR project, Efficiency Vermont's weatherization program (up to $7,000 custom incentive), if an HRV or ERV is bundled into a larger air-sealing and insulation job — see our insulation page for how that works. But there's no confirmed flat rebate specifically for a standalone HRV/ERV unit.

Barre's granite-industry-era housing stock includes a large share of older homes with minimal built-in ventilation, so pairing an HRV or ERV with any weatherization project is worth pricing out even without a dedicated rebate covering it.

Common questions

What's the HRV/ERV rebate in Barre?

We found no standalone Vermont rebate specifically for HRV or ERV systems in 2026. Ventilation work may be eligible as part of a broader Efficiency Vermont weatherization project, but there's no confirmed separate flat rebate through Green Mountain Power.

Do I need to confirm this is still accurate?

Yes. Utility and state rebate programs change funding levels and eligibility rules through the year. Confirm current status with Green Mountain Power or Efficiency Vermont before assuming a figure is live.
