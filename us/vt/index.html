Home Energy Rebates in Vermont

Complete 2026 guide to Vermont home energy rebates — Efficiency Vermont's statewide efficiency programs, Green Mountain Power's battery and EV incentives, and Burlington Electric Department's municipal rebates.

How Vermont's rebates are organized

Vermont works differently than most states. Instead of splitting rebates by investor-owned utility, almost all efficiency rebates — heat pumps (outside Burlington), water heaters, insulation, and thermostats — run through a single statewide nonprofit efficiency utility called Efficiency Vermont. Green Mountain Power (GMP), the state's largest electric utility, runs its own separate battery storage and EV charger programs. Burlington is the one exception: it's served by its own municipal utility, Burlington Electric Department (BED), which runs its own heat pump and EV charger rebates instead of Efficiency Vermont's. Statewide programs that apply no matter your utility

Open

🔥 Heat Pump Rebate (Efficiency Vermont)

Up to $2,200

Efficiency Vermont pays up to $2,200 for a ducted cold-climate heat pump ($475 ductless), plus a $200–$2,200 income-eligible bonus. Burlington runs its own separate program through BED instead. Efficiency Vermont →

Open

💧 Heat Pump Water Heater Rebate

Up to $1,000

Efficiency Vermont pays $600 standard, up to $1,000 for income-eligible households, statewide. Efficiency Vermont →

Open

🏭 Weatherization / Insulation

Up to $7,000 (income-qualified)

Home Performance with ENERGY STAR pays 75% of cost up to $3,000 for general households; income-qualified households get up to 90% back (moving to a $7,000 flat cap July 1, 2026). Very-low-income households may qualify for a fully free retrofit through the state Weatherization Assistance Program. Efficiency Vermont →

Expired

🌴 Federal 25C / 25D Credits

Expired 12/31/2025

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act ended the federal energy-efficiency (25C) and residential clean energy (25D) tax credits for installations after December 31, 2025. Neither applies to 2026 installs in Vermont. ENERGY STAR federal credit status →
