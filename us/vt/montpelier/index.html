Home Energy Rebates in Montpelier, Vermont

Montpelier's homes burn oil (45%), propane (23%), electricity (16%) and wood (9%) in a pattern no other city in our Vermont set shares. Here is what the state and Green Mountain Power pay, and who they pay it to.

The rebates that matter most in Montpelier

We re-read Efficiency Vermont's rebate list and Green Mountain Power's program pages on October 5, 2026. Montpelier is Green Mountain Power territory. Amounts are maximums.

Open

Heat pump

$375 to $2,200 off

Efficiency Vermont instant discounts by system type, plus up to $600 for controls. Green Mountain Power adds $2,000 per condenser for households at or under 80% of area median income ($91,200 for four in Washington County).

Open

Weatherization

Up to $3,000 ($7,000)

75% of qualifying insulation and air sealing, higher cap if income-eligible, while funding lasts.

Open

Heat pump water heater

$600, up to $1,000

Efficiency Vermont, through the Green Mountain Power page for your area.

Open

Home battery

$850 to $950 per kW

Green Mountain Power's Bring Your Own Device program pays by the kW of storage you share at peaks, with $100 more per kW for some solar retrofits.

Open

Electric vehicle and charger

Up to $2,500, plus $650

Green Mountain Power: up to $2,500 for an all-electric vehicle, and up to $650 toward a home charger installation if you buy or lease in 2026.

Open

Appliances

$25 to $400

Efficiency Vermont rebates on thermostats, dryers, dehumidifiers and stoves, and Green Mountain Power's $200 induction cooktop rebate.

Statewide pages cover solar, batteries, EV chargers, appliances and thermostats and windows and doors.

What the Census says about Montpelier

The Census Bureau's 2020 to 2024 American Community Survey counts 3,721 occupied homes. 1,678 heat mainly with oil or kerosene, 841 with propane, 583 with electricity, 322 with wood and 221 with piped gas. 1,487 of 3,921 housing units (38%) were built in 1939 or earlier. 53% of households own their home and 47% rent.

Propane and wood households

Propane is a larger share here than in Barre, and it arrives by truck like oil. A ductless heat pump can carry daytime heating, leaving propane for the coldest nights. Wood households (322) can use Efficiency Vermont's weatherization and heat pump offers, though wood-heating customers are excluded from its ground source rebate.

Renters and small buildings

911 units sit in two-, three- and four-unit buildings, and 894 in larger ones. Efficiency Vermont's heat pump rebates are open to rental properties, though the income bonus is not. A tenant who pays for heat should ask the landlord about weatherization, which is a primary-residence offer at the $7,000 tier.

Cold

Montpelier's average January low is -11.9°C, tied with Barre as the lowest average, and the record low between 2015 and 2024 was -30.7°C. That is why every offer on this site for Montpelier is for cold-climate rated equipment.

Dates to watch in Montpelier

Green Mountain Power's all-electric and plug-in hybrid vehicle rebates apply through December 31, 2026, as does its $200 induction cooktop rebate. Efficiency Vermont's weatherization offer, effective July 1, 2026, lasts while funding lasts. Heat pump discounts changed on January 1, 2026 for ductless systems and April 1, 2025 for ducted systems, so older figures you may see on other sites can be out of date.

Smaller upgrades add up

Efficiency Vermont lists up to $100 on a smart thermostat, up to $400 on a clothes dryer, $150 on a window air conditioner, $400 on a wood or pellet stove at purchase and from $25 on a dehumidifier. For a wood-heating household that is worth a look, because the stove rebate applies at the register. Our appliance guide lists the products and Green Mountain Power's own yard and kitchen rebates.

Common questions

Which utility serves Montpelier?

Green Mountain Power, per our verified utility list. Efficiency Vermont's statewide offers apply too.

What is the income limit for Green Mountain Power's $2,000 heat pump rebate?

$91,200 for a household of four in Washington County, which is 80% of area median income.

Is there a Montpelier city rebate?

We did not find one. The state and Green Mountain Power offers are the ones we verified.

Should I weatherize or buy a heat pump first?

Usually weatherize. Efficiency Vermont pays 75% of a qualifying project up to $3,000, or $7,000 if income-eligible.

Can I charge an EV at home with a rebate?

Green Mountain Power pays up to $650 toward charger installation if you buy or lease an EV in 2026, and up to $2,500 on the vehicle.
