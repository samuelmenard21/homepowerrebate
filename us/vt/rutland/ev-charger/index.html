EV Charger Rebates in Rutland

Up to $500 + free charger option

Green Mountain Power pays up to $500 toward a home Level 2 EV charger when it's connected to GMP and you're enrolled in a discounted off-peak charging rate, plus a $150 installation discount through GMP's partner Treehouse. GMP also offers a free Level 2 charger with the purchase or lease of an EV while enrolled in its home charging program, through December 31, 2026.

Rutland’s older housing stock

Rutland County has some of the oldest housing in Vermont — more than a quarter of the county’s homes were built before 1939, according to the Vermont Housing Needs Assessment, well ahead of the statewide median build year of 1974. Homes from that era often weren’t built with an attached garage or a 200-amp panel, so factor a possible panel upgrade into your Level 2 charger installation cost alongside the GMP rebate.

Common questions

What's the ev charger rebate in Rutland?

Green Mountain Power pays up to $500 toward a home Level 2 EV charger connected to GMP's discounted charging rate, plus a $150 installation discount, and offers a free Level 2 charger with an EV purchase through December 31, 2026 while enrolled in the program.

Do I need to confirm this rebate is still active in Rutland?

Yes. Utility and state rebate programs change funding levels and eligibility rules through the year. Confirm current status with Green Mountain Power or Efficiency Vermont before assuming a figure is live.
