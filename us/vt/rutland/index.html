Common questions

What's the heat pump rebate in Rutland?

Efficiency Vermont pays up to $475 for a ductless cold-climate heat pump or up to $2,200 for a ducted system, provided the equipment is ENERGY STAR Cold Climate certified. Income-eligible households can add a $200 to $2,200 bonus on top, depending on income tier and equipment type.

What's the battery storage rebate in Rutland?

Green Mountain Power's Bring Your Own Device program pays $850 per kW of enrolled storage for a 3-hour discharge commitment, or $950 per kW for 4-hour discharge, plus up to $100 per kW extra for retrofitting storage onto an existing solar system. You pick your own battery and installer.

What's the solar rebate in Rutland?

Vermont has no flat cash rebate for solar. Homeowners benefit from net metering (roughly $0.184/kWh blended credit through their utility), a state sales tax exemption on the system, and in some towns a property tax exemption. The federal 30% solar tax credit expired after December 31, 2025 and does not apply to 2026 installs.
