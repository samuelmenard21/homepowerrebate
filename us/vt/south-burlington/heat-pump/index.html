How much you get

open
Up to $2,200

Efficiency Vermont pays up to $475 for a qualifying ductless (mini-split) cold-climate heat pump, or up to $2,200 for a ducted cold-climate system. Equipment must carry ENERGY STAR Cold Climate certification to qualify — Vermont's design temperatures (-10°F to -15°F) make this a hard requirement, not a bonus tier. Income-eligible households (through Green Mountain Power/Efficiency Vermont's low- and moderate-income guidelines) can add a bonus of $200–$2,200 on top, depending on income tier and system type.

Common questions

What's the heat pump rebate in South Burlington?

Efficiency Vermont pays up to $475 for a ductless cold-climate heat pump or up to $2,200 for a ducted system, provided the equipment is ENERGY STAR Cold Climate certified. Income-eligible households can add a $200 to $2,200 bonus on top, depending on income tier and equipment type.

Do I need to confirm this rebate is still active in South Burlington?

Yes. Utility and state rebate programs change funding levels and eligibility rules through the year. Confirm current status with Green Mountain Power or Efficiency Vermont before assuming a figure is live.
