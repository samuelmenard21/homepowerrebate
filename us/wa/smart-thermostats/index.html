Smart Thermostat Rebates in Washington

Four Washington utilities pay for a smart thermostat, from $50 to $175. The rules differ by utility, so check what your home heats with before you buy.

What Washington utilities pay for smart thermostats

We read each utility's page on October 6, 2026. Avista was not checked for a thermostat rebate, so we list none for it.

Open

Clark Public Utilities

$140

An instant rebate through the utility's online marketplace on four models, for homes heated with an electric furnace or heat pump.

Open

Puget Sound Energy

$75

$175 by income for central systems or $130 for line-voltage thermostats. Heat must be electric or natural gas from PSE. Buy and install by December 31, 2026.

Open

Tacoma Power

$75

Up to two rebates, one per heating system, for homes with an electric forced-air furnace or central heat pump. Line-voltage thermostats earn $25.

Open

Seattle City Light

$50

An instant discount on Mysa and Sinope thermostats bought from the maker's City Light page.

Working it out

A Vancouver, Washington household with an electric furnace that buys a thermostat from Clark's marketplace gets $140 off at checkout, which Clark says can make one model free. A Tacoma household with an electric furnace and a separate heat pump in an addition could claim two $75 rebates. A Seattle household pays $50 less on a Mysa or Sinope unit.

Rules that differ

Puget Sound Energy requires a one-thermostat central system using a heat pump, furnace or boiler for the $75 rebate, a thermostat registered with the maker over Wi-Fi, and no earlier PSE thermostat rebate at the same address in the last five years. Apply within 60 days of purchase. Tacoma Power requires the thermostat to be on the Bonneville Power Administration approved list, with occupancy detection, scheduling, Wi-Fi and heat pump auxiliary heat control, and you must request the rebate within 90 days. Dual-fuel systems and cooling-only systems do not qualify at Tacoma Power.

Check the model before you buy

Each program accepts only listed models. Clark's marketplace shows four. Seattle City Light's discount applies to Mysa and Sinope thermostats bought through those makers' City Light pages. PSE and Tacoma Power keep lists of approved products. A thermostat that works with your system but is not on the list earns nothing. Match your heating type to the thermostat as well, since a line-voltage thermostat for baseboard heat is a different product from one for a central furnace.

Is a smart thermostat worth buying?

The pages we read say a smart thermostat helps control heating, but do not give a savings number that we can quote. The Seattle City Light page says setting your thermostat lower when you sleep or are away can save as much as $180 a year, though that is advice about any thermostat, not about a smart one. Treat the rebate as a way to lower the price of a device you would buy for convenience, and treat bill savings as a bonus.

Other Washington utilities

If your utility is not on this list, call it and ask. Avista's page that we read lists no thermostat rebate, but we did not search every sub-page. Our Washington rebate hub shows which programs we verified.

How to claim a Washington thermostat rebate

  1. Find your utility on your bill and note whether your heat is electric or gas.
  2. Pick a thermostat from your utility's approved list that fits your heating system.
  3. Buy through the utility's marketplace or store page if it has one, to get the discount at checkout.
  4. Install it and connect it to Wi-Fi so it is registered with the maker.
  5. If you must apply after the purchase, send the receipt inside the deadline: 60 days at PSE, 90 days at Tacoma Power.

Common questions

Which Washington utility pays the most for a smart thermostat?

On our October 6, 2026 reading, Puget Sound Energy pays up to $175 for income-qualified central systems, and Clark Public Utilities pays $140 for everyone who qualifies on heating type.

Can I get a rebate for a gas furnace thermostat?

PSE covers homes heated with PSE natural gas or electricity. Tacoma Power and Clark Public Utilities are for electric heat.

Do I need to apply after buying?

At PSE and Tacoma Power, yes, within 60 and 90 days. At Clark and Seattle City Light the discount comes at checkout.

How many can I claim?

Tacoma Power allows up to two, one per heating system. PSE allows one in five years at the same address for central systems.

Is there a federal credit?

No. The IRS says the home improvement credit covers improvements through December 31, 2025.
