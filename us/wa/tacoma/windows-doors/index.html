Window and Door Rebates in Tacoma

Tacoma Power pays $100 per window and $400 per sliding door when you replace single-pane glass, and half of that when you replace double-pane windows in metal frames. Your home must heat with electricity.

What window rebates pay in Tacoma

We read Tacoma Power's window page on October 6, 2026. A Tacoma Power Participating Contractor must install the windows. The page we read does not state a cap on the number of windows.

Open

$100 per window

Replacing existing single-pane windows in a home that heats mainly with electricity.

Open

Single-pane glass sliding doors

$400 per door

Replacing a single-pane glass slider.

Open

Double-pane metal-framed windows

$50 per window

Only if the old frames are metal.

Open

Double-pane metal-framed sliding doors

$200 per door

Same rule for the frame.

Closed

Federal tax credit

$0

The IRS says the energy efficient home improvement credit covers improvements made through December 31, 2025.

Working it out

A Tacoma home with ten single-pane windows and one single-pane slider could earn 10 times $100 plus $400, which is $1,400. The same home with double-pane metal-framed windows would earn 10 times $50 plus $200, or $700. Replacing double-pane windows that have vinyl or wood frames earns nothing, so check the frame before you plan.

Which windows count

Tacoma Power only pays when the existing windows and doors are single-pane or double-pane with metal frames. That is a narrow rule. Double-pane windows with vinyl or wood frames do not qualify. The US Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2020 to 2024) says about 64 percent of Tacoma homes were built before 1980 and 27 percent before 1940, so many older homes have single-pane glass still in place. About 63 percent of Tacoma homes heat with electricity, which also matches the program rule.

What does not qualify

Manufactured homes, new construction, do-it-yourself installs and buildings with five or more units are not eligible. Ask your contractor in writing what type of window you are replacing, because a mistake on that line can cancel the rebate. Take photos of the old windows before they are removed, showing the frames and glass. Keep the shipping slip for the new windows, which Tacoma Power requires.

Papers you need

Send the application for your project, a contractor bid (or the contractor's form), an invoice that meets Tacoma Power's rules and the window shipping slip. Tacoma Power can also point you to a list of required documents on its page. The rebate covers part of your costs.

Ask the contractor to measure and list every window and slider by room, with its glass type and frame type, before the bid is signed. That list becomes your proof of what was replaced. Also ask what the bid says about trim, disposal of the old windows and the warranty, since those costs are not part of the rebate. A written, itemized bid protects you if the number of qualifying windows changes during the job.

Windows are costly and the rebate is small, so think of it as a bonus on a project you wanted to do for comfort or repairs. If energy savings are your main goal, insulation usually pays more per dollar in the Tacoma Power program: $750 for each area, compared with $100 for each window.

If you rent or cannot qualify

With about 44 percent of Tacoma households renting, many readers cannot choose their windows. Ask your landlord whether they plan to apply, and point them to Tacoma Power's page. If your household income is at or below 150 percent of area median income, the City of Tacoma is a state partner for home electrification rebates, though that program lists heat pumps, water heaters, induction cooking, dryers and wiring, not windows. For help with drafty windows on a low income, ask your community action agency about the state weatherization program.

How to claim a Tacoma window rebate

  1. Confirm that Tacoma Power serves your home and that your main heat is electric.
  2. Photograph your old windows, including the frames.
  3. Hire a Tacoma Power Participating Contractor.
  4. Keep the shipping slip, invoice and bid.
  5. Send the application and papers to Tacoma Power.

Common questions

How much does Tacoma Power pay for windows?

$100 per window and $400 per sliding door for single-pane, and $50 and $200 for double-pane metal-framed windows.

Do vinyl double-pane windows qualify?

No. Existing windows must be single-pane or double-pane metal framed.

Do I need a contractor?

Yes. A Participating Contractor must install the windows.

Does my home need electric heat?

Yes. The page says the home's primary heat source must be electric.

Is there a federal credit?

No. It ended after 2025.
